# The Nine Sectors (नव खण्ड — *Nav Khanda*)

Rangbhoomi is divided into nine Sectors. Each Sector obeys a **different law of pulling** — the same
thread does different work depending on where you stand. A Naga who can drown a street in Jalkhand
is nearly powerless in Agnikhand.

This is the series' answer to "multiple powers and sectors": **one power system, nine expressions.**

## The Sectors we meet in Chapter 1

### AGNIKHAND — अग्निखण्ड — The Ash Sector  📍 Chapter 1's setting

**Law:** power is burned. Everything in Agnikhand spends fast and hard, including people.

**Geography:** a terraced city built inside the caldera of a volcano that stopped erupting four
hundred years ago and has not stopped *smoking*. Ash falls like snow, permanently. The terraces climb
the crater wall; the rich live high where the air is clear, the poor live low in the **Ashfall
Basin** where the drifts are waist-deep.

**The Spindle:** the Loom's local anchor — a pillar of light in the centre of the caldera, where
Unspoolings are held. Chapter 1 Page 1 opens on it.

**Economy:** thread-mining. Ash Sector thread is cheap, fast and low-quality. Agnikhand supplies
Rangbhoomi's labour and most of its Asura.

**Institutions:**
- **The Ash Council** — governs the Sector. Registers every Sutra-mark. **Refuses to register Manavkin.**
- **The Mendery** — a licensed workhouse where debt-carriers are bound. Ira's mother was bound there.
- **The Unspooling Court** — runs the ceremony. Public. Ranked. Recorded.

**Tone:** grimy, hot, crowded, funny at street level and brutal at the top. Ash in everything — teeth,
food, hair. The reader should be able to feel it.

## Sectors held back deliberately

- **Yantrakhand** — sealed for four hundred years. Graffiti in Agnikhand claims it isn't empty.
  Foreshadow only until ~Chapter 180.
- **Shunyakhand** — does not appear on any map. The series' final act. Nobody who has entered it has
  ever been confirmed dead, which is somehow worse.

## Cross-Sector travel rule

A character's thread **weakens outside its home Sector** and **strengthens inside it**. This is why
nobody conquers Rangbhoomi, why every war in the series is a stalemate, and why the day someone
solves it, everything breaks.
