JADI — जड़ी

First appearance: Chapter 008, Page 005 (the doorway of the Knot & Nail) Kind: Kshudra · Age: seventy-odd — enrolled at twelve, forty-four years ago Function: First pupil of the School of the Braided Thread. Keeper of the roster. The root. Ref sheet: jadi-ref.png — 8 panels (the house standard; drawn in the tall stacked layout) Ref sheet panels: front · side · back · face · detail (the left palm — grey braid, crimson second strand) · hands (right steady and square against left flattened and marked) · kit (mending cord, notched tally-stick, folding stool, blank sheets tied with cord, hand lamp, dark cloth, candle stub, purse) · action (the cellar-doorway lesson — three seated pupils, knots counted off on her fingers)

Visual sheet

  • Silhouette: tall for a basin woman, badly stooped, and — uniquely in this cast — wide in the shoulders from a lifetime of work. She does not read as frail. She reads as used hard and still standing.
  • Coat: the chapter's best costume. A good coat from forty years ago, mended by an expert roughly a hundred times. Each mend is visible, deliberate, and the work of somebody extremely skilled. The coat is her résumé. Do not draw it ragged — draw it maintained.
  • Left hand: the whole story. A braided grey mark flattened by decades of work, seam opened at the root, skin grown over part of the thread from nine months of patient cutting. From Chapter 008 Page 010 onward it carries a second strand — crimson-under-grey — braided into the first.
  • Right hand: steady, competent, a mender's hand that still works. Draw the contrast every time both are on panel.
  • Face: deep-lined, Kshudra, unsmiling without being grim. She has resting eyes that watch hands rather than faces — established in her first appearance, where she looks at Ira's palms for a full panel before meeting her eyes.
  • Tell: she does not fidget. When she is waiting to hear something she dislikes, she puts both hands flat and stops moving entirely — the same stillness Kessa uses, arrived at independently, which is how the reader knows they are the same kind of person.

Voice

Kshudra, flat, in order. She speaks in inventory — years, numbers, hands, who has family and who does not. She has kept forty years of a roster in her head and her sentences sound like a ledger read aloud.

She asks for things directly and states her case on the merits. When she is wrong she concedes without ceremony: "You've got a closed hand. I saw it at the door." When she is right she does not press.

Her only emotional register is the assessment. "It doesn't feel different from before." "It's the first time in my life anything's been done to me and my hand agreed to it." She does not thank people. She enrols — "When is the lesson."

What she is

The first thread the school ever put in a hand. Enrolled at twelve, two months before the Council bound Sutar, M. — which means she is the one pupil the school never finished. Rules one and two (two strands, braided) were never taught to her, because the teacher was taken before she got there. Jadi has carried a single strand for forty-four years.

She ran the school's roster for four decades: who came to the hatch, when, what went in, and what kind of hand they had. She held every name in the school in her head, deliberately, because the Roll held none.

She is the reason the mother left with a list. Five nights before Chapter 008, Sutar, M. came to the hatch and had Jadi recite all forty-one measured pupils twice.

Position

A believer in the school who was never fully inside it. Jadi's loyalty is to the work and to the roster, not to the founder. She is the only person in the cast who can say "I think she was wrong" about Sutar, M. without it being a betrayal, and in Chapter 008 Page 008 she effectively does — by demanding Ira cut the thread out.

She is also the school's living evidence that the founder's method was incomplete. Every argument Ira makes about consent in Chapter 008 is aimed at a woman who was enrolled without being asked, and Jadi is the only character who can hear that argument from the inside.

Ongoing threads

  • The next lesson. Chapter 008 closes with Jadi's one request: when is the lesson. She is the second school's first and only pupil, and she is also the only person alive who knows the roster, the years and the parts of the basin by heart.
  • The braid. Her hand is now braided; this makes her the first hand in Agnikhand that the Office's compliance chain has returned as unreadable. She is the proof of concept and the reason the Office is now looking for the source.
  • Her age and the dates. "Nobody in my family has ever lived past fifty." She has already told the reader she does not expect much time. Use this — but not as a death flag; as a clock.
