KESSA — केसा

Status: Major supporting — mentor / appraiser First appearance: Chapter 01, Page 002, Panel 4 Kind: Kshudra (क्षुद्र) — goblin, knotted thread Sector: Agnikhand (Ash Sector), Ashfall Basin Under-Market Age: unknown (Kshudra live long; she has "counted four Unspoolings") Occupation: pawnbroker / debt-appraiser — runs the stall The Knot & Nail

Appearance (art continuity)

  • Body: small, broad, low centre of gravity. Moves little, but when she moves it is final.
  • Hands: enormous for her body — scarred, knuckled, gentle anyway. They are the first thing drawn.
  • Eyes: large Kshudra cat eyes that catch light. One is always covered by the loupe once she starts appraising — so she literally sees the world two ways. Use this in panels.
  • Loupe: jeweller's loupe on a cord; pushed up on her brow when idle, flipped down over the left eye when working.
  • Clothes: layered pawned coats, mended with mismatched thread (she wears her own trade). A string of knotted tally-thread at her belt.
  • Silhouette test: tiny broad body + huge hands + loupe cord.

Rule: Kessa is never drawn rushing. Stillness is her power. If a panel shows her mid-sprint, the scene is wrong.

Personality

  • Dry, transactional speech. Everything is a price, and she says so kindly.
  • Appraises before she greets. She reads people's debt like a jeweller reads stones — out loud, without shame, because to her it is just information.
  • Secretly kind. Refuses to take Ira's needles; feeds people "on the house" and calls it a bad deal so they don't feel owed.
  • Knows more than she says. She recognises the stitch and does not explain it. She is a locked box that drips.

Voice

  • Calls Ira "mender girl" and "child" and, when serious, "be" (an affectionate Kshudra particle — keep it; it is her tell for real feeling).
  • Short declarative sentences. Ends appraisals with a number or a price.
  • Never raises her voice. Fear in Kessa is shown by her going quieter and stiller.

Abilities

Kshudra knotted thread: her thread is small but nearly impossible to cut or steal. She cannot be unweaved, and she cannot be robbed — a useful survival trait in a black market.

Debt-appraisal (learned, not magical): she can read debt-marks by eye — level, age, Sector. It is craft, honed over decades. This is why the basin trusts her valuations.

Hard canon rules:

  1. She cannot mend. She can only read marks.
  2. She will not say who sewed Ira until a later arc. Her fear of the stitch is real and personal.
  3. Her knot-thread is defensive only. She has never thrown a punch on panel; when she finally does, it must matter.

Relationship to Ira

  • First adult to show Ira kindness (refusing the needles).
  • First person to reframe "no debt" from defect to price — the series thesis.
  • Will become Ira's fence, banker, and reluctant guardian. She takes no payment for it and calls it "a bad investment," which is her way of saying she cares.

Card-game line (record from day one)

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey thread / light debt /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; knots cannot be cut /
"You're the only dry thing in the flood."

Open questions

  • Why does the stitch frighten her? What does she know about who sewed Ira?
  • Who does she owe? (A Kshudra who runs a bank always owes someone.)
  • What did she do before the basin? Her coats are mended with nine different Sector threads.
