PATRA — पत्र

Status: Supporting — broker / the principal's errand-face First appearance: Chapter 01, Page 005, Panel 3 Kind: Preta (प्रेत) — the Unwoven; thread cut Sector: unregistered. Operates in Agnikhand's grey (Pretas are legally unregistered in seven of nine Sectors; Agnikhand tolerates them the way it tolerates smoke) Age: unknown. "I was struck from a book once" is the only autobiography Patra offers Occupation: contract broker for principals who cannot or will not appear Mark location: none. Where a Sutra-mark should be, there is a smooth scar of absence over the heart. Do not draw it in Chapter 1; it is a later reveal.

Appearance (art continuity)

  • Edges: translucent. Fingertips, ear-tips, hair-tips and robe hem let light through faintly, like thin paper held to a lamp. Constant, every panel.
  • Shadow: falls toward the light source. Every panel, no exceptions. This is the cheapest and strongest Preta tell in the art language; never "fix" it.
  • Hair: long, loose, almost colourless white-silver, faintly translucent at the tips — it moves a half-beat after the head, like hair in water. (Set by images/page-005.png; the ref sheet matches it.)
  • Face: plain, average, symmetrical enough to be forgettable. Artist rule: draw Patra plain in every panel except one per page, where the face holds still and sharp and becomes memorable. The held face means Patra is about to cost someone something.
  • Name and face do not hold. People forget both mid-conversation (Page 005, Panels 3 → 6). This is canon physics, not rudeness, and not a power Patra controls.
  • Clothes: bone-grey layered broker robes, many pockets, every seam plain. No colour anywhere on the person — nothing crimson, ever, while the principal is unnamed.
  • Props: a cord at the belt hung with a dozen small blank wooden name-tags — names Patra carries for other people. None of them is Patra's.
  • Silhouette test: bowed posture + fluttering blank tags + hem glowing with transmitted light.

Rule: Patra is never drawn in motion blur, never mid-stride, never rushing. Pretas do not hurry; they are already where the paperwork is.

Personality

  • Mild, transactional, faintly kind. The almost-smile is the warmest thing in any room Patra is in, which is precisely why experienced people check their pockets.
  • Never lies. Omissions, misdirection, letting a listener mishear — constantly. An outright false sentence, never. Patra's entire market value is that every sentence is technically true.
  • No menace in the voice. The menace is in the physics: the shadow, the edges, the forgetting.
  • Envy, buried: Patra was struck from a register; Ira was never written in one. Patra considers Ira's version cleaner and says so only in later arcs: "There is a difference, and it is not the one you think."

Voice

  • Complete, unhurried sentences. Calls Ira "child" (बच्ची) and, when signing nothing, "Ira Sutar" in full — the full name is a courtesy from someone who knows what a missing record feels like.
  • Answers questions in the order they were asked, including the ones Ira decided not to ask aloud.
  • Never raises volume. When Patra repeats a sentence, it is because the first telling was the real one and somebody forgot it — which they always did.

Abilities

None that pull. The thread is cut. Patra cannot draw, store, burn or mend thread, ever.

What a Preta broker actually is:

  • Owes nothing. Nothing can be taken from Patra, so nothing can be leveraged against Patra. This is why principals who cannot appear hire Pretas: the errand cannot be squeezed for the principal's name.
  • Cannot be a party to a contract. Brokers arrange; they never sign. A contract Patra signs is void — and this is a hard rule with plot teeth later.
  • Anonymity by nature. Faces and names slide off Patra. Surveillance, memory-Sight and witness testimony all degrade.

Hard canon rules:

  1. Patra never pulls thread. Not even a spark. If a panel shows Patra using power, the panel is wrong.
  2. Patra never lies outright. Every line must survive a literal reading.
  3. The shadow is wrong in every panel Patra appears in, including crowd panels and flashbacks.
  4. Patra does not know the principal's name either — only the favour, the seal and the standing instructions. Do not let interrogation arcs shortcut through Patra.

Relationship to Ira

  • The mirror the series promised in the bible (01-the-nine-kinds.md §7): two people who owe nothing — one cut from the book, one never entered in it.
  • Patra is the only person in Chapter 1 who treats Ira's nullity as ordinary paperwork rather than defect, price or horror. That calm is more comforting than Kessa's kindness, and should feel worse.
  • Patra wants Ira registered. Not for the principal's sake. Do not explain this yet.
