Chapter 003 — Page 001 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md

Page 001 note: brings the charcoal rubbing of Bhan's turn-backs (Ch. 002 Page 009) to the reopened Knot & Nail. Stands across the counter from Kessa — first standing at the counter scene (Ch. 002's mend-pages had her seated on the crate). Reads the two turn-backs side by side, catches the school-match, and names it first: "The cutter copied my stitch." Reversed by Kessa: the cutter is the senior, not the student. Covers her palm-stitch with her hand — the wound that was made for her, not against her. Ends the page carrying the reversal: two strangers share a school, and she is the diploma.

KESSA — केसा (counter open, loupe down)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md

Page 001 note: opens the lockbox in daylight for the first time in the series — the shadowless cut-end on the counter, loupe down, performing a formal comparison against Ira's rubbing. Delivers the chapter's thesis sentence: "Child, your stitch didn't teach the cutter. The cutter taught your stitch." Wraps the cut-end back (hands-only, methodical) and draws the folded grey+crimson letter from under the counter lip — reading it again, in front of Ira as witness, after reading it alone last night (Ch. 002 Page 010's lock-turning). Her interiority is furniture arrangement: box open, comparison made, letter delivered. The lockbox is now both sides' filing cabinet, and she was not asked.

THE LETTER — पत्र (grey + crimson, two owners)

The folded posting order from Chapter 002 Page 010, opened on-panel for the first time. Formatted as a Council posting order with a crimson addition at the foot — the principal's co-signature. Transfers custody of the blink census (the pot-row's list from Ch. 002 Page 003, with the crimson cross-out) from the Reckoning Office to the Knot & Nail. Grey wax and crimson wax touching at the fold. The re-fold crease (Kessa's thumb, last night) is visible alongside the original fold.

THE LOCKBOX — ताला-बक्सा (first daylight appearance)

Kessa's Kshudra-made basalt lockbox, forty years in her possession. Small, black, one keyhole, no key visible. First on-panel appearance; first daylight consultation. The shadowless cut-end rests inside on old grey felt. The box is open but the sewer's name stays locked.

THE CUT-END — कट-सिरा (shadowless thread, first clear view)

A hand's-width piece of shadowless thread, severed cleanly at both ends with ancient turn-backs. Forty years older than Ira's stitch, same hand-school. Physically impossible: thread casts shadow, this thread does not. First clear on-panel view in the series (referenced in Ch. 001 Page 010's lockbox scene but never shown clearly).
