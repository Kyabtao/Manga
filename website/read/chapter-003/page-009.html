THREADBORN — Chapter 03 — Page 009

Chapter title: The Hand That Opens / वह हाथ जो खोलता है Arc: I — The Unspooling Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail (the next morning) Page type: The reading of the oath-link — 7 panels, chapter's chain-stop, hook at the bottom

Beats carried from Page 008: the stitch responds to the census, the cut-end moves, the four-object portrait. Page 007: Kessa's warning about reading the oath-link. Page 006: the four notes matched wrongly.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the morning decision (~14%)

Camera: Morning, Knot & Nail: Ira arrives and sits at the counter. She does not open her kit. She does not say good morning. She sets her hands flat on the counter — palm up, stitch visible — and looks at Kessa.

Image: The gesture says everything: I'm ready. Kessa reads it the way she reads everything — with her hands, not her words.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): I sat down and showed her my hands. She knew before I said it.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the preparation (~13%)

Camera: Kessa's hands: she closes the lockbox, moves it to one side, and sets the tally-thread on the counter between them — the thread she stores memories in, the thread that held the four notes. One end is in her hand; the other she offers to Ira.

Image: The tally-thread as a bridge: Kessa holds one end, Ira holds the other. The thread between them is the only object in the frame.

KESSA (dialogue, dry): If you read it, you carry it. Not the words — the weight. The chain stopped for those words. They cost something.

IRA (dialogue): I know.

PANEL 3 — Close, the visit (~14%)

Camera: The Knot & Nail's threshold: Rekhak Vahni walks past on his rounds — not stopping, procedure as usual. But today Ira is at the counter, palm up, and the stall's loupe-lamp is pointed at the door. She calls to him — one word.

Image: Ira's voice reaching the door. Rekhak stops. The chain in his hand does not stop. The oath-link is visible: dark, still, waiting.

IRA (dialogue, one word, clear): Reckoner.

PANEL 4 — Medium, the chain offered (~13%)

Camera: Rekhak at the counter's edge, chain held out at hip height — the counting-chain's standard position. He does not speak. He holds the chain the way a clerk holds a file: for inspection, not for interpretation. The oath-link is the fourth link from his thumb.

Image: The chain between Rekhak and Ira: brass links running, one dark, one still. Ira's hand hovers over the oath-link. Kessa's loupe is down; she is watching.

REKHAK (dialogue, formal, after a long silence): A held-stop oath is public record. Read it.

CAPTION (none spoken): The Council's Reckoner offers the chain. The girl who owes nothing reaches for the one link that holds his lie.

PANEL 5 — Macro, the reading (~14%)

Camera: Ira's fingers on the oath-link: the dark link, still among the running brass. Her touch is mender's-touch — she reads stitch-grammar; chain-grammar is different but the principle is the same: tension, knot, release.

Image: The oath-link opens for her. Not physically — the link does not break — but legibly: words appear in the brass, held-stop words, Rekhak's voice from Chapter 2 Page 006:

CAPTION (in the link, Rekhak's voice): "The palm held a fold, not a door. I saw the fold. In the palm, I saw nothing else."

Image continued: The words are true. They are also a lie. The limiting clause — "nothing else" — is the lie. The chain holds the truth and the omission in the same brass.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, smallest): He saw the fold. He said nothing else. The chain holds the truth and the lie in the same link.

PANEL 6 — Medium, the weight (~13%)

Camera: Ira releases the oath-link. Her hand is steady. Her face does the thing the series has shown only twice: asks the unforgivable question.

Image: Rekhak's eyes on hers. The chain runs. The oath-link is dark. Kessa's loupe catches the light.

IRA (dialogue, flat): You saw the fold. You said nothing else. What else did you see?

REKHAK (dialogue, after a beat, soft): I saw a thread I could not name. Sleeping. Sewn round with thread that does not cast shadow.

REKHAK (dialogue, softer): I saw what you are, Ira Sutar. And I lied to protect it.

PANEL 7 — Wide, the chain stops — HOOK (~19%)

Camera: The chain stops. Chapter 3's one chain-stop: the chain in Rekhak's hand stands perfectly still from his first word to his last. The held-stop oath is sworn again — not in the re-review chamber, but in the Knot & Nail, across a counter, in front of a girl and a goblin.

Image: The chain stopped, Rekhak's hand rigid, the brass frozen. The oath-link is darker than before. The words are committed: I saw what you are. I lied to protect it. The chain records them. The basin's one chain-reader has just heard the Reckoner's second lie, and this one is not a shield — it is a confession.

SFX: (the chain, stopped) —

CAPTION (none spoken): The chain stopped. His words were true. And the link that holds them will never move again.

CAPTION (smaller): Chapter 3's one chain-stop. Spent.

Writing & art notes

  • The chapter's one chain-stop is spent on Rekhak's confession: "I saw what you are. I lied to protect it." These are true words under a held-stop oath. The chain records them honestly. The oath-link darkens further — now holding two oaths.
  • The oath-link reading is the first time Ira uses her chain-reading ability. It is not a power burst; it is touch-literacy, the same as reading stitch-grammar.
  • Rekhak's confession is voluntary. He is not coerced; he chooses to tell her. The procedure of offering the chain is his way of making it official.
  • Kessa watches, silent. She does not intervene. The loupe says she is appraising.
