Chapter 015 — Page 003 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one. English-only pages (AUDIT R13.22).

NANDI (the reader — the school teaches in the street and refuses to choose)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manav / Agnikhand / sight-white, lens-borne / reads, and is paid /
Read: thread-years at the price of an ask, properly made /
"The old school has been unlawful before. It has never once been uncounted."

Page 003 note: takes the licence without ceremony, teaches the term on the step with the lens put away and her hands empty, and at night sits alone between the licence and her own book with her hands flat on the wood and on neither paper. Her last line is a leaning, not a ruling — the old school has been unlawful before and never uncounted — and she does not decide.

KESSA (the till — the column reopened as carrying)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"I will pay what this till has. I will not pretend it is enough for a whole term of children."

Page 003 note: chalks the school's column back open as a carrying column — eleven carried lines and no new money — and says plainly that the till cannot fund a term of children, so the quarter has been asked to carry the rest. She finds the phrase that turns the page: it was never about the money.

THE READER (the counting-lesson women — the licence read in full)

No full sheet; Ch. 011–015. Unties the notice at dawn, reads the single clause and the absent purse at the window, and later finds the border-fine line under the seal.

The Reader / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, learning / owes lessons, and is owed them /
Read: everything delivered, aloud, at the counter of record /
"There is one more line. Under the seal."

Page 003 note: reads the licence as flatly as the charter and then reads what is not on it, because that is the part the Office will not say. She is the one who finds the condition, and her voice slowing is the page's turn.

THE MOTHER (the quarter's practical arithmetic)

No full sheet; second appearance (Ch. 015 P.002, P.003). A mother in a patched shawl with a nine-year-old daughter who reads a tally; there at dawn before the notice is even read.

The Mother / Manav / Agnikhand / patched, practical / owes nothing, and wants one thing /
Ask: the lessons, paid or not — and a bench for her daughter /
"So the lessons are still there. They are just not paid for."

Page 003 note: hears unfunded and does the sum in one sentence, then enrols her daughter on the step anyway. No despair, no bitterness: the practical face of a quarter that has decided a school is worth having even when the Office has taken its money back.

IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the keeper at the edge, keeping the record)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "This is the first time unpaid work is a thing we chose."

Page 003 note: has the clause and the absence read aloud, enters the reopened carrying column, enters unpaid teaching as chosen for the first time in the book, and takes the condition of record without softening it. In the final panel she stands in the doorway and does not come in — the decision belongs to the school.

THE MACHINE (off-panel — the licence)

No full sheet; the Office entire. Speaks this page through one small leaf: one clause, one seal, no cord, and a condition set so fine under the seal that it reads as a border.

The Machine / — / Agnikhand / nineteen years of white spaces / counts, and does not refuse /
Licence: permit the teaching, withdraw the funding, forbid the count /
"An unfiled roll is an uncounted school."

Page 003 note: withdraws five gifts rather than refuse one request, and buys with nothing the only thing it ever wanted: the school's own book. The page never shows an Office interior and never explains the strategy; the missing cord and the border-fine line do all of it.
