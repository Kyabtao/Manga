THREADBORN — Chapter 08 — Page 009

Chapter title: The First Pupil / पहला शिष्य Arc: II — The Mendery Sector: Agnikhand — the Ash-sLums, the hatch alley behind the Mendery Page type: Turn page — 7 panels

Beats: Page 008 — the school's purpose revealed: rule three enrolls, rules one and two finish. A braid cannot be dated, so a braided hand cannot be counted. Jadi is the unfinished root. Ira's price: open the closed stitch, and open a live channel with it.

This page: the hatch alley. And the first time in nine days the door is open.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the alley at night (~14%)

Camera: The alley behind the Mendery, deep night, after third bell. The back door — shut — and the hatch in it: low, forearm-sized, a wooden shutter on a leather hinge, worn shiny at the edges by forty years of forearms going through.

Image: The school's real address. Not a stall, not an archive — a hole at knee height in a door.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Kessa said open it where they can't see it, and I thought about the cellar with a clerk in it and the meter on the stair and the slate that records who comes and who goes.

CAPTION: And then I thought about the fact that this school has had exactly one address for forty years and nobody has ever once written it down.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the hatch opens (~13%)

Camera: Ira on her knees on the wet stone. The hatch's shutter up. Above her, the Mendery's back wall goes up into the slums; below, the alley drains away into the basin. The clerk's crate is on the other side of an inch of door, above the cellar floor, out of sight.

Image: A girl kneeling in an alley at a hole the size of her forearm, unpacking a needle-roll.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The clerk's inside. Sitting on his crate, four feet up, on the other side of this door, with a slate and a lamp and orders to write down everyone who comes and goes.

CAPTION: He isn't going to write down a hand coming through a hole at knee height. Nobody ever has.

PANEL 3 — Close, the kit (~14%)

Camera: Close on the ground: needle-roll open, four needles, the wax knife, the thread-cake, shears. And Ira's left hand, palm up, laid across her own knee.

Image: The tools of the trade, spread on wet stone, and the hand that has to be opened first.

JADI: You don't have to do this for me. I'm forty-four. I've had a decade more than my mother got.

IRA: You didn't ask me to mend you.

IRA: You asked me to teach you. There's a difference and the whole school is it.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): She looks at me for a while after that and then she puts her hand through the hatch anyway, because she's Kshudra and Kshudra don't argue with a fact once it's been said out loud.

PANEL 4 — Large, the stitch opens (~16%)

Camera: Large panel, close on Ira's palm. The wax knife's tip at the seam. Then — the loops coming out, one at a time, from the inside, the Ch. 004 method, but this time done by her own hand, on her own hand, in a cold alley, by lamplight.

Image: A girl opening a door she closed herself, two nights after closing it. The most deliberate act in the series so far.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Mending is four moves and the first one is open the tear. You don't get to skip it because the tear is yours.

CAPTION: First loop. Second loop. The knife isn't cutting, it's lifting — same as she did it, same as the stitch has always come out, from the inside, without tearing.

CAPTION: Nine loops. It takes about a minute and a half and my hand doesn't shake once, which I will think about later and be afraid of.

PANEL 5 — Close, the mark (~14%)

Camera: The palm, opened. The Sutra-mark: projection type, braided, two strands wound around each other — and the crimson under the grey, lit from inside by the lamp.

Image: The open hand for the first time since Chapter 007. Everything the school is, in one palm.

SFX: hn — hn — hn — hn

CAPTION (Ira's voice): And there's the four notes.

CAPTION: Immediate. No lag, no memory, no echo off a chain — four notes, off-pitch, right order, coming up the braid like something that had its hand on a door waiting.

CAPTION: Her hum. Live. And then, right underneath it, two more stitches, dry thread, on and on — she's been sewing this whole time, four nights, and it is not a thing anybody does quietly.

PANEL 6 — Medium, the basin answers (~14%)

Camera: Wide, out over the Ashfall basin. Down the pot-row, on the terraces, at the dock: ordinary people in their doorways and on their steps, looking up. Not alarmed. Not knowing why. Just — pausing, mid-task, the way the whole city paused in Ch. 001 Page 008.

Image: A basin full of threaded people noticing that something in the dark has opened.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): And then the pot-row goes quiet.

CAPTION: Not silent. Quiet. Three hundred people down a row of stalls putting a hand up to their own chest without knowing why, and one man at the dock standing still with a crate on his shoulder, and a woman on the third terrace turning round to look at nothing at all.

CAPTION: Forty years of single strands in this basin. They don't know what's happening. They can feel the school breathing.

PANEL 7 — Close, the chain answers — CLOSING HOOK (~15%)

Camera: Cut to the Council Stair, far up-terrace, drawn small and cold at the top of the panel: a compliance chain hanging from a hook on a wall, and it has begun, very slightly, to swing. Below it, in the alley, Ira has Jadi's palm in her hand and has not started work yet.

Image: The answer, arriving from the wrong direction. The chapter's whole cost, in one cold small panel.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Kessa priced it and she was right, and it was cheaper than she said, and I took it anyway.

CAPTION (last line): There's a chain hanging on a wall in the Reckoning Office that has never moved on its own in its whole life, and tonight it's swinging.

CAPTION (smaller): It's about four minutes behind me. Which is four minutes more than I had.

CAPTION (smallest): So I pick up the needle.

Writing & art notes

  • **The hatch alley is the chapter's best location and must be drawn as a return. No nostalgia music — this is a woman's working station, wet, cold, used. The shutter has a shine on its edges from forty years of forearms; that shine is the only sentimental detail allowed.
  • Ira opening her own stitch is drawn with the Ch. 004 method — loops lifting from the inside, no tearing. She is not undoing her mother's work; she is continuing it. The distinction is visual and should be legible to anyone who has read Chapter 004.
  • The basin's answer should echo Ch. 001 Page 008 (the Loom's hum stopping citywide) but be smaller. That was the Loom. This is one girl in an alley. Keep it intimate — hands going to chests, people pausing — and keep the Loom out of it entirely.
  • The chain's swing is the chapter's only cutaway to the Office. One small cold panel, no dialogue, at the very end. The whole regulatory apparatus of this story reduces to an object moving on a hook.
  • "Four minutes more than I had." Ira now has a clock, which is the structural thing Chapter 008 Page 001 opened with (the clerk, the slate, the meter). Close the loop.
