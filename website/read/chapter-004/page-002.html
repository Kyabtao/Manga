THREADBORN — Chapter 04 — Page 002

Chapter title: The Weight of Thread / धागे का बोझ Arc: I — The Unspooling Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail (afternoon) Page type: The demonstration's weight — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 001 — the pattern on Bhan's arm, the Grey Clerk's visit, the coin on the slate.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the stall (~14%)

Ira returns from the dock, the rubbing of the new pattern in her belt. Kessa appraises the rubbing beside the cut-end. The pattern matches Ira's palm-stitch exactly.

KESSA: They're not practising. They're teaching.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the lesson (~13%)

Kessa's loupe on the rubbing: the stitch-grammar is identical to the cut-end's school, but the execution is fresher — the cutter used the same hand-school with younger thread.

KESSA: Forty years between the box thread and this. Same school. New thread.

PANEL 3 — Close, the question (~14%)

Ira's face: she understands now. The cutter is not random. The cutter is showing her how her palm was sewn. The nightly openings on Bhan are a lesson plan.

IRA: They want me to see the stitch from the outside.

PANEL 4 — Medium, Kessa's hands (~13%)

Kessa ties a new knot in the tally-thread: the lesson is filed. Her hands are steady but her knuckles are white — the first time the series shows Kessa's hands under stress.

CAPTION: Kessa's hands have appraised every debt in this basin. They have never been afraid of a thread before.

PANEL 5 — Wide, the basin evening (~13%)

Evening: the Knot & Nail stays open. The basin's filing queue has grown — six people now wait, each carrying papers they want in the lockbox. Kessa turns them all down. The coin on the grey slate stays on the counter.

CAPTION: The basin's fear has an address. Kessa refuses it daily.

PANEL 6 — Medium, Rekhak on the row (~13%)

Rekhak walks his rounds, chain running, oath-link visible. He passes the stall and does not stop. But his chain hums — the wrong-notes melody, Page 006's echo — and this time Ira hears it clearly. The four notes, wrong key, same shape.

CAPTION (Ira): The four notes again. Wrong. The same shape. Always when he passes.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the stitch trembles — HOOK (~19%)

Ira's palm, under the counter's lamplight: the stitch trembles again — third time this chapter. Not opening. Adjusting. The thread is responding to the proximity of the evidence: the pattern, the rubbing, the box. The stitch knows the lesson is being taught.

CAPTION: The stitch that has held for fifteen years is learning its own name.
