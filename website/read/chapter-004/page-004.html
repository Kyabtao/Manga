THREADBORN — Chapter 04 — Page 004

Chapter title: The Weight of Thread / धागे का बोझ Arc: I — The Unspooling Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail (morning) Page type: The stitch opens — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 003 — the continuation stitch, the cutter checking their own work.

PANEL 1 — Wide, morning at the stall (~14%)

Camera: Morning, Knot & Nail: Ira arrives with the rubbing of the continuation stitch. Kessa compares it to the cut-end, the pattern rubbing, and Ira's palm. Four versions of the same grammar.

KESSA: Four versions. Same school. The cutter is not copying. They're graduating.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the four versions (~13%)

Camera: The counter: four items side by side — cut-end (40 years old), Ira's palm (15 years old), pattern rubbing (2 days old), continuation rubbing (last night). A timeline of the hand-school.

CAPTION: Forty years. Fifteen years. Two days. One night. The school's curriculum.

PANEL 3 — Close, Ira's palm (~14%)

Camera: Ira's palm under the loupe-lamp: the stitch trembles — stronger now. The thread is responding to the proximity of its own history laid out on the counter. The stitch is recognizing itself.

SFX: (in the stitch) —

PANEL 4 — Macro, the stitch shifts (~13%)

Camera: The stitch shifts. Not a tremor — a movement. One loop of the old thread loosens by a hair's width. The stitch does not open. It breathes.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, smallest): It moved. Not a tremor. A breath.

PANEL 5 — Medium, Kessa's response (~14%)

Camera: Kessa's hands: she sets the loupe down and places her hand over Ira's palm — covering the stitch, protecting it. Her face is not visible; her hands say everything.

KESSA (dialogue, quiet): Don't force it. Don't pull. Let it breathe.

PANEL 6 — Wide, the basin at noon (~13%)

Camera: Noon: the basin works. The Knot & Nail's shutters are up. The filing queue has thinned — the coin on the grey slate has discouraged casual requests. The basin trusts Kessa's lock, but Kessa's lock is getting heavier.

CAPTION: The box holds four objects. The stitch breathes. The cutter graduates. The basin watches.

PANEL 7 — Close, the lockbox — HOOK (~19%)

Camera: Night: the lockbox on the counter, Kessa's hand on the lid. From inside, a sound — the cut-end settling again. The same sound as Ch. 003 Page 008. But tonight it is answered: from outside the stall, from the row, from the dark, a second sound — thread on wood. Someone has left something on the counter while Kessa's hand was on the box.

SFX: (inside box) — / (on counter) tk

CAPTION: The box answers. The counter answers back.

Writing & art notes

  • The four versions of the hand-school timeline sit side by side: same seam, four ages. Keep them visually distinct — the reader should be able to sort them without a caption.
  • "Let it breathe" is Kessa's instruction and the page's spine. Her hand over Ira's palm is protection offered by the stall, never a warning.
