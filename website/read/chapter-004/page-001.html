THREADBORN — Chapter 04 — Page 001

Chapter title: The Weight of Thread / धागे का बोझ Arc: I — The Unspooling Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail (morning, day after Ch. 003) Page type: Chapter opening — 7 panels, hook at the bottom

Beats carried from Chapter 003: the chain-stop confession, the five threads (supply line), the lockbox shared between Kessa and Ira, the stitch's movement, the four notes matched wrongly. The fifth thread was found at the Council Stair's foot.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the morning after (~14%)

Camera: Morning, basin market: the Knot & Nail opens. Kessa behind the counter, loupe up. The lockbox is under the counter — its new normal position. A Grey Clerk stands at the stall's threshold, slate in hand, waiting to be received.

Image: The first Council visit since the posting order. The Grey Clerk is different from Chapter 2's — older, more careful. The market watches from one row back.

CAPTION (none spoken): The morning after the thread reached the stair, the Council sent a clerk. Not to take. To ask.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the request (~13%)

Camera: The Grey Clerk at the counter, slate offered. The slate carries one line, grey ink: a request to inspect the lockbox. Not seize — inspect. Procedure has learned Kessa's grammar.

Image: Kessa reads the slate without touching it. Her loupe is up. Her hands are in her coats.

GREY CLERK (dialogue, formal): The Office requests inspection of the custodial lockbox. Three items registered by transfer.

KESSA (dialogue, dry): Three items were transferred. Four are stored. The fourth was a gift.

GREY CLERK (dialogue): The Office acknowledges four.

PANEL 3 — Close, the refusal (~13%)

Camera: Kessa's hands: she turns the slate face-down on the counter — her grammar from Ch. 003 Page 002. Then she does something new: she sets a coin on top of it. A Kshudra pawn-coin, the kind used when a debt is acknowledged but not yet priced.

Image: The coin on the slate: Kessa's counter-offer. Not refusal — pricing. She will allow inspection when the Office names its price for the census transfer.

KESSA (dialogue): When the Office prices the transfer, I'll price the inspection.

PANEL 4 — Medium, the basin watches (~14%)

Camera: The Grey Clerk leaves. The market exhales. Two stall-owners move another inch closer to the Knot & Nail — the trust measurement continues. Ira arrives with her kit, seeing the departing clerk.

Image: The basin's geometry: the Knot & Nail as the centre, the stalls orbiting closer.

IRA (dialogue, arriving): What did they want?

KESSA (dialogue): To look in the box.

IRA (dialogue): What did you say?

KESSA (dialogue): I said name a price first. They never do.

PANEL 5 — Wide, the dock approach (~13%)

Camera: Ira at the dock-gate, mid-morning. The chit-boy's slate shows her name: Sutar, I. — dock three — read. The pre-logged line from Ch. 003 Page 003, still there.

Image: The slate with her name, the dock-gate lamp, the grain-lifts behind.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): The slate still has my name on it. Written before I arrived. The principal's paperwork is always early.

PANEL 6 — Close, Bhan's arm (~13%)

Camera: Bhan's forearm: the nightly opening is there, as expected. But tonight's addition is different — the cutter has left a new mark. Not thread this time: a pattern, sewn into the mend's edge in ash-grey thread. A pattern that matches the stitch on Ira's palm.

Image: The pattern on Bhan's arm is the first direct echo of Ira's palm-stitch. The cutter is not just practising on Bhan — they are demonstrating.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, smallest): They sewed my stitch into his arm. Not a copy. The same stitch. The same pattern. As if to say: this is what was done to you, and this is how.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the pattern — HOOK (~19%)

Camera: Extreme close on the pattern in Bhan's arm: the stitch-grammar of Ira's palm, reproduced exactly, in thread that matches the cut-end in Kessa's box. The cutter has shown their hand: they know the stitch, they have the thread, and they want Ira to see it.

Image: The pattern is a mirror — Bhan's arm shows what Ira's palm looks like from the outside. The stitch, seen for the first time as an object rather than a mystery.

CAPTION (none spoken): The cutter demonstrated. The arm is the message. The stitch is the proof. And the girl who carries the original now sees it reproduced in a stranger's skin.

CAPTION (smaller): The hand that opens has shown itself. And it is not finished.

Writing & art notes

  • The Grey Clerk visit is procedure, not threat. The Office is learning Kessa's grammar: request, don't demand. The coin on the slate is her counter-offer.
  • The pattern on Bhan's arm is the chapter's opening escalation: the cutter has moved from practice to demonstration. The stitch is reproduced exactly.
  • Chain budget: ONE for Chapter 004. Not to be spent early.
