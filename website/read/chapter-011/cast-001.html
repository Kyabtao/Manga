Chapter 011 — Page 001 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one, including the unnamed ones.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Write it true or don't write it."

Page 001 note: keeper of the register and now the bounty's clerk, obliged to attend her own survey. Reads every entry aloud with Nandi. Kneels in the lamplight on her own step to read two knots. Does not speak in the hook panel except in caption.

NANDI — नन्दी (reader of the bigger book)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, knotted at the ends / light, paid in kind /
Read: identify any knot's kind, year and hand; cannot read a page and has never misread a knot /
"Entry read."

Page 001 note: her own condition — every entry read aloud with the keeper, same words, same time — is the instrument by which the Office hears forty years of the school. She knows it. Sleeves pinned, hands flat, voice flat.

NIMA — नीमा (reader-second)

Full sheet (Ch. 010): ../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md · Art: ../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png

Nima / Kshudra / Agnikhand / grey, undeclared / none declared — that is the bargain /
Count: tally hands without reading them; cannot read a braid /
"Don't write me."

Page 001 note: carries the card-tray and has not put it down. Not yet asked to hold a hand. Standing thread: the day she is told to put a hand on the stand she will refuse out loud, in the room, and the Office will test it.

THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक (the survey)

No full sheet; Ch. 009–011. The Office's voice with a pen in it. Never unkind, never hurried, never raises anything.

Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Survey: name a place and every hand in it becomes an entry /
"Then bring the register and stand where I can see your hands."

Page 001 note: states clause four aloud for the first time — the licence lapses if the place of keeping fails to produce — and answers Ira's "Produce what?" with "Whatever is in the register, keeper. You're holding it."

THE SECOND CLERK — दूसरा लिपिक (the survey's writer)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Page 001 only. Younger, folding slate, folding stool, writes what the two women say.

Second Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, thin / none /
Record: copy a reading as spoken, verbatim, without understanding it /
"I'm only writing what you said."

THE COUNTING WOMEN — गिनती वाली स्त्रियाँ (three, unnamed)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Page 001. Pupils of Jadi's counting lesson, now loose in three households. They face each other, not the counter.

Counting Woman / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, self-tied / light, self-held /
Count: declare your own knots before a clerk can declare them for you /
"Six, seven. Seven since the lesson."

THE TWO CLAIMANTS — दो दावेदार (unnamed)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Page 001. Men with a claimant's posture, rehearsing with their palms turned up. The bounty is still open.

Claimant / mixed / Agnikhand / borrowed / heavy /
Claim: enter a hand that is not yours for a reward that is not yours /
"You wanted a hand, sir."

KESSA — केसा (named, not on panel)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"I keep what's in the box. I don't sign for why it's there."

Page 001 note: on panel in name only — the cut-end in her lockbox is the measure by which Ira recognises the shadowless thread in the hook. Her tally-thread remains the only uncopyable record.

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is a knot, a thread and a hand at the foot of a page.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Lift: take a knot out of a record without cutting it; enrol without entering a name /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M."

Page note: the Office's grey lamp burning at first light where the school's lantern hung; a queue that is no longer only claimants; the survey from the fourth step to the water-butt with the keeper obliged to attend; clause four spoken aloud; Nandi's condition turned outward; year one and the founder's own knot — Do not enrol me. — S.M. — read into an Office book. Hook: the second knot is tied in shadowless thread, the same thread as the cut-end in Kessa's lockbox and the stitch in Ira's palm. The mother is teaching in the open, with Ira's own thread.
