Chapter title: The Fourth Step / चौथा पायदान Arc: III — The Second School Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the drawer, the square, the alley Page type: The pledge; the count not taken — 7 panels, ends at a lamp that is not hers to carry

Beats: the drawer opens and the chapter's one kept thing is named at last — the Loom's last knot, left at the counter in the Freedom Year by a woman who gave no name and never came back: "It is the last thing my mother ever brought me." The roll-call at morning — forty-one names called and answered; Nima first of the entered; the twenty-two called by length, and the Office must ask the keeper to run the measuring. The spending — Ira does not refuse and does not measure: she ties the knot into the count-cord, and a chain with a link that fits everything cannot pull. The cost — the knot leaves with the requisition into the Office's chest, spent, gone; the empty fold comes back to Kessa, all of it. The close — the school keeps its own lamp now.

This page: the chapter's ONE chain-stop, spent — and the count postponed, not won.

Chapter 011 budget: the chain-stop is SPENT on Page 010 — the Loom's last knot, one home, ever. Loom never speaks — its last utterance was this knot, said in thread. Mother never on panel, never speaks — her only visit in nine years was the pledge. Four notes silent since Chapter 008 Page 009 — eighteen days now.

PANEL 1 — Close, the drawer (~13%)

Camera: Close: dawn, before anything else. The drawer open under the counter, and in Ira's palm: a small dark knot on a fold of cloth, wrapped in one turn of grey thread. The chapter's one kept thing, shown at last, in the first light.

Image: It has been in this drawer for nine years, and it has never once been read.

CAPTION: The Freedom Year. The loom stopped speaking to anybody. Somebody came to this counter in the dark, left a fold of cloth on the wood, and did not give a name. In the fold: this.

CAPTION: The Loom never speaks. It said one thing more, after everybody stopped listening, and it said it in thread.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): A knot that matches every hand. Tied into any chain, it fits every link — and a chain with a link that fits everything cannot pull. Nine years I kept it. I never spent it. I never once told anybody what it is.

CAPTION (smallest): It is the last thing my mother ever brought me.

PANEL 2 — Wide, the count (~14%)

Camera: Wide: the square at morning, the same stone the declarations happened on. The count assembled: the grey clerk with slate and count-cord, the counting women with their tallies, Nandi with the big book, the basin standing where it has stood all week. Last night's circle did not go home. The roll-call begins.

Image: The basin answering to names, one by one, in the open, like it has done it all its life.

NANDI: (reading the call) Year six, Sarno's lad. Year two, the widow at the rope-walk. Year four, the ferry-knuckles.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Forty-one names, called and answered. Then the entered — and Nima steps out before her name is finished.

NIMA: Present. Wearing the coat, clerk. Mark it properly this time.

GREY CLERK: The night's entries stand required. Called by length. Keeper — your book, and your hands.

PANEL 3 — Medium, the measuring (~14%)

Camera: Medium: the measuring, and the Office's trap closing exactly as designed — Ira at the front with the register open, half-lengths to knuckles, the clerk writing down what she finds. The twenty-two in the square with their fists closed on their halves.

Image: The only instrument that can run the call, run by the woman who built the shield it is unmaking.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Every match I confirm makes them the Office's hands. Every match I refuse breaks my own register's law in front of the whole basin — and the Office gets to write down the breaking.

CAPTION: I built the only instrument that could do this. Of course they asked me to run it.

PANEL 4 — Close, the tie (~15%)

Camera: Close: she does not refuse, and she does not measure. She unties the fold and ties the knot into the clerk's count-cord — in front of everyone, unhurried, a mender's hands doing the one thing they have never done. Nandi stops reading. The square does not make a sound.

Image: A chain with a link that fits everything cannot pull.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): A mender does not cut. A mender puts in the piece that was missing.

(no balloon; Nandi closes the big book — off-schedule, for the first time in her life — and holds it shut with both pinned sleeves)

PANEL 5 — Medium, the collapse (~15%)

Camera: Medium: the clerk, procedural to the end. He summons the purchased reader out of the crowd — fee unspent since noon — to read the new link. The honest instrument kills the case in one line.

Image: The Office paying, once more, to have its own instrument proven useless.

GREY CLERK: Read the new link.

PURCHASED READER: (lens up on its cord; a long moment; professionally) Kind: all. Year: all. Hand: all.

PURCHASED READER: It matches every hand you will ever call, clerk. Including yours. That will be two coppers.

GREY CLERK: (writing, in the voice of a man closing a window) Count not taken. The count will be taken when the roll is whole.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The Office does not lose. It postpones.

PANEL 6 — Close, the cost (~14%)

Camera: Close: the clerk leaves with the cord — the knot goes with it, into the Office's chest, spent and gone. Ira folds the empty cloth. Kessa holds out one scarred hand for it.

Image: Custody, paid in full: the fold comes back, and there is nothing left in it.

KESSA: All of it, keeper. You owe me the cloth.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Nine years I never told anybody what it is, and now it hangs in an Office chest. My mother came to my counter once in nine years, in the dark, and left the Loom's last word with me. I spent it on twenty-two people she has never met.

CAPTION: It stops a chain. It does not stop a list. The roll is still whole. The school is still open. Those are not the same as winning. I am learning to tell the difference.

PANEL 7 — Wide, the lamp — FINAL HOOK (~15%)

Camera: Wide: dusk. The alley. The crate-lamp is already burning — lit by nobody Ira asked. Nima at the crate in the lender's coat, two flat hands demonstrating a hold to a half-circle that grows as the light goes. The riveter's fist, up. Ira on her step, the stall behind her, the drawer open behind her.

Image: The school, keeping its own light.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The four notes are still silent. Eighteen days. Somewhere a woman who does not speak to me knows what I spent — because the whole basin knows, and she taught the whole basin.

IRA: (from the step; her only line on the page) Leave the lamp burning.

CAPTION (smallest): Nobody writes anything down.

Writing & art notes

  • The reveal must be small and it must be the mother. The pledge was left at the counter in the Freedom Year by someone who gave no name — nine years of silence, one visit, made in the dark, and the pledge was it. The page never says my mother came beyond the one caption; the reader assembles it from "nine years", "did not give a name", and "the last thing my mother ever brought me." She is never on panel and never speaks — the pledge IS the visit, and spending it is spending the visit. Do not soften, do not explain, do not show her face in memory.
  • The Loom's knot is the Loom speaking, once, in the only language it has. Canon honored: Loom never speaks — because its last utterance was a knot. Its property is simple and needs no new physics: it fits every hand. A link that fits everything carries no load; a chain containing it cannot pull. Card version, for the bible: Fit — become any link in any chain; a chain containing the Fit cannot pull. One home, ever. Once tied in, it never comes back out — that is the one-use cost, and it is spent in front of the whole basin with no take-backs.
  • The measuring trap must be seen to be of Ira's own making. The Office cannot run a by-length call without the register and the keeper's hands — the chapter built the only instrument in the basin that can do it, so the Office asks her to. Panel 3 is the floor-dropping panel and it plays as procedure: half-lengths to knuckles, the clerk writing. She does not refuse — refusing breaks her own law in front of everyone and hands the Office the story. What she does instead is a mender's answer: put in the piece that was missing.
  • Nandi closes the big book. No line. Off-schedule, for the first time in her life, with both pinned sleeves holding it shut. Her chapter: read (003), declined a name (005), that one is mine (006), read the lie in all night (007), chose the open (009) — and now ends the reading herself. She has not forgiven Page 007 and the page does not ask her to. The book closing is her sentence.
  • The purchased reader's reading is the chapter's last rhyme. Kind: basin. Year: true. Hand: none (Page 008) becomes Kind: all. Year: all. Hand: all — same professional, same fee-book, and his honesty serves the basin this time because it serves accuracy either way. Two coppers, as before. He is not redeemed. He is paid.
  • The postponement is the honest ending. "The count will be taken when the roll is whole" — the Office writes down its own defeat as procedure and sets a clock for Chapter 012. Ira's caption says it plainly: the Office does not lose, it postpones. Nobody celebrates in the square. The basin watched a woman spend the last thing her mother gave her, and it understood what that cost, and it went quiet.
  • Kessa's custody pays off in one sentence. "The fold comes back to me when it is done — all of it" (Page 007) returns as "All of it, keeper. You owe me the cloth." The fold is empty. Forty years of custody, discharged in a pawnbroker's flat voice. Do not let her embrace anybody.
  • The hook is a lamp Ira did not light. The school no longer needs the keeper's counter, the keeper's lamp, or the keeper. Ira's only line on the page — four words, from the step, permission and blessing and surrender at once: "Leave the lamp burning." The smallest caption is the mother's method, inherited by the school: Nobody writes anything down.
  • The four notes stay silent. Eighteen days, one caption, carried into Chapter 012. The second knot in the untrained hand (Chapter 010's closing hook) also stays open. Rekhak ends the chapter unspent and off-panel — his chain stopped nothing because he never appeared; that is a chapter decision, not an oversight, and the summary should say so.
  • Continuity: forty-one names answered, twenty-two called by length and uncollected, sixty-three hands on a whole roll; the count-cord and the knot go into the Office's chest; Nima present in the coat and teaching at dusk; the drawer left open behind Ira all day. No §3.1 props; no lettering anywhere — the count-cord is knot-script, the slate holds marks. Numbers: sixty-three hands, twenty-two uncollectable, four notes silent eighteen days, chain-stop ONE — SPENT.
