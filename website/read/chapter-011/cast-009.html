Chapter 011 — Page 009 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one, including the unnamed ones.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "I put the lamp where it always stands, and I open the drawer."

Page 009 note: takes the fourth step — nine years of counter-design broken by her own foot, one step down, lamp out, into the open where her mother taught. Says almost nothing all page; carries the light, sets it on a crate she will never know was her mother's. Returns alone to the counter at the end, and the last panel is her hand on the drawer that holds the only thing in the stall that stops a chain — never spent, never named.

NANDI — नन्दी (reader of the bigger book — reading in the open)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, cut from one piece / none — she was never entered /
Read: identify any knot's kind, year and hand; cannot read a page; reads every entry ALOUD by
schedule / "The schedule puts me at the big book at dawn. It does not say the rest of the day is mine

Page 009 note: comes out unasked, big book under her arm, and sits at the crate's edge to read the twenty-two into the alley. Her third choice to read in this chapter, and the first that is entirely hers — off-schedule, unpaid, public. Reads straight through the requisition and over the clerk's objection. Not forgiveness for Page 007; craft.

NIMA — नीमा (the lender's coat)

Full sheet (Ch. 010): ../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md · Art: ../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png

Page 009 note: one line from the doorway, quietly, to Ira — and it is the page's ear: she hears the Office's verbs repeating across pages. Required is the new hold. The roll is about to get bigger by twenty-two, and she is the proof the machinery works.

THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक (the counterpoint)

No full sheet; Ch. 009–011. The Office's voice with a pen in it.

Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Counterpoint: read the order over the reading, same voice, same volume /
"The basin's count runs short. Entered hands stand required for the count at morning. The roll will
be called."

Page 009 note: does not argue with the reading and does not stop it — he reads the requisition over it, unhurried. The Office never competes; it collects. Loses the collision in Panel 6 on the rules and loses nothing that matters: both things stand.

THE RIVETER — रिवेट-कार (one of the nine; first fist up)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 007, 009. Burn-scarred knuckles.

The Riveter / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, half-cut / heavy, now answered /
Prove: his word is the half in his fist /
"Your Office taught us different, clerk. First morning of the survey. In the square."

Page 009 note: first fist up when the reading starts, still up when the clerk objects — and his line is the row's whole defence: the Office's own precedent, cited back by the first man it almost worked on.

THE PURCHASED READER — ख़रीदा हुआ पाठक (neutral instrument, fee unspent)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 008–009. The first city instrument in the basin.

Purchased Reader / Manav / city / brass thumb-ring, lens on a cord / whatever the fee is /
Read for pay: a competent reading, honestly reported, honestly priced /
"A reading is a reading. I charge for mine. She does not. That does not stop it being one."

Page 009 note: four words from the crowd, settling the off-schedule objection — a paid instrument certifying the free one. Not redeemed, not hostile: a professional protecting the market. No second line; never comes inside.

THE THIRTEEN — तेरह (answering thread)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 007–009. The mother's pupils who keep the teaching.

The Thirteen / mixed / Agnikhand / grey, half-cut in fist / various /
Answer: thread answers thread — a fist up for every line read /
No line on this page. The half in the fist answers the line in the book.

Page 009 note: the alley is the room they were taught to trust — no book of their own in it, so they cross it. They stand in a half-circle around the lamp and raise their fists one by one as the lines land. Not one of them speaks; the silence is the teaching, kept.

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is an upturned crate she may or may not have stood on.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Teach: in the open, in the street, writing nothing down /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M." — the alley is not a room. It never was. That is what she taught in it.

Page note: the keeper steps down — the chapter title, on-panel, as geography. The reading happens in the open by lamplight and the basin gathers to hear it: a secret read aloud stops being a handle. Mid-reading, the requisition arrives and is read over the reading — the roll will be called — and the off-schedule objection dies on the Office's own precedent, refereed by the purchased reader. Both things stand. And the last realization drops: thread answers thread, so the roll-call does not need names — it has the other halves. Hook: "I put the lamp where it always stands, and I open the drawer."
