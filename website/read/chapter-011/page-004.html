THREADBORN — Chapter 11 — Page 004

Chapter title: The Fourth Step / चौथा पायदान Arc: III — The Second School Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Mendery counter and the hatch alley Page type: The precedent; the count — 7 panels, ends on the lengths

Beats: the gap the clerk left in clause four is walked into — a counting woman brings her own hands to be entered at her own word, and clause two becomes everybody's instrument; Nima's entry costs her — the Office offers the choice on-panel and she chooses to be written down rather than lend her hands; and Ira measures — the felt's impressions against the Roll of Hands, and the numbers do not match.

This page: the keeper's own instrument turns in her hands.

Chapter 011 budget: ONE chain-stop, not spent on Page 004 (still held). Loom never speaks. Mother never on panel, never speaks. Four notes silent since Chapter 008 Page 009 — twelve days now. Wax: grey for the Council and the Office, crimson for the principal's private hand.

PANEL 1 — Medium, the counter the next morning (~13%)

Camera: Medium: the Mendery counter at first bell, the morning after the survey closed. At the front of the queue stands one of the counting women — the one whose hand stopped mid-count — with her palm turned up, not to be counted: to be entered. In her other hand a fold of cloth with her own knots in it. Behind her, two more women waiting with the same posture.

Image: The keeper's instrument, handed back to her across her own counter by the alley.

COUNTING WOMAN: Enter them. At my word. Like you did.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): She was standing in the row. She heard me say it, and she heard the clerk leave the hole in it standing up in the air, and she has brought her own hands to my counter before the second bell.

PANEL 2 — Close, the precedent (~14%)

Camera: Close: Ira's face first, then her hands — the register, the pen, the flat working stillness she has when she is doing a thing properly. She writes what she is told. And halfway through the entry she understands what she made yesterday.

Image: A woman writing, and then stopping, and then going on writing.

IRA: Your word is the entry. Say it.

COUNTING WOMAN: Mine. I tied them.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Yesterday clause two was the only shield this basin had against the bounty — enter the hands first and there is nothing left to claim but the person.

CAPTION: Today it is a door, and I am the one who showed the whole row where the latch is.

PANEL 3 — Medium, the queue learns a trade (~13%)

Camera: Wider: the queue now holds a trade that did not exist on Monday. Three people with folds of cloth, waiting to declare their own hands. Behind them the claimants — the ones who came for the bounty — and one of them is watching the counting woman with an expression Ira does not like at all. Nandi is at the counter's end with the bigger book, sleeves pinned, saying nothing.

Image: A line learning a new trade, and the old trade standing behind it taking notes.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Eleven hands wanting to be counted on Monday. This morning it is declarations, and they are all honest.

CAPTION: By the second bell it will be people declaring hands that are not theirs, and there is nothing anywhere in clause two that says I am allowed to refuse.

PANEL 4 — Two-shot, Nima's choice (~13%)

Camera: Two-shot in the alley: the grey clerk and Nima, the card-tray in her hands again. He offers the choice flatly, in the street, where the row can hear. He is not being cruel; he is being exact.

Image: The Office pricing a girl who has already said no once.

GREY CLERK: Reader-second. Two entries are open to you. Hold for the count and your wage stays undeclared. Or decline again and be entered as a hand like any other in this alley.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Her bargain was I count, I am not written down. He has just offered her the same bargain back with a price on it, in daylight, in front of everybody she lives among.

PANEL 5 — Medium, the second refusal (~14%)

Camera: Medium: Nima sets the tray down — and this time she leaves it down. She says it in the alley, in daylight, with three counting women and a claimant listening. She does not raise her voice and she does not look at Ira.

Image: A girl choosing to be written down rather than be borrowed.

NIMA: Enter me.

NIMA: I'd rather be a line in your book than a pair of hands you can borrow whenever the count is short.

GREY CLERK: (writing) Entry: Nima, ninth terrace, Kshudra, enrolled at the hatch, year nine.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): She has chosen the exact thing her whole wage existed to avoid. Two of the people in the declaration-queue look up when she says it. I think that is the beginning of something, and I do not yet know what.

PANEL 6 — Medium, the measuring (~14%)

Camera: Medium: the stall interior, lamp lit, shutters down to the last slat. The lockbox open on the counter, and beside it the Roll of Hands unrolled, held flat at each end by a needle and a thimble. Ira is measuring: a length of thread laid against each impression in the felt, one at a time, counting on her fingers. Kessa watches with the loupe down and says nothing.

Image: Arithmetic done in thread and felt instead of ink, by a woman who has never trusted a written count.

IRA: Forty-one names on the roll.

IRA: (and she does not look up, and her hand does not stop) There are more marks in this felt than that.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the count — FINAL HOOK (~19%)

Camera: Macro: straight down at the counter. On the left, the unrolled roll — forty-one entries of abstract strokes and tally marks, no lettering anywhere. On the right, laid out on the wood in a row, one length of grey thread for every impression in the felt. The row of lengths is longer than the roll. Ira's hand is arrested in the middle of counting, two fingers still down.

Image: Two counts that should be the same number, and are not.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Forty-one names. Sixty-three lengths.

CAPTION: Twenty-two hands this basin has never written down. Twenty-two people with no entry, no year and no keeper — taught in the open, in daylight, by a woman who is not on anybody's roll.

CAPTION (smallest): The Office is counting doors and I have been helping it. My mother has been counting something else entirely for forty years, and I have been handing out needles to both of them.

Writing & art notes

  • The precedent is the page's engine and it must arrive as a good thing first. The counting woman is not a villain and not a fool: she wants her hands in the register under her own name, which is exactly what clause two is for. The horror is structural, not personal — Ira's instrument works, and that is why it is dangerous.
  • Panel 2 must show her continuing to write. Not pausing in shock, not putting the pen down. She understands mid-stroke and keeps going, because the woman in front of her has done nothing wrong. That is the trap closing.
  • The claimant's look in Panel 3 is the page's only threat and it should be small. One man, at the back, watching a woman declare her own hands and doing arithmetic. No dialogue. Ira notices; the page does not.
  • Nima's beat is a cost, not a triumph. She chooses to be entered — the one thing her wage existed to prevent — because the alternative is lending her hands to a count she does not control. The clerk writes it without comment. Do not play this as heroic music; play it as a girl paying for something in the only currency she has.
  • The measuring is the chapter's thesis and it must be physical. Ira counts lengths of thread against impressions in felt, on a counter, with her hands — not in a book, not by a clerk. This is the same gesture the series has always given her: she sits down at a thing and counts it.
  • Numbers: forty-one (the roll, canon from Ch. 010) against sixty-three (the felt). The gap is twenty-two. Sixty-three deliberately echoes the sixty hour-knots and the sixty-first knot of Chapter 001 — the series counts in that register.
  • The hook must be drawn as two counts side by side, not as a revelation. Abstract tally rows on the roll (no lettering), a physical row of thread lengths on the wood, and the row being visibly longer. Ira's hand arrested mid-count. No glowing thread, no aura — these are lengths of ordinary grey filament lying on a counter.
  • Continuity: Nandi is present and silent; whether she knew the entry was a lie stays open. Kessa gives a date and no name and does not answer here. The four notes are silent twelve days. The mother is off panel; her presence is twenty-two people nobody wrote down.
