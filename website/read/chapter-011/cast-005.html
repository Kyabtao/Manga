Chapter 011 — Page 005 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one, including the unnamed ones.

Note: Kessa is deliberately not on this page — she gave a date and no name on Page 003 and has nothing further until Ira asks her a better question. Rekhak is named in a caption and does not appear.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "I wrote it so the Office could not refuse an honest
woman. I did not write a way to refuse a dishonest man."

Page 005 note: looks for the line in her own clause that lets her refuse, and finds there isn't one. Her answer is to refuse nothing and call for a reading — the cleanest thing she does all chapter, and it costs her nothing, which should bother her. Ends the page doing arithmetic on a counter: doors, roll, lengths, twenty-two.

THE ARITHMETIC CLAIMANT — हिसाबी दावेदार (unnamed)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 004–005. Stood at the back of the declaration queue on Page 004 doing arithmetic. Comes back with a fold of knots that are not his.

Arithmetic Claimant / Manav / Agnikhand / bought, not tied / heavy /
Declare: say the form's sentence and let clause two do the rest /
"Mine. I tied them."

Page 005 note: not a villain. He watched a woman declare her own hands and worked out what that is worth — the same arithmetic Ira has been doing all week. He speaks the form's sentence in the form's order, does not argue with the reading, folds his cloth and goes. The horror is that he is correct about the form, not that he is evil.

NANDI — नन्दी (reader of the bigger book)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, knotted at the ends / light, paid in kind /
Read: identify any knot's kind, year and hand; cannot read a page and has never misread a knot /
"Kind: basin. Year: nine. Hand: not this one's."

Page 005 note: performs the reading she is obliged to perform — the schedule's condition, imposed on her as a humiliation — and it becomes the keeper's only defence. She can name the hand and does not. Second time in two days she has declined to hand a woman's hands to a man. The page still does not settle whether she knew the Page 003 entry was a lie.

THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक

No full sheet; Ch. 009–011. The Office's voice with a pen in it.

Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Ratify: confirm the Office's own rule even when it costs the Office /
"Reading stands. Entry refused: declaration and reading do not agree."

Page 005 note: ratifies the reading against the claimant without expression, because the Office cannot disown its own rule in front of the row. Then reads Nima her own entry back and charges her for it. Exact, not cruel — which is worse.

NIMA — नीमा (entered, and paying for it)

Full sheet (Ch. 010): ../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md · Art: ../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png

Nima / Kshudra / Agnikhand / grey, declared / none — that was the bargain, and it went /
Count: tally hands without reading them; cannot read a braid /
"I said I'd rather be a line in your book than a pair of hands you can borrow."

Page 005 note: the bill arrives. Enrolled hands may be required when the count runs short — she bought the right to be on the record by surrendering the right to say no. Not stupid, does not cry, does not grandstand: she sets the tray down and counts.

THE SLATE-MEN — पट्टी वाले (three men at the back of the queue, unnamed)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Page 005. Watching the reading and writing it down. No lines on the page.

Slate-Men / mixed / Agnikhand / none — they buy readings instead / heavy /
Copy: record a working method and sell it on to the next claimant /
"No line on this page."

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is twenty-two lengths and the geometry of an open square.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Lift: take a knot out of a record without cutting it; enrol without entering a name /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M."

Page note: the first false declaration, and the keeper refuses nothing. Clause two has **no line for no — Ira wrote it to protect an honest woman and cannot now refuse a dishonest man, because the Office holds the copy. So she asks for a reading**, and Nandi gives it: kind, year, hand — not this one's, stopping short of the name she could have given. The Office ratifies its own rule against itself. Nima pays the specific price of being enrolled — you are the line; the line is what lets us borrow you. And the second school and the survey turn out to be the same shape: doors with hands in them, hands with no entry, a sum anybody could do. Hook: the survey reaches the end of the alley tomorrow.
