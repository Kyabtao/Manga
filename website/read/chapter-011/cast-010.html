Chapter 011 — Page 010 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one, including the unnamed ones.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Leave the lamp burning."

Page 010 note — the spending. Opens the drawer, names the chapter's one chain-stop at last — the Loom's last knot, left as an unnamed pledge in the Freedom Year: "It is the last thing my mother ever brought me." Runs the Office's by-length call until the trap is undeniable, then does the mender's answer instead of refusing: puts in the piece that was missing. Ties the knot into the count-cord in front of the whole basin — one home, ever. Her only line on the page, and the chapter's last: "Leave the lamp burning."

NANDI — नन्दी (reader of the bigger book — ending the reading)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, cut from one piece / none — she was never entered /
Read: identify any knot's kind, year and hand; cannot read a page; reads every entry ALOUD by
schedule / *(closes the big book, off-schedule, for the first time in her life)*

Page 010 note: reads the morning call — forty-one names answered in the open — and stops the moment the knot goes into the cord. No line. She closes the big book with both pinned sleeves and holds it shut. Her chapter: read (003), declined a name (005), that one is mine (006), read the lie in all night (007), chose the open (009), and now ends the reading herself. She has not forgiven Page 007; the book closing is her sentence.

KESSA — केसा (custody, discharged)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"All of it, keeper. You owe me the cloth."

Page 010 note: forty years of custody, discharged in one flat sentence. The fold comes back to her — empty — exactly as Page 007 promised: all of it. She holds out one scarred hand and does not embrace anybody. The cloth goes back in the box, and the box stays hers.

NIMA — नीमा (present, and teaching)

Full sheet (Ch. 010): ../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md · Art: ../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png

Nima / Kshudra / Agnikhand / grey, declared / none — that was the bargain /
Answer: steps out before her name is finished /
"Present. Wearing the coat, clerk. Mark it properly this time."

Page 010 note: first of the entered to answer the roll — loudly, in the coat, making the clerk mark it. Then at dusk she is at the crate in the lender's coat, demonstrating a hold to a growing half-circle: the school's newest teacher, and the keeper never asked her. The coat comes off on nobody's command; the chapter ends and it is still on.

THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक (procedural to the end)

No full sheet; Ch. 009–011. The Office's voice with a pen in it.

Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Postpone: write down the defeat as procedure and set a clock /
"Count not taken. The count will be taken when the roll is whole."

Page 010 note: runs the count as instructed, summons the purchased reader to verify, and when the verification kills the case he writes the loss down like a closing window. Not cruel, not stupid — a man whose instrument met something it could not call. He leaves with the cord and the knot, and the clock he sets is Chapter 012's problem.

THE PURCHASED READER — ख़रीदा हुआ पाठक (paid twice, honest twice)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 008–010. The first city instrument in the basin.

Purchased Reader / Manav / city / brass thumb-ring, lens on a cord / whatever the fee is /
Read for pay: a competent reading, honestly reported, honestly priced /
"Kind: all. Year: all. Hand: all. ... That will be two coppers."

Page 010 note: the chapter's last rhyme. Page 008's hand: none becomes all: all — same professional, same fee-book, and his honesty serves the basin this time because it serves accuracy either way. The Office pays him to verify its own defeat. He is not redeemed. He is paid.

THE RIVETER — रिवेट-कार (one of the nine; fist up at dusk)

No line on this page. His fist is up in the last panel.

THE TWENTY-TWO — बाईस (called by length; uncollected)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 007–010. Nine who spoke and thirteen who keep the teaching.

The Twenty-Two / mixed / Agnikhand / grey, half-cut in fist / various /
Stand: called by length and uncollected — a count with a link that fits everything cannot pull /
No line on this page. The count will be taken when the roll is whole.

Page 010 note: present in the square with fists closed on their halves while the keeper's measure could have made them Office hands. The knot in the cord makes the call unable to tell them from anyone. The requisition leaves with the clerk postponed, not cancelled.

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is a pledge: one visit in nine years, made in the dark, and spent at dawn.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Give: leave the Loom's last word with the daughter who keeps the counter /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M." — the Freedom Year pledge was the visit. It is spent now, and the whole
basin knows, and she taught the whole basin.

Page note: the chapter's one chain-stop is spent. The Loom's last knot — pawned unnamed in the Freedom Year, kept nine years, never read — goes into the count-cord in front of the whole basin, and the requisition chain dies of universal fit: kind: all, year: all, hand: all. The Office writes down its own defeat as procedure and postpones. The empty fold comes back to Kessa, all of it. At dusk the school keeps its own lamp, with Nima teaching. Ira's only line: "Leave the lamp burning." Smallest caption, the mother's method inherited: "Nobody writes anything down."
