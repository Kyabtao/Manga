Chapter 011 — Page 004 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one, including the unnamed ones.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Enter them. At my word. Like you did."

Page 004 note: enters another woman's hands at that woman's word and understands mid-stroke that she has made clause two everybody's instrument. She keeps writing — the woman has done nothing wrong. Ends the page counting thread against felt and finding forty-one names against sixty-three lengths.

THE FIRST DECLARANT — प्रथम घोषणाकर्ता (a counting woman, unnamed)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 001–004. One of Jadi's counting pupils — the one whose hand stopped mid-count on Page 003. Brings her own knots in a fold of cloth to be entered under her own name.

First Declarant / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, self-tied / light, self-held /
Declare: enter your own hand at your own word, before anyone can claim it /
"Mine. I tied them."

Page 004 note: not a villain and not a fool. She wants the thing clause two was built to give, and getting it is exactly what breaks it. The horror of the page is structural, not personal.

NIMA — नीमा (reader-second → entered)

Full sheet (Ch. 010): ../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md · Art: ../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png

Nima / Kshudra / Agnikhand / grey, now declared / none — that was the bargain /
Count: tally hands without reading them; cannot read a braid /
"Enter me. I'd rather be a line in your book than a pair of hands you can borrow."

Page 004 note: the cost lands. Offered the choice — hold for the count and stay undeclared, or be entered as a hand — she chooses to be written down, which is the one thing her wage existed to prevent. She sets the tray down and leaves it down. Not played as triumph: a girl paying in the only currency she has.

THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक

No full sheet; Ch. 009–011. The Office's voice with a pen in it.

Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Offer: name two entries and let the subject choose which one costs her /
"Entry: Nima, ninth terrace, Kshudra, enrolled at the hatch, year nine."

Page 004 note: not cruel, exact. He offers Nima the choice in the street where the row can hear, and writes her answer without comment.

THE WATCHING CLAIMANT — देखता हुआ दावेदार (unnamed)

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is twenty-two people nobody wrote down.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Lift: take a knot out of a record without cutting it; enrol without entering a name /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M."

Page note: the gap the clerk left in clause four is walked into — a counting woman declares her own hands and clause two becomes public property; Nima is offered the choice and chooses to be entered rather than lend her hands; and Ira measures the felt's impressions against the Roll of Hands. Hook: forty-one names, sixty-three lengths, twenty-two hands this basin has never written down — taught in the open, in daylight, by a woman who is on nobody's roll. The Office is counting doors; my mother has been counting something else for forty years, and I have been handing out needles to both of them.
