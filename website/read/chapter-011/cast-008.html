Chapter 011 — Page 008 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one, including the unnamed ones.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "He has sold you the news that thread is thread."

Page 008 note: wins the morning and loses the month. The purchase fails on her testimony — "Read the forty-one" — and the clerk's pivot lands while she is still standing in the win: the twenty-two are on the roll, and the roll lends. Feels the floor move without moving. At dusk she picks up the lamp: the fourth step is hers, out of the counter's geography, into the open where her mother taught. Does not step down on-panel — the page ends on the hand and the pick-up.

NANDI — नन्दी (reader of the bigger book)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, cut from one piece / none — she was never entered /
Read: identify any knot's kind, year and hand; cannot read a page; reads every entry ALOUD by
schedule / "You have been copying basin thread for years, clerk. You knew that before I said it."

Page 008 note: reads the morning exactly as she reads every morning — including the twenty-two she read in at night — unperturbed, while the clerk's composure comes apart page by page. Her verdict and the purchased reader's are the same words at different fees. No mention of her own entry, her own night, or her year; whatever it costs her is not drawn.

KESSA — केसा (custody, acknowledged)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"One other person has ever had the fold in her hands, keeper."

Page 008 note: unwraps the fold at the box and finds it light — thirteen halves gone between dark and dawn, the fold tied off again in her own knot, done properly. Forty years of custody, opened from outside, and she is not afraid; she is acknowledged. She names the telling: "The telling has started." At dusk she puts out the box's lamp and gives the page its turn: "They are not waiting for the book, keeper. They are waiting for you."

NIMA — नीमा (the bill, in her voice)

Full sheet (Ch. 010): ../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md · Art: ../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png

Nima / Kshudra / Agnikhand / grey, declared / none — that was the bargain /
Count: everything she did yesterday, she did as Office /
"Now you have twenty-two more hands where mine are."

Page 008 note: two beats, both devastating, both flat. The pivot: she warned exactly this on Page 005 and now it is policy. The suspicion: the survey's flags were read to four and three are accounted for — the fourth is on the Office's own roll, so discipline her and the Office admits its roll was used against it. "If the Office wants to ask me, it asks in writing." Still in the coat; the coat comes off on nobody's command, ever, in this chapter.

THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक (the pivot)

No full sheet; Ch. 009–011. The Office's voice with a pen in it.

Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Pivot: what cannot be refused can be collected /
"They are on the roll, keeper. The roll is the roll."

Page 008 note: not stupid, not kind. Follows his own copy through the morning reading; watches the purchase fail publicly; cannot dispute the entries after Page 005's precedent — so he accepts them and collects them: the count runs short, entered hands may be required. Then tries to count his own losses, finds one of them on his own roll, and writes nothing. The chapter's best adversary does the Office's will without once raising his voice.

THE ARITHMETIC CLAIMANT — हिसाब-वाला दावेदार (recurring; bought and paid for)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 004, 005, 008. The man who does arithmetic at the back of queues.

Arithmetic Claimant / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, for hire / heavy — he spends other people's /
Arithmetic: watch one lawful thing work, then cost out doing it too /
"Bought and paid for. Read the night's lengths, city man."

Page 008 note: watched a woman declare her own hands on Page 004, tried the form himself on Page 005, and now buys the strongest counter the basin has seen: a city reader, paid to read the night's lengths in public. His arithmetic is right and the answer sinks him — the thread cannot testify against anyone because it cannot testify at all. Not a villain: a man whose sums keep coming out true and meaning nothing.

THE PURCHASED READER — ख़रीदा हुआ पाठक (new; one page, probably more)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Page 008. The first city instrument to enter the basin on-panel.

Purchased Reader / Manav / city / brass thumb-ring, lens on a cord / whatever the fee is /
Read for pay: a competent reading, honestly reported, honestly priced /
"Kind: basin. Year: true. Hand: none."

Page 008 note: not a fraud and not drawn as a villain — he reports exactly what basin thread shows, which is nothing, twice, the second time knowing it wrecks the man who hired him: "he was paid for a reading and not for a side." His verdict rhymes with Nandi's — same words, different mouth, different fee. Fee-book in knot-script; no §3.1 props anywhere near him.

THE THIRTEEN — तेरह (told; waiting)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 007–008. The mother's pupils who keep the teaching.

The Thirteen / mixed / Agnikhand / grey, half-cut in fist / various /
Keep: the teaching is silence in rooms with books — they will not enter /
No line on this page. They are not waiting for the book. They are waiting for you.

Page 008 note: told overnight, by the only person who could have told them — each woke with a half-cut that matches a line in the register. They arrive through the afternoon and hold the alley without crossing the threshold: a count standing in a gutter-lit lane, keeping the teaching. The stall is a room with a book in it, and they will not enter.

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is a lightness in a bundle and a knot tied from outside.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Tell: tell her people what was done for them, in her only language — thread, delivered /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M." — thirteen halves, between dark and dawn, and Kessa's knot tied after.

Page note: the morning reading happens with the clerk standing in it and he finds what he copied: not one hand. The purchase fails honestly — hand: none is true of all sixty-three lengths — and the pivot lands in the same hour: the entries stand, and the count runs short, and entered hands may be required. Nima's bill comes due in her own voice, and she is untouchable by the Office's own construction. The fold comes back light by thirteen, tied off in Kessa's knot. By dusk the alley is full of people who will not come in. Hook: "They are not waiting for the book, keeper. They are waiting for you." — and the smallest caption: "I pick up the lamp."
