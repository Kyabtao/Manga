Chapter 011 — Page 006 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one, including the unnamed ones.

Note: this page contains a reveal that retroactively re-reads Pages 003 and 005. See the Nandi entry and the page note at the bottom.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "I can find them. Not by reading — by measuring."

Page 006 note: asks Kessa the question she should have asked on Page 003 and finds the way past the read-aloud condition (not under the schedule — at night, with the book shut). Watches Nima be used and does nothing, which the page must show. Works out the instrument — measure, don't read — and then the clock: twenty-two people, one night. Brings the chain-stop within reach and finds it does not fit: it stops a chain, it does not stop a list.

NANDI — नन्दी (reader of the bigger book — and one of the twenty-two)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, cut from one piece / none — she was never entered /
Read: identify any knot's kind, year and hand; cannot read a page; reads every entry ALOUD by
schedule / "That one is mine."

Page 006 note — REVEAL. She reads the mother's fold of cloth and gets kind: basin. Year: nineteen. Then fails: "There is a year in it. There is no hand in it." The mother's shadowless thread will not hold a hand to be read — which is why no list has ever held the mother's people. Then, at the end of the page, she lays her own hand down beside one of the twenty-two lengths without being asked: "That one is mine."

Canon effect: this retroactively explains the two times she declined to name a hand (Pages 003 and 005) without contradicting them, and it does not settle whether she knew the Page 003 entry was a lie — it makes both readings worse. She has been reading the Office's register aloud every morning while unenrolled herself, which means the Office has been hearing her. Her card line's debt tendency changes from light, paid in kind to none — she was never entered.

KESSA — केसा

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"Forty years I kept these without asking what they were. Do not make me sorry I let you look."

Page 006 note: does not refuse — prices it. A pawnbroker does not say no, she says that will cost you this. She names the cost of the reading out loud: every reading under the schedule is read aloud, so a reading names twenty-two hands straight into an Office book. Ira's answer — night, book shut, no clerk — is the first way anybody has found past that condition, and she opens her hand.

NIMA — नीमा (being used)

Page 006 note: the bill is collected. She is told to hold and she holds. She does not look at Ira once, because being looked at would make it worse. Silent all page. Not rescued, not grandstanding — the point is that the machinery runs exactly as written.

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is thread that will not hold a hand.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Lift: take a knot out of a record without cutting it; enrol without entering a name /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M."

Page note: the survey closes at the water-butt and the twenty-two stop being a sum and become a list of addresses, in the Office by tonight. Nima is told to hold and holds. Kessa prices the reading and hands over the box anyway. The reading fails — Nandi gets kind and year out of the mother's thread and no hand, because shadowless thread will not take an impression. So Ira finds the instrument that would work (measure, don't read) and the clock that kills it (twenty-two people, one night), and discovers the chain-stop she has carried since the loom stopped speaking stops a chain, not a list. Then Nandi lays her hand down beside one of the twenty-two lengths and says four words. Hook: "One of them has been standing at my counter every morning this week reading my register aloud into an Office book. And she has never once been entered."
