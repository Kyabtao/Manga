IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "It does not find truth. It makes it."

Page 007 note: enters all twenty-two — nine at their spoken word, thirteen at thread alone. Every entry is false on its face (teaching-years read as enrollment-years) and true anyway, which is Page 002's lie twenty-two times. Says less and less as the night goes on; at dawn, when the clerk flinches at the years, she says nothing at all — the Office taught the basin that rule itself, on Tuesday, in the square.

NANDI — नन्दी (reader of the bigger book — reading the lie in, and then herself in)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, cut from one piece / none — she was never entered /
Read: identify any knot's kind, year and hand; cannot read a page; reads every entry ALOUD by
schedule / "How many more?" — and then she reads them.

Page 007 note — the cost is real time now. She confirms kind and year on every length, repeats the shadowless verdict (there is no hand in it), pauses exactly once — "How many more?" / "Eleven." — and then reads twenty-two face-false entries into the Office's roll knowing exactly what they are. At the end of the night she reads her own entry aloud: "Entered at the owner's word" — her word being four words long. After tonight the question is no longer whether she knew about Page 003; the question is what a person is who reads this in and keeps reading. Unresolved, on purpose.

KESSA — केसा (custody)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"One at a time, keeper. The fold comes back to me when it is done — all of it."

Page 007 note: stands all night and controls disbursement — one length at a time, the fold visibly shrinking, and the fold returns to her when it is done. The Office copies the keeper's book, never the pawnbroker's box — the difference between the record and the evidence is going to matter. Names the consequence of the thirteen: when it gets told, it gets told by the one who taught them to stay out of books.

NIMA — नीमा (the coat at the shutter)

Full sheet (Ch. 010): ../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md · Art: ../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png

Nima / Kshudra / Agnikhand / grey, declared / none — that was the bargain /
Recall: she held every flagged hand this afternoon and knows which door each of them walked back

Page 007 note: her purchase pays out. She stands in the doorway and does not come inside, still in the Office's grey coat and will not take it off — the Office is in the room while its roll is being loaded against it. Recites the flagged doors from memory; nothing written, nothing carried. Not doing it for Ira, and says so. Four sentences, then the page moves on.

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is the fold, the true years, and the telling she is owed.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Lift: take a knot out of a record without cutting it; enrol without entering a name /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M." — and now twenty-two of her people are in the Office's roll anyway.

Page note: the night's work — nine come and speak their word, thirteen stay away and are entered at thread alone, and all twenty-two entries go into the roll with no name on any of them, their years true and their meaning false on its face. Nandi reads every one aloud, including her own, and knows exactly what she is reading. At dawn the grey clerk copies the impossible years because the Office cannot disown its own rule, the list of addresses is already wrong — and somewhere a claimant is asking what a name costs. Hook: "Twenty-two hands entered. Not one name."
