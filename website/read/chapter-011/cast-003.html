Chapter 011 — Page 003 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one, including the unnamed ones.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "I tied it."

Page 003 note: says her own lie out loud, in the machinery of Nandi's condition, and it costs her nothing — that is the point. Do not draw guilt on her face. She hears clause four discharged with a gap left standing in it, then takes the cut-end to Kessa and gets a date instead of an answer.

NANDI — नन्दी (reader of the bigger book)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, knotted at the ends / light, paid in kind /
Read: identify any knot's kind, year and hand; cannot read a page and has never misread a knot /
"Entry: one knot, ash-grey, tied within the week."

Page 003 note: reads the day's entries back, including the keeper's own. She does not know it is a lie — or she does, and reads it anyway, because her condition is that nothing goes unread. Her face gives nothing either way, and the page should not settle it.

KESSA — केसा

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"If you want to know what it was cut from, ask the woman they bound. All I ever kept was the day."

Page 003 note: answers when, not what or who, and says out loud why she never asked — a pawnbroker who asks loses the thing she was told to keep. Fifteen years: the cloth, the thread and a blank note, all kept since the day Ira's mother was bound. Offers the tally-thread as the record of the date.

THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक (the survey)

No full sheet; Ch. 009–011. The Office's voice with a pen in it.

Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Survey: close the measurement, then leave the gap in it standing up in the air /
"It stands because the keeper produced an object on the step. It does not say the object was hers."

Page 003 note: the page's coldest beat, delivered flat with his hands still on the slate. He is not threatening her — he is reading her own entry back and leaving the hole in it on the record.

THE COUNTING WOMEN — गिनती वाली स्त्रियाँ (three, unnamed)

No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 001–003. Followed the survey the whole way and did not go home. On this page one of them stops counting — hand still mid-gesture — and that is the only reaction in the page.

Counting Woman / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, self-tied / light, self-held /

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is a date, a length of thread, and a blank note — plus the impressions in the felt.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Lift: take a knot out of a record without cutting it; enrol without entering a name /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M."

Page note: the survey closes at the water-butt — eleven doors, one morning, forty years counted; the entries are read back and Ira says her own lie out loud without flinching; clause four is discharged and the clerk leaves the gap standing. Kessa gives a date instead of an answer: fifteen years, the day they bound her, a fold of cloth, a hand's width of thread, and a note that says nothing. Hook: the grey felt in the lockbox keeps impressions — same width, same length, pressed in and lifted out, more than there is light to count. She has been cutting lengths off one piece for forty years, and Kessa's box has been the measure the whole time.
