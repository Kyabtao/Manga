THREADBORN — Chapter 07 — Page 005

Chapter title: The Crimson Line / क़िरमिज़ी पंक्ति Arc: II — The Mendery Sector: Agnikhand — the Knot & Nail, Ashfall Basin Page type: Reveal page — 7 panels

Beats: Page 004 — the crimson line is crease-writ, drawn in a bead of wax dug out of the letter's own seal. Somebody read the principal's letter, warned Ira inside the fold, and re-pressed the crimson with a thumb. Ira has worked out who. She has not said it.

This page: the thread confirms it. And then the thread does something it has never done.

PANEL 1 — Medium, Ira alone with the sheet (~13%)

Camera: Ira has walked out of the stall. She is at the back of the stall, under the awning, facing away from Kessa, holding the letter flat in both hands in front of her chest — not reading it, holding it. The market noise continues behind the panel edge.

Image: The girl leaving the counter to answer a question in the only room she trusts, which is her own hand.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Kessa doesn't follow. She never follows. She keeps the stall and I keep the sky and the back of the awning is not the sky, but it'll do for a minute.

PANEL 2 — Close, palm to page (~14%)

Camera: Close: Ira turns the letter over and lays the crease-writ line flat across her open left palm — directly across the stitch. Her right hand comes down on top, pressing page to palm.

Image: The line laid on the stitch. Two sewn-shut things meeting. The panel should be quiet: no motion lines, no glow yet.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): There's one way to ask a thread a question. You put the question on your skin and you wait.

PANEL 3 — Macro, the strand moves (~14%)

Camera: Macro. Under her fingers, at the edge of the stitch, the blank strand comes loose from the palm — the mother's thread, the teacher's strand, ash-grey and shadowless. It rises, and it goes not to the grey wax and not to the letter's grey ink. It goes to the crimson, threads itself along the eleven words, and lies down inside them.

Image: A thread taking its place inside a line of writing. Two strands of the same woman: one sewn into a daughter's hand, one drawn in wax into a fold.

SFX: —

CAPTION (none spoken): It doesn't reach. It doesn't hunt. It settles — the way a thread settles into a seam that was cut for it.

CAPTION: Same gauge. Same twist. Same hand at the spindle.

PANEL 4 — Large, the mark opens (~15%)

Camera: The palm, opened. The stitch comes undone from the inside — thread lifting out loop by loop without tearing (the Ch. 004 method, reused). Underneath, the Sutra-mark: projection type, dormant, braided — two strands wound around each other, and in this panel, for the first time, the two strands are the same colour as the crease-writ.

Image: The mark answering. Not glowing — matching. The reveal is likeness, not power.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The braid's two strands have always looked like the same grey to me. They aren't.

CAPTION: One of them is crimson under the grey. It's been crimson under the grey since I was four years old and I have looked at this hand every single day since and never once seen it.

CAPTION: You can't see it in daylight. You can only see it next to something else she touched.

PANEL 5 — Close, the four notes (~14%)

Camera: Ira's ear, close, in profile — but the panel is drawn so the sound comes from the palm, not the air. Editorial art note: no sound waves from the market; the notes are drawn as thin crimson threads moving up from her hand.

SFX (through the thread, not the air): hn — hn — hn — hn

Four notes. Off-key. In the correct order. Exactly as they were on Ch. 006 Page 007.

Image: The four notes arriving through the thread. The sound drawn as thread because that is where it lives now.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, very small): Four notes. Off by the same amount they have always been off by.

CAPTION: Her hum.

CAPTION: It's the same four notes the chain has been echoing at me for six chapters, and this time they aren't coming off the chain and they aren't coming out of the air. They're coming up the strand into the middle of my hand like somebody tuning a string from the other end.

PANEL 6 — Extreme close, something else (~14%)

Camera: The palm again, macro. The four notes have stopped. In their place — thin, dry, exact, twice, with the small uneven pause between them that a person makes when they turn a needle around: a needle going through cloth. Not a memory. Not an echo. Present tense.

Image: The single most important panel of the chapter. A sound with no visual source, in a series where the mother has never spoken.

SFX: tsk ... tsk

CAPTION (Ira's voice, unsteady for the first time): Then it stops.

CAPTION: And then I hear it.

CAPTION: Two stitches. Dry thread. A needle going through worked cloth, and turning, and going through again. Somebody's hand is heavy on the needle and there's a small drag on the second pull, like the cloth is doubled.

PANEL 7 — Close, Ira standing very still — CLOSING HOOK (~16%)

Camera: Ira, back of the awning, hands still palm-up, letter on the palm. She is not looking at it. She is looking up and out, past the awning edge, over the pot-row, at the whole city.

Image: The girl with her hand open and the city in front of her, listening to something a street away or a mile away and having no way to tell which.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Memory sounds like a room you've left.

CAPTION: This doesn't sound like a room I've left. It sounds like a room somebody is in.

CAPTION (last line): She's sewing. Right now, somewhere in Agnikhand, with a needle, the way she has been sewing for forty years — and the strand she sewed into my hand when I was four years old is picking up the work.

CAPTION (smaller): Three days gone. A crease-writ in a fold. Four notes. Two stitches.

CAPTION (smallest): And the only thread in the world that runs from me to her, running the wrong way.

Writing & art notes

  • The confirmation is material, not magical. The braid doesn't tell her a name. It shows her a match: same gauge, same twist, same hand at the spindle — and a colour in the palm strand she has never been able to see in daylight.
  • The two-strand braid's second colour is now locked canon. The braid is Ira's grey and the mother's crimson-under-grey. Do not render the second strand crimson outside the presence of the mother's material (wax, thread, cloth) — the constraint is what makes it land.
  • Four notes: correct order, correct off-pitch, drawn as thread. Chapter 006 p007 established hearing them correctly through the thread's memory. The escalation here is present tense — it is a live channel now, not an archive. Keep the sound tight and small. This series has never played the notes as a fanfare; do not start.
  • The needle is the hook, not the voice. The mother still does not speak. On the page where she finally, verifiably is, she communicates by working. The Loom never speaks; the mother does not either — she sews.
  • Do not let Kessa hear any of it. Kessa's scene is Page 006: Ira comes back to the counter and tells her, and Kessa's response is practical, immediate and slightly devastating.
