THREADBORN — Chapter 07 — Page 001

Chapter title: The Crimson Line / क़िरमिज़ी पंक्ति Arc: II — The Mendery Sector: Agnikhand — the Knot & Nail, the roof over it, the Council Stair Page type: Chapter opener — 7 panels

Beats: Chapter 006 closed on the counter — the principal's letter, grey wax and crimson wax touching on one fold, unopened, Kessa's loupe down beside it. Chapter 007 opens it. But not on page 001. Page 001 is the night of not opening it — the cost of restraint, and the first sign that the letter is not inert.

Chapter 007 premise: a name is a hook. The letter gives Ira a name — and a name is how paperwork finds you. Everything the principal has ever wanted, he has taken by writing it down. This chapter is Ira learning that the gift is the trap, and answering it in the only language she has.

Continuity: chain echo fading (Ch. 006 p007, p010). Eight objects in the lockbox plus the charter. Four notes now match correctly through the thread's memory. Mother three days gone. Chain-stop budget unspent — Chapter 007 holds it in reserve, second chapter running.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the roof (~14%)

Camera: Night. The roof over the Knot & Nail. Ira lies on her back under the turning Loom, the letter flat on her open palm — on the stitch, on the mark. Ash falls past her. The Loom's light crosses her face every few seconds, slow.

Image: The girl, the Loom and the letter. The letter held exactly where the thread lives. She is not reading it. She is listening to it. She has been here for hours.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Kessa said: not here. Not alone. Not with your hand on it.

CAPTION: So I did the next best thing. I lay down with it and let the thread do the reading.

PANEL 2 — Close, the palm (~14%)

Camera: Extreme close on Ira's open palm. The stitch, sewn shut fifteen years ago. On top of it, the letter. The braided mark's blank strand has come loose from the palm and is climbing, slowly, toward the fold — not toward the grey wax. Toward the crimson.

Image: The strand choosing. Out of two seals on one fold, the thread goes to the crimson one. It knows that wax.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): The strand went past the grey. It didn't even slow at the grey.

CAPTION: It's been three days since the sewer went quiet. Three days since the last mending thread came up out of the dark. And the strand on my palm is climbing toward the principal's seal like a dog that heard a door.

PANEL 3 — Macro, the crimson wax (~14%)

Camera: Macro. The crimson seal fills the panel. At this magnification the truth is visible: the crimson wax is not one pour. There is an outer ring — smooth, clean, a stamp's edge. And inside it, a slightly different red, faintly cloudy, pressed in by something that was not a stamp. A fingertip.

Image: The seal that was opened and closed again. The panel is the evidence and nothing else.

CAPTION (none spoken): One pour of wax has one edge. This seal has two.

CAPTION: Somebody opened the principal's letter before it reached the stair. Somebody read it. And then somebody pressed the crimson back down with a thumb and put it in the dark for a girl to find.

PANEL 4 — Medium, Kessa at the counter (~13%)

Camera: Below, in the stall, Kessa has not gone to bed. She sits with the loupe up and the tally-thread across her knuckles, counting nothing — just holding it. She is looking at the hatch in the ceiling that leads to the roof. She has been looking at it for a while.

Image: The woman who raised her, awake, not going up. Waiting to be asked. Kessa's interiority is hands and shutters — here, both: hands full, shutters half.

CAPTION (none spoken): She doesn't come up. That's the whole of it. She doesn't come up, and she doesn't call down, and she doesn't put the lamp out.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Four Unspoolings I've lived on that roof. She has never once climbed it. She keeps the stall and I keep the sky, and the hatch is the border, and neither of us crosses it for nothing.

PANEL 5 — Close, two hands on one fold (~14%)

Camera: Dawn. The Knot & Nail counter, shutters up, market still thin. The letter lies between them. Ira's hand on the left corner, Kessa's scarred hand on the right. Kessa's rule, spoken plainly, no ornament.

Image: Two hands, one fold. The witness, done properly. This is a Kshudra counter and a Kshudra rule: nothing is opened by one person.

KESSA: A letter sealed in two waxes is a letter with two owners. Open it with one hand and you're the only person who saw what it said — which means you're the only person anyone can blame.

KESSA: So. Two hands. You take grey. I take crimson.

PANEL 6 — Close, the grey breaks (~13%)

Camera: Ira's thumbnail under the grey seal. One clean motion. The grey wax does what wax does when no one has touched it — it snaps, the whole stamp lifting off in one piece, the Council's mark intact and unbroken in her hand like a coin.

Image: A clean break. The grey was never opened. The grey is honest. Whatever is wrong with this letter, it is not wrong at the Council's end.

SFX: tk

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Grey comes off in one piece. Clean. Never touched. The Council's stamp whole in my hand like a coin somebody dropped and forgot to want.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the crimson gives — CLOSING HOOK (~18%)

Camera: Kessa's thumbnail under the crimson seal. It does not snap. It lifts — the whole seal comes up as one soft piece, slightly cloudy, the way wax comes up when it has already been broken once and pressed back down warm. Kessa goes completely still. Her thumb stays where it is. She does not look at Ira. She looks at the wax.

Image: The crimson seal, whole, soft, already-opened, sitting on Kessa's thumb. The proof.

KESSA: ...Child.

KESSA: This one's already open.

CAPTION (none spoken): Three days. A letter sealed in two waxes, carried down a stair, and left in the dark for a girl to find. And somebody got to the crimson before she did.

CAPTION (last line): The principal wrote the letter. Somebody else read it first.

CAPTION (smaller): Somebody who could reach the crimson wax and no further.

Writing & art notes

  • Page 001 is the night of not opening it. The chapter's restraint is the point: Ira's whole ethic is nothing gets sewn till I've seen the tear. She applies it to paper. Kessa's rule — two hands on one fold — is a Kshudra counter-law and mirrors the school's rule one (thread is braided, never single): nothing is true that one person holds alone.
  • The strand goes to the crimson, not the grey. Established here so that Page 005 pays it: the braid does not know the principal's hand. It knows the wax.
  • The two-pour seal is the chapter's engine. It is a physical, verifiable clue — no magic, no Sight. A mender's evidence: you read a seam by whether it has been sewn twice.
  • Kessa's interiority stays shutters-and-hands. She never says I was worried. She says the lamp is still lit and the hatch is still shut and that is the same sentence.
  • Do not name the principal. He is the principal throughout Chapter 007. First naming is planned for Ch. 20–30 (see Ch. 001 summary).
  • The Loom never speaks. It turns; it does not comment.
  • Four notes are not in this page — deliberately. Chapter 007 keeps the notes rare so that their next appearance lands.
