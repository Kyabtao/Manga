THREADBORN — Chapter 07 — Page 006

Chapter title: The Crimson Line / क़िरमिज़ी पंक्ति Arc: II — The Mendery Sector: Agnikhand — the Knot & Nail, Ashfall Basin Page type: Regular — 7 panels

Beats: Page 005 — the strand settled into the crease-writ. The braid showed crimson under the grey. Four notes, correct, through the thread. Then two stitches of live needlework, present tense. The mother is working, right now, somewhere in Agnikhand.

This page: Kessa does the one thing Ira has never been able to do — she reads a person by her stitches.

PANEL 1 — Medium, Ira back at the counter (~13%)

Camera: Ira returns round the awning and sets the letter on the counter without a word. Kessa does not ask what happened out there. She looks at Ira's thumb — red — and at her palm, where the stitch has visibly loosened, thread lifted, not yet re-sewn.

Image: Two women at a counter and one piece of evidence, which is Ira's hand.

KESSA: You opened it with your hand on it.

KESSA: ...Right. Come here.

PANEL 2 — Close, Kessa takes the hand (~14%)

Camera: Kessa takes Ira's left hand in both of hers, turns it palm-up, and reads it the way she read it on Ch. 001 Page 002 — appraiser's grip, thumb across the palm, but this time she is reading work, not debt.

Image: The appraiser's hands, the mender's palm, and a loose stitch. The series' central gesture, page 002 of chapter 001 and page 006 of chapter 007, two hundred pages of story held between them.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): She reads the stitch first. Then she reads the seam — the new line of lifted thread where the strand came out to lie down in the writing.

CAPTION: Her thumb goes still on it. That's the tell. Kessa's thumb only stops for real numbers.

PANEL 3 — Close, Kessa asks the right question (~14%)

Camera: Kessa, looking at Ira's palm and not at her face. She asks the question the whole chapter has been circling.

Image: The appraiser asking for the material fact. Not who was it. Not are you all right.

KESSA: When it was down in the wax — what did you hear?

IRA: Four notes. Her hum. Right order, wrong pitch, same as the thread's memory in the archive.

IRA: And then something else. Two stitches. Dry thread, worked cloth, and a drag on the second pull — like the needle was going through two layers and not one.

KESSA: ...

KESSA: Say the second part again.

PANEL 4 — Large, Kessa reads the stitches (~15%)

Camera: Kessa's own hands come up into the panel and she shows Ira what she means — needle mimed, no needle in hand, the gesture of turning a needle through doubled cloth.

Image: The woman who has never been able to teach Ira threadwork, teaching Ira the one thing she does know: how to identify a trade from the sound of it.

KESSA: Two layers, uneven drag, and the turn is slow. Menders don't sew doubled cloth. You don't mend two layers at once, you open them and mend them one at a time — that's the whole craft.

KESSA: Doubled cloth with a heavy needle and a slow turn isn't mending, child. That's binding.

KESSA: Quires. Signatures. Hand-bound books — the thread has to go through the fold of every single sheet or the block falls apart, and the needle turns inside the fold where you can't see it, so the hand goes slow.

PANEL 5 — Close, Kessa lands it (~14%)

Camera: Kessa, loupe up, eyes narrow. She has done the geography and it has not taken her long, because there is only one place that answers.

Image: The basin narrowing to one address.

KESSA: Hand-binding. Doubled folds. In this basin.

KESSA: The Office uses a sew-through machine and a glue block — you can hear it from the stair, it sounds like a market closing. Nobody in the Ashfall does quires by hand. Nobody.

KESSA: Except the place that has been doing it by hand since before the Council kept records at all.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The Mendery.

CAPTION: The back wall. The files older than the Council's own records. And Nandi's hands, which have been keeping them since before I was born.

PANEL 6 — Medium, the box (~13%)

Camera: The counter. Kessa's hand on the lockbox lid — not opening it, just resting, the Ch. 006 Page 009 gesture. Behind her, the stall, shutters half down. The letter lies open on the counter.

Image: The woman who counts deciding what to spend.

IRA: I have to go back in.

KESSA: You have a key, child. You've had a key since the posting order. That's not the part I'm worried about.

KESSA: The Inspector has an audit warrant with the charter written on it. He has had it since the day you brought the school's name out of that cellar. And the last thing out of his mouth was files were supposed to be destroyed.

PANEL 7 — Close, Ira packs the kit — CLOSING HOOK (~17%)

Camera: Ira's hands, close, packing: the needle-roll, the shears, the thread-cake, the blank strand coiled under the wrist-wrap. On top of it all she places the principal's letter — folded, but not shut.

Image: A girl packing to go into the place where her mother is working, with the thing her mother's captor wrote, carried like a tool.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): She's binding quires two streets away, in the one building in Agnikhand that the Council has been trying to burn down quietly for forty years. And she's doing it badly enough to hear, with a needle she doesn't hurry, in a room where the walls are paper.

CAPTION (last line): She isn't hiding from me.

CAPTION (smaller): She's leaving the work where I'll find it on the third morning, the way she left Bhan's arm, and the four notes, and the crease. The school only has one way to say anything, and it is never with a mouth.

CAPTION (smallest): So I'm going to answer her. And I'm going to do it the same way.

Writing & art notes

  • Kessa's competence is the engine of this page. She cannot hold a needle. She has never once done threadwork. And she is the one who identifies the trade from the sound, because appraising is knowing what things are by how they wear. This is her page; keep Ira quiet through the middle of it.
  • "Binding" must land as a discovery, not a detour. Have Kessa's hands do the mime. The reader should be able to work out the Mendery one beat before Ira says it.
  • The stitch on Ira's palm is now visibly loosened — lifted thread, not yet re-sewn. This is a running visual state for Chapter 007. Chapter 008 must account for whether she re-sews it.
  • **The letter is carried folded but *not shut*** — a deliberate physical detail. A closed fold hides the crease-writ again; she is keeping the conversation open on her palm. Pay it on Page 010.
  • The Inspector's warrant stays offstage — mentioned, not shown. His escalation is Chapter 008's opening problem, not this chapter's.
