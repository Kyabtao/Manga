THREADBORN — Chapter 06 — Page 006

Chapter title: The Mother's File / माँ की फ़ाइल Arc: II — The Mendery Sector: Agnikhand — The Mendery archive → the Knot & Nail Page type: The return — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 005 — the workshop, the empty chair, the mother left three days ago to find the principal. The thread is cut.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the archive exit (~14%)

Camera: Ira leaves the Mendery through the back door, Nandi behind her. Dawn light in the ash-slums. The key is in Ira's belt. The braided mark is open. The mother's file and the charter are in her hands.

Image: Ira walks into the dawn. The Mendery's back door closes behind her. The ash-slums stretch ahead, the basin beyond.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): The sewer is my mother. The cutter is my mother. The supply line is my mother. And my mother has gone to find the principal.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the basin approach (~13%)

Camera: Ira walks through the basin toward the Knot & Nail. The market is waking. The Spindle is still brighter than usual. The basin does not know what she has learned.

Image: The basin normal: pots steaming, stalls opening, ash falling. Ira walks through it carrying twenty years of history.

CAPTION (none spoken): The basin does not know. The basin does not need to know. Not yet.

PANEL 3 — Close, the stall (~14%)

Camera: The Knot & Nail: Kessa behind the counter, loupe up. Ira arrives. Kessa reads her face the way she reads debt-marks — instantly, completely.

Image: Kessa's loupe is up. Her hands are on the counter. She sees Ira and she knows something has changed.

KESSA (dialogue, dry): You found it.

PANEL 4 — Medium, the telling (~13%)

Camera: Ira sets the mother's file and the charter on the counter. Kessa's loupe goes down. She reads the charter first — she can read knot-script, she is Kshudra. The name Sutar in the charter's founder column.

Image: Kessa reads. Her hands are steady. Her knuckles are white.

KESSA (dialogue, slow): Your mother founded the school.

IRA (dialogue): My mother is the sewer. My mother is the cutter. My mother ran the supply line from inside the Mendery for twenty years.

PANEL 5 — Wide, Kessa's response (~13%)

Camera: Kessa's hands: she closes the charter and sets it face-down on the counter — her refusal grammar. Then she opens the lockbox and sets the charter inside. Eight objects now: the seven from before plus the charter.

Image: The lockbox grows again. Eight objects. The box is the chapter's accumulating icon.

KESSA (dialogue, flat): Eight objects. All about one family.

PANEL 6 — Close, the question (~14%)

Camera: Kessa's loupe is up. Her eyes on Ira. The question she has held since Chapter 1: the same question Ira asked, the same question she postponed.

Image: Two women across a counter. The same question, different faces.

KESSA (dialogue, quiet): She went to find the principal.

IRA (dialogue): Yes.

KESSA (dialogue): Then the principal knows you're here. And the principal has your mother.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the lockbox — HOOK (~19%)

Camera: The lockbox on the counter, Kessa's hand on the lid. Eight objects inside. The charter is the newest. The mother's file is beside it. Outside, the basin works. Above, the Loom turns. And somewhere in the city, the mother is looking for the principal.

Image: The box is full. The history is complete. The girl is awake. And the mother is missing.

CAPTION (none spoken): Eight objects. One family. Twenty years of thread. The mother has gone to find the principal. The daughter has found the school.

CAPTION (smaller): The hand has opened. The file has been read. The charter has been found. And the woman who sewed a girl's palm shut is no longer in the archive she built.

Writing & art notes

  • The return to the Knot & Nail is the chapter's emotional pivot: Ira brings the archive's

truth to Kessa, and Kessa files it in the lockbox. The box grows to eight objects.

  • Kessa's refusal grammar is consistent: she sets the charter face-down before putting it

in the box. She refuses to acknowledge the school's existence formally; she stores it physically.

  • The mother is missing — the chapter's hook for the next chapter. The principal has the

mother, or the mother is looking for the principal. Either way, the supply line has been cut.

  • Chain budget: unspent. Four notes silent. Loom never speaks.
