THREADBORN — Chapter 06 — Page 009

Chapter title: The Mother's File / माँ की फ़ाइल Arc: II — The Mendery Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail Page type: The decision — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 008 — the Inspector with warrant, the charter recognized, "they weren't destroyed."

PANEL 1 — Wide, the stall after (~14%)

Camera: The Knot & Nail, evening: the Inspector has gone, taking the warrant but not the charter. Ira's compromise held. The lockbox is closed, eight objects inside.

Image: The stall after the standoff. Kessa's loupe is up. Ira sits at the counter. The box is under the counter.

CAPTION (none spoken): The Inspector took the warrant. The charter stayed. The box holds.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the decision (~13%)

Camera: Ira's face: the decision she has been building toward since Chapter 1. Not who sewed me — answered. Not what is the school — answered. The new question: what do I do with the braid.

Image: Ira's palm open, mark visible, braided thread contained. The question is in her eyes.

IRA (dialogue, flat): The school is named. The mother is gone. The principal has her. The Office wants the charter. The braid is awake.

IRA (dialogue): What do I do with it?

PANEL 3 — Close, Kessa's answer (~14%)

Camera: Kessa's hands: she reaches across the counter and sets her hand on Ira's palm — covering the mark, the same gesture as Ch. 004 p005. The first time Kessa has touched Ira's palm.

Image: Kessa's scarred hand on Ira's palm. The mark is covered. The thread is held. The appraiser's hand on the mender's hand.

KESSA (dialogue, quiet): You mend. That's what the braid is for. That's what the school taught. That's what your mother built.

KESSA (dialogue): Mend. The rest will follow.

PANEL 4 — Wide, the basin evening (~13%)

Camera: Evening: the Knot & Nail's shutters are up. The basin quietens. Ira sits at the counter, palm open, mark visible. The lockbox is under the counter. Kessa is behind it.

Image: The stall at rest. The box is full. The girl is ready.

CAPTION (none spoken): The box is full. The school is named. The mother is missing. And the girl who was sewn shut has decided what to do with the thread she was given.

PANEL 5 — Medium, the mend (~14%)

Camera: Ira mends — a dock worker with a torn mark, the routine work. The braided thread moves through the needle. The mark is open. The mend is debtless. The whorl-grain appears in the seam.

Image: The mend as it has always been: needle, thread, skin. But now the thread is braided, the mark is open, and the mender knows her own name.

SFX: tchk (needle through skin)

PANEL 6 — Close, the stitch (~13%)

Camera: The mend: the braided thread in the seam. The whorl-grain — the same uncanny whorl as the cut-end, the sixty-first knot, every piece in the box. The mother's signature, in the daughter's work.

Image: The whorl-grain in the finished seam. The mother's fingerprint in the daughter's mend.

CAPTION (none spoken): The whorl-grain. The same signature. The mother's fingerprint in the daughter's work. The school continues.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the lockbox — HOOK (~19%)

Camera: Night: the lockbox on the counter, Kessa's hand on the lid. Eight objects inside. Outside, the basin sleeps. Above, the Loom turns. On Ira's roof, the mark glows faintly. And somewhere in the city, the mother is looking for the principal. The girl is mending. The school continues.

Image: The box, the girl, the night. The same image as every chapter's end. But the box is full, the girl is awake, and the school's last student is doing the work the school was built for.

CAPTION (none spoken): Eight objects. One school. One girl. One missing mother. The hand has opened. The file has been read. The charter has been found. And the mender is mending.

CAPTION (smaller): The school of the braided thread continues. In one girl's hands. In one lockbox. In one basin that does not know what it has.

Writing & art notes

  • Kessa's hand on Ira's palm is the chapter's emotional climax: the first touch between

mentor and student. The mark is covered. The thread is held. The appraiser's hand on the mender's hand.

  • "Mend. The rest will follow." is Kessa's thesis: the school's purpose was mending, and

the daughter's purpose is the same.

  • The whorl-grain in the seam connects the mother's work to the daughter's: the same

signature, twenty years apart. The school continues through the mend.

  • Chain budget: unspent. Four notes silent. Loom never speaks.
