THREADBORN — Chapter 06 — Page 008

Chapter title: The Mother's File / माँ की फ़ाइल Arc: II — The Mendery Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail + up-terrace Page type: The Office escalates — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 007 — the four notes heard through the thread, Kessa reads the charter, eight objects in the lockbox.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the morning (~14%)

Camera: Morning, Knot & Nail: Ira mends at the counter. The queue is long. Kessa appraises. The lockbox is under the counter. Normal.

Image: The Knot & Nail at work. The basin's centre.

CAPTION (none spoken): The morning after the mother's voice was heard. The basin works. The stall mends.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the Inspector returns (~13%)

Camera: The Council Stair: the Inspector descends again — the same broad figure from Ch. 005 p005. But today he is not alone. Behind him, two Grey Clerks carry a sealed slate — the Office's highest instrument: an audit warrant.

Image: The Inspector and two clerks. The sealed slate. The basin's early warning: the pot's steam bends toward the stair.

CAPTION (none spoken): The Inspector returns. This time with a warrant. The basin's steam bends.

PANEL 3 — Close, the warrant (~14%)

Camera: The Inspector at the Knot & Nail's counter. The sealed slate is set on the wood. The warrant's text: the Office requires access to the Mendery archive. The Inspector has traced the thread-school's charter to the Mendery. He has traced the Mendery to Kessa's lockbox.

Image: The warrant on the counter. Grey ink, official. The Inspector's face is procedural, not hostile.

INSPECTOR (dialogue, official): The Office requires access to the Mendery archive. The charter has been traced to this stall.

INSPECTOR (dialogue): The warrant permits inspection of all objects in the custodial lockbox.

PANEL 4 — Medium, Kessa's refusal (~13%)

Camera: Kessa's hands: she sets the warrant face-down — her grammar. Then she sets the coin on top. The counter-offer. But this time the Inspector does not leave. He waits.

Image: The coin on the warrant. The Inspector waits. The basin watches.

KESSA (dialogue, flat): When the Office prices the charter, I'll price the inspection.

INSPECTOR (dialogue): The warrant is the price.

PANEL 5 — Wide, the standoff (~14%)

Camera: The Knot & Nail: Kessa behind the counter, loupe up. The Inspector across from her, warrant on the counter, coin on top. Ira stands to Kessa's left, palm visible, mark open. The basin watches from one row back.

Image: The standoff: the appraiser and the Inspector, the lockbox between them. The basin's two guardians.

CAPTION (none spoken): The appraiser and the Inspector. The box between them. The basin behind them. The warrant on the counter.

PANEL 6 — Close, Ira's choice (~13%)

Camera: Ira's face: she looks at the Inspector, at Kessa, at the lockbox. The warrant permits inspection. Kessa refuses to sign. Ira can submit (Ch. 005 p006 grammar) or refuse (Kessa's grammar).

Image: Ira's mismatched eyes. The choice.

IRA (dialogue, flat): The charter is in the box. The box is in the stall. The stall is in the basin. The basin is in the Sector. The Sector is under the Council.

IRA (dialogue): You can inspect the box. But the charter stays.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the lockbox — HOOK (~19%)

Camera: The lockbox on the counter, open. The Inspector's eyes on the eight objects inside. His face changes — the same recognition as Ch. 005 p006. He sees the charter. He sees the mother's file. He sees the crimson lines.

Image: The Inspector reads the charter. His face: he has seen this before. The same recognition. The same word.

INSPECTOR (dialogue, quiet): The School of the Braided Thread.

INSPECTON (dialogue, quieter): This school was shut twenty years ago. The files were supposed to be destroyed.

INSPECTOR (dialogue, quietest): They weren't.

Writing & art notes

  • The Inspector returns with a warrant — the Office escalates from request to demand. The

warrant permits inspection of the lockbox.

  • Kessa's refusal grammar is consistent: face-down + coin. But the warrant is the price this

time — the Office has priced the inspection itself.

  • Ira's compromise is her negotiation grammar: the Inspector can look, but the charter stays.

The charter is the school's last record; it cannot leave the box.

  • The Inspector recognizes the charter — "The files were supposed to be destroyed." The

Council tried to erase the school. The archive kept it alive.

  • Chain budget: unspent. Four notes silent. Loom never speaks.
