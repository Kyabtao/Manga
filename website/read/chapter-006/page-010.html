THREADBORN — Chapter 06 — Page 010 — FINALE

Chapter title: The Mother's File / माँ की फ़ाइल Arc: II — The Mendery Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail, the Council Stair Page type: Chapter finale — 7 panels

Beats: Page 009 — "Mend. The rest will follow." The whorl-grain in the seam. The school continues. The mother is missing.

Chapter 006 budget closed: chain-stop not spent this chapter (saved for later arc). Four notes heard correctly through the thread (Page 007). Sewer identified as the mother (Page 003). The Loom never speaks.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the morning (~14%)

Camera: Morning, Knot & Nail: the stall opens. Ira arrives with the kit. Kessa is behind the counter, loupe up. The lockbox is under the counter. Eight objects inside. The basin works. Ash falls.

Image: The Knot & Nail, normalised. The basin's centre. The stall that holds the braided thread's entire history.

CAPTION (none spoken): The morning after the decision. The stall works. The box holds. The girl mends.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the mend (~13%)

Camera: Ira mends at the counter — a dock worker with a torn mark. The braided thread moves through the needle. The mark is open. The mend is debtless. The whorl-grain appears. Routine.

Image: The mend as routine. The school's work, continued.

SFX: tchk

PANEL 3 — Close, the chain (~14%)

Camera: Through the market: Rekhak walks his rounds, chain running, oath-link dark. The chain hums — the four notes, wrong key, same shape. But tonight the hum is weaker. The echo is fading. The thread has heard the real voice; the chain's echo is no longer needed.

Image: The chain's hum, weaker. The echo fading. The real voice has been heard through the thread.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): The chain hums the four notes. Weaker tonight. The echo is fading. The thread has heard the real voice.

PANEL 4 — Wide, the Council Stair (~13%)

Camera: The Council Stair, night: at the foot of the stair, a new object — not thread this time. A letter. Grey wax and crimson wax, touching. The same two-wax grammar as the posting order. But this letter is addressed: To the girl with the braided mark. From the principal.

Image: The letter at the stair's foot. Two waxes touching. Addressed to Ira. From the principal. The first direct communication from the principal to the girl.

CAPTION (none spoken): The principal writes. For the first time, directly. To the girl.

PANEL 5 — Close, the letter (~14%)

Camera: The letter on the stair, unopened. Grey wax and crimson wax. The address: To the girl with the braided mark. No name. The principal knows the mark but not the name — or the principal chooses the mark over the name.

Image: The letter, unopened. The waxes touching. The address.

CAPTION (none spoken): Grey wax. Crimson wax. Touching. To the girl with the braided mark. The principal knows what she is. The principal has always known.

PANEL 6 — Medium, Ira finds the letter (~13%)

Camera: Ira at the foot of the Council Stair, dawn. She finds the letter. She reads the address. She does not open it — not here, not now. She carries it to the Knot & Nail.

Image: Ira picks up the letter. Her braided mark responds — the blank strand reaches toward the crimson wax. The principal's ink recognizes the braid.

SFX: —

CAPTION: The mark responds to the wax. The ink recognizes the braid. The principal and the girl are connected by thread.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the lockbox — FINAL HOOK (~19%)

Camera: The Knot & Nail, morning: Ira sets the unopened letter on the counter beside the lockbox. Kessa's loupe is down. Her hand is on the box's lid. The letter is the ninth object — or it will be, when it is opened.

Image: The letter and the box, side by side. The ninth object waits. The box is full. The letter is unopened. The principal has written to the girl. And the girl has not yet read it.

CAPTION (none spoken): Eight objects in the box. One letter on the counter. The principal writes to the girl. The girl has not yet read.

CAPTION (smaller): The box is full. The school is named. The mother is missing. The Inspector has seen the charter. The chain's echo is fading. And the principal — the hand behind every crimson line, every pre-filed slip, every self-initialled cross-out — has written directly to the girl who carries the braided thread.

CAPTION (last line): The letter is unopened. The braid is awake. The hand has opened. And the story has just begun.

END OF CHAPTER SIX.
