THREADBORN — Chapter 06 — Page 007

Chapter title: The Mother's File / माँ की फ़ाइल Arc: II — The Mendery Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail Page type: The basin reacts — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 006 — the charter in the lockbox (8 objects), the mother is missing, the principal has the mother.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the basin morning (~14%)

Camera: Morning, Knot & Nail: the stall works normally. Ira mends at the counter, braided thread in her needle, mark open. The basin's queue is long — the girl with the debtless mend is famous now.

Image: The Knot & Nail as the basin's centre. The queue stretches past the next stall. Ash falls. The Spindle is still bright.

CAPTION (none spoken): The basin does not know about the archive. It knows about the mend. That is enough.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the chain hums (~13%)

Camera: Rekhak walks his rounds, chain running, oath-link dark. As he passes the Knot & Nail, the chain hums — the four notes, wrong key, same shape. But tonight the hum is different: stronger, closer, as if the chain is reaching for the mark.

Image: The chain's hum reaches the stall. Ira's mark responds — the blank strand stirs.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): His chain hummed the four notes again. Stronger tonight. As if the chain knows the file has been read.

PANEL 3 — Close, the mark responds (~14%)

Camera: Ira's palm: the blank strand — her mother's thread — reaches toward the chain's hum. Not toward the chain itself, toward the echo. The echo is the four notes. The four notes are her mother's sewing melody. The blank strand recognizes its own melody.

Image: The blank strand and the chain's hum in the same frame. The thread recognizes the melody that was in the room when it was sewn.

CAPTION (none spoken): The blank strand recognizes the melody. The melody was in the room when the braid was sewn. The chain carries the echo. The thread carries the memory.

PANEL 4 — Medium, the four notes (~13%)

Camera: Through the braided mark, Ira hears the four notes clearly for the first time — not through the chain's echo, but through the thread's memory. The notes are her mother's voice, humming while she sewed. Off-key. Always the same four.

Image: Ira's face, listening to a voice no one else can hear. The thread is playing the melody back.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, smallest): I hear her. Through the thread. Off-key. The same four notes. Her voice. Not the chain's echo. Her voice.

PANEL 5 — Wide, the basin evening (~14%)

Camera: Evening: the Knot & Nail's shutters are up. The basin quietens. Kessa sits behind the counter, loupe up, lockbox under the counter. Ira sits across from her, palm open, mark glowing faintly.

Image: Two women and a box. The same image as always. But the box holds eight objects now, and the girl has heard her mother's voice.

KESSA (dialogue, dry): You heard her.

IRA (dialogue): Through the thread. Her voice.

PANEL 6 — Close, Kessa's hands (~13%)

Camera: Kessa's hands: she ties a new knot in the tally-thread. The hearing is filed. Then she does something new: she reaches into the lockbox and draws out the charter. She reads it again. Her loupe-eye moves across the knot-script.

Image: Kessa reading the charter, loupe down. The same gesture as reading a debt-mark.

KESSA (dialogue, slow): The school's charter. The first rule: thread is braided, never single.

KESSA (dialogue): Your mother's school and my lockbox have the same rule: nothing is kept alone.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the lockbox — HOOK (~19%)

Camera: The lockbox on the counter, open. Eight objects inside: cut-end, census, posting order, gift-thread, sixth thread, seventh thread (child's knot), sewer's thread, charter. The box is full. The history is complete.

Image: The eight objects in the open box. The chapter's icon, final form.

CAPTION (none spoken): Eight objects. One family. One school. One box. The lockbox holds the entire history of the braided thread — from the charter's founding to the girl's opening.

CAPTION (smaller): The box is full. The history is read. The mother is missing. And the thread that was hidden for fifteen years is now the only thread in the basin that knows its own name.

  • Kessa's reading of the charter connects the mother's school to Kessa's lockbox: nothing is

kept alone. The rule of the school and the rule of the box are the same.

  • The box is full — eight objects, the complete history. The icon reaches its final form.
  • Chain budget: unspent. Four notes: heard correctly for the first time (through the thread,

not the chain). Loom never speaks.
