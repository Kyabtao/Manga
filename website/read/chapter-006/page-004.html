THREADBORN — Chapter 06 — Page 004

Chapter title: The Mother's File / माँ की फ़ाइल Arc: II — The Mendery Sector: Agnikhand — The Mendery archive Page type: The mother's history — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 003 — Nandi reveals: the sewer is Ira's mother. The cutter is Ira's mother. The supply line runs from inside the archive. Twenty years.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the archive bench (~14%)

Camera: Nandi leads Ira to a bench at the archive's centre — the place where her mother worked. A worn surface, thread-scarred, with twenty years of stitch-grammar marks in the wood.

Image: The bench is the school's last classroom. Every mark in the wood is a lesson. Nandi sits on one side; Ira sits on the other. The charter and the mother's file are between them.

CAPTION (none spoken): Twenty years of lessons. In the wood. In the files. In the thread.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the mother's story (~13%)

Camera: Nandi's hands on the charter: she tells the story the way Kessa appraises — dry, factual, without sentiment.

Image: Nandi's gnarled hands trace the charter's knot-script.

NANDI (dialogue): Your mother founded the school fifteen years before you were born. She taught the braided thread to anyone who could hold a needle. Kshudra, Manav, Manavkin — she didn't choose by Kind. She chose by hand.

PANEL 3 — Close, the fall (~14%)

Camera: Nandi's face: the memory of the fall.

Image: Nandi's cat-eye catches the light.

NANDI (dialogue): The Council shut the school. Called it unlicensed. Called the braided thread illegal. Your mother was bound. Five years later, you were born.

NANDI (dialogue): She sewed you shut the day you were born. The teacher's strand into the student. Before speech. Before the Council could register you.

PANEL 4 — Medium, the protection (~13%)

Camera: Ira's palm: the braided mark, open. The blank strand — her mother's thread. The stitch that held the mark shut for fifteen years was her mother's work, her mother's protection.

Image: The mark and the blank strand in the same frame. The strand that was sewn in before speech. The strand that was the teacher's gift.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): She sewed me shut to hide me. To keep the thread alive. To protect the school's last student.

PANEL 5 — Wide, the supply line explained (~14%)

Camera: Nandi reaches under the bench and produces a spool of thread — ash-grey, same gauge, same colour as every piece in Kessa's lockbox. The supply line's source.

Image: The spool is old, well-used, the thread continuous. Twenty years of supply from one spool. The same thread that was in the box, on the counter, in Bhan's arm, and in Ira's palm.

NANDI (dialogue): She runs the thread from here. Through the archive's back door. To the basin. To the dock. To the girl.

NANDI (dialogue): Every piece you found — the box, the counter, the arm — was her.

PANEL 6 — Close, the question (~13%)

Camera: Ira's face: the question she has held since Chapter 1. Not who sewed me — she knows now. The new question.

Image: Ira's mismatched eyes, level. The question.

IRA (dialogue, flat): Where is she?

PANEL 7 — Medium, Nandi's answer — HOOK (~19%)

Camera: Nandi's hands: she sets the spool on the bench and points to the archive's deepest corner — a door Ira has not seen, hidden behind the oldest shelves. The door is Kshudra-made, the same craft as the back door and the lockbox.

Image: The door at the archive's deepest point. The same Kshudra craft as every lock in the story. The same hand-school. The same woman.

NANDI (dialogue, quiet): She's here. She's always been here. Behind the last door. The one the key doesn't open.

NANDI (dialogue): The key opens the back door. The braid opens the last door. Your mother designed it that way.

Writing & art notes

  • The mother's story is told by Nandi, not shown in flashback. Keep the telling dry, factual, the way Kessa tells stories. The emotion is in the objects: the bench, the spool, the door.
  • The supply line's source is one spool, twenty years old, kept under the bench. The same thread that ran through every piece of evidence.
  • The last door is the chapter's hook: the mother is inside the Mendery, behind a door that only the braided mark can open. The key opens the back door; the braid opens the last door.
  • Chain budget: unspent. Four notes silent. Loom never speaks.
