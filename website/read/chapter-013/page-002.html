THREADBORN — Chapter 13 — Page 002

Chapter title: The Second Office Arc: III — The Second School Sector: Agnikhand — the stall and the square, first morning into first dusk Page type: The ruling — 7 panels; the register rules, the costs arrive, the counter-book mints its first credit and is given its limit

Beats: the ruling — enforceable but unbindable: the Office's own grammar turned against the Office's own paper; the appraisal — Kessa crowns it: the paper is genuine; what it claims does not exist; the costs by dusk — the clerk answers with an inspection demand on the school's own room, and sends for the amendment file; the queue learns the price — a ruling against a bigger paper costs the ruler, and the basin chooses anyway; the certificate — the register cannot stamp, so it certifies: a debt disputed pending the count, entered of record, defiable only by a named no; honoured — the foreman pays against paper, and the basin learns the counter-book can mint credit; the limit — set the same hour, by the till: a keep is not a mint.

This page: the second Office grows teeth — and someone sets a jaw's limits before the lamp goes low.

Chapter 013 budget: ONE chain-stop, unspent. Mother never on panel, never speaks — no thread. Loom never speaks, does not appear. The three entered hands remain unnamed — today the namelessness is armour.

PANEL 1 — Medium-close, the ruling (~15%)

Camera: Medium-close: the counter, lamp-low. The lease flat under the lamp, the register open beside it, and Ira's pen moving through the Office's grammar line by line while the queue holds its breath at her shoulders.

Image: She does not grant it and she does not refuse it. She reads what it is.

IRA: (of record) The paper is enforceable. Sealed proper, filed proper, old stock — the schedule honours it. It binds whoever's names are written on it. And the register so finds: the names written on it are names the Office itself never entered. The tenancy holds hands, not names. Possession by knot. Entry by knot. The lease is enforceable — and unbindable. There is no key cut that fits this lock.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Their own grammar. A paper binds names — and somebody sealed a paper against three people they never allowed to be named.

PANEL 2 — Close, the appraisal (~14%)

Camera: Close: Kessa takes the lease before the ink is dry, holds it up to the lamp flame, one eye through the fibres — an appraiser weighing a stone she already knows is paste.

Image: The till's verdict on the day's big paper.

KESSA: (handing it back) Genuine stock. Genuine seal. Old enough to be worth respecting. The paper is real, keeper. What it claims does not exist. I would not lend a cup of water against it.

(tilting her head at the docket) And your complainant — I know that name. It is a name that has never signed its own paper. Somebody lent him the ink.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): A front, or a fool. The register does not need to know which. It only needs the name on file — and a name is exactly what he is.

PANEL 3 — Medium, dusk, the costs (~14%)

Camera: Medium: day's end, the light through the shutters gone copper to blue. Rekhak Vahni at the counter again, shift done, a knot-marked notice — one seal — flat on the wood between them.

Image: The ruling is not free, and the bill arrives before the lamp needs trimming.

REKHAK: (quiet, professional to professional) You ruled before noon; they answered before dusk. An inspection demand — filed on the alley room. They want to count what is in it. And the clerk has sent for the amendment file. Dusted off, keeper. The one that redefines entry.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Granary sealed at noon; fuel next; then lamp-oil — he said it this morning like weather coming. Now they want my room on an inventory. Good. Let the basin watch the ruling cost. A ruling that costs the ruler nothing is only weather.

PANEL 4 — Wide, the queue chooses (~14%)

Camera: Wide, dusk: the queue split down its length by the news. A few fold their papers away and go. The rest step forward and lay them flat on the counter — the widow's tally-stick, the boundary-cord, the debt-braid — laid down in view of the street.

Image: The basin learns what rulings cost when a bigger paper disagrees with them — and gets in line anyway.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Three left. The rest put their paper down where the square could watch them do it. Nobody asked them to. That is the part I will enter tonight and not explain: the queue saw the price and stepped up to pay it.

PANEL 5 — Medium, the certificate (~14%)

Camera: Medium: the claimant from the morning back at the counter, his pay-chit on the wood beside the register. Ira is writing — not a stamp. A certificate, in the register's grammar, with the keeper's own name at its foot.

Image: The register cannot stamp. It can certify.

IRA: The register cannot stamp. I will not pretend otherwise, and I will not become them to fix it. What it can do is certify: the debt named on your chit is disputed, pending the count, entered of record. Take it to your foreman. He honours it — or he defies a record, in writing, with his name where mine is.

CLAIMANT: (not moving) And if he will not read it?

IRA: Then his no is on file too. Tell him the counter keeps copies.

PANEL 6 — Medium-wide, honoured (~14%)

Camera: Medium-wide, full dusk, the square: the claimant walking away from the stall with the certificate folded into his fist and a measure-sack that has weight again. The queue watches him the whole way. Across the square the Office hatch is a black shutter with its knot-marked notice.

Image: The foreman paid against paper. What the basin learns, it learns by watching him walk.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The foreman honoured it before the last light went. So the basin learned it tonight, in the square, for free: paper from this counter is worth something at a yard gate. The second Office can mint credit. That is not a victory. That is a furnace door standing open.

PANEL 7 — Medium-close, the limit — FINAL HOOK (~15%)

Camera: Medium-close: the counter, lamp low. Kessa's hand flat on her shut box, the inspection notice at the lamp's edge, and Ira already writing — the limit, into the register itself.

Image: Teeth, and a jaw's limits, set in the same hour by the same hand that keeps the till.

KESSA: You saw what they watched, keeper. A foreman took your paper rather than the Office's silence. Tomorrow every owed hand in the basin wants one. So, while the ink is wet and on your own record: a keep is not a mint. Certify the debt. Never create it.

IRA: (entering) One certificate per disputed debt. No interest. Void at the count's end. And every one signed with my name — so each paper that leaves this counter goes out with a neck attached.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): They are coming to count my room with a paper that counts things. Very well. The room keeps its own books now.

CAPTION (smallest): Count it, then.

Writing & art notes

  • The page's three jobs, in order: the ruling, the costs, the certificate. The ruling must read as the Office's own grammar used against the Office's own paper — enforceable but unbindable is a finding, not a favour. Do not let Ira gloat; the register reads what is there. The costs land by dusk the same page: inspection demand on the school's room, amendment file requested — armed, not fired. The certificate must be small, procedural, and devastating: the foreman honours it because defying a record by name is more expensive.
  • The landlord is never on-panel. A name on the docket that "has never signed its own paper" — front or fool, unresolved on-panel, forever. The clerk's fingerprints stay off-panel too: the notice speaks in inventory grammar; nobody appears to gloat on the Office's side this page.
  • Kessa's two lines are the page's crown and bridle. The appraisal ("the paper is genuine; what it claims does not exist") crowns the ruling; the limit ("a keep is not a mint") bridle the mint. Her box stays shut; her hand goes on the lid, not inside it.
  • The queue beat must show the price being paid, not cheer it. Three leave; the rest lay their papers flat where the square can see. Objects first, faces second — the same rule as Page 001. No speeches from the queue.
  • Honoured, not dramatized. The foreman never appears; we see the claimant walk and the sack's weight. The furnace-door caption is the page's strategic dread: minting credit is power, and unlimitable power is how the first Office got built. The limit answers it inside the same page.
  • The imperative ladder opens its second run: Count it, then. (Ladder so far: knock / ask him / read it back / sign nothing / keep it better / ask her / tie her in / the roll is whole / rule it, keeper / count it, then.)
