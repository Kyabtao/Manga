Chapter 013 — Page 009 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one. English-only pages (AUDIT R13.22).

IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the register against erasure)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "It may not decline his mark."

Page 009 note: reads the disavowal flat — the horror is the grammar, not her performance of it — and answers with the register's whole spine: the Office may decline its clerk's act; it may not decline his mark. Then points the newest institution at the enemy: his name entered under protection, not read aloud, sealed into the back pocket. The register does not compare parties or explain the parallel. It protects them.

KESSA (the till; the mirror)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"They name everyone, keeper. And they unnamed him."

Page 009 note: for once her face is older than the appraiser's. She has known them to erase a debt and watched them try to erase a founder; this is the first time she has watched them erase a man for being useful. Her hands do the sealing — the protected page's fold and wax are hers, and she does not delegate a protection.

THE CLERK (off-panel but everywhere; one distant cameo)

Reference sheet (Ch. 009): ../../chapter-009/characters/grey-clerk-ref.png. On-panel ONCE this page — distant, dusk, in the hatch queue: plain undyed coat, empty cord, holding no paper. His name is inside the register now, under protection, and is never shown to the reader.

The Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / unnamed by his own office / erased for being useful /
Ask: what the machine's hands are worth the day they are inconvenient /
*(standing in the queue of the basin's people, asking for nothing)*

Page 009 note: the machine said he does not exist; the register entered where he does — at the hatch, at dusk, queueing. Him, too. The counter protects him without his asking and without his knowing — which is the first thing anyone in this story has ever done for him for free.

REKHAK VAHNI (the professional — the fold)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni-ref.png

Rekhak Vahni / Manav / Agnikhand / cold-white Sight thread / deep debt /
Audit: reveal target's Kind, thread colour and debt level; each use adds 2 debt to self /
"Today it told the square that any hand can be an individual."

Page 009 note: his folded paper — carried since before the counter existed — lies on the wood for the first time, under his flat hand, and goes back into his coat one fold at a time. He does not present it. He shows Ira the FOLD, and tells her where it will eventually be presented: her table. The disavowal made it inevitable; the machine threatened every hand it has.

THE COUNTING-LESSON WOMEN (sixth fetch — the disavowal)

No full sheet; Ch. 011–013. At the hatch board before anyone else, as always — and this time they do not run it across the square.

The Counting-Lesson Women / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, learning / owe each other lessons /
Fetch: bring the Office's paper to the counter for reading of record /
"This one is sealed, and nobody is running. Read it first."

Page 009 note: the street reads the fetchers' pace before it reads the paper — a carried-slow notice is the basin's signal that something is being erased, not offered. The women have learned to read speed as grammar. The counter taught them that.
