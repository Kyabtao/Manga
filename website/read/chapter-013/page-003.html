Chapter title: The Second Office Arc: III — The Second School Sector: Agnikhand — the school's alley room, morning into the stall at dusk Page type: The inspection — 7 panels; the Office's inventory grammar meets a room that keeps its own books, and the counter discovers its light is on the Office's ledger

Beats: the count arrives — a surveyor at the school's door with a tally-slate, a measuring cord, and one seal, under the inspection demand; the room is read — beds, bench, fuel, door, window: exactly enough of everything a room must have, and none of it the point; the empty row — the slate's PERSONS row takes no mark from this room, and the menace is the blank; the knot offered — the three entered hands cooperate the way water cooperates: they give the slate the one thing it can copy — the knot — and no name to hang it on; the count concludes — sealed, copied, filed, while the certificate economy walks past the alley mouth; the lamp-oil lands — Rekhak, at the counter at dusk: lamp-oil is on the inventory schedule now, and the stall's lamp burns Office-counted oil; the answer not said aloud — Kessa's hand flat on her shut box, where the fold already is.

This page: procedure as weather. The most dangerous object in the basin is a slate with a blank row.

Chapter 013 budget: ONE chain-stop, unspent. Mother never on panel, never speaks — no thread. Loom never speaks, does not appear. The three entered hands remain unnamed — present, visible, cooperative, and impossible to enter.

PANEL 1 — Wide, morning, the count arrives (~15%)

Camera: Wide: the alley, the school's door under its door-frame knot. The surveyor — a plain grey figure with a waxed-canvas coat, a tally-slate slung on cord, a measuring cord coiled at the belt, one wax seal on a neck-cord — stands at the threshold with the inspection demand copied flat. At the door: the three entered hands — an elderly Kshudra woman with rope-scarred knuckles, a stout middle-aged Manav man with dock-worker's hands, a young Manavkin with a counting-cord wound at the wrist — waiting, unhurried, making room. Ira stands of record at the wall, her own book open, the register attending the count.

Image: The Office's grammar has come to read the school's room. The room has been expecting it since dusk yesterday.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): They filed it proper, so it must be honoured proper. That is the rule I ruled, and it walks both ways. The register attends.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the room is read (~15%)

Camera: Medium: inside the alley room — the entry-racks of knots along one wall (the roll, whole), a bench, bedding, a fuel store, one door, one window, the crate. The surveyor works down the tally-slate's rows with a neutral hand: measuring cord to the bench, fingers to the fuel store, a mark for the door. The three hands stand aside from each counted thing.

Image: Exactly enough of everything a room must have. The count goes through. The count is not wrong. The count is beside the point.

SURVEYOR: (flat, entering) Bench, one. Beds, three. Door, one. Window, one, shuttered. Fuel, one store, part used. Racks — (a pause, the first one) — racks, four. Contents by count?

IRA: (of record) Contents by knot. The room keeps its own books.

PANEL 3 — Close, the empty row (~13%)

Camera: Close: the tally-slate in the surveyor's hands — rows of neat tally groups, and one row with nothing in it. The brush hovers over the blank. The surveyor's thumb rests beside a row of marks that are not lettering, not names — only counts.

Image: The row that cannot take a mark from this room is the row the whole demand was filed to fill.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Inventory does not refuse. Inventory does not gloat. Inventory stands in my school with a blank row and waits for the room to make a mistake.

PANEL 4 — Medium-close, the knot offered (~14%)

Camera: Medium-close: the elderly Kshudra woman steps forward and offers her wrist — the entered knot at it, cord old and honest. The surveyor's brush copies the KNOT onto the slate: mark for mark, a knot-shape in the count column. The name column stays blank. Neither party acts like anything unusual is happening. The young Manavkin and the Manav man wait their turn.

Image: Cooperation, weaponized by the ruling: the slate can carry the knot. The slate cannot carry the hand.

KESSA (does not say it; the caption does): CAPTION (Ira's voice): Possession by knot, entry by knot, so finds the register — my own finding, walked into the room ahead of me. Let them copy knots until the cord runs out. There is no name at the end of any of them.

PANEL 5 — Wide, the count concludes (~14%)

Camera: Wide, noon: the alley mouth. The surveyor walks out with the slate sealed under wax — one seal — and a copy left proper at the door, knotted to the door-frame knot by a neutral hand. In the alley, basin folk watch the way they watch weather. Down the lane, a yard hand with a folded paper in his fist — the second certificate gone to a gate, a third being argued polite at the stall in the distance.

Image: The demand is answered: counted, sealed, filed — and the room is exactly as uncountable as it was at dawn.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Answered proper. Sealed proper. Let the clerk read what his grammar caught: one room, part-used fuel, four racks, and no persons — a room, on the Office's books, where nobody lives. He will notice too late that a room where nobody lives is a room the Office cannot tax.

PANEL 6 — Medium, dusk, the lamp-oil lands (~14%)

Camera: Medium: the stall, dusk, the lamp burning low and warm. Rekhak at the counter, shift done, a knot-marked schedule slip flat on the wood — one seal. Kessa's hand comes to rest, flat and quiet, on the lid of her shut box. Ira's pen has stopped.

Image: Rekhak's weather, arrived on schedule: the inventory has reached the stall's own light.

REKHAK: (quiet, professional to professional) Fuel-yards are sealed. Lamp-oil is on the schedule now — rationed by count, sold by permit. Your lamp burns their oil, keeper. So does every lamp in the basin. When the ration turns, it turns for everyone whose light is on their books.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): They cannot make the basin's paper stop, so they will make the basin's light ask permission.

PANEL 7 — Medium-close, the answer not said — FINAL HOOK (~15%)

Camera: Medium-close: the counter, the lamp flame standing small and steady between them. Kessa's hand does not leave the box. Ira looks at the flame a moment — then writes the day into the register: the count answered, the empty row, the knot copied, the oil scheduled. The register takes it all without complaint.

Image: The counter's answer is already bought and folded, and it lives in a box that stays shut until it is needed.

IRA: (of record, even now) Entered: the room is counted and no one is in it. Entered: the light is theirs until it isn't.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Kessa has not said a word. She has not touched the lid's catch. The fold has been in that box since a winter none of us invoice — and whatever it is, it is not on the Office's books, because it was never on anybody's books at all.

CAPTION (smallest): Light it anyway.

Writing & art notes

  • Three jobs, in order: the count that cannot read the room; the three hands present and unnamed; the lamp-oil landing. The surveyor is procedure, not a villain — flat voice, neutral hand, no gloating, no threats; the menace is the blank row and the measuring cord. Do not let anyone argue with the surveyor; the room does not argue with weather, it cooperates past the point where cooperation gives the Office nothing.
  • The three entered hands are shown, not named — and described once, for continuity: an elderly Kshudra woman, rope-scarred knuckles; a stout middle-aged Manav man, dock-worker's hands; a young Manavkin, counting-cord at the wrist. They never speak this page. Their cooperation is the page's weapon: the slate copies the knot, never a name. Do not let Ira explain the trick on-panel — the captions carry it, and Kessa says nothing at all this page (her silence is the till working).
  • The room's contents are the school's own grammar: entry-racks (the roll, whole), bench, bedding, fuel store, the crate, one door, one window. The count is correct — do not cheat it. The victory is that a correct count catches nothing, and files a room where nobody lives.
  • The certificate economy stays background texture: one folded paper in a fist down the lane; a third being argued polite at the stall, distant. No speeches. The basin is learning by watching, again.
  • The lamp-oil beat is small and cold. Rekhak delivers it like a forecast, not a warning — he is not alarmed; alarms are for things that might not happen.
