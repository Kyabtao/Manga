Chapter 013 — Page 005 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one. English-only pages (AUDIT R13.22).

IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the register pays first)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "They may not rule what was already witnessed."

Page 005 note: answers doubt with the record — in duplicate, sealed, before noon: the certificate stands until the count ends, because a definition rules forward and cannot reach backward over what was already entered. Then grants the page's new institution: protection of record — entered, not read aloud. The register now holds names the Office would pay to read.

THE FOREMAN (on-panel for the first time — the gate that chose)

No full sheet; Ch. 013. First on-panel appearance. Keep him CAREFUL, not warm: broad, heavy-jawed, greying stubble, leather apron over a wool coat, gate-keys at his belt, a gate-keeper's face that gives away nothing early. Reads everything twice.

The Foreman / Manav / Agnikhand / ledger-brown, cautious / pays against paper /
Honour: accept the certificate or defy a record by name /
"Paid against the keeper's paper. That is this gate's rule now."

Page 005 note: read the register's answer slow, twice — then honoured the certificate in view of his own yard and made the gate's rule out loud where his men could hold him to it. Then came to the counter in person, cap in hands, and bought the one thing the record can actually sell: his name, entered, not read aloud. Not a convert. A gate-keeper who chose which paper his gate trusts — and paid for the choice.

THE CLAIMANT (the answer in his fist)

No full sheet; Ch. 013. The worn yard hand, fourth visit: present at the gate when the record arrives, paid in view of the yard, and at the hatch ring at night when the trap goes up — his certificate a promise with no road to keep it, until morning.

The Claimant / Manav / Agnikhand / worn brown, unpaid no longer / owed a winter /
Ask: what a paper is worth when its office stops /
"It stands till the count ends. The keeper wrote it twice."

Page 005 note: the register's credibility, made flesh at a yard gate — a full measure-sack, carried home against the keeper's paper. His night face at the trap notice is the page's wobble: the same man the Office's new grammar is aimed at, twice in one day.

THE RUNNER (the Office's, then the register's — one day, two roads)

No full sheet; Ch. 004, 013. Plain grey; carries what the powerful write and never says what he thinks. Carried the amendment to the gates (Page 004); carries the register's answer to the same gates (Page 005).

The Runner / Manav / Agnikhand / plain grey, uncommitted / paid by the road /
Carry: what a seal decides, to whose door it lands /
"Same gate. Different paper."

Page 005 note: the war in logistics, wearing out shoe-leather: yesterday he delivered the definition; today he delivered its answer. Neither paper is his. Both doors know his knock now.

KESSA (the till; the wick and the witness)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
*(trims the wick; misses nothing)*

Page 005 note: small scissors, exact hand — the counted lamp trimmed while the duplicate answers are sealed; still at the till when the foreman asks his quiet question, which is how the till knows it was true. She lays the trap notice on the counter herself at night: the basin's readers bring the Office's paper to the counter, and this time the counter was already expecting it.
