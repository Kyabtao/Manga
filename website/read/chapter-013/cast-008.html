Chapter 013 — Page 008 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one. English-only pages (AUDIT R13.22).

IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the register, at its most dangerous)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "The register sees a man of no office, at a counter of record."

Page 008 note: reads the sealed notice and the unsigned slip side by side and lets the Office's two grammars impeach each other; speaks the arriving clerk in as "a man of no office" — the register sees capacities, not ranks; writes the terms fair with no softened clause; and enters his walk as delivery of record. She never thanks him, never offers him a name, and never changes a knot. The warmth is all structural: a lamp burning late.

THE CLERK (FIRST ON-PANEL — the threshold)

Reference sheet (Ch. 009): ../../chapter-009/characters/grey-clerk-ref.png — minus the office-grey coat and the seal this page: plain undyed wool coat, bare head, the cord at his collar an empty loop. Plain, forgettable, middle-aged human man, greying flat hair, knot-marked record book left behind tonight. He comes alone, off-hours, with nothing to speak for him.

The Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / office-grey — checked at the door / files, and learns /
Draft: redefine a word to win what an argument lost /
"Put your terms in writing, keeper. I will sign for receiving it."

Page 008 note: the chapter's chain-stop is spent on him — the machine's hands entering the counter as a man for the first time in nine chapters. He does not threaten and does not concede: he asks for the terms in writing and sets his THUMB into the wax beside the keeper's knot — the basin's grammar, unpracticed in his hand. Two years of reading the register taught him which paper is real. He is not a convert. He is the only man in the Office who knows what the register is.

KESSA (the till; the box under her palm)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
*(hand flat on the box from his arrival to his exit — then, at last, not)*

Page 008 note: the page's tension lives in her palm: the box is under her hand from the moment the bare-corded stranger appears until the moment he crosses back — and the release at the final beat is the till's verdict, unspoken: whatever that was, it was not a trap. She melts the wax for the fair copy herself; the appraiser does not delegate a sealing.

THE COUNTING-LESSON WOMEN (fifth fetch — the sealed notice)

No full sheet; Ch. 011–013. The street's readers, at the hatch board before anyone else, as always.

The Counting-Lesson Women / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, learning / owe each other lessons /
Fetch: bring the Office's paper to the counter for reading of record /
"Sealed this time. They learned."

Page 008 note: their fifth fetch is the machine's most official paper yet — regular wax, regular knot-style, posted square. They carry it across the square at the pace that has become the basin's own signal: this one is for reading aloud, and everyone should hear it first.
