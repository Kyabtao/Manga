Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Observed, and not obeyed."

Page 004 note: reads the amendment aloud of record before the basin — both clauses, level, unsanitized: the word rewritten (no other entry is entry) and the mint clause (paper under stamp, all else not record). Does not comfort the claimant and does not resolve the wobble: she enters the question of record and owes an answer by dusk tomorrow, by name, in writing. Her evening entry: the Office's hours — observed, and not obeyed.

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /

Page 004 note: the rationed dusk is her discipline: the lamp trimmed low and exact, four hours a night knotted into a short cord by the till. When the ration turns, the till will know before the Office posts it. Oil can and cord this page; the box stays shut.

THE CLAIMANT (the wobble, holding it in one fist)

No full sheet; Ch. 013. The worn yard hand from Pages 001–002 — third time at this counter. Same face: worn brown tunic, calloused hands, a winter behind the eyes.

The Claimant / Manav / Agnikhand / worn brown, unpaid / owed a winter /
Ask: what a paper is worth when its office stops /
"Does mine still count?"

Page 004 note: the Office's definition has reached his gate and his courage in the same morning. He is not a coward; the foreman is not a villain; both have gates to keep. He leaves with no comfort and something better: the question entered of record, owed an answer by dusk tomorrow, in the keeper's name. Page 005 is his answer.

THE COUNTING-LESSON WOMEN (couriers of record)

No full sheet; Ch. 011–013. Third appearance: the p001 queue, the counting lessons, now the pair who fetched the Office's amendment off the hatch board and carried it across the square to be read aloud.

The Counting-Lesson Women / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, learning / owe each other lessons /
Fetch: bring the Office's paper to the counter for reading of record /
"Read it loud, keeper. We will say it back at the well tonight."

Page 004 note: the equilibrium with legs: the basin cannot read Office grammar, so the street routes the Office's own publication through the counter. They carry the amendment flat, like something live, and take the reading home to repeat at the well.

THE CLERK (off-panel — the amendment's author)
