Chapter 013 — Page 001 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one. English-only pages (AUDIT R13.22).

IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the second Office's hand)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "The register will rule."

Page 001 note: opens her own counter to a street that decided overnight it now has one. Rules the morning's small debts fast and clean in the Office's own grammar — then meets the first case her book cannot simply read: an old Office-stamped lease against the school's own unnamed hands. Takes the ruling of record before the basin, pen in hand.

KESSA (the till; the queue-reader)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"A queue is a verdict the street has already reached, keeper — you are only the hand that writes it down."

Page 001 note: let them gather from the fourth bell and reads the line like a rich man reads weather. The appraiser's verdict on the new equilibrium: a queue is already a ruling. Her box stays shut under the counter; the fold stays hers.

REKHAK VAHNI (the honest man's warning)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni-ref.png

Rekhak Vahni / Manav / Agnikhand / cold-white Sight thread / deep debt /
Audit: reveal target's Kind, thread colour and debt level; each use adds 2 debt to self /
"A machine that cannot count people will count things — and things do not have words on file to stop it."

Page 001 note: comes to the counter off shift, a folded paper of his own in hand (not explained this page), and gives the counter-book its strategic clock at a murmur: the inventory count has begun — granary seals, fuel-yards, lamp-oil. The suspension did not stop the Office; it changed the Office's units.

THE CLAIMANT (a paper hostage to a suspension)

No full sheet; Ch. 013. A worn-out yard hand with an unstamped pay-chit.

The Claimant / Manav / Agnikhand / worn brown, unpaid / owed a winter /
Ask: what a paper is worth when its office stops /
"No stamp, no pay. Pending the count. Nothing to eat by winter."

Page 001 note: the suspension's human cost and the page's tutorial-in-reverse — the first case the register cannot mend with grammar, because his paper needs the one stamp in the basin and the stamp is behind a shutter. Logged, not solved: the stamp question is the chapter's slow poison.

THE THREE ENTERED HANDS (the lease's unnamed tenants)

No full sheet; Ch. 012–013. Entered by knot, never by name — and today that is a vulnerability.

The Three Entered Hands / mixed / Agnikhand / grey, entered by knot / a year's back-rent, filed against them /
Hold: they held; that was the whole of the lesson /
No line on this page. Papers bind names; the nameless can be dispossessed on a technicality.

Page 001 note: present only as the tenants named on an old Office-stamped lease — the school's alley room, a year's back-rent, filed proper, sealed proper, predating the school's name. Somebody saved this paper for exactly this morning.

THE GREY CLERK (off-panel, everywhere)

No full sheet; Ch. 009–013. The Office's voice with a pen in it — and now an inventory to count.

The Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Count: what hands hold, when hands cannot be counted /
No line on this page. The shut hatch and the inventory seals are his voice enough.

Page 001 note: the counter is shut pending the count; the granary, the fuel-yards and the lamp-oil are being sealed instead. Whether the lease was his idea or his tool is not said on-panel. Procedure does not need to be seen to be everywhere.

Page note: the street chose its table. A dozen-deep queue at dawn; the widow's tally-stick ruled in a morning (six measures, hollow-interest void — the Office's grammar, the keeper's book, the basin's thumb-mark); the Office's own hatch shut pending the count; a pay-chit that needs the one stamp in the basin; Rekhak's warning — they are counting what hands hold now; and the crack: an old lease against the school's unnamed three, saved for exactly this morning. The register's first hard ruling, taken of record. Hook: "Rule it, keeper."
