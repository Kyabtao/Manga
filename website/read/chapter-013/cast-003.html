Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one. English-only pages (AUDIT R13.22).

IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the register attends)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "The room keeps its own books."

Page 003 note: her own ruling walks both ways — the demand was filed proper, so it is honoured proper, and the register attends the count of record. She does not argue with the surveyor; she answers one question ("contents by knot") and enters the day at dusk: the room counted and no one in it, the light theirs until it isn't.

THE SURVEYOR (the Office's inventory grammar, walking)

No full sheet; Ch. 013. Never named. A plain grey figure: waxed-canvas coat, tally-slate slung on cord, measuring cord at the belt, one wax seal on a neck-cord.

The Surveyor / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, unspooled / counts, and does not refuse /
Slate: enter a thing's count — things only /
"Racks, four. Contents by count?"

Page 003 note: not a villain — procedure with a brush. Counts the room correctly and completely, copies the one thing the school's people can safely give (a knot), leaves the name column blank without comment, seals the count, and files a room where nobody lives. The menace is the empty row, not the hand that holds it.

THE THREE ENTERED HANDS (shown, not named — cooperation as the weapon)

No full sheet; Ch. 012–013. Entered by knot at the school's racks (Ch. 012), never by name. Shown this page, standing test: an elderly Kshudra woman, rope-scarred knuckles; a stout middle-aged Manav man, dock-worker's hands; a young Manavkin, counting-cord wound at the wrist. None speaks.

The Three Entered Hands / Manavkin, Kshudra, Manav / Agnikhand / knot-entered, no names /
Hold: possession by knot, entry by knot /
"Racks, four. Persons —" *(the row stays blank)*

Page 003 note: present, visible, cooperative, and impossible to enter: they offer the entered knot for the slate to copy and no name to hang it on. The Page 002 finding — the tenancy holds hands, not names — walks into the room ahead of the register. Keep the three visual descriptions stable for future pages; their unnamed-ness is now load-bearing.
