Chapter 013 — Page 006 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one. English-only pages (AUDIT R13.22).

IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the window, and the pen not moving)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "The record does not answer to a count-house."

Page 006 note: builds a door in the middle of the clerk's trap — a hinged window in the stall's shutter, witnessed, entered, answerable at this counter. Endorses at the keeper's neck-price: her name at every foot. Then, at night, the register waits on HER for once: the Office's offer is unwritten, and the answer she writes next has to be true with the whole square watching the lamp burn. Page 007 opens on what she writes.

KESSA (the till; the window's ledger)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"A promise witnessed is a debt that can be counted."

Page 006 note: two beats, both ledger: she receives the seal-less hire-cord with both hands (the window takes papers no office would read), and she knots the window's running price into the till-cord — one candle a week, two till-hours a day, a name at every foot. Her quoting of the limit is not deference; it is the till protecting the register from its own success. We witness. We do not mint.

THE CLAIMANT (fourth morning — the wobble closed)

No full sheet; Ch. 013. The worn yard hand, first through the window-line, certificate in both hands like something he has been afraid for.

The Claimant / Manav / Agnikhand / worn brown, unpaid no longer / owed a winter /
Ask: what a paper is worth when its office stops /
"Now it says so on its own face."

Page 006 note: his certificate comes back through the frame with the window's endorsement at its foot — witnessed true, road intact, valid till the count ends, stated on its own face. His arc-wobble closes in one line and one seal. The Office's new grammar will never reach him again before the count ends; the page lets him walk off without ceremony.

THE OLD WOMAN WITH THE ACKNOWLEDGED DEBT (one-off — the paper nobody expected)

No full sheet; Ch. 013. Small, bent, a folded scrap kept soft with years, carried next to the skin: a debt acknowledged under a dead man's seal, never paid, never deniable, never worth anything at their hatch.

The Old Creditor / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, patient / owed by the dead /
Present: a debt acknowledged, years kept, never paid /
"It was true the whole time. It just was not worth anything."

Page 006 note: the window's proof of scale, first of the two: the basin has been holding papers no office would ever read — acknowledged debts, kept warm against the skin for years. The window cannot pay her either. It can make the debt true on the record, which is where payment eventually comes from.

THE YOUNG DOCK-HAND (one-off — the promise with no seal)

No full sheet; Ch. 013. A young hire-hand with a knot-cord, promises at both ends, no seal anywhere: hired by word, recorded by knot, worth nothing anywhere a stamp rules.

The Young Dock-Hand / Manav / Agnikhand / tar-brown, hopeful / owed a season's wage /
Offer: a hire promised and knotted, never sealed /
"He tied it at both ends. That was the whole paper."

Page 006 note: Kessa receives the cord with both hands and makes the page's appraisal: a promise witnessed is a debt that can be counted. Presented, hand. The window will make it true.

THE RUNNER (third appearance — the unwritten offer)

No full sheet; Ch. 004, 005, 013. Plain grey, empty-handed for the first time. Leans in at the window-frame at dusk and delivers the Office's terms as sent-speech: flat, complete, deniable — the first Office move in this war that exists nowhere on paper.

The Runner / Manav / Agnikhand / plain grey, uncommitted / paid by the road /
Carry: what a seal decides, to whose door it lands /
"File, and the window stands."

Page 006 note: yesterday he carried the amendment; the day before, the register's answer. Today he carries nothing — because the offer must not exist as record. The register taught the basin to make the Office write things down; the Office's reply is to stop writing. The arms race nobody planned, proceeding by runner.
