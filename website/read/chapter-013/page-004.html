THREADBORN — Chapter 13 — Page 004

Chapter title: The Second Office Arc: III — The Second School Sector: Agnikhand — the stall and the square, one day from reading to rationed dusk Page type: The amendment — 7 panels; the Office rewrites a word to win an argument, in public, under one lamp

Beats: the street fetches the paper — the Office posts its amendment at the shut hatch, and the basin brings it to the counter to be read, because the counter is where paper gets read now; the reading of record — Ira reads ENTRY, redefined, line by line, aloud, before the queue: a person is entered who is named upon a roll kept by the Office — the empty-row finding, closed by definition; the mint clause — and what counts as RECORD: paper under stamp only — the keeper's certificates, void on taking; the wobble — the claimant comes back holding his certificate and his doubt together, and the register does not resolve it: the question goes in of record; the notice at the yard — the Office's own copy of the amendment reaches the foreman's gate the same day, off-panel, by knot and runner: the wobble delivered; the first rationed dusk — the permit-dark posted at the hatch, lamps dying on the Office's hour, the square going dark but one; the counter stays lit — the flame trimmed small and counted, the till keeping its own hours, the register entering the day: read, of record; unanswered.

This page: grammar as siegecraft — and a lamp that will not ask permission.

Chapter 013 budget: ONE chain-stop, unspent. Mother never on panel, never speaks — no thread. Loom never speaks, does not appear. The three entered hands are not on-panel this page — the amendment is not aimed at the school's room; it is aimed at the mint.

PANEL 1 — Wide, morning, the street fetches the paper (~14%)

Camera: Wide: the square, morning. At the shut hatch, the Office has posted a fresh paper — long, narrow, one seal at the foot. Two of the counting-lesson women are already untying it from the board with a basin crowd watching — and they carry it across the square toward the stall, held flat like something live.

Image: The Office published its answer where the Office always publishes. The street read the first line and decided the counter does the reading now.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): They posted it at their own hatch, in their own grammar, for a basin that reads knots. It took the street one morning to bring it to my counter for reading aloud. That is not rebellion. That is logistics.

PANEL 2 — Medium-close, the reading of record (~15%)

Camera: Medium-close: the counter, the amendment flat under the lamp, the register open beside it. Ira stands of record — reading aloud, level, one finger on the line. The queue is pressed in close to hear. Kessa at the till, still.

Image: A public reading under one lamp: the Office rewriting a word to win an argument.

IRA: (of record, reading) "Amendment to the count, entry clause. A person is entered who is named upon a roll kept by the Office. No other entry is entry."

(the pause of a keeper, not a lawyer) You heard it. By their new word, whoever is not on their roll is not entered — anywhere, in any book, by any knot. Not because it is true. Because they have written that it is.

PANEL 3 — Close, the mint clause (~14%)

Camera: Close: the lower clause, Ira's finger under it — the paper's second blade.

Image: The amendment's real target is not the school's room. It is the certificate.

IRA: (of record, reading) "What is record: paper under stamp. What is not record: all else."

CAPTION (Ira's voice): There it is. Not one word about the school. Every word about the mint. My certificates are not ruled out — they are ruled out of existence, on taking, by definition. Their clerk finally learned to aim.

PANEL 4 — Medium, the wobble (~14%)

Camera: Medium: the claimant — the worn yard hand, third time at this counter — holding his keeper's certificate and his doubt in the same fist. The queue turns to watch how the keeper answers doubt.

Image: The wobble, on-panel, unresolved: the basin's first taste of what a definition costs.

CLAIMANT: The foreman got his own paper this morning. He says come dusk, by this, your word counts for nothing at my gate. He is not decided. I am not decided. Keeper — does mine still count?

IRA: (level, of record) The register does not answer fear with comfort. It answers with the record. Entered, of record: the question. The answer is owed by dusk tomorrow — and the register will owe it to you, by name, in writing.

PANEL 5 — Wide, the notice at the yard (~13%)

Camera: Wide, midday: the fuel-yard gate across the basin — the foreman's runner accepting a long sealed paper from an Office errand-hand at the gate's knot-board. The yard behind: sacks, split wood, men pausing mid-load to look at the paper. No foreman's face — the gate is the event.

Image: The wobble, delivered: the Office did not wait for the register's answer. It sent its definition straight to the gates.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): My ruling will take a day and a signature they cannot erase. Their counter-move took a runner and a definition. That is the whole war, in logistics.

PANEL 6 — Wide, the first rationed dusk (~14%)

Camera: Wide, dusk, the square from the stall's side: along the square, household lamps dying one by one at the posted hour — window by window going dark. On the hatch board, a fresh knot-marked notice: the permit-dark, the Office's lamp-hours, one seal. The stall's lamp alone stays lit — a small warm island, the queue still at the rail in its light.

Image: The basin goes dark on the Office's hour. The counter does not.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): First rationed dusk. They posted the hours the basin may keep light in, sealed, at their hatch — as if dusk were theirs to license. Across the square, the windows obeyed. Then they looked at the one lamp that did not.

PANEL 7 — Medium-close, the counter stays lit — FINAL HOOK (~16%)

Camera: Medium-close: the counter by the one small flame — trimmed low and exact. Kessa fills the lamp from a narrow oil can, unhurried, and ties a knot into a short cord by the till: the till now counts its own light. Ira writes. The register takes the day without complaint.

Image: The flame is smaller tonight, and it is not asking anything.

KESSA: (quiet, knotting the cord) We burn by our own count now, keeper. Four hours a night, knotted. When the ration turns, we will know it before they post it — the till counts faster than their board.

IRA: (of record, entering) Entered: the amendment, read aloud of record before the basin. Entered: the question at the yard gate, owed an answer by dusk tomorrow. Entered: the Office's hours — observed, and not obeyed.

CAPTION (smallest): Enter it anyway.

  • The street fetching the paper is the equilibrium deepening, not a gag: the basin cannot read Office grammar, and the counter reads it aloud of record — so the Office's own publication is answered at the counter.
