IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the register in its first winter)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "The record will not care why."

Page 001 note: enters the machine's first miss without excuse and holds the day open to dusk; grants the second protection of record — the first ever volunteered; and buys the year-knot's reading with her OWN line: mine — the keeper enters herself into somebody's count. Then hears what the knot says: the machine's nineteen years sit inside the school's thirty-nine. The chapter's marching order lands as the smallest caption: Invoice it.

KESSA (the till; the sealing hand)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"Your box keeps things private, mother. The register keeps them TRUE."

Page 001 note: hot cups into the window-frame, the oil-cord's three knots hanging, and the fold-in done right: Rekhak's paper into the back pocket unopened, wax along the seal, no peeking. Her line to him is the two institutions in one breath — her box keeps things private; the register keeps them true. Different needs. Both hers, in the end.

REKHAK VAHNI (the volunteer — the fold, entered unread)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni-ref.png

Rekhak Vahni / Manav / Agnikhand / cold-white Sight thread / deep debt /
Audit: reveal target's Kind, thread colour and debt level; each use adds 2 debt to self /
"Entered unread. Under protection. My name with it — the way you held his."

Page 001 note: his paper — carried since before the counter existed, shown as a FOLD in Ch. 013 — comes out of his coat for the second time ever and goes into the register UNOPENED. He does not show it; he asks for what the clerk got, on the first day it might matter. His thumb-mark goes into the wax beside the keeper's knot: the basin's grammar, chosen. What the paper IS stays his — the register protects the fold, not the contents. That is why it can be trusted with both.

NANDI (the reader — the year's price)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manav / Agnikhand / sight-white, lens-borne / reads, and is paid /
Read: thread-years at the price of an ask, properly made /
"The knot is not a date, seamstress. It is a DIRECTION."

Page 001 note: at the oldest rack with the lens, reading the year-knot at her canon price — an ask, a line in somebody's count. Ira pays with her own. The reading lands as direction, not date: the year of the second chain — nineteen years before their Freedom. Nandi does not editorialize; she reads, and lets the arithmetic be horrifying on its own.

THE COUNT-HOUSE HAND (one-off — thin, and late)

No full sheet; Ch. 013–014. A lesser clerk, snow-bound, near midnight: the first late delivery, one bundle where there are usually three, the permit-ledger missing. The machine is under strain too.

The Count-House Hand / Manav / Agnikhand / dust, wax, snow / counts for wages /
Deliver: the machine's weeks, to the keeper's grammar — late, and thin /
"Sign here. Only the one this week."

Page 001 note: arrives in P7's background trudging through snow with a visibly thin satchel. No explanation is offered or asked for. The miss is entered; the thinness is entered as received; the permit-ledger's absence stays unspoken — the winter's cold current, on the record by omission.
