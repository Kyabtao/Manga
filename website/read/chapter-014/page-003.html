THREADBORN — Chapter 14 — Page 003

Chapter title: Year Nineteen Arc: III — The Second School Sector: Agnikhand — the hatch, the school's table, and the register, deep winter Page type: The hold — 7 panels; the machine explains itself one page too late, and the tag reads as what it is: protection, tied from inside

Beats: the machine explains itself — a sealed notice at the hatch: the permit-ledger discontinued, lamp-oil folded into the fuel-yards' general schedule, for the season — the machine's own paper admitting, by reorganizing, that it is short; the reading of record — the counter reads it aloud and enters the obvious: the basin's light is now counted as part of the basin's fuel, two public schedules merged into one, and nobody living wrote the rule that merges them; the tag comes to the school's table — the new knot from the satchel, and Ira pays her SECOND line: Nandi reads it at her price; a hold mark — the machine's grammar for a count paused by its own office — and Nandi's colder finding: tied by a hand she does not recognize as the machine's current hand; the clerk hypothesis, entered as a question — somebody inside is pausing the paper — the man who was erased, or his like — slowing the basin's paper so it cannot be READ; the turn — the thin week was not a shortage: it was a shield, using the counter's own public-reading rule as cover; the winter's problem stated — the register enters it: how to make a machine that is protecting you tell you the truth.

This page: the enemy's grammar used gently, from inside, by a hand the basin already knows.

Chapter 014 budget: ONE chain-stop, unspent. Mother never on panel, never speaks — the thread quiet. Loom never speaks, does not appear. The three entered hands remain unnamed. Rekhak's paper stays folded — untouched, unmentioned beyond its place.

PANEL 1 — Wide, morning, the machine explains itself (~14%)

Camera: Wide: the hatch board, snow dusting its roof — a fresh sealed notice posted square, and the counting-lesson women already untying it as the crowd gathers. The basin moves on the hatch the way it moves on weather now: together, and before anyone official arrives.

Image: The machine answered the thin week — one page too late to be anything but a confession.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The thin delivery arrived three days ago. This notice is dated the day before yesterday. Machines do not apologize. They reorganize.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the reading of record (~15%)

Camera: Medium: at the stall's window, the women read the notice aloud at the counter of record — their voice now, not Ira's — while Ira listens inside and enters. Kessa at the till, one eyebrow up at the phrase for the season.

Image: The basin's light, counted as part of the basin's fuel — two public schedules merged by a rule nobody living wrote.

THE ELDER READER: (reading, slow and clear) "...the permit-ledger is discontinued. Lamp-oil shall be counted within the fuel-yards' general schedule, for the season. So it is filed."

IRA: (of record, entering) Entered: the light and the fuel, one schedule now. Read aloud: the machine is short of something, and has told us which shelf it hid it on. For the season. The season of what — it does not say.

PANEL 3 — Medium, the tag comes to the school's table (~15%)

Camera: Medium: the school's side room, the archive table — the new tag laid across Nandi's thick dark book, Ira standing over it with the satchel's knot in her gloved hand. Nandi's lens already coming up. The ask is priced before Ira finishes sitting down: her second line, going on the old school's books.

Image: The winter's only warm room, and the winter's coldest fact, laid side by side.

NANDI: (pricing, not looking up) Second line, keeper. It is a knot in the machine's grammar and it wants the old school's eyes — that combination is above your rate.

IRA: (of record) Enter it. Ira Sutar. Took: one reading. Paid: one line.

PANEL 4 — Close, a hold mark (~15%)

Camera: Close: the tag under the lens, Nandi's fingers turning it once: machine knot-work — and then the OLD READING, spoken flat while Ira writes: the machine's grammar for a count paused by its own office. Not broken. Not lost. PAUSED — deliberately, reversibly, by whoever holds the pen.

Image: The machine's coldest instrument: a knot that stops paper without stopping the machine.

NANDI: (flat) It is a hold mark. Their grammar for a count paused by its own office — tied from the inside, with the authority to untie it later. Not a shortage. A decision.

IRA: (of record, writing) Entered: the tag, a hold. The week's paper was not lost. It was withheld — by a hand with keys.

PANEL 5 — Medium-close, the colder finding (~14%)

Camera: Medium-close: Nandi still at the lens, and her face has gone from pricing to puzzled to something older: she turns the tag again, and shakes her head once — not refusal; REVISION. The knot-work's hand does not match the machine's current hand. She has been reading Office thread-work longer than the Office has been reading her.

Image: The hold was tied by a hand she does not recognize as the machine's current hand.

NANDI: (slow, certain) The grammar is theirs. The HAND is not. Whoever tied this learned it inside those walls — and is not tying it for them. A count paused from inside, keeper. There is exactly one man we have seen cross a threshold with that grammar in his fingers and no office left to answer for it.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The clerk. Or his like. I protected one erased hand at my counter — it appears the winter is full of them.

PANEL 6 — Medium, the turn (~14%)

Camera: Medium: the school's table, the rod, the book, the tag in a line between the two women — a hearing. Ira states the shape of it aloud, of record, and the room holds it: the thin week was not a shortage — it was a SHIELD. Somebody slowed the basin's paper using the counter's own public-reading rule as cover: paper paused cannot be read. Protection, wearing the machine's face.

Image: The enemy's grammar, used gently, from inside — to keep the machine from reading what the basin's paper says.

IRA: (of record, the shape of it) Our terms made our counter public: everything delivered here is read aloud. A hold on the delivery is a hold on the reading. If the machine's own rolls were coming to my table this winter carrying something it cannot afford read —

(the pause where the arithmetic finishes itself)

— then somebody tied that hold to keep the machine blind. The thin week was not a shortage. It was a shield.

PANEL 7 — Medium-close, the winter's problem — FINAL HOOK (~13%)

Camera: Medium-close: night, the stall: the register open, the tag lying at the page's corner beside the fresh entry, the lamp trimmed, snow past the shutters. Ira's pen moves through the entry and stops at its final line — the winter's problem, stated in the register's own grammar, with no answer under it. Kessa's hand rests on the till. Three knots on the cord.

Image: How to make a machine that is protecting you tell you the truth.

IRA: (of record, the problem, entered) Entered: the hold, tied from inside, by an unknown hand with machine grammar. Entered: the shield. The winter's count, open: a register that cannot ask its protector what it is protecting it from is a register with a knot in its own thread.

CAPTION (smallest): Read the tag.

Writing & art notes

  • Three jobs, in order: the machine's explanation (a reorganization that confesses); the tag's reading (a hold mark, tied by the wrong hand); the turn (the thin week was a shield). The explanation must be machine-flat — for the season, no referent, no apology. The confession is structural: the permit-ledger only needed discontinuing if the light's count mattered to someone.
  • Nandi's reading is the page's spine, and it has two layers. First the grammar (a hold — paused by its own office, reversible by its holder), then the revision (the HAND is wrong — inside the walls, and not tying for the machine). Play the revision small: one turn of the tag, one shake of the head, one slow sentence. The clerk is never named on-panel — the man we have seen cross a threshold is as far as anyone goes; Ira's caption does the rest.
  • The turn is the chapter's thesis landing: the counter's own rule (public reading of everything delivered) became the shield's cover — paper paused cannot be read. The basin's greatest instrument, used to protect the basin, by a hand inside the machine. Do not let Ira celebrate it; the register's problem is stated in the same breath: you cannot subpoena your protector. The winter's count stays open.
  • Nandi's second line is entered exactly like the first — same words, same grammar, no haggling: Ira Sutar. Took: one reading. Paid: one line. The repetition IS the discipline.
  • The imperative ladder takes its next run: Read the tag.
