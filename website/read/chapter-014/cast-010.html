Chapter 014 — Page 010 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one. English-only pages (AUDIT R13.22).

IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the keeper who gives the paper back read)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Give it back read, and the machine has to hold its own dodge."

Page 010 note: opens the shutter, sets the terms of the reading (once, aloud, slowly, numbers repeated on request), enters the sum — the shortage and the deliveries are the same fuel — chooses publication over silence, writes the counter's reading in the margin of the machine's own count, and sends the page back up the stair already read. The chapter's last entry closes the year without a single claim of victory.

KESSA (the till — the price of publishing, and one smile at the arithmetic)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"They cannot demand a paper they have disowned."

Page 010 note: holds the sheet flat while the margin is written and lays out the two ways — publish and the hand is safe because the machine cannot punish what it denied, or keep it quiet and hold a rope the counter would have to pull. She notes the crowd will be angry within the hour and says to let them be angry while they are still counting. One smile, at the arithmetic, and no more.

THE READER (the counting-lesson women — the numbers read flat)

No full sheet; Ch. 011–014. The school's reader at the window, holding the machine's own night-count open in the dawn wind and reading it clause by clause.

The Reader / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, learning / owes lessons, and is owed them /
Read: everything delivered, aloud, at the counter of record /
"Drawn: four. Struck from the roll: none. Carried down: four."

Page 010 note: reads the sheet without emphasis, row by row, so that the arithmetic belongs to the people hearing it. She is the basin's voice at the exact moment the basin learns what was done for it — the arc from reader-of-record at Ch. 013 to this page complete.

NANDI (the reader — the year, whole)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manav / Agnikhand / sight-white, lens-borne / reads, and is paid /
Read: thread-years at the price of an ask, properly made /
"The year is whole, keeper. It ran forward and it did not break."

Page 010 note: writes the school's winter's last line in her own book and closes the chapter on the only verdict the old school ever gives — not good, not just, but whole: it ran forward and it did not break. Her book is older than the Office by twenty years and now one line longer.

THE RUNNER (seventh appearance — carrying the page back up already read)

No full sheet; Ch. 004–006, 013–014. Takes the corded sheet with both hands and does not put it in his coat; asks what he is to say, and is told to say exactly what it is.

The Runner / Manav / Agnikhand / plain grey, uncommitted / paid by the road /
Carry: what a seal decides — and now, what a reading answers /
"Say: read at dawn, at the window, by the basin, aloud."

Page 010 note: the machine's own errand-boy becomes the courier of its disavowal read back to it. His question — what do I say, if they ask me what it is — is the page's honesty: he is the only person in the winter who has to stand in both rooms and be asked in each.

THE SQUARE (the sum, done aloud)

No sheet; the quarter entire. Present as faces counting in the blue light, a fire-measure held up like evidence, an elder sitting down on the step, a cap crushed in a fist — and one voice at the back carrying the arithmetic.

The Square / mixed kin / Agnikhand / as the day finds them / owed, and keeping count /
Sum: the shortage and the deliveries are the same fuel /
"Four a night. That is the whole thin week, given away."

Page 010 note: the page's real protagonist. They arrive before the hour is announced, they do their own arithmetic, and they do not cheer — which is the register's measure of how serious the gift was. No individual lines except the one carried voice.

THE SNOW-HEAVY FIGURE (gone before the reading finished — prints only)

No sheet; never faced, never named. Present this page as one line of prints softening in the slush beyond the open door, and nothing else.

The Snow-Heavy Figure / Manav (presumed) / Agnikhand / none given — snow takes the face / uncollected /
Hold: pause a count from inside; carry the page to the one room that reads — and leave before it is read /
*(a line of prints going away from the door)*

Page 010 note: he does not appear. The chapter's most important absence: the man who kept the basin warm all winter is not at his own vindication. If the prints are the clerk's, the page does not say so; the register does not guess — it enters what it can see, which is a line of prints.
