Chapter 014 — Page 006 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one. English-only pages (AUDIT R13.22).

IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the keeper who reads a gift where it hangs)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "If it was given to the board, the board is where the reading happens."

Page 006 note: refuses to lift the Office's new cord down — a gift gets read in public like anything else. Finds the gap by comparison, not intuition (the old tally's six groups against the new cord's seven), and enters it without a guess. Enters the till's risk in both halves when Kessa pays the school's lines, and closes the day with the only honest entry available: the fold was tested and it held.

KESSA (the till — now a purse, with its price said first)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"A school paid by the till is a school the till owns. They will say it in the spring."

Page 006 note: names the uncounted hour for what it looks like from the till — a hole with a rope around it — then lifts the lid for the first time in the chapter and founds the second Office's first public purse in front of witnesses: nine lines, one entering, the risk named before anyone else can name it. Generosity priced; that is her signature.

THE ELDER READER (the counting-lesson women — the machine's hours, in the basin's voice)

No full sheet; Ch. 011–014. The reading of record performed by the basin at dawn, in snow, with a hand lamp — the law the counter wrote, turned on the machine.

The Elder Reader / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, learning / owes lessons, and is owed them /
Read: the machine's paper, aloud, wherever it hangs /
"Six of them. Six is the whole Office."

Page 006 note: walks the hour-groups with her fingers in lamplight and reads six shifts into the cold, then the seventh — and the fuel-yards, at night — in a voice that does not know it has just found the winter's hole. The pause before the last group is the page's whole tension.

REKHAK VAHNI (the fold's owner — silent, in public, testing the register's word)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni-ref.png

Rekhak Vahni / Manav / Agnikhand / single-strand, burning fast / deep, and paying /
Audit: read the debt a thing cannot hide / *(two fingers on the fold; no words at all)*

Page 006 note: off shift, at dusk, without a word: two fingers on the register's back pocket, eyes on the fold's edge, chain looped still around his hand, ash left on his shoulders. He is not threatening the fold and the page must not play it as threat — he is confirming that the register keeps its word where a whole square can see. He does not ask what is written on the paper. He leaves through the cold door without a line.

THE MACHINE (off-panel — the posting)

No full sheet; the Office entire. Speaks this page as a cord of knot-groups hung at night by nobody.

The Machine / — / Agnikhand / nineteen years of white spaces / counts, and does not refuse /
Post: its hours in public, six counted and one not /
"Six of them. Six is the whole Office."

Page 006 note: complies with the symmetry within a night — no clerk, no seal-cart, no boots in the snow — and thereby confesses twice: that it will do the public thing only while the public sleeps, and that somewhere in its own schedule is an hour it does not count. The page never shows an Office interior and never explains the night shift.

THE BASIN QUEUE (witnesses, and a purse's first creditors)

No sheet; the square entire, Ch. 001 onward. Present as the crowd that arrives before first light, hears the hours read, watches the till open, and sees a Council Reckoner touch a fold in public and go.

The Queue / mixed kin / Agnikhand / as the day finds them / owed, and keeping count /
Read: everything delivered, aloud, at the counter of record /
*(they will remember the price of the school in the spring)*

Page 006 note: the page's memory. They are close enough to see the fold, count the tallies and hear the risk entered — which is the point of doing all three at an open window. No individual lines; the crowd's stillness does the work.
