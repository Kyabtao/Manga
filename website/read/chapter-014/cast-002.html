IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the keeper, on the wrong side of an entry)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Enter the shortness, not the guess."

Page 002 note: enters the thin delivery without guessing the cause; enters HERSELF on the school's books as a debtor in the old grammar — took: one reading; paid: one line — and shakes, once, at the grammar, not the debt. The week's open count is written as a question: what is the machine saving its paper for?

KESSA (the till; almost fond)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"Ten weeks of putting other people in books, keeper. And it took one reading to put YOU in one."

Page 002 note: turns the till's lid once without opening it, and delivers the appraisal the till has been saving: the keeper has finally learned what an entry feels like from the other side. The register's family shows — bookkeeping that happens to be tender.

NANDI (the reader — two steps of the year, and one priced refusal)

Full sheet (Ch. 006): ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md · Art: ../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png

Nandi / Manav / Agnikhand / sight-white, lens-borne / reads, and is paid /
Read: thread-years at the price of an ask, properly made /
"Ask me again when your line is paid. Forward, and whole."

Page 002 note: enters Ira's debtor line in her OWN book — small, thick, older than any Office roll, the old grammar, no lettering — and unrolls the oldest rod a hand's width: thirty-nine season-knots, unbroken. Refuses the second-chain question at her price: she reads what is ON the thread, and the old school counts forward, not back. The only refusal in the basin that is honest about being one.

THE COUNTING-LESSON WOMEN (cameo — the flat line)

No full sheet; Ch. 011–014. At the rail's end while the thin bundles are entered.

The Counting-Lesson Women / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, learning / owe each other lessons /
Read: the machine's paper, aloud, at the counter of record /
*(the elder's mouth a flat line)*

Page 002 note: no lines this page; their faces are the basin's reading of the shortness — ten weeks of rhythm, then the year read at midnight, then paper comes in short. Nobody here believes in accidents. The queue does not need the arithmetic explained; it did the arithmetic.

THE COUNT-HOUSE HAND (off-panel — the subtraction with a knot on it)

No full sheet; Ch. 013–014. Does not appear this page; the satchel speaks: two bundles of three, re-tagged in the machine's grammar, the permit-ledger not in it.

The Count-House Hand / Manav / Agnikhand / dust, wax, snow / counts for wages /
Deliver: the machine's weeks, to the keeper's grammar /
*(this week: absent, and the absence tagged)*

Page 002 note: his missing delivery is the page's cold fact. Whoever tied the new tag knew the counter reads everything now — a message, not a mishap. Entered as received. The register does not answer messages; it holds them until they mean something on their own.
