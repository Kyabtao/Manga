THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks — presence)

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Keep: maintain a room that does not exist; sweep after yourself /
"Writing, for nine years. Then directions. Now presence." — the channel widens. The hook stays empty.

Page 003 note: never seen. The step-knot was tied from here — the hook's curve carries years of grey, old-style thread-scars and was swept clean this week, and the lamp that lights the school's whole roll is kept full by somebody's hands. Ira's inference is certain in feeling, inference in fact: this week I cannot take a step without landing in her grammar. No whorl-mark; no second knot; the hook remains empty.

Page note: the knock answers, but nobody answers it — a door filed as nothing answers by not stopping you. Inside: the archive. The school kept its own roll all along, in thread, its own way; the twenty-two the Office calls nameless have been on it by length for years; the oldest rod begins with a year Ira cannot read; and the hook by the door is swept, empty, and speaks her mother's grammar. The Office's reader enters lawfully, reads by reflex, and then makes the page's choice: the warrant goes back unwritten — today's report is a drain-mend — and the price of the reading is named. Not coin. An ask, properly made. Hook: "Ask him, keeper."
