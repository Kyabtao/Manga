Chapter 012 — Page 002 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one, including the unnamed ones and the door's watchers.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST — the walker)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Let him see exactly this: the keeper of the register, walking to the low gate on a mend."

Page 002 note: walks the chapter's answer instead of speaking it. Reads the step-knot literally and refuses to untie it; turns the tail's paid honesty into her cover — let him see a drain-mend — and is the first person in the basin's living memory to stand before the door and read the one line of Office paper she has ever believed: void, no hands. Her hand rises. The page ends before it lands.

KESSA — केसा (the stall keeps)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut / "The stall keeps. I keep. Go."

Page 002 note: takes the stall without being asked and names the carrying in one sentence: a pledge in a pocket still pulls — the tether travels, and the weight is Ira's now. Does not look up when she says it. The only person who never asked where Ira was going; this morning the only one who already knows.

NIMA — नीमा (the two lamps)

Full sheet (Ch. 010): ../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md · Art: ../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png

Nima / Kshudra / Agnikhand / grey, declared / none — that was the bargain /
Teach: the holding that made three strangers come to learn / "It is the best kind. It becomes one later."

Page 002 note: hangs the school's brass lantern back on its hook under the Office's grey lamp — the alley goes on being watched and taught at the same time — and issues the page's engine: if anyone asks, you are mending a drain. The lie that is true, and that becomes true by arrival, and false by door.

THE THREE NEW HANDS — तीन नए हाथ (unnamed, unrolled, early again)

No full sheet; Ch. 012. The school's new pupils — faces at the circle's edge at dawn.

The Three New Hands / mixed / Agnikhand / grey, uncounted / various /
Come: they watched a girl hold a hand steady and came to learn the holding /
No line on this page. Every day the keeper is gone, the roll gets less whole.

Page 002 note: early again — the school teaches on while Ira walks, which is exactly what the Office's definition converts into leverage. Deliberately unnamed, deliberately uncounted.

THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक (buys eyes)

No full sheet; Ch. 009–012. The Office's voice with a pen in it.

Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Copy: reproduce any record in the Office's own hand / "Mouths are fed. Eyes are cheaper."

Page 002 note: the count is by mouth and slow, so he rents the fastest eyes in the basin and praises the exactness he will soon have reason to regret. Calls Rekhak's honesty the only honest arithmetic left in this building — and it is, which is the problem.

REKHAK VAHNI — रेखक वह्नि (the warrant; the grey coat)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni-ref.png

Rekhak Vahni / Manav / Agnikhand / cold-white Sight thread / deep debt /
Audit: reveal target's Kind, thread colour and debt level; each use adds 2 debt to self / "I report what I see. Exactly."

Page 002 note: takes the day-warrant because exactness is his product — no menace on-panel, just arithmetic. On the rim road he is only a coat the grey of paperwork at an interval that never changes; at the gallery mouth he arrives exactly when the cover story dies. He will report what he sees, exactly: a keeper at a wall filed void, before a door with no lock. What exactness is worth, and to whom, is Page 003's problem.

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is the knot she left — and nothing else.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Point: the one thing of hers that has ever pointed at her daughter /
"Here, then there." — the knot stays tied. Untied, it is only thread.

Page note: the quietest open in the series — a walk, not a quest. The stall keeps (Kessa), the lantern teaches (Nima), the tether travels (the note, in Ira's inside pocket), the Office buys eyes (the clerk's day-warrant to Rekhak), and the walk ends where it was always going: behind the low gate, at a wall of seasons of seals that have gone around one small door forever — no lock, no bar, not one seal on it, the log plate reading void, no hands: the only true line the Office ever filed. It was never hiding. It was filed. Hook: "Knock, keeper." — with the paid coat arriving exactly on time to see it.
