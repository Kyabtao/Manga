Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Bad grammar. Tied with the whole heart."

Page 009 note: unties the one thing of her mother's that ever pointed at her, because the asking shape requires it — a turn (who signs the whorl) and a loop left open (will you stand). KESSA (the reader of the verdict)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"Tightened, it holds forever — her knots do not come undone. Loose is a condition, keeper."

Page 009 note: stood the whole night's wait without being asked to, and reads the geometry at dawn the way only she can: she will be tied in. By the school's hand. Not by theirs. Nobody tightens it but the book that counts her fair. The appraiser's verdict, delivered on the appraiser's own step, over a knot neither of them touches.

THE MOTHER (never on panel, never speaks — the answer)

No full sheet, by canon. Her appearance on this page is two knots and a long end: the biggest presence nine chapters have allowed her.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Answer: retie the direction; tie an entry and leave it loose /
She did not close the loop. She tied the thing the loop was asking for. Finish it properly, and I am already on the roll.

Page 009 note: restores the direction she was asked through, and answers the question with an act: the tying-in knot, in her hand, in her thread, at her daughter's own door — left loose, its long end running down-lane to the archive. Forty years keeping the school's roll while keeping herself off every roll the basin has. She is not refusing the count. She has been waiting for a count worth standing in.

THE COUNT (all night, over the rooftops)

No full sheet; Ch. 012. The mouth-count, reading into the dark.

The Count / Office process / Agnikhand / none — read aloud, line by line / spends the readers' voices /
Stall: a name, a line, a pause, a resume — as if the Office could out-last the dark by reading at it /
No line on this page. The pause is still a person.

Page 009 note: the wait's other clock, stumbling over the rooftops all night and resuming at dawn. The Office reads at the dark; the step answers by morning.

Page note: thread answering thread — the chapter's secrets finish their last turns. The untie (untied, it is only thread); the asking shape taught once and used once (a turn and a loop left open); the night's wait with the count stumbling overhead; and at dawn, the answer: the direction retied, and beside it an entry-knot left loose, its long end lying down-lane toward the school's roll. Kessa's verdict: nobody tightens it but the book that counts her fair. The circle closes — the coat writes, Nandi keeps her grammar (grammar is not gossip), the amendment waits armed — and the endgame stands inventoried: the school's roll whole but for three hands, the Office's count stalled at the nameless, the founder waiting for a count worth standing in. Hook: "Tie her in, keeper."
