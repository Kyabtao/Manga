Chapter 012 — Page 001 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md (Name / Kind / Sector / Thread colour / Debt tendency / Signature ability / One-line hook) — every entry on this page carries one, including the unnamed ones.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "It is not a song. It is directions."

Page 001 note: reads her mother's only written sentence aloud in the Office's voice — and finds the legal spine of the chapter: the note on file is a standing no, older than the roll, and clause two contains nothing about the word being yes. Receives the fifth note on her own step — her mother's first request in her life — and the chapter's destination: year nineteen.

KESSA — केसा (custody, transferred)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"Forty years it was hers. Nine years it was the box's. Today it is yours."

Page 001 note: opens the box unasked and hands the note across, naming the custody transfer in one sentence. The empty fold stays hers; the note becomes Ira's. She never opened it in forty years — a pledge is not a letter; you keep it, or you do not — and she is the first to raise her head when the fifth note lands.

REKHAK VAHNI — रेखक वह्नि (the count returns to him)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni-ref.png

Rekhak Vahni / Manav / Agnikhand / cold-white Sight thread / deep debt /
Audit: reveal target's Kind, thread colour and debt level; each use adds 2 debt to self /
"The cord is dead."

Page 001 note: back after a chapter off-panel — reader-of-record for a count that cannot be completed. His first observation breaks the Office's instrument on the record: the Loom's knot, spent by the basin, has stopped the counting-cord from inside the chest. The count must be run by mouth, line by line, name by name — and twenty-two of the sixty-three have none.

THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक (the definition)

No full sheet; Ch. 009–012. The Office's voice with a pen in it.

Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Define: legislate a word and let arithmetic do the rest /
"The roll is whole when every hand taught by the school stands entered upon it."

Page 001 note: stops banging on doors and edits what words mean. Reads the definition like a tide-table — the Office's plainest weapon, and the page's villain precisely because it will work.

NIMA — नीमा (the school's voice)

Full sheet (Ch. 010): ../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md · Art: ../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png

Nima / Kshudra / Agnikhand / grey, declared / none — that was the bargain /
Teach: the holding that made three strangers come to learn /
"Whole means we stop."

Page 001 note: reads the posted definition to the morning circle and states the trap in two sentences. Teaching in the lender's coat — and three new faces came to learn the holding. She knows naming them would be entering them, so the page does not.

THE THREE NEW HANDS — तीन नए हाथ (unnamed, unrolled, the chapter's clock)

No full sheet; Ch. 012. The school's new pupils — faces only.

The Three New Hands / mixed / Agnikhand / grey, uncounted / various /
Come: they watched a girl hold a hand steady and came to learn the holding /
No line on this page. Every week the school teaches, the roll gets less whole.

Page 001 note: the Office's leverage made of the school's success. Introduced as faces at the circle's edge; deliberately unnamed, deliberately uncounted.

THE MOTHER — माँ (never on panel, never speaks)

No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is a note on file, a fifth note at dusk, and a knot on the step.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Ask: her first request in her daughter's life, in the language that leaves no record /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M." — the no on file, and the directions out of it. Year nineteen.

Page note: the Office posts its definition — whole means the school stops — and hands the count to Rekhak, whose first finding is that the counting-cord is dead inside the Office's own chest. The blank note leaves Kessa's box at last: "Do not enrol me" is the owner's standing no, the one document that can stop clause two. At dusk the four notes return after eighteen days — and add a fifth. On the step, a knot tied the old way: not a message, a direction. Hook: "It is not a song. It is directions." — Year nineteen.
