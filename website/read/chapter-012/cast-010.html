KESSA (the box; the echo)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md · Art: ../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /

Page 010 note: rides the cart with her box, watches the street bring its paper to a book nine days old, and lands the page's last warmth: the box kept a pledge forty years and never asked whose — the register is not a rival, it is a keep, and she of all people knows. The fold stays hers. Nothing is surrendered.

THE GREY CLERK (the naming)

No full sheet; Ch. 009–012. The Office's voice with a pen in it — and, for one midday, terrible in its fairness.

"The count is suspended. Not postponed. Suspended."

Page 010 note: names the trap by its builder's mouth: the founder stands entered on the school's roll and against entry on the Office's — by her word, on file, older than their paper — and the count cannot proceed. The postponement was a clock; the suspension is an admission. The amendment stays armed and unsigned in Room Nine, and his silence about it is his defeat.

THE MOTHER (never on panel, never speaks — the entrance)

No full sheet, by canon. Her appearance on this page is a knot going tight.

— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered — now tied in /
Stand: entered upon the school's roll by her own mark, whole, in her own school /
"Do not enrol me." — Nobody has. The school does not enrol. It ties in.

Page 010 note: her entrance is a pull: two gloved hands, a long silvered end, and the founder entered beside the year. She stands in the count that is hers and no other's — and her no is still on file, still older than their roll, which is why the Office's count stops cold with her word in it. The fifth note does not come tonight. It does not need to.

Page note: taught, walked, tied, entered, named, filed, whole. The lesson finishes; the school walks in the open; Nima's hands tie the three in and then Nandi (enter me whole); the founder's entry is tightened by the last hand it was waiting for — the whorl enters beside the year. The clerk, terrible in his fairness, names the machine to the whole basin and stops the clock: suspended, not postponed. The register takes the street's paper at the counter; the box's echo lands; and on the step at dusk the direction sits intact, the loose entry's space is swept clean, and the four-note sentence comes back around whole. Hook: "The roll is whole, keeper." — Year nineteen.
