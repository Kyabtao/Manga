Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Read it again. Slowly, the way you post things. Which roll did it name?"

Page 005 note: performs the chapter's firing in public and in the Office's own grammar: the definition read back verbatim at posting speed, then one entry added to the record — the school's roll is whole, as of the last finished teaching, of record. THE GREY CLERK (the grammar that met its own paper)

No full sheet; Ch. 009–012. The Office's voice with a pen in it.

The Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Copy: reproduce any record in the Office's own hand / "The Office will discuss its roll with the Office's keeper — in the Office."

Page 005 note: loses a public argument without conceding one: the roll means the Office's roll, obviously fails against a basin that heard the posting, and his hand rises to take the paper down and learns in mid-air that posted means posted. Falls back to what was always in his coat: a pre-cut, pre-sealed summons — no square, no crowd, Room Nine. He came with both papers.

THE BASIN (the readers it was posted to)

No full sheet; the crowd as an entry.

The Basin / mixed / Agnikhand / every thread the square has ever seen / debts, most of them /
Witness: hear a paper read aloud once and remember it wrong in useful ways /
"Posted means posted. We heard it posted."

Page 005 note: the crowd of a hearing: survey veterans, stall-holders, the counting-lesson women, a woman mouthing the definition back to herself to keep it exact. The trap closed because they heard it posted once and will not need to hear it twice. The only audience that was ever meant.

Page note: the basin's one newspaper, read aloud. The definition verbatim — the count will be taken when the roll is whole; the roll is whole when every hand taught by the school stands entered upon it — then the reading of record: the school's roll is whole, as of the last finished teaching. The clerk's grammar (the Office's roll, obviously) fails against a basin that remembers what was posted; his hand rises to take the paper down and drops — posted means posted, and taking it down unmakes every paper the Office ever posted. The clock turns in public: three mid-thread faces become the race between a mouth-count and a school. And the paper moves: he came with both papers, and the one that comes out is a summons. Hook: "Bring your own law, keeper."
