THREADBORN — Chapter 12 — Page 010

Chapter title: The Whole Roll Arc: III — The Second School Sector: Agnikhand — the alley, the low gate, the archive, the square, the stall, the step at dusk Page type: Chapter finale — 7 panels, ends with the roll whole and the sentence whole

Beats: the lesson finishes — the three mid-thread hands hold steady at last, and Nima judges them taught; the open walk — the whole school to the low gate, the basin after it, the coat writing; the tying-in — Nima's hands tie the three in, then Nandi, price paid to the hour (when the school ties a hand in, it ties mine); the founder enters — the loose entry's long end met by the roll at last, and the daughter's gloved hands pull it tight; the Office names its trap — the count stops at the twenty-two, publicly, by the clerk's own mouth; the counter-book — the register open at the Mendery counter, the basin's second Office; and the step at dusk — the direction intact, the four-note sentence whole, and a page-sized silence where the fifth note used to hang.

This page: a chapter ending the way a knot ends — pulled tight, no slack, nothing left to tie.

Chapter 012 budget: ONE chain-stop, UNspent — the chapter keeps its unspent budget to the last page: the Office never gets its hardware moment. Mother never on panel, never speaks — her entrance is a knot tightening, and it is the biggest thing in the chapter. Loom never speaks, does not appear.

PANEL 1 — Medium, the alley, morning (~14%)

Camera: Medium: the alley, morning, the grey lamp burning in daylight. The circle around the crate — and in it, the three mid-thread hands, each holding a stranger's hand steady: not badly, not bravely. Steady. Nima watches, arms folded, and gives the basin her judgment with a single nod.

Image: Taught. The school's own word for it, given by the school's own mouth.

NIMA: (to the circle, level) They hold. That is the whole of the lesson. The rest is years.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The school finishes when Nima says it finishes. She says it on a morning like any other, over a crate, with ash in the air — and the roll that has been whole-but-for-three since the winter is whole at the next tying.

PANEL 2 — Wide, the open walk (~13%)

Camera: Wide: the procession out — the school to the low gate in the open, in daylight: Nima, the three, Kessa riding the cart with her box, Nandi in her shawl with the lens at her throat, and the basin trailing half a street behind. On the rim road's edge, at the fixed interval, the grey coat — walking with them now, writing.

Image: Nobody sneaks. The school was never a secret. Today the walk is the announcement.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): We go in the front, the way we went in the first time, and the basin comes to watch, and the coat comes to be exact. Let the Office read its own report and choke on it: the tally-keeper entered the school. The school entered the school. Nobody stopped anybody.

PANEL 3 — Wide, the tying-in (~16%)

Camera: Wide: the archive, lamplit, racks and all — the basin packed to the walls and spilling into the gallery. At the oldest rack, Nima's hands tie the three: entry-knots, tightened, one by one — the pupils entered by their teacher. And then, to the hour, her price: Nima turns to Nandi, and Nandi — chin up, lens at her throat — holds out both wrists like a woman being measured for cuffs, and is tied in.

Image: The Office's most ruined reader, entered by the school that ruined her — at her own asking, at her own price.

NANDI: (as the knot goes tight) Forty years I served a book that lost my page.

(looking up, wet-eyed, exact)

Here is a book that keeps what it counts. Enter me whole.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The basin does not cheer. It does something better: it goes quiet, the way a room goes quiet when a debt is actually paid.

PANEL 4 — Close, the founder enters (~17%)

Camera: Close: the last knot in the room. The oldest rod, held level — the year-knot at its head, the founder's whorled entry below it, and the loose tying-in at its end, long silvered thread lying down the step toward the door. Ira's gloved hands come into frame: one hand takes the long end, one hand takes the roll's own standing thread, and the pull — slow, deliberate, all the way — tightens the founder's entry into the school's roll.

Image: She never appears. She does not have to. The knot tightening IS her entrance.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): Nobody tightens it but the book that counts her fair. The book is here. It has been here the whole time, in a wet room, in thread, kept by her hand — and the last hand it was waiting for is mine.

CAPTION: The whorl enters beside the year. The founder of the grammar both books count in stands entered, whole, by her own mark, in her own school — and the note in my register is still her no, still on file, still older than their roll. She stands in the count that is hers and no other's.

CAPTION: Do not enrol me, she wrote, forty years ago. Nobody has. The school does not enrol. It ties in. There is a difference, and the difference is the whole chapter.

PANEL 5 — Medium, the Office names its trap (~15%)

Camera: Medium: the square, midday, the list-post. The grey clerk, composed, reads the Office's progress to the basin from a slate of knot-marks — and stops. Lays the slate down on the post's ledge. Faces the basin he posted to.

Image: The trap has to be named by its builder to close. He names it.

GREY CLERK: (public, level, terrible in its fairness) The count has reached the nameless. The count cannot proceed. The founder of the roll stands entered upon the school's roll — and stands against entry upon ours, by her word, on file, older than this Office's paper. The Office will not take that roll while her word stands.

GREY CLERK: The count is suspended. Not postponed. Suspended.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): And there it is, in front of the whole basin, by the Office's own mouth: the postponement was a clock. The suspension is an admission. They built a machine out of my mother's silence, and the same silence has stopped it cold — and every soul in this square heard the machine get named.

PANEL 6 — Medium, the counter-book (~12%)

Camera: Medium: the Mendery counter, dusk, the lamp lit. The register lies open — the archive pocket fat now: the no, the ask, the reading, the refusal, and the day's last thing: a slip of the school's own knot-work recording the tying-in, witnessed. Kessa across the counter, box closed, fold inside, watching Ira file the day.

Image: The basin's second Office, open for business, one book older than the first.

KESSA: The box kept a pledge forty years and never asked whose. Your book keeps a roll nine days old and already the street brings it its paper.

IRA: (not looking up from the entry) It is not a rival, Kessa. It is a keep.

KESSA: (taking up her thread) So was the box, keeper. So was the box.

PANEL 7 — Medium-close, the step at dusk — FINAL HOOK (~13%)

Camera: Medium-close: the step at dusk. The direction knot, silver, intact, tail down-lane. Beside it, nothing — the space where the loose entry lay is swept clean; the entry is in the book where it belongs. Ira sits on the step with her hands empty. And over the lane, faint and unhurried, the four-note sentence comes back around — the oldest thing in her life, whole again, nothing added, nothing hanging.

Image: The roll is whole. The sentence is whole. What is left is the number that does not fit — and the door it belongs to.

CAPTION (Ira's voice): The fifth note does not come tonight. It does not need to. The question it asked has been answered in thread, at the archive, by everybody's hands but hers.

CAPTION (smallest): The roll is whole, keeper.
