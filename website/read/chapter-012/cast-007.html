Page 007 note: walks the day down — out of the Office unhurried, past the count stumbling at the nameless, through the square that keeps the posted paper alive — and comes home to do the page's real work: lay three papers on the counter (her mother's no; the summons; the amendment copy for the keeper's files — he gave her the weapon and kept the receipt) and file them all in the register's archive pocket. THE THREE NEW HANDS (inside the circle now)

THE DUSK READERS (the basin's new institution)

No full sheet; Ch. 012. The square's evening knot of readers.

The Dusk Readers / mixed / Agnikhand / every thread at dusk / none that shows /
Keep: read a posted thing daily so it cannot quietly die /
"Posted means posted. We heard it posted."

Page 007 note: stall-holders on the way home, the counting-lesson women, a child — murmuring the definition back and forth like a psalm with a number in it. The basin is keeping the paper alive on the Office's behalf, and it has learned the grammar better than the man who wrote it.

Page note: two clocks running, one dusk. Out of Room Nine with the thesis (I signed nothing; he wrote everything) converting into the page's discovery — a filed refusal is a shut door, and shut doors open from either side, later. The race said plain at the gate (exact at the end); the count audible over the yard wall, stumbling at the twenty-two; the school answering by continuing — three mid-thread hands inside the circle now, holding other hands badly, watched like sand in a glass; the posted paper kept alive by dusk readers. Home: boxes first, wars after. And the counter at lamp-low: three papers out of one pocket, the register opened flat — a no, an ask, a reading, and a refusal, everything the basin has that the Office wants and cannot take. Hook: "A second Office, keeper. Keep it better."
