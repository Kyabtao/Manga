Positions for the fallout without melodrama: what the basin does with a plain reading is not my fee.

Page 004 note: never seen, never heard. The newest rod's last three finished knots are this season's, tied in her grammar — and beyond them, windings past the knots: three hands mid-teaching, not yet tied in. Wholeness is a moving number, and her hand is the hand that moves it.

Page note: the ask, properly made and entered of record; the fee named — you become a line in somebody's count; the reading aloud, by length and turn, no names needed — the basin's nameless read better than the basin ever read its own; the caveat that keeps the clock alive; the verdict, flat as weather: the school's roll is whole. And the assembly, built in public, caption by caption: he never said the Office's roll — the postponement is over, the count is owed, now. Nothing leaves the archive but a sentence. Hook: "Read it back to them, keeper."
