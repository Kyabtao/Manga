THE CLERK (Panel 2, voice only)

Procedural, like the Court's Official — but where the Official was indifferent, the clerk is tidy. The up-terrace is not cruel; it is organised.

Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / n/a (off-stage) / light debt /
sort the world into visitors and records / "Then you're a record. Which line?"

If the clerk returns, name them Lekh. Keep them seated; up-terrace staff never stand for the basin.

THE UP-TERRACE (Panel 1, as a character)

Clean stone, laundered banners, hazeless sun, no ash. The first panel in the series without falling ash. The terraces are drawn as a person from here on: beautiful, organised, and allergic to the basin.

Page 005 — nobody else
