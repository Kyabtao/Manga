Chapter 001 — Page 010 Cast · CHAPTER FINALE

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet: ira-sutar.md · Art: ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / colourless thread / zero debt /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Not one of them was mine."

Page 010 note: on her own roof, at night, she re-reads her hand: not empty — packed. Covers the stitch like something asleep and speaks her first vow, one word: "Sleep." Chapter 1 ends with her owing one true thing and owning one folded thread.

KESSA — केसा

Full sheet: kessa.md · Art: kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey thread / light debt /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; knots cannot be cut /
"You're the only dry thing in the flood."

Page 010 note: opens the lockbox at last: forty years of kept cut-end, never asked what it was cut from. Her eyeline from Page 007 pays out. Two lines of dialogue, both to nobody. Fear with tenderness under it — first time on panel.

THE LOOM (Panels 1, 7 — presence, not character)

Turns overhead exactly as on Page 001. Does not speak. Does not notice. Its hum ends the chapter one note lower than last night, and no one in-world hears it. Hard canon holds: the Loom is a structure, never a voice.

THE SHADOWLESS SWATCH (Panels 4–7, an object with a card line)

A short cut end of unpulled thread, kept by Kessa from before the first Unspooling she counted. Catches no lamplight; casts no shadow in ordinary light. Its far end carries the knot-shape — third appearance (vision whorl, Page 008 → fist-whorl, Page 009 → swatch knot, Page 010).

Severed End / — / — / unpulled, colourless / owes nothing /
casts no shadow in any light; cannot be pulled, owed or Frayed /
"Never once asked what it was cut from."

Do not give the knot-shape an owner on panel. Not this arc, not this year of arcs.

Page 010 — nobody else

Two named characters, one object, one structure. A finale is a held chord, not a crowd: no Patra, no Rekhak, no market. Their absence is what makes the last page quiet enough to hear the hum change.
