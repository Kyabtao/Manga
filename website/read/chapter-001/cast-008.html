Chapter 001 — Page 008 Cast

Everyone on the page. Card-game line format per series-bible/04-card-game-notes.md.

IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet: ira-sutar.md · Art: ira-sutar-ref.png

Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / colourless thread / zero debt /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Not one of them was mine."

Page 008 note: her palm is read from the inside for the first time in her life. She feels the fold turn over in its sleep. She does not scream, negotiate or price it — she whispers. First page where Ira has no transactional line at all.

KESSA — केसा

Full sheet: kessa.md · Art: kessa-ref.png

Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey thread / light debt /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; knots cannot be cut /
"You're the only dry thing in the flood."

Page 008 note: thumb on the knot-cord the whole hour; overrules Rekhak on when the hour begins and ends and wins without raising her voice; frozen between knots fifty-nine and sixty at the silent second. Her count is the chapter's clock.

REKHAK VAHNI — रेखक वह्नि

Full sheet: rekhak-vahni.md · Art: rekhak-vahni-ref.png

Rekhak Vahni / Manav / Agnikhand / cold-white Sight thread / deep debt /
Audit: reveal target's Kind, thread colour and debt level; each use adds 2 debt to self /
"I'm here for a hole."

Page 008 note: kneels twice (the Council does not kneel); spends the commissioned hour; lines climb the cheek toward the under-eye; chain quickens then stops (second stop in the series) inside the vision's aftermath. Stands again on Page 009 — read it as re-entering his uniform.

PATRA — पत्र (WITNESS, CORNER)

Full sheet: patra.md · Art: patra-ref.png

Patra / Preta / unregistered / cut thread / owes nothing /
Broker: arrange a contract between two other players; Patra can never be a party to it /
"I was struck from a book once. Now I put people into other books."

Page 008 note: witnesses from the lamppool, shadow puddling toward the lamp; at the silent second the shadow straightens half a beat before the body — new artist rule for Preta shadows.

THE BASIN (Panel 6, the silent second)

Every threaded soul on every rooftop, hands to their marks, looking up at a Spindle that flickered for the first time in four hundred years. No faces close enough to name. The crowd gets one wide shot and the absence of sound; that is its line.

Page 008 — nobody else

Four named characters and a city holding its breath. The hour is a sealed room inside a sealed minute: no clerks, no market, no fourth chair filled. The empty chair is the principal's and stays empty.
