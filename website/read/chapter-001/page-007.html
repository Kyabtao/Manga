THREADBORN — Chapter 01 — Page 007

Chapter title: The Girl With No Thread Arc: I — The Unspooling Sector: Agnikhand — Council Stair (descent) → the Knot & Nail, night Page type: Consult / promise — 7 panels, ends on a debt Canvas: vertical webtoon strip, full colour

Beats carried from Page 006: three terms accepted, the unpulled thread, "whoever sewed you was lending", Rekhak's Don't., Patra's real smile. This page brings the deal home to the only adult Ira trusts to count — and charges her the first debt she will ever owe.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the descent (~13%)

Camera: From below on the Council Stair, looking up at Ira coming down at dusk. Image: The exact reverse of Page 005 Panel 1: lantern-light rising to meet her, and from the third landing down, ash resumes. The first flake lands on her raised wrist and she does not brush it — she lets it pile. Behind her above, the clear-air terraces glow clean and far away, like a shop window.

CAPTION: On the way down, the sky remembered her.

IRA (thought, italic): There's the ash.

IRA (thought, italic): There's home.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the Knot & Nail at night (~13%)

Camera: From the stall door inward. Shutters half-down, one lamp, the whole shop breathing slow. Image: Kessa behind the counter counting her knotted tally-thread without looking at it. Ira enters and lays the registration form on the counter folded, blank side up — she has carried it like that all the way down.

IRA (dialogue): I've taken a job. I need a counter.

KESSA (dialogue, not looking up): I'm not a counter. I'm an appraiser.

KESSA (dialogue, loupe flipping down): Appraise it.

PANEL 3 — Medium close, the terms in mender's shorthand (~14%)

Camera: On Kessa's enormous hands as they work — and stop, one term at a time. Image: Her hands keep counting through term one; stop at witness who can count; nod once at my stall, before dawn; and at one true question, already answered the loupe flips back up, so both cat eyes are equal and on Ira.

IRA (dialogue): An hour of my palm, under the Reckoner's Sight. Witnessed by someone who can count. Spent here, before dawn.

IRA (dialogue): Paid in a registration line. Name, Kind, thread.

KESSA (dialogue, still): And the tear? You saw the tear?

PANEL 4 — Tight two-shot, the sealing (~15%)

Camera: Foreheads close across the counter, lamp between them. The market noise is gone; even the steam pot has stopped. Image: Kessa's face has gone appraiser-still, then something older than appraisal. At the word unpulled, her cat eyes flick — once, half a second — toward the lockbox under the counter. Then back to Ira.

IRA (dialogue): The stitch was sewn with thread that has never been pulled.

KESSA (dialogue, very quiet): Then it wasn't a mending that went into you, be.

KESSA (dialogue): Mendings leave a debt-shaped hole. Closing a mark with thread that owes nothing isn't mending.

KESSA (dialogue, the word landing like a stamp): That was a sealing.

PANEL 5 — Macro, the hour-knots (~13%)

Camera: Down at the counter edge: Kessa's huge hands tying her own knotted tally-thread around Ira's wrist, over the glove, knot by knot. Image: Small dense Kshudra knots, sixty of them, running round the wrist like a rope of minutes. The lamp catches every knot; none of them casts a shadow quite long enough.

KESSA (dialogue): Sixty knots. One for each minute of his hour. When the last one passes through my fingers, the hour is over. Council or no Council.

IRA (dialogue): And if he won't stop?

KESSA (dialogue, tying): My knots don't ask permission to end.

PANEL 6 — Medium, the price (~13%)

Camera: Level, both faces, the knotted wrist between them on the counter. Image: Kessa's hands have stopped working entirely — the rarest thing she does. The loupe is up. She is not appraising. She is asking.

KESSA (dialogue): My price. When this is over, you tell me one true thing about your mother.

IRA (dialogue): I don't know any true things about her.

KESSA (dialogue): Then you'll owe me one.

KESSA (dialogue, smaller): Your first debt, be.

PANEL 7 — HOOK — two hands, one knot held (~19%)

Camera: Extreme close on the two hands — Kessa's enormous scarred one closed over Ira's small knotted wrist, the last knot still between her fingers, untightened. Direct rhyme of Page 002 Panel 5's hand shot; same counter, same lamp, different weight. Image: Ira's mismatched eyes out of focus above; Kessa's cat eyes out of focus opposite. Neither blinks. The sixty-first minute of the page is this one.

KESSA (dialogue, small): I'll keep it safe till you can pay.

SFX: chrr… tap (the last knot seating)

CAPTION: The girl who owed nothing

CAPTION (final box, bottom): went to sleep owing one true thing.

Page notes for continuity

  • The descent rhymes the ascent. Page 005 Panel 1: no ash, raised hand, wrong sky. Page 007 Panel 1: ash resumes, hand lets it pile, sky becomes a shop window. Ira's body is the Sector map; keep drawing it that way every time she crosses the third landing.
  • "Sealing" is Kessa's word and now canon vocabulary. A mending reweaves and leaves a debt-shaped hole; a sealing closes a mark with thread that owes nothing and leaves no hole — which is why appraisal can't find it and why Kessa's loupe saw only clean skin and a tidy stitch on Page 002. Glossary updated. Do not let any later page call the stitch a mending.
  • Kessa's half-second look at the lockbox (Panel 4) is the page's planted seed. It pays on Page 010 (the shadowless swatch). She already owns something that owes nothing. Nobody in the room knows she knows; the reader only gets the eyeline.
  • The hour-knots are the chapter's clock. Sixty knots, Panel 5. Page 008 runs the hour; Page 009 counts it — and finds sixty-one. From now on every panel of the hour must be countable against knots if the reader pauses. Keep knot visibility consistent.
  • "My knots don't ask permission to end" is Kessa's knot-thread rule restated as threat: her knots cannot be cut by anyone, and they end when she says they end. This is the only leverage in the room against a Council officer, and it is a piece of string. That is exactly the series' thesis again: the basin's weapons are its tools.
  • The mother-debt is Ira's first debt and must never be cancelled. No Mend, no forgiveness, no clever escape: it can only be paid, with a true thing, in person, to Kessa. Track it in the glossary from this page forward.
  • The form stays folded, blank side up, all page. Ira never carries the written side against her body. Small rule, keep it.
  • Sewer locked, buyer unnamed, principal unseen. Kessa says sealing, not who.
