THREADBORN — Chapter 05 — Page 006

Chapter title: The First Pull / पहला खिंचाव Arc: I — The Unspooling Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail (afternoon) Page type: The Inspector — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 005 — the Inspector descends, the Office escalates.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the stall (~14%)

Afternoon, Knot & Nail: the Inspector at the counter. Broad, official, carrying a slate and a measuring-chain — not Rekhak's counting-chain, a compliance chain. The basin watches.

CAPTION: The Inspector measures. The Reckoner counts. Different tools. Same Office.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the inspection (~13%)

The Inspector's slate: grey columns, official. A request to measure Ira's mark — Kind, thread colour, strain, debt level. The Office wants a formal registration.

INSPECTOR (dialogue, official): The Office requires a formal mark measurement. Kind. Thread. Strain. Debt.

PANEL 3 — Close, Kessa's refusal (~14%)

Kessa's hands: she sets the Inspector's slate face-down — her grammar. Then she sets the coin on top, the same gesture as the Grey Clerk's visit. The counter-offer.

KESSA (dialogue): When the Office prices the measurement, I'll price the inspection.

PANEL 4 — Medium, the Inspector's response (~13%)

The Inspector does not leave. He stands at the counter, compliance chain in hand, and waits. Procedure has patience. The basin watches. The queue for mending holds.

CAPTION: The Inspector waits. Procedure has patience. The basin holds its breath.

PANEL 5 — Close, Ira's choice (~14%)

Ira's face: she looks at the Inspector, at Kessa, at her own palm. The mark is open. The thread is there. The Office wants to measure it. She can refuse (Kessa's grammar) or submit (procedure's grammar).

IRA (dialogue, flat): Measure.

KESSA (dialogue, sharp): Child—

IRA (dialogue): They'll measure eventually. Better here than up-terrace.

PANEL 6 — Wide, the measurement (~13%)

The Inspector's compliance chain on Ira's palm: the chain reads the mark. Kind: Manavkin. Thread: braided, ash-grey + blank. Strain: the chain hums — the braid's strain is higher than any single-strand Manavkin. Debt: zero. The chain's reading echoes through the basin.

CAPTION: Kind: Manavkin. Thread: braided. Strain: high. Debt: zero. The basin hears the reading.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the Inspector's face — HOOK (~19%)

The Inspector's face: he reads the chain's measurement and his expression changes. Not surprise. Not fear. Recognition. He has seen a braided mark before. He knows what it means.

INSPECTOR (dialogue, quiet, to himself): Braided. Projection. Zero debt.

INSPECTOR (dialogue, quieter): Like the Mendery records.
