THREADBORN — Chapter 05 — Page 003

अध्याय शीर्षक: The First Pull / पहला खिंचाव खंड: I — अनुकुलन क्षेत्र: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail (mid-morning) पृष्ठ प्रकार: The Reckoner arrives — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 002 — the thread is contained, the basin whispers, Rekhak descends.

चित्र-खाना 1 — Wide, the stall (~14%)

Mid-morning, Knot & Nail: Rekhak at the counter's threshold, chain running, oath-link dark. Ira sits with her palm open. Kessa stands behind, loupe down.

CAPTION: The Reckoner arrives. Not to audit. To witness.

चित्र-खाना 2 — Medium, Rekhak reads (~13%)

Rekhak's Sight-mark activates: the mark above his left eye glows, debt-marks spread under the eye. He reads Ira's palm — the open mark, the braided thread, the two strands. His chain hums.

रेखक (संवाद, soft): Two strands. One Manavkin. One blank.

चित्र-खाना 3 — Close, the Sight reading (~14%)

Through Rekhak's Sight: the mark's interior, the braid. One strand carries Ira's Sutra-signature (ash-grey, Agnikhand, projection). The other carries nothing — no Sector, no Kind, no age. The blank strand is older than Ira. Older than the cut-end. Older than the box.

रेखक (संवाद, quiet): खाली तंतु क्षेत्र से पुराना है। Older than the Council. Older than the Loom's current turn.

चित्र-खाना 4 — Medium, the cost (~13%)

Rekhak's debt-marks: the Sight reading costs him. New lines appear under his left eye, spreading toward his temple. Deep debt climbing toward terminal. He does not stop.

CAPTION: Each Sight-reading costs. He pays. He does not stop.

चित्र-खाना 5 — Close, the question (~14%)

Ira's face: she sees the debt-marks spreading and asks the question.

इरा (संवाद, flat): यह तुम्हें कितना पड़ता है?

रेखक (संवाद, soft): मेरे पास से ज़्यादा। इसकी कीमत से कम।

चित्र-खाना 6 — Wide, Kessa's hands (~13%)

Kessa's hands: she sets a cup of water on the counter for Rekhak — the first time she has offered him anything. The basin's two guardians across one counter: the appraiser and the Reckoner, both watching the girl with the open mark.

CAPTION: The first cup. The basin's oldest currency.

चित्र-खाना 7 — Macro, the thread — HOOK (~19%)

Ira's palm: the braided thread stirs. Not pulling — responding. The blank strand reaches toward Rekhak's chain, toward the oath-link, toward the echo of the four notes. The thread recognizes the chain's memory.

ध्वनि: —

CAPTION: The blank strand reaches for the chain. The thread that owes nothing recognizes the chain that carries the echo. And the echo is her mother's.
