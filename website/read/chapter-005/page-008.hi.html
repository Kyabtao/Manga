THREADBORN — Chapter 05 — Page 008

अध्याय शीर्षक: The First Pull / पहला खिंचाव खंड: I — अनुकुलन क्षेत्र: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail (morning) पृष्ठ प्रकार: The decision — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 007 — the Mendery connection, the thread-school and the Mendery.

चित्र-खाना 1 — Wide, the morning (~14%)

Morning, Knot & Nail: Ira arrives with the kit. Kessa is behind the counter, loupe up. The lockbox is open. Seven objects on the counter. The posting order's Mendery reference is visible.

शीर्षक (none spoken): The morning after the connection. The box is open. The reference is waiting.

चित्र-खाना 2 — Medium, the question (~13%)

Ira's face: the question she has held since Chapter 1. Not who sewed me — who was my mother in the Mendery, and what did she sew into me.

इरा (संवाद, flat): मेरी माँ मेंडरी में थी। The thread is braided. The Inspector knew.

IRA (dialogue): I need the Mendery file.

चित्र-खाना 3 — Close, Kessa's hands (~14%)

Kessa's hands: she closes the lockbox. Then she does something unexpected: she reaches under the counter and produces a key — not the lockbox's key, a different one. Older. Kshudra-made.

केसा (संवाद, dry): The Mendery keeps files. The Knot & Nail keeps keys.

KESSA (dialogue): This key opens the Mendery's back door. Your mother gave it to me.

चित्र-खाना 4 — Medium, the key (~13%)

The key in Kessa's hand: small, black, Kshudra-forged. Old. It has been under the counter for fifteen years — since the day Ira's mother was bound.

CAPTION: The key has been under the counter since the day Ira was sewn shut. Kessa has been waiting for the girl to ask.

चित्र-खाना 5 — Close, Ira's palm (~14%)

Ira's palm: the mark is open, the thread is held. The braided thread responds to the key's proximity — the blank strand reaches toward it, the way it reached toward Rekhak's chain.

ध्वनि: —

CAPTION: The thread recognizes the key. The way it recognized the chain. The way it recognized the box.

चित्र-खाना 6 — Wide, the basin (~13%)

The basin: the Knot & Nail works, the market adjusts. The stall-row trusts the lock. The filing queue is gone. The mending queue grows. The girl with the open mark mends without debt.

CAPTION: The basin's hope has a name now: the girl at the Knot & Nail.

चित्र-खाना 7 — Macro, the key in Ira's hand — HOOK (~19%)

Ira's hand: the key from Kessa. Her palm is open, the mark visible, the braided thread held. The key and the mark in the same frame: the two objects that will take her to the Mendery.

शीर्षक (none spoken): The hand has opened. The mark is awake. The thread is braided. The key is given.

शीर्षक (smaller): The girl who owes nothing is going to the place that binds debts. And the key that opens the back door has been waiting under the counter for fifteen years.

शीर्षक (last line): The first pull is over. The first journey begins.
