THREADBORN — Chapter 05 — Page 008

Chapter title: The First Pull / पहला खिंचाव Arc: I — The Unspooling Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail (morning) Page type: The decision — 7 panels, hook at bottom

Beats: Page 007 — the Mendery connection, the thread-school and the Mendery.

PANEL 1 — Wide, the morning (~14%)

Camera: Morning, Knot & Nail: Ira arrives with the kit. Kessa is behind the counter, loupe up. The lockbox is open. Seven objects on the counter. The posting order's Mendery reference is visible.

CAPTION (none spoken): The morning after the connection. The box is open. The reference is waiting.

PANEL 2 — Medium, the question (~13%)

Camera: Ira's face: the question she has held since Chapter 1. Not who sewed me — who was my mother in the Mendery, and what did she sew into me.

IRA (dialogue, flat): My mother was in the Mendery. The thread is braided. The Inspector knew.

IRA (dialogue): I need the Mendery file.

PANEL 3 — Close, Kessa's hands (~14%)

Camera: Kessa's hands: she closes the lockbox. Then she does something unexpected: she reaches under the counter and produces a key — not the lockbox's key, a different one. Older. Kshudra-made.

KESSA (dialogue, dry): The Mendery keeps files. The Knot & Nail keeps keys.

KESSA (dialogue): This key opens the Mendery's back door. Your mother gave it to me.

PANEL 4 — Medium, the key (~13%)

Camera: The key in Kessa's hand: small, black, Kshudra-forged. Old. It has been under the counter for fifteen years — since the day Ira's mother was bound.

CAPTION: The key has been under the counter since the day Ira was sewn shut. Kessa has been waiting for the girl to ask.

PANEL 5 — Close, Ira's palm (~14%)

Camera: Ira's palm: the mark is open, the thread is held. The braided thread responds to the key's proximity — the blank strand reaches toward it, the way it reached toward Rekhak's chain.

SFX: —

CAPTION: The thread recognizes the key. The way it recognized the chain. The way it recognized the box.

PANEL 6 — Wide, the basin (~13%)

Camera: The basin: the Knot & Nail works, the market adjusts. The stall-row trusts the lock. The filing queue is gone. The mending queue grows. The girl with the open mark mends without debt.

CAPTION: The basin's hope has a name now: the girl at the Knot & Nail.

PANEL 7 — Macro, the key in Ira's hand — HOOK (~19%)

Camera: Ira's hand: the key from Kessa. Her palm is open, the mark visible, the braided thread held. The key and the mark in the same frame: the two objects that will take her to the Mendery.

CAPTION (none spoken): The hand has opened. The mark is awake. The thread is braided. The key is given.

CAPTION (smaller): The girl who owes nothing is going to the place that binds debts. And the key that opens the back door has been waiting under the counter for fifteen years.

CAPTION (last line): The first pull is over. The first journey begins.

Writing & art notes

  • The key is the chapter's smallest object and its largest inheritance. Kessa kept it safe and hands it over without ceremony.
  • Thread memory: the mark recognises the key before Ira does. Give it one panel and no explanation.
