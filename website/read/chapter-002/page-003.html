Chapter 002 — Page 003

Chapter title: The Minute That Owes / वह मिनट जो ऋणी है Arc: I — The Unspooling Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin (pot-row, Council Stair mouth) Page type: Rumour census / Council fear enters the market — 7 panels, hook at the bottom

Beats carried from Page 001: the rumour register ("four hundred blinked"), the shut Knot & Nail (day three), the re-stamped Office. Beats carried from Page 002: Bhan's sleeve stayed down all week; Ira waits at the doorstep every day. Beats carried from Chapter 001 Page 003: the Council Stair is the market's early-warning system — light changes before officials do. Daytime variant here: the pot's steam bends toward the stair.

Registers kept separate: the list records who blinked — witnesses, never the event. Nobody on this page describes the silent second; the list has no column for it, and neither does the dialogue.

PANEL 1 — Wide (~14%)

Camera: Pot-row, morning, day three of the shutters. The steam pot centre-left; the stall-row running to the Council Stair mouth right. Image: The rumour has grown paperwork: a long paper list is nailed to the pot-post at eye height, and a queue of basin folk moves past it — each adds a mark in whatever they carry (charcoal, ink stub, fingernail, a knot in the cord at the list's foot). GUTHLI works the pot and the list at once, ladle in one hand, charcoal in the other. PIRA argues her way to the front. At frame left, on her crate before the shut Knot & Nail, IRA watches instead of waiting today. Background, stair mouth: BHAN hauling with his sleeve down in the heat — the mark that doesn't itch stays covered. The pot's steam bends, thin, toward the stair. Nobody has noticed yet.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): "Four hundred" lasted one day. Then it became a count. Counts want paper. Paper wants names.

PANEL 2 — Close (~13%)

Camera: On the list itself, slightly angled; hands entering from three sides. Image: The list in its many hands: neat ink names, charcoal names, one name scratched with a fingernail's ridge — and at the foot, three knots tied in the cord instead of signatures: Kshudra who don't write tie their blink. Guthli's charcoal hovers over a blank line. Pira's finger stabs at that line.

PIRA (dialogue, fast): Write me. I blinked. Whole half-second, both eyes, ask my sister—

GUTHLI (dialogue, flat, not looking up from the pot): Names only. The list doesn't carry stories.

PANEL 3 — Wide (~13%)

Camera: From the pot-row looking at the Council Stair mouth; the crowd between. Image: The market parts without being asked, a lane of nothing down its middle: a GREY CLERK descends — first Council fear ever to enter the basin market. Grey waistcoat, grey chit-book, a grey wax signet on his thumb-ring; he carries no guard and needs none. Ash falls on him and does not stay. At frame left Ira is standing — first time off the crate in three days. Behind her, at head height, the Knot & Nail shutter opens its finger-width slit. Once. No face in it.

CAPTION (none spoken): The Council Stair warns the market before officials do. Daylight has no lanterns to gutter. It has the pot's steam. The steam is bending toward the stair.

PANEL 4 — Medium (~14%)

Camera: At the pot, waist-up: Guthli behind pot and list, the Grey Clerk facing her across the ladle's reach. The queue has evaporated; Pira is suddenly elsewhere. Image: The purchase, procedural as breakfast: the clerk's grey fingers counting Office coin into Guthli's upturned steam-ladle; the list already rolled and tucked under his arm, nail-holes weeping from the pot-post. Guthli's charcoal hand hasn't moved. Behind them both, the shutter slit is closed.

GREY CLERK (dialogue, level, unhurried): The Office requires the count.

GUTHLI (dialogue, flat): It's names. Not a count.

GREY CLERK (dialogue, level): Names are a count.

PANEL 5 — Two-shot (~13%)

Camera: After: the clerk small in frame-right, climbing back with the lane closing behind him. Guthli and Ira at the pot, foreheads low. Image: Ira has crossed to the pot — three days off the crate, ten steps, and the market pretends not to log it. Guthli's ladle still holds the coin, unmoved, like a bowl of something dead. Ira's cut-finger glove touches one coin's edge, turns it: new-struck, last night's mint-shine under this morning's ash.

IRA (dialogue, quiet): How much?

GUTHLI (dialogue, quieter): More than the pot makes in a month.

IRA (dialogue, quietest, eyes on the stair): Then who counts a rumour in coin?

PANEL 6 — Medium (~13%)

Camera: Pot-row reacting: Pira centre, half the stall-row behind her; Guthli at frame edge not turning around. Image: Pira does the maths aloud and gets it wrong in the happy direction — arms wide, laundry under one arm, delight total. Behind her the stall-row catches it like weather. Guthli alone doesn't turn. Her charcoal hand finally moves: to the pot-post, where the nail-holes weep. The market's quiet changes key: before, it was a secret being kept. Now it is a secret being owned elsewhere.

PIRA (dialogue, loud, delighted): The Office bought the blink! We're rich, we're rich, the whole row's rich—

GUTHLI (dialogue, to the post, not loud, heard anyway): It's not ours anymore.

PANEL 7 — Macro (~17%) — HOOK

Camera: Mid-stair: the rolled list open across the Grey Clerk's knee — he has paused one step, which for a clerk is a full stop. Shallow focus; beyond the stair rail, far and soft: the shut Knot & Nail, and a small grey-robed figure standing ten steps from the pot. Image: The list's foot: the three Kshudra knots, the last charcoal name — and below them, in a fine formal ink no hand in the market owns, one name added and then crossed out in crimson. The crimson is dry — dry before this morning's coin was counted, dry before the list had a post to be nailed to. The clerk's grey thumb hovers over the cross-out and finds no line in procedure for it. He does not file it. He re-rolls, and his next step is faster than the one before.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): The name under the crimson was mine. I didn't blink. I don't have a thread to blink with.

CAPTION (none spoken, smaller): The principal's paperwork counted her among the witnesses anyway. Then corrected itself. Procedure does not hurry. Procedure re-rolls and quickens its step, which is worse.

Writing & art notes

  • The list never letters in art. No readable text anywhere on the page: names are strokes, scratches and knots; the crossed-out name is a crimson cross-out over one blank-feeling line. The reader learns whose name it was from caption only (same grammar as the never-lettered registration form in Chapter 001).
  • Rumour register only. The list, the queue, the dialogue and the coin all concern who blinked. No character describes the silent second; the truth register stays shut.
  • Council fear = grey, always. The clerk's wax, waistcoat, chit-book and coin-purse string are grey; the crimson in Panel 7 is the only warm seal-tone on the page and it is dry. Colour rule: crimson never shares a panel with grey wax touching it.
  • Kessa = shutters, day three. The slit opens once (Panel 3), closes by Panel 4. No face, no hand, no interior. Her opinion of the census is the closed slit during Panel 6's noise.
  • Chain-stop budget: unspent. Rekhak does not appear. No chain on panel.
  • The four notes stay silent. Nobody hums. The pot's steam makes no sound on this page.
  • Bhan's covered sleeve is background continuity from Page 002's hook; no dialogue, no beat, no character looks at it.
  • The Grey Clerk is an extra, not a cast member. He gets no name, no face close-up wider than Panel 4's waist-up, and no second scene this chapter. Council fear enters the market once here; it must not become routine.
  • New-struck coin is the page's forensic seed: the Office minted coin overnight to buy a rumour — fear spending faster than procedure. Ira notices; nobody explains.
  • The Loom never speaks. No hum change on panel.
