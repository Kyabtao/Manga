First named Ch. 002 Page 001; seated-or-leaning rule holds through Panels 1–7 until the final caption. IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST, basin thread)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md

Page 004 note: crate-side observer of the owned quiet (Panel 5) and of Bhan's double shift (Panel 6). Her captions carry the basin thread; her suspicion line — "I don't believe it for one second" — is the first time she distrusts a kindness, which retro-fits Page 002's bleed.

GUTHLI — गुठली (silent)

The census-keeper with nothing to keep: pot working for nobody, ladle hand idle — first idle hand in the series. She does not touch the list-post. Her silence is not grief this page; it is property law.

PIRA — पीरा (silent)

Present and not speaking, eyes down, passing the post without touching it. Loyalty to the sold list, not shame. The rumour register's loudest instrument, muted by ownership — measure every later Pira outburst against this panel's silence.

BHAN — भान (background, double shift)

Two sacks, sleeve down in the heat, gait unhurt. The itch-less mark's dividend. Nobody watches him; Ira's sightline does the watching for the reader.

KESSA — केसा (SHUTTERS ONLY, day four)

Day four breaks the pattern: the slit does not open at all (Panel 5) — a shutter refusing even its own slit is her comment on the owned quiet. No face, no hand, no interior.

REKHAK VAHNI — रेखक वह्नि (ABSENT)

Off-panel at the re-review until Page 005. No chain on this page; the Chapter 2 chain-stop budget remains unspent.
