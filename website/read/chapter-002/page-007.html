Chapter 002 — Page 007

Chapter title: The Minute That Owes / वह मिनट जो ऋणी है Arc: I — The Unspooling Sector: Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin (shut-stall doorstep, dock road, night shift) Page type: The commission — principal's paperwork reaches Ira's trade — 7 panels, hook at the bottom

Beats carried from Page 002: Bhan's re-tears and the itch-less mark; from Page 003: new-struck coin; from Page 006: the oath spent, Rekhak off-panel with his oath-link. Beats carried from Chapter 001: Patra the Preta broker (Pages 005–009, shadow falling toward the light, face and name that don't hold), Ira's mender terms (Page 006: nothing gets sewn till I've seen the tear), the registration form's three blank lines (Page 005).

Principal rules hold: the principal never appears; acts only through paperwork; Patra is the broker, in person, as in Chapter 001. Crimson on panel only as the unbroken seal; the prose-matching happens in caption, art stays letterless.

PANEL 1 — Wide (~14%)

Camera: Dusk, basin, day seven of the shutters: stall-row lamps guttering (the market's old warning habit firing for a broker now, not an official). Ira on her crate at the shut Knot & Nail; from the stair mouth, PATRA approaches — shadow falling toward the light as always, the face that doesn't hold, grey coat over something darker. Image: The owned quiet reacts: stall-row eyes go down, not up; Guthli's pot-lid comes on like a door closing. Patra walks the middle of the row the way the Grey Clerk did — a lane of nothing — but where the clerk's lane was fear, Patra's is credit: people owe him looks and don't spend them.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): The broker came back on the seventh day. Brokers don't hurry. They compound.

PANEL 2 — Medium (~13%)

Camera: Crate-distance: Patra facing Ira, both hands offering a single slip; the crimson seal unbroken on its fold. Ira seated, gloves on, kit closed on her knee — not rising. Image: The broker's courtesy is the menace: both hands, waist-deep bow, the seal toward her eyes first. Behind Patra, the shut shutter; behind Ira, the stair. Neither of them looks at either door.

PATRA (dialogue, warm as ledger-paper): A commission. At the principal's expense.

IRA (dialogue, flat): My terms.

PATRA (dialogue, bow deepening one degree): Known. Kept.

PANEL 3 — Close (~13%)

Camera: On the slip in Ira's cut-finger gloves, seal unbroken at frame top; her eyes at frame edge reading the opened prose under it (a broker's slip opens at the fold-line, never at the seal). Image: Paperwork prose in abstract strokes, but set like a ledger: line, line, line, margin. Her thumb rests exactly where a mender's thumb rests on a tear's edge — reading paper the way other people read skin.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): Prose, not poetry. Poetry hides. Prose bills. Whatever tore this mark wrote its description the way my kit lists thread: by weight, by hour, by habit.

PANEL 4 — Two-shot (~14%)

Camera: Side-on, both seated now — Patra perched on the crate's edge, the only seat the basin ever gave him; Ira square-on, kit still closed. Image: The terms, spoken once, kept forever: Ira's three conditions from Chapter 001 Page 006, re-stated in her mender's flat tone. Patra's smile is small and expects nothing else — that expectation is the page's second menace: the principal's paperwork already priced her terms in.

IRA (dialogue, flat, three beats): Nothing gets sewn till I've seen the tear. Names I don't need. The hour I do.

PATRA (dialogue, warm): The tear attends you. Dock road, third night-shift bell. Bring your own light.

PANEL 5 — Wide (~13%)

Camera: Dock road, third night-shift bell: rope-walk shadows, crate stacks, one lantern; BHAN waiting against a bollard, left sleeve rolled: the third tear, fresh, its fray-edge catching the lantern like a wet line. Image: Bhan's face is not patient tonight. It is tired — the specific tired of a man who has started doing arithmetic on his own body. His other hand holds a small coin-purse he doesn't open. Ira arrives at frame edge, kit open, lantern hooded.

BHAN (dialogue, tired, not looking at the tear): They doubled my wage today. Before I asked.

PANEL 6 — Macro (~14%)

Camera: Under her hooded lantern: the tear mid-mend, needle and filament working the fray — and the mender's-eye reveal, drawn as texture: the fray's inner edge is not frayed. It is cut. One clean stroke, nightly, the same angle, the same depth: a steady hand with a very small blade. Image: "Nothing touched it" was the wage's lie. Something touches it every night-shift with a surgeon's patience — and the fold in Ira's palm lends again, deeper than Page 002, the whorl-grain in this seam a shade darker than the last. She feels the lend and, this time, notices it. She does not yet stop it.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): Frays wander. Cuts don't. This tear was re-opened nightly by a hand steadier than my needle — and my needle is the steadiest thing the basin owns.

PANEL 7 — Macro (~17%) — HOOK

Camera: Her other hand, flat on the crate lid beside the working one: the commission slip open at the prose; the lantern's hood-shadow crossing it. Shallow focus; the mend's glow soft at frame edge. Image: The matching, line by line, in caption not lettering: same fray-weight, same night-shift hour, same "nothing touched it" clause — the commission prose and the tear on Bhan's arm are the same document, one written in ink and one in skin. And at the slip's foot, the payment line, smaller than the rest, in the same formal hand: one line of your registration form, restored. Existence, offered back one line at a time.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small, reading flat): Same fray. Same hour. Same "nothing touched it". The tear has a schedule, and the schedule has an owner on paper.

CAPTION (none spoken, smaller): And the owner pays in lines of a form the Council never let her finish. Every mend she sews from here buys back one stroke of her own name.

Writing & art notes

  • Principal rules hold: no principal on panel, ever; crimson appears only as the unbroken seal and the slip's fold-shadow; the prose is abstract strokes; the payment line lives in caption only (same grammar as the never-lettered registration form, Ch. 001 Page 005).
  • Patra's continuity: shadow falls toward the light in every panel he occupies; face and name don't hold (Ch. 001 Pages 005–009) — art keeps him slightly out of focus even in close shots; no face close-up wider than Chapter 001 established. He sits on the crate edge exactly once (Panel 4); a broker who sits is a broker who plans to return.
  • The cut-fray is the page's forensic turn: "nothing touched it" (Page 002/003 dialogue) is retconned in-world as the wage's lie, not the script's error — Bhan was told nothing touched it; the lantern says otherwise. Keep Bhan's belief intact until Page 008.
  • Bleed escalation: the fold's lend is deeper than Page 002 (whorl-grain a shade darker). Ira notices the lend here for the first time; she does not stop it — Page 002's kindness-memory still covers it.
  • Lamps gutter for Patra (Panel 1): the market's early-warning habit (Ch. 001 Page 003) now fires for brokers too — fear has learned new shapes; note it, don't explain it.
  • Chain budget closed: no chain stops anywhere; Rekhak is off-panel with his oath-link.
  • The four notes stay silent; the Loom never speaks; shutters day seven, no slit; Kessa absent; Lekh's cuff-chit sleeps; the Grey Clerk stays gone; census suppression continues off-panel.
  • Night palette: dock road is lantern-warm against rope-walk blue; the basin panels keep ember undertone; no ash-free panel on this page (the dock road has drifts).
