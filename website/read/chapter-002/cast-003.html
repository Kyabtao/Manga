IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार (PROTAGONIST)

Full sheet (Ch. 001): ../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md

Page 003 note: stands off the crate for the first time in three days (Panel 3), crosses to the pot (Panel 5). Her mender's eye does the page's forensics: new-struck coin = the Office minted money overnight to buy a rumour. She asks the page's only real question: "Then who counts a rumour in coin?" The hook caption is hers: the crossed-out name was hers.

GUTHLI — गुठली (promoted from background)

Steam-pot rumour-carrier (Ch. 002 Page 001). This page makes her the census-keeper: ladle in one hand, charcoal in the other. Sells the list because procedure asks in coin and she has no line for refusing grey wax. Her grief is the nail-holes.

Guthli / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted (light) / light debt /
keeps the blink census, sells it, mourns the nail-holes / "It's not ours anymore."

PIRA — पीरा

Doctrine-repeater (Ch. 002 Page 001). First to queue, first to demand entry ("Write me. I blinked."), first to celebrate the sale and get the maths wrong in the happy direction. The rumour register's loudest instrument; never malicious, only loud.

THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक (EXTRA — no name, no return this chapter)

First Council fear to enter the basin market. Grey waistcoat, grey chit-book, grey wax signet on the thumb-ring; no guard, no hurry, no face wider than a waist-up. Buys the blink census with new-struck Office coin; pauses one step on the stair at the crimson cross-out; re-rolls and quickens.

Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / pale grey (spent-adjacent) / mid debt /
procedure's foot in the market / "Names are a count."

Rule: an extra, not a cast member. Council fear enters the market once here; it must not become routine. No second scene this chapter.

KESSA — केसा (SHUTTERS ONLY, day three)

The finger-width slit opens once (Panel 3), closed by Panel 4. Her opinion of the census is the closed slit during Panel 6's noise. No face, no hand, no interior panel.

BHAN — भान (background)

Stair mouth, hauling, sleeve down in the heat — Page 002's hook worn as clothing. No dialogue, no beat, nobody looks at it.

Page 003 — nobody else

The queue is the chorus: ink names, charcoal names, one fingernail scratch, three Kshudra knots. They are witnesses to a blink, never to the second itself — the list has no column for the truth register and neither does this page.
