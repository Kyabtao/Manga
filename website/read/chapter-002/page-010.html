Chapter 002 — Page 010 — FINALE

Chapter title: The Minute That Owes / वह मिनट जो ऋणी है Arc: I — The Unspooling Sector: Agnikhand — basin (crate, reopened stall) → up-terrace (corridor, filing end), one panel each Page type: Chapter finale — refusal-sign, harvest receipt, shutters open, first letter — 7 panels

Beats carried from Page 007: the commission and its payment line; Page 008: the two-wax chit; Page 009: the unpick-grammar and Kessa's voice; Page 006: the oath-link relic. Beats carried from Chapter 001: Ira's mender terms and refusal grammar (Page 006), Kessa's shutters-as-alarm (Ch. 002 Page 001), the lockbox's shadowless cut-end (Ch. 001 Page 010).

Chapter budget closed: the chain-stop was spent on Page 006; the oath-link travels with Rekhak in Panel 4. The four notes stayed silent all chapter (payoff reserved for Chapter 3, matching wrongly first). The sewer box stayed locked; the cutter's name stays unrevealed until Chapter 3.

PANEL 1 — Wide (~14%)

Camera: Dusk, day ten: the crate at the shut stall for the last time this chapter; Patra arriving up the row's middle, lane of nothing, shadow toward the light. Ira standing this time, kit closed, the commission slip in her gloved hands. Image: The return, in her trade's own sign-language: the seal unbroken (she never accepted its price) but one ash-grey mender's stitch runs through the payment line, knot-off at the fold — sewn paper, the basin's oldest refusal-sign, older than the Council's wax. Patra's bow receives it before her hands do.

IRA (dialogue, flat): The prose I keep. The price I sew shut.

PATRA (dialogue, warm, both hands): Recorded as a mending declined. Recorded as a mending pending.

PANEL 2 — Macro (~13%)

Camera: The stitched slip in Patra's hands: the single grey stitch across the payment line in macro, knot-off exact, seal unbroken above; his thumbs either side of it, careful as a man holding a wasp. Image: His parting line arrives over it, warm as ledger-paper, and it is the harvest receipt the whole chapter has been accruing: every whorl-grain seam she has sewn so far — Bhan's three, the alley's one — already counted, already thanked for, already filed somewhere with her grain in it.

PATRA (dialogue, warm, leaving): The principal thanks you for the seams so far.

PANEL 3 — Medium close (~13%)

Camera: Ira alone after: the row's lamps guttering out one by one (the market standing the broker down); her eyes on her own glove, palm-side — the stitched side, the sealed side, the side the lend comes from. Image: No panic; ledger-face. Caption does her arithmetic: she refused the price and kept the prose, which means she still knows the schedule, the bells, the cut-angle — and the principal still owns every seam she has sewn since the first tear. Refusal is not refund. Menders know: a sewn tear stays sewn whoever paid for the thread.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): I sewed the price shut and kept the prose. Which means I know their bells and they own my grain. Both true. Both mine to carry now.

PANEL 4 — Medium (~14%) — up-terrace strand

Camera: Up-terrace corridor, morning: an Office runner handing Rekhak a small slate and already gone; Rekhak standing (he stands; it is not Lekh's rule), collar high, thumb finding the oath-link in the running chain as he reads. Image: The slate's single line, abstract strokes: Reckoner-of-record: Basin Market. Demotion in form — the basin is the Office's least ledger — and watch in fact: the man who falsified an audit to shield a threadless girl is now, procedurally, the girl's nearest official. The oath-link darkens one link of the brass at frame edge, travelling with him like a filed sentence.

CAPTION (none spoken): Procedure files its punishments as postings. The basin gets the Reckoner who lied for it; the Reckoner gets the basin he lied for. Neither was consulted. Both, procedurally, asked.

PANEL 5 — Close (~13%) — up-terrace strand

Camera: Filing end, same morning: Lekh seated at the rail (seated-or-leaving), cuff turned back one fold; macro on the cuff-chit: his smallest hand appending one line — a date-stroke and one abstract name-line, unlettered. Image: His off-book ledger grows its second entry: the date Ira Sutar's name first rode on crimson paperwork (the commission, Page 007). Entry one was the cross-out; entry two is the girl. Clerks never stand for anyone; he doesn't. He only keeps the only true record in the building, one cuff-line at a time.

CAPTION (none spoken): The Office's memory is drawers. The basin's memory is knots. Lekh's memory is a cuff, and it is the only one of the three that admits the crimson exists.

PANEL 6 — Medium (~13%)

Camera: Morning, day eleven: the Knot & Nail open: shutters up, counter wiped, loupe back on Kessa's brow; Ira arriving mid-row with the kit, stopping one step short. Image: No word about the ten days. Kessa's first word of the chapter's new chapter is business, which is her love-language: one syllable, hand out. Ira's kit is open before the syllable lands — she was already handing over thread. Caption states the alarm's true object, finally: it was never the rumour, never the census, never the broker. It was waiting for the girl's choice; the choice came at dusk on day ten; the shutters rose at dawn on day eleven.

KESSA (dialogue, one word, hand out): Thread.

CAPTION (Ira's voice, small): Ten days shut. She opened the morning after I sewed the price shut. Her alarm was never their noise. It was my answer.

PANEL 7 — Macro (~17%) — FINAL HOOK

Camera: The reopened counter, daylight: unwrapped and unexplained, a grey Council chit and a crimson slip folded together, the two waxes touching at the fold — the touching-waxes image returning from Page 008's chit, escalated from one arm to two institutions. Kessa's loupe-hand at frame edge, not touching it. Ira's gloved hand at the other edge, also not touching. Image: Neither seal broken; the fold creased once by a third hand that read it and re-folded it (Kessa's, last night, while the lock turned). Caption delivers the chapter's last sentence and Chapter 3's first premise: the Council and the principal have begun corresponding — and the first letter is about Ira.

CAPTION (none spoken): Two waxes on one chit was two owners on one arm. Two waxes on one letter is two owners on one girl.

CAPTION (none spoken, smaller, chapter's last line): The Council and the principal have begun corresponding. The first letter is about Ira Sutar. The second, Kessa already knows, will be about the hand that answers it.

Writing & art notes

  • The refusal-sign is stitch, not wax: sewn paper (Panel 1–2) is the basin's oldest refusal; keep the stitch ash-grey (her thread colour) and the knot-off exact — menders' signatures are knot-offs.
  • "Recorded as a mending pending" is Patra's menace-grammar: refusal does not close a file, it opens a queue. Do not let any later page treat the commission as dead.
  • Harvest receipt: "the principal thanks you for the seams so far" makes every whorl-grain seam sewn in Chapter 2 (alley + three doorstep/dock mends) already-harvested. Chapter 3 must treat the grain as collected data, not potential.
  • Rekhak's posting (Panel 4) is Chapter 3's geography: Reckoner-of-record for the basin market. The oath-link travels in every panel of his, series-long.
  • Lekh's cuff-chit entry two (Panel 5): date + unlettered name-line; art keeps it abstract. His ledger now tracks Ira; the stand (Page 004) still has not become an act.
  • Shutters open = Kessa's verdict on Ira's choice (Panel 6): never explain it aloud; the one-word dialogue and the already-open kit carry it. Loupe returns to her brow (it was up, alarmed, since Ch. 002 Page 001).
  • The folded letter (Panel 7): grey + crimson waxes touching, both unbroken, one re-fold crease (Kessa read it last night — which is what the lock-turning on Page 009 was consulting about). This image is the chapter's closing icon; Chapter 3 Page 001 opens on its contents.
  • Chain budget: closed and spent (Page 006). No stop on this page; the chain runs in Panel 4 with its dark link. Four notes: silent all chapter. Sewer box: locked; cut-end not on panel. Loom never speaks. Patra: no face close-up wider than Chapter 001 established.
