Usage notes

  • The re-stamp is the first time fear appears in the series as process rather than as a face. Keep it bureaucratic, quiet, and doubled: two seals, half a finger apart, same page.
  • Crimson wax may appear on paper again in Chapter 2, but never on a person, a door, or a gift. The principal acts through paperwork only (canon rule from chapter-001/chapter-summary.md).
  • Neither term is spoken aloud on Page 001. They are seen, not named — the naming happens in Page 002 dialogue at the earliest.

Chapter 003 — Glossary

New or sharpened terms in Chapter 3. Devanagari column is the in-world script of Agnikhand record-keeping.

TermDevanagariMeaning
Cut-endकट-सिराThe shadowless thread-segment in Kessa's lockbox: hand's-width, severed cleanly at both ends, turn-backs ancient and precise. Forty years in the box; first daylight consultation Ch. 003 Page 001. Same hand-school as Ira's stitch.
Hand-schoolहाथ-विद्याThe grammar of a mending hand: the angle of exit, the knot-off style, the turn-back at each extremity. A hand-school is inherited, not invented — the cutter taught Ira's stitch, not the reverse. Ch. 003 Page 001.
Turn-backमोड़-सिराThe tiny curl at the end of a stitch or cut-stroke where the thread (or blade-imitation) folds back on itself before ending. Identifies a hand-school the way a signature identifies a hand. First named Ch. 002 Page 009; comparison scene Ch. 003 Page 001.
Posting orderस्थानांतरण-आदेशCouncil procedure: a formal transfer of custody from one office or holder to another. The grey+crimson letter is formatted as a posting order — grey Council stamp, crimson co-signature at the foot — transferring the blink census to the Knot & Nail. Ch. 003 Page 001 Panel 7.
Custody transferहस्तांतरणProcedure's way of moving a dangerous record into a safer box: the Council and the principal agree on a location, and the holder (Kessa) is not asked. The posting order is the instrument. Ch. 003 Page 001.
Diplomaप्रमाण-पत्रIra's self-assessment, voice-over: the palm-stitch is proof that the cutter's hand-school touched her — she is the school's living certificate, not its student or its teacher. Ch. 003 Page 001 Panel 6.

Usage notes

  • The cut-end's shadowlessness is a property of the thread, not an intervention by the Loom. No glow, no aura — just the absence of a shadow where one should be. Let the art sell the impossibility before the story names it.
  • "Hand-school" is the canonical term for the stitch-grammar family going forward. Use it instead of "method" or "style" when the text needs the precise concept.
  • The posting order's two waxes (grey + crimson touching) are the same visual as the two-wax chit (Ch. 002 Page 008) and the folded letter (Ch. 002 Page 010), escalated from one arm to one girl to one lockbox. This icon belongs to the principal's correspondence; reuse it carefully.

No hand in itइसमें कोई हाथ नहींNandi's verdict on the mother's thread. She gets kind and year and no hand, because shadowless thread will not take an impression. This is why no list has ever held the mother's people — not cunning, not hiding, just material that will not hold a hand.

The twinजुड़वाँThe half-cut. A length of the mother's thread is measured against a living hand, cut in two: half stays in the owner's fist, half goes into the book. A word made portable — "your word is the half in your fist." Named and shown in full on Ch. 011 Page 007.
Nine and thirteenनौ और तेरहThe night's split. Nine of the twenty-two came in person and spoke their word; thirteen did not come — taught too well to enter a room with a book in it — and were entered anyway, at thread alone. For thirteen, entered at the owner's word is written about a word that was never asked for and never said.
False on its faceचेहरे पर झूठीThe entries' double state. The years are the mother's own count and are true; read as enrollment-years by the Office's roll they are impossible — the basin's roll is not old enough to hold them. Every entry is attackable at a glance and true anyway. The register "does not find truth. It makes it."
