## IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार  (PROTAGONIST)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md)

> **Page 001 note:** brings the charcoal rubbing of Bhan's turn-backs (Ch. 002 Page 009) to the
> reopened Knot & Nail. Stands across the counter from Kessa — first *standing at the counter*
> scene (Ch. 002's mend-pages had her seated on the crate). Reads the two turn-backs side by side,
> catches the school-match, and names it first: *"The cutter copied my stitch."* Reversed by Kessa:
> the cutter is the senior, not the student. Covers her palm-stitch with her hand — the wound that
> was made *for* her, not *against* her. Ends the page carrying the reversal: two strangers share
> a school, and she is the diploma.

## THE LETTER — पत्र  (grey + crimson, two owners)
The folded posting order from Chapter 002 Page 010, opened on-panel for the first time. Formatted
as a Council posting order with a crimson addition at the foot — the principal's co-signature. Transfers
custody of the **blink census** (the pot-row's list from Ch. 002 Page 003, with the crimson cross-out)
from the Reckoning Office to the Knot & Nail. Grey wax and crimson wax touching at the fold. The
re-fold crease (Kessa's thumb, last night) is visible alongside the original fold.

## THE LOCKBOX — ताला-बक्सा  (first daylight appearance)
Kessa's Kshudra-made basalt lockbox, forty years in her possession. Small, black, one keyhole, no key
visible. First on-panel appearance; first daylight consultation. The shadowless cut-end rests inside
on old grey felt. The box is open but the sewer's name stays locked.

## THE CUT-END — कट-सिरा  (shadowless thread, first clear view)
A hand's-width piece of shadowless thread, severed cleanly at both ends with ancient turn-backs.
Forty years older than Ira's stitch, same hand-school. Physically impossible: thread casts shadow,
this thread does not. First clear on-panel view in the series (referenced in Ch. 001 Page 010's
lockbox scene but never shown clearly).
