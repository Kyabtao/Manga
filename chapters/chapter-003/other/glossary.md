# Chapter 003 — Glossary

New or sharpened terms in Chapter 3. Devanagari column is the in-world script of Agnikhand
record-keeping.

| Term | Devanagari | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Cut-end** | कट-सिरा | The shadowless thread-segment in Kessa's lockbox: hand's-width, severed cleanly at both ends, turn-backs ancient and precise. Forty years in the box; first daylight consultation Ch. 003 Page 001. Same hand-school as Ira's stitch. |
| **Hand-school** | हाथ-विद्या | The grammar of a mending hand: the angle of exit, the knot-off style, the turn-back at each extremity. A hand-school is inherited, not invented — the cutter taught Ira's stitch, not the reverse. Ch. 003 Page 001. |
| **Turn-back** | मोड़-सिरा | The tiny curl at the end of a stitch or cut-stroke where the thread (or blade-imitation) folds back on itself before ending. Identifies a hand-school the way a signature identifies a hand. First named Ch. 002 Page 009; comparison scene Ch. 003 Page 001. |
| **Posting order** | स्थानांतरण-आदेश | Council procedure: a formal transfer of custody from one office or holder to another. The grey+crimson letter is formatted as a posting order — grey Council stamp, crimson co-signature at the foot — transferring the blink census to the Knot & Nail. Ch. 003 Page 001 Panel 7. |
| **Custody transfer** | हस्तांतरण | Procedure's way of moving a dangerous record into a safer box: the Council and the principal agree on a location, and the holder (Kessa) is not asked. The posting order is the instrument. Ch. 003 Page 001. |
| **Diploma** | प्रमाण-पत्र | Ira's self-assessment, voice-over: the palm-stitch is proof that the cutter's hand-school touched her — she is the school's living certificate, not its student or its teacher. Ch. 003 Page 001 Panel 6. |

## Usage notes
- The cut-end's shadowlessness is a *property of the thread*, not an intervention by the Loom. No
  glow, no aura — just the absence of a shadow where one should be. Let the art sell the
  impossibility before the story names it.
- "Hand-school" is the canonical term for the stitch-grammar family going forward. Use it instead of
  "method" or "style" when the text needs the precise concept.
- The posting order's two waxes (grey + crimson touching) are the same visual as the two-wax chit
  (Ch. 002 Page 008) and the folded letter (Ch. 002 Page 010), escalated from one arm to one
  girl to one lockbox. This icon belongs to the principal's correspondence; reuse it carefully.
