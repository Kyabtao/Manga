## IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार  (PROTAGONIST)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png)

```
Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Leave the lamp burning."
```
> **Page 010 note — the spending.** Opens the drawer, names the chapter's one chain-stop at last —
> the Loom's last knot, left as an unnamed pledge in the Freedom Year: *"It is the last thing my
> mother ever brought me."* Runs the Office's by-length call until the trap is undeniable, then does
> the mender's answer instead of refusing: puts in the piece that was missing. Ties the knot into the
> count-cord in front of the whole basin — one home, ever. Her only line on the page, and the
> chapter's last: *"Leave the lamp burning."*

## THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक  (procedural to the end)

> **Page 010 note:** runs the count as instructed, summons the purchased reader to verify, and when

## THE MOTHER — माँ  (never on panel, never speaks)
No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is a pledge: one visit in nine years, made in the
dark, and spent at dawn.

```
— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Give: leave the Loom's last word with the daughter who keeps the counter /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M." — the Freedom Year pledge was the visit. It is spent now, and the whole
basin knows, and she taught the whole basin.
```

---

**Page note:** the chapter's one chain-stop is spent. The Loom's last knot — pawned unnamed in the
Freedom Year, kept nine years, never read — goes into the count-cord in front of the whole basin, and
the requisition chain dies of universal fit: *kind: all, year: all, hand: all.* The Office writes
down its own defeat as procedure and postpones. The empty fold comes back to Kessa, all of it. At
dusk the school keeps its own lamp, with Nima teaching. Ira's only line: *"Leave the lamp burning."*
Smallest caption, the mother's method inherited: *"Nobody writes anything down."*
