**Page note:** the survey reaches the keeper's own step and asks what is on it; Ira uses clause two
without an owner and puts the first lie in her own register; the Office asks Nima to hold a hand and
she sets the tray down and refuses out loud in the alley, which the clerk writes down rather than
punishes; Ira asks Kessa what the cut-end was cut from and gets a delay instead of an answer. Hook:
in the open lockbox, the cut-end and the second knot lie side by side under the loupe — **same gauge,
same twist, same refusal to cast a shadow, and the same whorl at each end.** Two ends of one thread,
forty years apart. **The mother has spent forty years handing out the rest.**
