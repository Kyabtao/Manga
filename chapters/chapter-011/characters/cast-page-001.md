## THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक  (the survey)
No full sheet; Ch. 009–011. The Office's voice with a pen in it. Never unkind, never hurried, never
raises anything.

```
Grey Clerk / Manav / Agnikhand / grey, procedural / none — spends other people's /
Survey: name a place and every hand in it becomes an entry /
"Then bring the register and stand where I can see your hands."
```
> **Page 001 note:** states clause four aloud for the first time — *the licence lapses if the place of
> keeping fails to produce* — and answers Ira's "Produce what?" with "Whatever is in the register,
> keeper. You're holding it."

## THE COUNTING WOMEN — गिनती वाली स्त्रियाँ  (three, unnamed)

---

**Page note:** the Office's grey lamp burning at first light where the school's lantern hung; a queue
that is no longer only claimants; the survey from the fourth step to the water-butt with the keeper
obliged to attend; clause four spoken aloud; Nandi's condition turned outward; year one and the
founder's own knot — *Do not enrol me. — S.M.* — read into an Office book. Hook: the second knot is
tied in **shadowless thread**, the same thread as the cut-end in Kessa's lockbox and the stitch in
Ira's palm. **The mother is teaching in the open, with Ira's own thread.**
