## THE FIRST DECLARANT — प्रथम घोषणाकर्ता  (a counting woman, unnamed)
No full sheet; Ch. 011 Pages 001–004. One of Jadi's counting pupils — the one whose hand stopped
mid-count on Page 003. Brings her own knots in a fold of cloth to be entered under her own name.

```
First Declarant / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, self-tied / light, self-held /
Declare: enter your own hand at your own word, before anyone can claim it /
"Mine. I tied them."
```
> **Page 004 note:** not a villain and not a fool. She wants the thing clause two was built to give, and
> getting it is exactly what breaks it. The horror of the page is structural, not personal.

## NIMA — नीमा  (reader-second → entered)
Full sheet (Ch. 010): [`../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md`](../../chapter-010/characters/nima.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png`](../../chapter-010/characters/nima-ref.png)

```
Nima / Kshudra / Agnikhand / grey, now declared / none — that was the bargain /
Count: tally hands without reading them; cannot read a braid /
"Enter me. I'd rather be a line in your book than a pair of hands you can borrow."
```
> **Page 004 note:** **the cost lands.** Offered the choice — hold for the count and stay undeclared, or
> be entered as a hand — she chooses to be written down, which is the one thing her wage existed to
> prevent. She sets the tray down and leaves it down. Not played as triumph: a girl paying in the only
> currency she has.

**Page note:** the gap the clerk left in clause four is walked into — a counting woman declares her own
hands and clause two becomes public property; Nima is offered the choice and chooses to be **entered**
rather than lend her hands; and Ira measures the felt's impressions against the Roll of Hands. Hook:
**forty-one names, sixty-three lengths, twenty-two hands this basin has never written down** — taught in
the open, in daylight, by a woman who is on nobody's roll. *The Office is counting doors; my mother has
been counting something else for forty years, and I have been handing out needles to both of them.*
