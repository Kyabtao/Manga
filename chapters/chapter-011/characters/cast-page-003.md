## KESSA — केसा
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png)

```
Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"If you want to know what it was cut from, ask the woman they bound. All I ever kept was the day."
```
> **Page 003 note:** answers *when*, not *what* or *who*, and says out loud why she never asked — a
> pawnbroker who asks loses the thing she was told to keep. Fifteen years: the cloth, the thread and a
> blank note, all kept since the day Ira's mother was bound. Offers the tally-thread as the record of
> the date.

---

**Page note:** the survey closes at the water-butt — eleven doors, one morning, forty years counted;
the entries are read back and Ira says her own lie out loud without flinching; clause four is
discharged and the clerk leaves the gap standing. Kessa gives a date instead of an answer: fifteen
years, the day they bound her, a fold of cloth, a hand's width of thread, and a note that says nothing.
Hook: the grey felt in the lockbox keeps impressions — same width, same length, pressed in and lifted
out, more than there is light to count. **She has been cutting lengths off one piece for forty years,
and Kessa's box has been the measure the whole time.**
