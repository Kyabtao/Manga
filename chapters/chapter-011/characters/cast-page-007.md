Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "It does not find truth. It makes it."

> **Page 007 note:** enters all twenty-two — nine at their spoken word, thirteen at thread alone.
> Every entry is false on its face (teaching-years read as enrollment-years) and true anyway, which

## NANDI — नन्दी  (reader of the bigger book — reading the lie in, and then herself in)
Full sheet (Ch. 006): [`../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md`](../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png`](../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png)

```
Nandi / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, cut from one piece / none — she was never entered /
Read: identify any knot's kind, year and hand; cannot read a page; reads every entry ALOUD by
schedule / "How many more?" — and then she reads them.
```
> **Page 007 note — the cost is real time now.** She confirms kind and year on every length, repeats
> the shadowless verdict (*there is no hand in it*), pauses exactly once — *"How many more?" / 
> "Eleven."* — and then reads twenty-two face-false entries into the Office's roll knowing exactly
> what they are. At the end of the night she reads her own entry aloud: *"Entered at the owner's
> word"* — her word being four words long. After tonight the question is no longer whether she knew
> about Page 003; the question is what a person is who reads this in and keeps reading. Unresolved,
> on purpose.

---

**Page note:** the night's work — nine come and speak their word, thirteen stay away and are entered
at thread alone, and all twenty-two entries go into the roll with no name on any of them, their years
true and their meaning false on its face. Nandi reads every one aloud, including her own, and knows
exactly what she is reading. At dawn the grey clerk copies the impossible years because the Office
cannot disown its own rule, the list of addresses is already wrong — and somewhere a claimant is
asking what a name costs. Hook: *"Twenty-two hands entered. Not one name."*
