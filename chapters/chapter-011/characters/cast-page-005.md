---

**Page note:** the first false declaration, and the keeper refuses nothing. Clause two has **no line
for *no*** — Ira wrote it to protect an honest woman and cannot now refuse a dishonest man, because the
Office holds the copy. So she asks for a **reading**, and Nandi gives it: *kind, year, hand — not this
one's*, stopping short of the name she could have given. The Office ratifies its own rule against
itself. Nima pays the specific price of being enrolled — *you are the line; the line is what lets us
borrow you.* And the second school and the survey turn out to be the same shape: **doors with hands in
them, hands with no entry**, a sum anybody could do. Hook: **the survey reaches the end of the alley
tomorrow.**
