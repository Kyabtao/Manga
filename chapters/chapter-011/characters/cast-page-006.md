## NANDI — नन्दी  (reader of the bigger book — **and one of the twenty-two**)
Full sheet (Ch. 006): [`../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md`](../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png`](../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png)

```
Nandi / Manavkin / Agnikhand / grey, cut from one piece / none — she was never entered /
Read: identify any knot's kind, year and hand; cannot read a page; reads every entry ALOUD by
schedule / "That one is mine."
```
> **Page 006 note — REVEAL.** She reads the mother's fold of cloth and gets *kind: basin. Year:
> nineteen.* Then fails: **"There is a year in it. There is no hand in it."** The mother's shadowless
> thread will not hold a hand to be read — which is why no list has ever held the mother's people.
> Then, at the end of the page, she lays her own hand down beside one of the twenty-two lengths without
> being asked: **"That one is mine."**
>
> **Canon effect:** this retroactively explains the two times she declined to name a hand (Pages 003 and
> 005) without contradicting them, and it does **not** settle whether she knew the Page 003 entry was a
> lie — it makes both readings worse. She has been reading the Office's register aloud every morning
> while unenrolled herself, which means **the Office has been hearing her.** Her card line's debt
> tendency changes from *light, paid in kind* to **none — she was never entered.**

**Page note:** the survey closes at the water-butt and the twenty-two stop being a sum and become a
**list of addresses**, in the Office by tonight. Nima is told to hold and holds. Kessa prices the
reading and hands over the box anyway. **The reading fails** — Nandi gets kind and year out of the
mother's thread and no hand, because shadowless thread will not take an impression. So Ira finds the
instrument that would work (measure, don't read) and the clock that kills it (twenty-two people, one
night), and discovers the chain-stop she has carried since the loom stopped speaking **stops a chain,
not a list**. Then Nandi lays her hand down beside one of the twenty-two lengths and says four words.
Hook: *"One of them has been standing at my counter every morning this week reading my register aloud
into an Office book. And she has never once been entered."*
