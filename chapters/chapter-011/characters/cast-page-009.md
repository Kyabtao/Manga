## IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार  (PROTAGONIST)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png)

```
Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "I put the lamp where it always stands, and I open the drawer."
```
> **Page 009 note:** takes the fourth step — nine years of counter-design broken by her own foot, one
> step down, lamp out, into the open where her mother taught. Says almost nothing all page; carries the
> light, sets it on a crate she will never know was her mother's. Returns alone to the counter at the
> end, and the last panel is her hand on the drawer that holds the only thing in the stall that stops
> a chain — never spent, never named.

## THE MOTHER — माँ  (never on panel, never speaks)
No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is an upturned crate she may or may not have
stood on.

```
— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Teach: in the open, in the street, writing nothing down /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M." — the alley is not a room. It never was. That is what she taught in it.
```

---

**Page note:** the keeper steps down — the chapter title, on-panel, as geography. The reading happens
in the open by lamplight and the basin gathers to hear it: a secret read aloud stops being a handle.
Mid-reading, the requisition arrives and is read over the reading — *the roll will be called* — and
the off-schedule objection dies on the Office's own precedent, refereed by the purchased reader. Both
things stand. And the last realization drops: thread answers thread, so the roll-call does not need
names — it has the other halves. Hook: *"I put the lamp where it always stands, and I open the
drawer."*
