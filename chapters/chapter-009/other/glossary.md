# Chapter 009 — Glossary


| Term | Devanagari | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Provisional Ordinance forty-one** | अनंतिम अध्यादेश इकतालीस | *Unreadable Hands.* Three clauses, effective from the third bell of the previous night. |
| **Unreadable** | अपठनीय | A chain's return for a braided palm: both years at once, neither readable. Now a legal category. |
| **Place of keeping** | रखने का स्थान | Where the Register of Unreadable Hands is kept — the Mendery, slot four, because the stall answers for itself. |
| **The Register of Unreadable Hands** | अपठनीय हाथों की पंजी | Three-sealed case; forty-one ruled lines; entry one stands **RESERVED**. |
| **Keeper** | अभिरक्षक | Ira, by the licence's provisional entry. Marks the boxes, reads every entry aloud. |
| **Reader (of the bigger book)** | पाठक | Nandi, entered at her own word. Reads with the keeper, in the same words, at the same time. |
| **Reader-second** | प्रशिक्षु पाठक | **Nima**, ninth terrace, Kshudra, enrolled at nine. Named on Ch. 009 Page 004, seated on Ch. 010. |
| **Held stop** | स्थिर रोक | A chain motionless from the first word to the last. A false word under it is a **deposit**. |
| **Deposit** | जमा | The true version of a false sworn word, written in the owner's own hand into his own ledger. |
| **Party of record** | अभिलेख-पक्ष | Ch. 010: whoever produces a hand or a place is entered as the party of record for it. |
| **Declarant** | घोषक | Any person who knows a hand may declare it; no liability attaches. |
| **Tally-thread** | बही-डोर | Kessa's strand of grouped knots — the crease-writs, the reply, the objection. The only copy that cannot be audited. |
| **Book of the Hand** | हाथ की किताब | The school's method book; its last third, one knot per pupil. The knots are returned to their owners on Ch. 009 Page 009. |
| **Attestation** | प्रमाणन | The keeper's written line on the certification sheet: seats with their owners, no name entered. |
