# Chapter 009 — Locations


## THE COUNCIL STAIR (BOARD) — परिषद-सीढ़ी (सूचना-पट्ट)
Forty-one steps. The ordinance posted at first bell, a second sheet wired under it by the end of the day.

## THE MENDERY CELLAR — मेंडरी का तहख़ाना
Salt jars, cold racks, one Office-made desk shimmed with a folded ordinance. The register stands open on it; the Book of the Hand stays on its shelf four feet away.

## THE MENDERY COUNTER — मेंडरी का काउंटर
Where the queue declares itself. The keeper writes knots; Kessa keeps the stall's ordinary ledger with slips and coins.

## THE HATCH ALLEY — खिड़की-गली
Forty years of the school's only address: a shin-high hatch in the back door, worn shiny at the edges. Posted by the Office with a grey lamp on a pole at the end of Ch. 010.

## THE RECKONING OFFICE — लेखा-कार्यालय
Vestibule with benches, chits and a slate; room nine with the portable chain on its stand and light on the subject's chair; and, up-terrace, the translation room — one clerk, one high desk, one bigger book.

## THE COUNCIL STAIR / ROOM NINE (CH. 010) — कमरा नौ
Nine feet by eleven. High window, subject faces the light, reader sits with his back to the dark. The reserve/certification slate stays on the table; one line is left in chalk.
