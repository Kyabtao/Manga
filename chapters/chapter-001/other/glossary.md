# Chapter 001 — Glossary

Terms the reader must understand by the end of Page 1. English first, Devanagari after.

| Term | Devanagari | Meaning (as the reader should learn it) |
|---|---|---|
| **Rangbhoomi** | रंगभूमि | The world. "The Stage." It is a weave, not a planet. |
| **The Loom / Sutra** | सूत्र | The weave of reality. Visible in the sky. Does not speak. |
| **Sutra-mark** | सूत्रचिह्न | The mark on every body that opens at fifteen. |
| **Unspooling** | अनुकुलन | The public ceremony at fifteen when a thread is drawn. |
| **Thread** | सूत्र / रेशा | A person's power. It is a **loan**, never owned. |
| **Strain** | तनाव | How taut the drawn thread is = how strong the child is ranked. |
| **Debt** | ऋण | What you owe for every pull of power. Written on the skin as dark lines. |
| **Debt-mark** | ऋण-चिह्न | The dark line on skin. **Dark line = debt. Red = a wound.** Teach this early. |
| **Fraying** | रेशा-भंग | Coming apart when debt goes terminal. Becomes an Asura. |
| **Mending** | सांधन | Reweaving torn thread. Creates no debt. Rare, licensed, illegal in six Sectors. |
| **Manavkin** | मानवकिण | Humankin — braided, mixed-blood, no homeland. |
| **Ashfall Basin** | भस्म-गर्त | The poor low terraces of Agnikhand. |
| **The Spindle** | तकुआ | The Loom's anchor; site of the Unspooling. |
| **Under-Market** | अधो-बाज़ार | The basin's black market; where the Court's rejects are re-valued. |
| **The Knot & Nail** | गाँठ और कील | Kessa's pawn stall; Ira's bank. |
| **Null / Unranked** | शून्य | A child who drew no thread. An insult in the Court; a price to Kessa. |
| **Dry** | सूखा | (card-game keyword) owing no debt; immune to debt-based effects. Ira's signature. |
| **Debt-appraiser** | ऋण-परखक | One who reads debt-marks by eye. Kessa's craft. |
| **be** | बे | Affectionate Kshudra particle. Kessa's tell for real feeling. |

> **Teaching order on Page 1:** Loom → Unspooling → thread & strain (Panel 5) → debt (implied by
> Panel 3's torn mark) → the absence of a thread (Panel 6) → the seam (Panel 7). Do not explain all
> terms in captions; let them be *demonstrated* before they are *named*.
