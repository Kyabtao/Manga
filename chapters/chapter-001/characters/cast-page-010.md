## THE LOOM (Panels 1, 7 — presence, not character)
Turns overhead exactly as on Page 001. Does not speak. Does not notice. Its hum ends the chapter **one
note lower than last night**, and no one in-world hears it. Hard canon holds: the Loom is a structure,
never a voice.

## THE SHADOWLESS SWATCH (Panels 4–7, an object with a card line)
A short cut end of **unpulled thread**, kept by Kessa from before the first Unspooling she counted.
Catches no lamplight; casts no shadow in ordinary light. Its far end carries **the knot-shape** — third
appearance (vision whorl, Page 008 → fist-whorl, Page 009 → swatch knot, Page 010).

```
Severed End / — / — / unpulled, colourless / owes nothing /
casts no shadow in any light; cannot be pulled, owed or Frayed /
"Never once asked what it was cut from."
```
> **Do not give the knot-shape an owner on panel.** Not this arc, not this year of arcs.

## Page 010 — nobody else

Two named characters, one object, one structure. A finale is a held chord, not a crowd: no Patra, no
Rekhak, no market. Their absence is what makes the last page quiet enough to hear the hum change.
