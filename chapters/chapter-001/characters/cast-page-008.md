## THE BASIN (Panel 6, the silent second)
Every threaded soul on every rooftop, hands to their marks, looking up at a Spindle that flickered for
the first time in four hundred years. **No faces close enough to name.** The crowd gets one wide shot
and the absence of sound; that is its line.

## Page 008 — nobody else

Four named characters and a city holding its breath. The hour is a sealed room inside a sealed minute:
no clerks, no market, no fourth chair filled. The empty chair is the principal's and stays empty.
