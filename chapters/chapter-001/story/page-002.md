# THREADBORN — Chapter 01 — Page 002

**Chapter title:** *The Girl With No Thread*
**Arc:** I — The Unspooling
**Sector:** Agnikhand (Ash Sector) — Unspooling Court → Ashfall Basin Under-Market
**Page type:** Character / thesis — 7 panels, ends on a question
**Canvas:** vertical webtoon strip, full colour

> Beats carried from Page 001: the stitched palm (§hook), Ira's zero debt, the Court's indifference.
> This page **teaches the thesis**: *no debt is not a defect — it is a price.*

---

## PANEL 1 — Medium (~13%)

**Camera:** Over Ira's shoulder at a registration desk. The Official does not look up.
**Image:** A slate stamped by a heavy Court seal. The stamp reads (on the slate only, not lettered in
art) a red mark of rejection. Ira's hand rests beside it, small. Ash drifts through the window light.

> **OFFICIAL (dialogue, flat):**
> Ira Sutar. Unspooled. Thread: none. Rank: none.
>
> **OFFICIAL (dialogue, already turning):**
> Next.
>
> **SFX:** *THUNK* (seal)

---

## PANEL 2 — Wide, quiet (~12%)

**Camera:** Side-on. Ira walks **against** the current.
**Image:** The processional street again, but reversed: hundreds of newly-threaded children stream
*uphill toward the Spindle*, glowing faintly, while Ira alone walks *downhill*, unlit, holding her
stamped slip. Ash falls between the two flows.

> **CAPTION:**
> Four thousand children drew thread today.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> One drew nothing.

---

## PANEL 3 — Medium wide (~13%)

**Camera:** Ira descending a worn stair into a cut in the basin wall.
**Image:** The **Under-Market**: a tight lantern-lit alley of stalls under the terraces — pawned rings,
thread-spools, debt-ledgers on string, steam from a food pot. Crowded, warm, crooked. A bell jingles
as Ira ducks in.

> **CAPTION:**
> There is one person in this city who appraises what the Court throws away.
>
> **SFX:** *jingle*

---

## PANEL 4 — Medium (~13%)

**Camera:** Behind the counter, up at Kessa (she is short; the counter is tall — shot low to give her
weight anyway).
**Image:** **KESSA**, an old Kshudra woman — small, broad, huge scarred hands, large light-catching
cat eyes, a jeweller's loupe pushed up on her brow. Her stall, **The Knot & Nail**, is cluttered to the
rafters. She counts a string of knotted thread without looking at it.

> **KESSA (dialogue, dry):**
> Mender girl. You walk like a debt writ.
>
> **IRA (dialogue):**
> I drew nothing, Kessa.

---

## PANEL 5 — Medium close (~13%)

**Camera:** On the counter, two hands.
**Image:** Ira lays her needle-roll on the counter to pawn it. Kessa's big hand closes over it and
pushes it back, then takes Ira's wrist and turns the palm up. With the other hand she pulls the loupe
down over her cat eye.

> **KESSA (dialogue):**
> Keep your needles. They're the only honest tools in this basin.
>
> **KESSA (dialogue):**
> Give me the hand.
>
> **SFX:** *tick* (loupe flips down)

---

## PANEL 6 — Macro through the loupe (~16%)

**Camera:** The inside of the lens. Ira's palm fills the round field.
**Image:** Under magnification: **no debt-marks anywhere** — skin clean as river stone — and crossing
it, the old neat **stitch**. Kessa's enormous cat eye, unblinking, at the edge of the lens.

> **KESSA (small, slow):**
> No marks. Not one. Fifteen years and you have never pulled, never owed.
>
> **IRA (small):**
> That's why I'm null.

---

## PANEL 7 — HOOK — tight two-shot (~20%)

**Camera:** Kessa looks up over the loupe, dead still. Ira holds her gaze. The stall noise drops away.
**Image:** Kessa's face — the dry humour gone, replaced by appraisal and, underneath it, **fear**. The
loupe still over one eye, so one eye is huge and one is small: she is literally seeing Ira two ways.

> **KESSA (dialogue):**
> *Null?* Child, every soul in this basin is drowning in debt.
>
> **KESSA (dialogue, leaning in):**
> You're the only dry thing in the flood. That is not a defect. That is a **price**.
>
> **KESSA (smaller, eyes dropping to the stitch):**
> …and somebody *stitched* you. Who sewed you, child?
>
> **IRA (smallest, at the edge):**
> I don't know. I was a baby.

## Page notes for continuity

- **The thesis lands here.** "No debt isn't a defect, it's a price" is the line the whole series will
  keep paying interest on. Do not dilute it with extra dialogue.
- Kessa recognises **two separate things**: (a) zero debt — which she calls a *price*; (b) the stitch —
  which she reacts to with *fear*. Keep the two reactions distinct. She knows what a stitch is.
- Ira pawning her needles shows she is **ashamed and practical at once** — she came to sell, not to
  confess. Kessa refusing the needles is the first kindness Ira has been shown. Let it sit.
- The Under-Market is warm and crowded — the opposite of the Court's cold white desk. Status by
  temperature, not altitude, this page.
- **Do not** answer "who sewed you." Kessa will not say this page either. It stays a locked box.
