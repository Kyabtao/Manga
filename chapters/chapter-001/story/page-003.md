# THREADBORN — Chapter 01 — Page 003

**Chapter title:** *The Girl With No Thread*
**Arc:** I — The Unspooling
**Sector:** Agnikhand (Ash Sector) — Under-Market, **The Knot & Nail**
**Page type:** Reveal + intrusion — 7 panels, ends on a **name**
**Canvas:** vertical webtoon strip, full colour

> Beats carried from Page 002: Kessa's question (*"Who sewed you, child?"*), the thesis
> (*no debt is not a defect, it is a price*), and the locked box.
> This page answers **what a stitch is** — and never **who sewed it**. Then it breaks Chapter 1's
> rule of indifference: for the first time, somebody comes *looking* for the dry girl.

---

## PANEL 1 — Medium two-shot (~13%)

**Camera:** Level with the counter, both faces in frame. Kessa's huge hands still around Ira's wrist.
**Image:** Kessa flips the loupe **up** onto her brow with her thumb — she has stopped appraising. The
stall is unnaturally quiet: behind them two Kshudra customers have turned their backs on purpose,
deliberately not listening. Ash drifts through the lantern light.

> **IRA (dialogue):**
> Who sewed me, Kessa?
>
> **KESSA (dialogue, flat):**
> I know what a stitch *is*. I don't know whose hand it was.
>
> **KESSA (dialogue, smaller):**
> Don't ask again inside this stall. The walls here have debt too.

---

## PANEL 2 — Teaching panel: macro + diagram (~15%)

**Camera:** Straight down onto the counter-top, where Kessa's thumbnail is drawing in spilled ash.
**Image:** Two diagrams side by side in the ash. **Left:** a small closed circle with lines radiating
outward — a sutra-mark opening, thread drawn. **Right:** the same circle with a bar of neat stitches
across it — closed. Kessa's finger points at the right one. In the far corner, soft and out of focus,
a **memory with no identity in it**: two gloved hands holding a baby's palm and a curved needle.
**No face. No uniform. No insignia. No Mendery cuff.**

> **KESSA (dialogue):**
> A sutra-mark is a door. At fifteen it opens, and the Loom lends you thread through it.
>
> **KESSA (dialogue):**
> A stitch is a door nailed shut **before** it could open.
>
> **KESSA (dialogue, eyes on the corner of the panel):**
> That is not an accident. Accidents are ugly. This is *tidy*.

## PANEL 3 — Close on Ira (~12%)

**Camera:** Tight on Ira's face. Her own stitched palm is raised in the foreground, out of focus.
**Image:** Not fear — **recalculation**. Both mismatched eyes (left amber, right grey-green) are on her
own hand. Her mouth is slightly open. She has just learned that her emptiness was *done to her*.

> **IRA (dialogue):**
> So my thread isn't missing.
>
> **KESSA (off-panel, small):**
> Hidden.
>
> **IRA (dialogue):**
> Hidden from who?
>
> **KESSA (off-panel):**
> The Court. The Loom. Or you. I don't know which — and I won't guess out loud.

## PANEL 4 — Wide, the temperature drops (~13%)

**Camera:** From inside the stall, looking down the Under-Market alley toward the stair mouth.
**Image:** The market has gone silent. The hanging lanterns gutter and dim **one after another toward
the stairs** — a wave of cold walking in. In the stair mouth, backlit by grey daylight and falling ash,
stands a **tall thin figure in an Ash Council greatcoat**, a ledger-slate hanging at his hip on a cord,
a chain of knotted brass rings in one hand. Every face in the alley is turned away from him.

> **CAPTION:**
> The Under-Market is warm. That is the entire point of it.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> When it goes cold, somebody official has come down the stairs.
>
> **SFX:** *hush* (the whole market's noise pulled in on one breath)

## PANEL 5 — Medium, the Reckoner reads the room (~14%)

**Camera:** Low, from behind the counter. He is not looking at Kessa — he is reading the crowd.
**Image:** **REKHAK VAHNI**, Debt-Reckoner of the Ash Council — tall, thin, ash-grey greatcoat over a
high collar, pale eyes, a fine dark **Sight-mark** above his left brow, and thin debt-lines climbing
his neck to the jaw (**Deep debt**). He lets the brass counting-chain run through his fingers. Around
him three market customers flinch as invisible arithmetic lands on them. Full sheet:
`characters/rekhak-vahni.md`.

> **REKHAK (dialogue, reading aloud, polite):**
> Two stalls down: heavy, four years old, Jalkhand law. You bought a drowning you couldn't pay for.
>
> **REKHAK (dialogue, to a Kshudra):**
> Knot-thread. Light. Irrelevant.
>
> **REKHAK (dialogue, still not looking at anyone):**
> I'm not here for any of you. I'm here for a **hole**.

## PANEL 6 — Tight, on the counter (~14%)

**Camera:** Down at the ledger-slate as he lays it on Kessa's counter.
**Image:** The slate shows the Court's own record — a stamped page. One facing page is folded and
sealed with **crimson wax**, a colour that does not belong anywhere in Agnikhand. *Do not letter the
seal.* Kessa's big hand slides a small **knotted pawn-token** across the counter: a bribe, offered
without a single word. Rekhak does not look at it.

> **KESSA (dialogue, even):**
> This basin is four thousand holes, Reckoner. Pick one and go back up-terrace.
>
> **REKHAK (dialogue):**
> The Court recorded an Unspooling the Council never registered. Manavkin aren't registered.
>
> **REKHAK (dialogue):**
> Two ledgers disagree about you. I'm the man who finds out which one is lying.

## PANEL 7 — HOOK — tight three-shot, the name (~19%)

**Camera:** Over Rekhak's shoulder at Kessa and Ira. Kessa's hand has closed over Ira's stitched palm,
hiding it. Rekhak's pale eyes are on the **hand**, not the face.
**Image:** He leans a fraction closer. The debt-lines on his neck tighten, and a hairline crack of dark
marks spreads under his Sight-mark as he **spends** it (canon: power used = marks on-panel). Ira's
mismatched eyes do not drop. Kessa is very, very still.

> **REKHAK (dialogue):**
> Somebody up-terrace wants to meet the child who owes nothing. The Council is only the errand.
>
> **REKHAK (dialogue, soft, exact — and for the first time in this chapter, *interested*):**
> **Ira Sutar.**
>
> **CAPTION:**
> In fifteen years, this city had never wanted Ira for anything.
>
> **CAPTION (final box, bottom):**
> That ended today.
>
> **SFX:** (very small, in his throat, *under* the words) *hmmmm* — the Loom, audible through a
> Deep-debt voice.

## Writing & art notes

- **The box stays locked.** Kessa explains the *what* of a stitch, never the *who*. The Panel 2 memory
  must contain no face, no uniform, no insignia and no Mendery cuff. If the artist draws an identifying
  detail, the panel is wrong.
- **Rekhak is not cruel. He is interested**, which is worse. Chapter 1's rule (Page 001 notes: nobody
  is kind, nobody is cruel, the city is indifferent) is *deliberately* cracked here — he is the first
  person in the series who wants something from Ira. Do not let him sneer, threaten, or shout.
- **Deep debt canon** (`series-bible/02-power-system.md` §4): marks reaching the throat fray the voice,
  and others can hear the Loom through it. That is the small *hmmmm* under his dialogue — the same SFX
  as the Loom on Page 001. Reuse it; it is his audio signature.
- **Sight-mark** = mark over the eye = power put into *knowing* (`02-power-system.md` §2). He spends it
  in Panel 7, so new marks must appear in-panel. No exceptions.
- **The crimson wax seal** is the first hint of an off-Sector buyer (Raktakhand / the Crimson Veil).
  **Do not name it.** Not this chapter, not this arc. It is a colour, and colours are cheap to plant.
- **The two-ledger contradiction is intentional canon:** the Ash Council refuses to register Manavkin
  (`03-the-nine-sectors.md`), yet the Unspooling Court recorded her. That gap is Chapter 1's engine —
  a girl who officially does not exist but is on a public record.
- **Kessa's bribe is knotted thread**, Rekhak's counting is **brass rings**. The basin counts with
  thread; the Council counts with metal. Keep that contrast in every panel both are in.
- **Ira does not speak in Panel 7.** Her silence is the beat. Her first words to an official come on
  Page 004, and they should land harder for the wait.
- Kessa covering Ira's palm with her own hand is the second kindness she has shown her (the first was
  refusing the needles, Page 002 Panel 5). She still hasn't said a kind word. Keep it that way.
