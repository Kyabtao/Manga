# THREADBORN — Chapter 01 — Page 010  ·  **CHAPTER FINALE**

**Chapter title:** *The Girl With No Thread*
**Arc:** I — The Unspooling
**Sector:** Agnikhand — Ira's roof (night) → the Knot & Nail (late)
**Page type:** Finale / motif return — 7 panels, ends on an end card
**Canvas:** vertical webtoon strip, full colour

> Beats carried from the whole chapter: the Page 001 captions, the stitch, the fold, the silent second,
> the sixty-first knot, Kessa's eyeline to the lockbox (Page 007). This page closes Chapter 1 by giving
> Ira back her own hand — and by showing the reader that Kessa has been keeping a door of her own.

---

## PANEL 1 — Wide, Ira's roof (~14%)

**Camera:** From the parapet, looking across the flat tenement roof.
**Image:** **Ira's roof** (new location): ash drifts banked against the parapet like snow, a line of
drying mending-rags, one crate for sitting. Above it all the **Loom** turns — the continent-sized
lattice of pale light, exactly as on Page 001, Panel 1. Ira sits with her knees up, her stitched right
palm laid open on her knee, looking at it the way you look at a stranger who has just saved your life.

> **CAPTION:**
> Everyone is born owing the Loom a thread.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> Ira Sutar was born with hers already taken.

---

## PANEL 2 — Medium close, the flex (~13%)

**Camera:** On her hand as she flexes it: fingers open, close, open. Nothing happens. No light, no
thread, no colour. Outside, she is exactly as null as the Court recorded her.
**Image:** Ash settling on her knuckles. The stitch catches no particular light. The city glows under
her, indifferent as ever, and for the first time in the chapter the indifference reads as *room*.

> **IRA (thought, italic):**
> Nothing comes out.
>
> **IRA (thought, italic):**
> Nothing is missing. It's folded.
>
> **IRA (thought, italic):**
> Not empty. Packed.

---

## PANEL 3 — Close, the cover (~13%)

**Camera:** Tight on her face as she lifts the closed hand — the fist she has just folded over the
stitch — up to her mouth. The vow is whispered into her own knuckles.
**Image:** Her mismatched eyes above the covering hand, dry and steady; the stitched palm hidden inside
the fist against her lips, as rendered in `images/page-010.png`. Not a fist of anger: a hand over a
lamp. One flake of ash lands on the covering hand and stays.

> **IRA (dialogue, one word, the smallest vow in the series):**
> Sleep.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> That was the first lie anyone told about her.

---

## PANEL 4 — Medium, the lockbox opens (~14%)

**Camera:** Inside the Knot & Nail, late. Shutters down, lamp turned low. Kessa alone behind the
counter, the brass-banded **lockbox** open on her knees, its lid lined with pawn-tickets older than Ira.
**Image:** Under a coil of good thread she lifts out a wrapped bundle: oilcloth, then waxed paper, then
a third wrapping of something like skin. Her enormous hands are careful in a way the reader has never
seen them.

> **CAPTION:**
> Kessa had counted four Unspoolings.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> Only one thread had ever refused to cast a shadow in her lamp.

---

## PANEL 5 — Macro, the shadowless swatch (~13%)

**Camera:** Straight down at her open palm: a short **swatch of colourless thread**, unrolled.
**Image:** The lamp burns beside it and the swatch catches **no light at all** — and casts **no shadow**,
in lamp-light, in the real world, where the vision's shadow-rule has no business applying. Everything
else in the panel casts: her thumb, the box lid, the lamp itself. The swatch simply declines.

> **CAPTION:**
> She had kept it from before the first Unspooling she counted.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> Under the good thread, where the light could not find it.

---

## PANEL 6 — Close, Kessa's face (~13%)

**Camera:** Up at Kessa in the low lamp. No loupe. Both cat eyes bare, and old, and afraid — and under
the fear, tenderness of a kind she has never once shown on panel.
**Image:** She is not looking at the swatch. She is looking **past** it, up and out, in the general
direction of the tenement roofs — in the general direction of a girl asleep with her hand closed over
her palm.

> **KESSA (dialogue, to nobody, the smallest line in the chapter):**
> Forty years I kept the cut end.
>
> **KESSA (dialogue):**
> Never once asked what it was cut *from*.

---

## PANEL 7 — HOOK / END CARD — the knot at the end of the swatch (~20%)

**Camera:** Extreme macro on the swatch in her palm, at its far end: a **knot**. Small, tight, tied in a
whorl no hand in this room owns — the same uncanny grain as the sixty-first knot on Page 009. Kessa's
thumb hovers a hair above it and does not touch.
**Image:** The lamp guttering once, softly, like a breath. The dark of the stall pressing in round the
light. Somewhere far above, the Loom turns.

> **CAPTION:**
> Some doors, Kessa kept shut herself.
>
> **SFX:** (faint, everywhere, under the whole panel) *hmmmmmm* — the Loom's hum…
>
> **SFX (smaller, beneath it):** …one note lower than last night.
>
> **CAPTION (end card, centred, bottom):**
> **END OF CHAPTER ONE**

## Page notes for continuity

- **The callback structure is the chapter's spine closing.** Page 001's two captions return verbatim in
  Panel 1 and are answered by a third in Panel 3: *"That was the first lie anyone told about her."* From
  Page 011 onward, never repeat the "already taken" line as truth; it is a lie the city tells, and Ira
  now knows it.
- **"Sleep." is Ira's first vow and her signature word.** One word, spoken to her own hand. It rhymes
  with the fold's sleeping whorl without naming it. If she ever says it to anything else — person,
  thread, city — it must cost her something.
- **The swatch is a CUT END of unpulled thread.** Kessa has kept it forty years and never asked what it
  was cut from (Panel 6). Do not reveal its origin this arc. Its knot matches the sixty-first knot's
  whorl and the vision's grown whorl: three appearances of one knot-shape. **The knot-shape is the
  series' fingerprint; never give it an owner on panel before its reveal.**
- **The swatch breaks the shadow-rule in lamp-light on purpose.** In Sight-vision, unpulled thread casts
  no shadow while everything else does (Page 008). Here it casts no shadow in ordinary light — which
  means the rule is not about Sight, it is about the *thread*. Let attentive readers notice; never
  explain.
- **Kessa's eyeline (Page 007 Panel 4) is paid here and only here.** She knew she owned unpulled thread;
  she did not know Ira's palm was sewn with it until this chapter. Her fear in Panel 6 is the fear of a
  woman whose forty-year-old evidence suddenly has a witness.
- **The hum one note lower is Chapter 2's first thread.** The fold turned over in its sleep during the
  hour; the Loom's note changed after. Nobody in-world notices this but the reader. Do not let any
  character hear it until much later.
- **Ira's roof enters the location canon** (see `other/locations.md`): flat tenement roof, ash drifts,
  mending-rags, one crate. It is the only place in the basin where she is nobody's null. Use it the way
  other stories use a bedroom.
- Chapter 1 closes with **three locked boxes** (the sewer, the principal, Kessa's swatch) and **one open
  hand**. That ratio is the series' contract with the reader.
