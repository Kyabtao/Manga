# THREADBORN — Chapter 01 — Page 001

**Chapter title:** *The Girl With No Thread*
**Arc:** I — The Unspooling
**Sector:** Agnikhand (Ash Sector)
**Page type:** Opening / hook — 7 panels, ends on reveal
**Canvas:** vertical webtoon strip, full colour

---

## PANEL 1 — Wide establishing (large, top ~22% of strip)

**Camera:** Extreme wide, low angle looking up the crater wall.
**Image:** The Ash Sector from inside the caldera — terraced city climbing a black basalt wall, ash
falling like snow. Above it all, the **Loom**: a continent-sized lattice of pale light turning slowly
in the sky. Dead centre, **the Spindle**, a pillar of light rising from the caldera floor.

> **CAPTION (narration box, top-left):**
> Rangbhoomi. The Stage.
>
> **CAPTION (top-right):**
> Nine Sectors. Nine Kinds. One Loom.
>
> **SFX:** (low, continuous) *hmmmmmmm*

---

## PANEL 2 — Medium wide (~12%)

**Camera:** Street level, crowded. Ash drifts through the frame.
**Image:** A packed processional street in the **Ashfall Basin**. Hundreds of fifteen-year-olds in
grey ceremony robes walk uphill toward the Spindle. Families press against rope barriers. A cloth
banner spans the street: **"UNSPOOLING — 412TH CYCLE"**.

> **CAPTION:**
> Every fifteen years, the Loom opens.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> Today, four thousand children in this city find out what they are worth.

---

## PANEL 3 — Medium close (~14%)

**Camera:** Side-on, tight. Focus on hands.
**Image:** **IRA SUTAR**, 15, Manavkin — mismatched eyes (one amber, one grey-green), a patch of fine
scale along her left cheekbone, thread-mender's leather gloves with the fingertips cut off. She is
sitting on an upturned crate in an alley mouth, **sewing a stranger's forearm** with a curved needle
and glowing filament. The stranger is a dock worker with a torn debt-mark.

> **IRA (dialogue, small):**
> Hold still. This'll pull.
>
> **DOCK WORKER (dialogue):**
> You're the mender girl. Aren't you late for your own Unspooling?
>
> **SFX:** *tchk* (needle through skin)

---

## PANEL 4 — Close-up on Ira's face, looking up (~12%)

**Camera:** Low, up at her face against the sky. Ash on her eyelashes.
**Image:** Ira looking up at the Loom. Her expression is not awe — it is **appraisal**. Like a tailor
looking at a garment she didn't cut.

> **IRA (thought, italic):**
> Four hundred marks I've mended in this city.
>
> **IRA (thought, italic):**
> Not one of them was mine.

---

## PANEL 5 — Dynamic, tilted (~13%)

**Camera:** Snapped toward the Spindle. Motion blur.
**Image:** A boy at the Spindle's base, arms thrown wide, a **bright taut orange thread** bursting from
his palm and snapping up into the pillar of light. The crowd recoils from the heat of it. Embers.

> **CROWD (shout balloon):**
> Taut! Look at it — that's a taut one!
>
> **OFFICIAL (amplified, shouting):**
> Agnikhand thread, high strain! Name him and rank him!
>
> **SFX:** *FWOOM*

---

## PANEL 6 — Tight, quiet (~12%)

**Camera:** Straight down onto Ira's open palm. Nothing else in frame.
**Image:** The Loom's light sweeps across her. Her palm is **open and empty**. No thread. No colour.
The light passes over her hand and keeps going, like water off stone.

> **SFX:** (small, in the corner of the panel) *…*
>
> **CROWD (off-panel, small balloons):**
> …nothing?
>
> **CROWD (off-panel):**
> The mender girl drew nothing.

---

## PANEL 7 — HOOK — extreme close-up, largest panel of the lower half (~15%)

**Camera:** Macro on the inside of Ira's empty palm.
**Image:** Across her bare palm, faintly catching the light, runs a **seam** — a neat, deliberate
**stitch line**, closed with thread that is not hers. It is old. It is surgical. Someone sewed this
hand shut a long time ago, and they were *good at it*.

> **CAPTION:**
> Everyone is born owing the Loom a thread.
>
> **CAPTION (final box, bottom):**
> Ira Sutar was born with hers already taken.
>
> **IRA (small, quiet, at the very edge):**
> …that's a *stitch*.

## Page notes for continuity

- This is the **first time mending is shown**. The reader must understand it is *labour* — needle,
  thread, sitting down, pain. Not a superpower.
- Ira's mark is on her **palm** → projection type. But it has been **sewn shut**. Do not explain why.
- The dock worker's torn mark in Panel 3 is a **debt-mark**, not a wound. Establish the difference
  visually now: debt-marks are dark lines under the skin, wounds are red.
- Nobody in this page is kind to her, and nobody is cruel either. The city is simply *indifferent*.
  That is the tone for Chapter 1.
- **Do not** reveal who stitched her hand. That is a long-arc box.
