# THREADBORN — Chapter 10 — Page 008

**Chapter title:** *Room Nine* / *कमरा नौ*
**Arc:** II — The Mendery
**Sector:** Agnikhand — the Mendery, shutters down, midday
**Page type:** Turn page — 7 panels

> Beats: a claimant accepted on a thumbprint will produce the founder in room nine at fourth bell
> tomorrow; the keeper is required to attend; the pot-row is filing claims against itself for money.
>
> **This page:** the school decides what it is, out loud, at a counter, with the shutters down.

---

## PANEL 1 — Medium, the shutters down (~13%)

**Camera:** Medium: the Mendery at midday with the shutters down and the queue turned away with a chalk
notice. Inside: Ira, Kessa, Jadi, and — on the counter between them, unwound and visible for the first
time at full length — **Kessa's tally-thread**: thirty-odd knots in groups, the crease-writs and the
reply and the objection, the only copy of anything the school has said in writing for a year.

**Image:** Four hundred claimants outside; every record the school has, in one strand, on a counter.

> **KESSA:**
> Right. Nobody's opening those shutters until we've had this out between us, because the minute they're
> up, every word we say is the street's business.
>
> **KESSA:**
> Now: what do we actually know? *(finger on a knot)* A letter came down the chute. Two crease-writs went
> up. The Office has a copy of the Roll. The Office does not have a name, a face, or a district. And as of
> an hour ago a man with no name has told them he can produce her tomorrow at fourth bell.

---

## PANEL 2 — Close, the forty-one (~14%)

**Camera:** Close: Ira's hands and the claims list, and on the reverse of it her own hand-drawn column —
**forty-one short strokes**, the roster she has been counting since Chapter 008 Page 007. Around it, the
room's faces: Kessa flat, Jadi with her palm open on her knee.

**Image:** The arithmetic of a woman who spent six nights visiting, and the forty-one doors she knocked on.

> **IRA:**
> She went down the list. Forty-one names, four nights, maybe more — Jadi gave her the roster twice,
> remember, made her say it twice.
>
> **IRA:**
> That means forty-one doors in this basin were knocked on, in the dark, by a Kshudra woman nobody has
> seen in twenty years. Forty-one households know she is *real.* Forty-one households know she is
> *current*, and know she is *asking questions about who got measured.*
>
> **JADI:**
> *(flat)*
> And now forty-one households know what she's worth.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> Eleven words in crimson, and every door my mother knocked on turned into a *sale.*

---

## PANEL 3 — Close, Jadi's proposal (~15%)

**Camera:** Close: Jadi, sitting forward now, her knot at her cuff, speaking in the same voice she used to
order tea — the flat, itemised voice of the school's first pupil, and the panel should let the reader see
that she has been waiting forty-four years to say this sentence.

**Image:** The root of the school, proposing the school's own answer to a bounty.

> **JADI:**
> Then finish us.
>
> **IRA:**
> Jadi —
>
> **JADI:**
> Forty years that woman put a first strand into people and never finished one of them, and you told me
> yourself what the rules are for: one strand is one date, a braid is two years, and a chain that reads
> two years gets neither. *So finish us.* Me, the sister in your queue this morning, the woman at the
> hatch in the ninth, everyone on that roster who is still breathing. Braid them. Don't declare one of
> them.
>
> **JADI:**
> They built a law about *declared* hands, girl. So let nobody declare. The Office can read every palm in
> this basin twice a week for the rest of its life and it will get nothing off any of us, and there's no
> clause in forty-one that says a hand has to be *told on.*

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> And that is the moment I understood my mother's whole life. Not the sewing. The *silence.* Forty years
> of it, in a cellar, through a hole, one forearm at a time — and every single person she enrolled walked
> out of that alley safer, and none of them ever knew why, and none of them could ever say a word about
> it, because they had never been told.

---

## PANEL 4 — Close, Ira's answer (~16%)

**Camera:** Close: Ira, standing, needle-roll in her hand — not open — and she does not put it down. Her
face in this panel is the whole chapter: she is about to refuse the school's method and keep the
school's purpose, and she has to do it to Jadi's face, in the same room, with the same tool her mother
used.

**Image:** The amended rule three, applied to the whole basin.

> **IRA:**
> No. Not like that.
>
> **IRA:**
> Jadi, rule three, as amended: the teacher's strand goes in first, and then the teacher tells you what
> she did. That is not a nicety, it's the *only* thing my school is. Your school made forty-one people
> who could never be found and could never be *asked.* You grew up in a hand that was written about and
> never read to — I know exactly what that made of you, because you sat in my doorway and asked me to cut
> the only thread you had out of your palm so that nobody could ever find you again.
>
> **IRA:**
> If I finish this basin in the dark, I have built your mother's school twice. So here's what we do
> instead. We teach. Up here, in the open, with the shutters up, and every person who comes in is told —
> by me, out loud — what the strand does, what the second strand does, what declaring costs, and what not
> declaring costs.
>
> **IRA:**
> And then they decide. Every one, for themselves.
>
> **JADI:**
> *(a long pause; and then, with something that is not quite approval)*
> Then say it's a lesson, and set a day.

---

## PANEL 5 — Medium, Nima at the door (~14%)

**Camera:** Medium: the side door, and **Nima** in it, Office grey, the card-tray under her arm — she has
walked down from the Reckoning Office to do the keeper's joint read-out, which is the procedure Nandi
wrote into the schedule that morning. She stops in the doorway with about four hundred claimants' worth
of queue on the street behind her.

**Image:** The Office's reader, arriving at the school's counter, in Office grey, carrying the Office's
cards.

> **NIMA:**
> Keeper. First joint session. Nandi says start with the fourteen from yesterday and the twenty-six
> today —
>
> **NIMA:**
> *(seeing the room's faces; stopping)*
> ...What's happened?
>
> **IRA:**
> Tomorrow they're producing the founder. Fourth bell, room nine. Somebody's selling her for money.
>
> **NIMA:**
> *(and this is where the reader sees what Nima is, because her second question is not the Office's)*
> If they produce her — do I have to read her?

---

## PANEL 6 — Close, Nima's terms (~14%)

**Camera:** Close: Nima, on the stool, tray on her knees, and the panel's composition makes her look
nineteen for the first time — a girl with Office grey on and a braid in her palm that she keeps turned
against her leg.

**Image:** The Office's reader working out, at the school's counter, exactly what she has been hired for.

> **IRA:**
> Nobody reads anybody who hasn't said yes. That's my rule and you've just heard me say it out loud in
> front of two witnesses.
>
> **NIMA:**
> Then I want something, keeper. I've got Office pay because there's no entry next to my name. The day
> Nandi writes me down, I'm declared, and clause one takes the wage, and I go back to the ninth terrace
> at nineteen with nothing but the desk I trained on.
>
> **NIMA:**
> So don't write me. Not for money and not for safety. *(and she turns her palm up on her knee, showing
> them all the braid)* I'll read every card in that tray for the Office, and I'll keep the seam out of
> the light, and one day when I'm *told* to put a hand on that stand I'll say no in the room, and that's
> the most I've got to give anybody.
>
> **JADI:**
> *(to Ira, mild)*
> See? Every one, for themselves.

---

## PANEL 7 — Close, two bells — CLOSING HOOK (~14%)

**Camera:** Close: the shutters going up from inside — Jadi on one end of the pole, Ira on the other —
and through the opening glass, the queue turning back toward the stall. Then, one panel-width away, the
clean, cold shape of the Council Stair at the top of the row, with a clerk on the third step nailing a
fresh notice to the board: a **second crimson sheet**, next to the bounty.

**Image:** The school opening its shutters, and the Office posting an addition to the board at the same
hour.

> **JADI:**
> Then tomorrow. Lesson at dawn, up the hatch alley, shutters up, your name on it.
>
> **IRA:**
> Dawn at the alley. And fourth bell in room nine.
>
> **JADI:**
> Both in the same day. *(she checks the light out of long habit)* It'll do. This school's done worse
> with less.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice, smallest):**
> Notice up-terrace, nailed at midday, sheet two, in crimson. I read it off the board from the top of the
> pot-row: *where a hand is produced and verified, the producing party shall be entered as the party of
> record.*
>
> **CAPTION (smallest):**
> Party of record. Which means whoever hands my mother over tomorrow is not a neighbour who needed the
> money. It means the Office intends to give them *the right to be her family* — and then everything she
> has ever had is theirs, including the chute, including the hatch, including me.

## Writing & art notes

- **Jadi's proposal is the honest one and must read that way.** *"Finish us. Don't declare one of them."*
  It is not villainy and not cowardice: it is the school's forty-year method described accurately, by
  the person who lived it. Let her win the argument in the room and lose it only to Ira's memory of a
  woman who was never told what had been written about her.
- **Ira's counter-argument must be *about telling*, not about legality or safety.** The chapter's spine
  is the amended rule three. Her last clause — *and then they decide, every one for themselves* — is the
  invention of the second school as an open practice, and it is what Chapter 011 will test in public.
- **Nima's bargain is the page's coldest and best logic:** Office pay depends on *not* being declared, so
  the Office's own reader is structurally unable to be entered. Do not soften it. Her offer — to refuse
  out loud, in the room, on the day she is told to read the founder — is the character's whole future.
- **Kessa's thread is finally shown at length and stays unexplained.** Knots in groups, no reading out,
  no caption describing its contents beyond what earlier chapters established. It is the one record the
  Office cannot copy because it is not a document, and Ira must not say so on the page.
- **The second crimson sheet is the chapter's forward engine and it must be small and clerical.** *The
  producing party shall be entered as the party of record.* No threat, no announcement, nailed at midday
  by a clerk on the third step. Ira's caption draws the consequence — the traitor becomes family, and
  inherits everything — and stops there.
- **Shutters-up is a *decision* here and must be drawn as one.** This is the school's first act of
  public teaching in forty years, done in daylight, at a stall, with a queue of claimants outside who are
  about to be offered the truth instead of a fee.
