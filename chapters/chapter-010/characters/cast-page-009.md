**Page note:** the second school's first public lesson, in the hatch alley, in daylight, with the neighbours' shutters up. One pupil leaves at the price; nobody calls after her. Jadi — who cannot read — teaches three women to **count their own knots.** The clerk's instruction was written a week early: *if the practitioner tells the subject beforehand, the practice is not concealment.* Hook: reader and keeper, same words, same time, at fourth bell.
