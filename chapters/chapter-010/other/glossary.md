# Chapter 010 — Glossary


Terms introduced, seated or paid off in this chapter. Terms already listed in earlier chapters keep their
first entry; the Ch. 010 column notes what this chapter adds.

| Term | Devanagari | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Room nine** | कमरा नौ | Nine feet by eleven. One table, two chairs, a high window. Light on the subject's chair, dark on the reader's back. Holds a hearing, a production and an audit in one day. |
| **The portable chain** | चल ज़ंजीर | The chain on its low brass stand — furniture, not machinery, and movable to any room the Office wants. |
| **Chit** | चिट | The vestibule's slip: a number, a room, a time, and one line of small print. *"You'll be called by chit number, not by name."* |
| **The audit's fee** | ऑडिट का शुल्क | *Auditing is charged to the party whose entry is audited.* Not a fine — a fee, and fees are lawful all the way down. |
| **PRODUCE THE HAND. REWARD RESERVED.** | *हाथ पेश करो। इनाम आरक्षित।* | The bounty, eleven words in crimson, on the board beside the slate. *Payment on verification. No name required. Any hand.* |
| **Party of record** | अभिलेख-पक्ष | Second crimson sheet: whoever produces a hand **or a place** is entered as the party of record for it — and inherits everything that person had. |
| **The bigger book** | बड़ी किताब | The translation room's own register, kept upstairs. Its reader must read every entry aloud with the keeper, same words, same time. |
| **Reader of the bigger book** | पाठक | **Nandi**, entered at her own word. Cannot read a page and has never misread a knot. |
| **Reader-second** | प्रशिक्षु पाठक | **Nima**, ninth terrace, Kshudra, enrolled at the hatch at nine. Seated in this chapter; reads an undeclared hand for the Office and takes the Office's pay for not being in the book. |
| **Declared / declaration** | घोषित / घोषणा | A hand entered at its **owner's own word**. Clause two, verbatim: no fee, and no reward for anyone else. |
| **Subject harmed** | विषय को हानि पहुँची | The last box on the clerk's form. First ticked in that room on Ch. 010 Page 010, after a forged strand was sewn into a woman who was not asked. |
| **The second school** | दूसरा विद्यालय | Teach in the open, tell every person what the strand does and what it costs, then let each one decide for themselves. Opened on Ch. 010 Page 009. |
| **The door** | दरवाज़ा | The hatch, the chute, the nights, the alley — what the bounty was actually priced for. *You cannot catch a woman who has been invisible for twenty years; you can buy the place where she knocks.* |
| **The keeping of the place** | रखने का स्थान | The Mendery, Ashfall, slot four — the register's address, and now the survey line the Office posts a lamp over. |
| **Counting** | गिनती | Jadi's lesson: three women taught to count their own knots so no clerk can ever tell them what their own hands say. |
| **Crimson and grey** | किरमिज़ी / धूसर | Crimson: the principal's private hand and the bounty's sheets. Grey: the Council, the Office, the lamp on the pole. |
