# Chapter 010 — Locations


## THE COUNCIL STAIR — परिषद-सीढ़ी
Forty-one steps, ash falling slow, plain unmarked doors at the top. Worn pale at handle height and *below*
it, where a hundred years of palms have been held out. Jadi counts the steps; Ira counts the minutes.

## THE VESTIBULE — प्रतीक्षा-कक्ष
Low benches in rows, a high desk, a slate with numbers chalked on it, one clock, three clerks moving along
the rows. Full of people who have been *trained* to wait. Chits, not names. The bounty board stands beside
the slate: crimson sheet one (the bounty) and, by afternoon, crimson sheet two (party of record).

## ROOM NINE — कमरा नौ
Nine feet by eleven, high window, one table, two chairs. The subject's chair faces the light and the reader
sits with her back to the dark; a cushion is put on it for an old woman who never comes. The portable chain
stands on its low brass frame; the register lies open in its case; the tray of cards and the reserve slate
stay on the table. By dusk one chalk line has been left on the slate on purpose.

## ROOM ELEVEN — कमरा ग्यारह
A table, a lantern, a shelf, two bells a day and a girl to train. Offered to Nandi with a key on the table.
Its window looks onto the same stair the school has climbed for forty years.

## THE MENDERY COUNTER (FIRST LIGHT) — मेंडरी का काउंटर
The queue runs past three stalls before second bell: claimants, not pupils, two of them rehearsing with
their own palms turned up. Ira reads palms at the counter with the register open and the claims list under
her elbow; Kessa keeps the stall's ordinary ledger at her end; Jadi works inside the shutters with a bowl
and an expression.

## THE HATCH ALLEY — खिड़की-गली
The school's only address for forty years: a shin-high, forearm-sized hatch in the back door, worn shiny at
the edges. On Ch. 010 Page 009 it becomes a classroom in daylight, with the neighbours' shutters up and a
clerk watching from the alley mouth. By dusk the Office has posted it: a grey lamp on a pole at the mouth
of the alley, standing exactly where the school's lantern used to hang, and two neighbouring shutters down.

## THE RECKONING OFFICE CORRIDOR — लेखा-कार्यालय का गलियारा
A bench under a high window, ash-light, and nobody in it by the time the two women sit down. Where the
school's record is handed from the woman who kept it for forty years to the girl who will read it next.
