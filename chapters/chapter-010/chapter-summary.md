## Synopsis

The Office's answer to Chapter 009 was a department: a copyist, a bigger book, and four words in
crimson on the register's reserved first line — ***PRODUCE THE HAND. REWARD RESERVED.*** Chapter 010
is the day that instrument is used.

Ira walks up the Council Stair with Jadi and with the register's line two, to produce a hand. Nobody
arrests anybody. The building does what buildings do: it seats them, numbers them, and asks for the
paperwork — and by the time they reach room nine, the bounty has already begun to work on the pot-row
below, because a reward needs no enforcement officials, only neighbours.

Room nine holds the chapter's three clocks at once: the **production** of Jadi's hand (the Office's
portable chain, read by its new reader-second — a Kshudra girl named **Nima** who can count but cannot
read a braid), the **hearing** of **Nandi** (produced as a witness and asked to identify knots from a
tray), and the register's **reserved line one**, which is not about the school at all.

The chapter's cost is Nandi's honesty. She has never lied about a knot in forty years and she does not
start in room nine: she gives kinds, years and hands — everything the school ever recorded and never
a name, because the school never wrote one. It is enough. Nandi's answers complete the translation the
Office began in Chapter 009, and they hand the Office its instrument. The Office offers the woman who
kept the shelf forty years a desk of her own — and offers, in front of her, to *hire* her rather than
charge her. Jadi's production is filed as **verified: unreadable**, which costs her the right to hold,
own, license, claim or be owed anything in the basin: legal non-existence, granted at a counter, with
a receipt she refuses to mark.

The chapter's second half is the one the Office never had to write: the bounty comes down to the
Mendery **as commerce.** Fourteen claims at first bell and twenty-six by the second, a palm with seven
healed cuts from a night search, and a sister's braided hand offered for her brother's rent — all of it
to be entered at Ira's own counter, in her own handwriting, because the Office made the keeper of the
register **the bounty's clerk.** Her answer is the smallest lawful possible act, twice over: move a fee
to the Office by dispute, and enter a hand at its owner's own word so that no claimant is ever paid.
Then the shutters come down, Jadi proposes the school's old answer (*finish us, declare nobody*), Ira
refuses it and opens the **second school** — teach, tell, let every one decide for themselves — and
Nima bargains for the only thing she has: *don't write me.*

The chapter ends with the price of that openness. The second school's **first public lesson** is held in
the hatch alley at dawn, in daylight, with a clerk at the alley mouth taking notes to instructions
written a week early — *if the practitioner tells the subject beforehand, the practice is not
concealment.* At fourth bell the promised production arrives: not the founder, but a crimson strand
sewn two nights ago into an unasked old woman's palm, which Nandi reads aloud as **not the founder**
while the schedule's condition makes Ira say the same words at the same time on the record — and the
claimant, having failed, sells the only thing he has left: **the door.** The Office enters him as party
of record, posts a grey lamp on a pole where the school's lantern hung, and at dusk the lifted knot is
back on the Mendery's step with a **second knot beside it, in an untrained hand.** A new enrolment,
made this week, not by Ira, and not in the dark.

## Canon rules added in Chapter 10 (binding forward)

1. **Room nine is an argument in furniture.** Light on the subject's chair, the reader's back to the
   dark, one table, two chairs, and the chain on a low portable stand: the Office does not own a
   machine, it owns a *room arrangement* it can carry anywhere.
2. **Chits, not names.** The vestibule runs on numbers — *"we don't use names in the vestibule"* — and
   has done so for a hundred years, for free, on everybody.
3. **The audit's fee is charged to the party audited.** Not a fine: a fee, and fees are lawful all the
   way down. *Claimant unable to discharge* is still an entry, and an entry is a name.
4. **Sheet two, in crimson: the producing party is entered as the party of record.** Whoever produces a
   hand — or a place — stands in the produced person's shoes and inherits everything they had.
5. **The reader and the keeper read every entry aloud, in the same words, at the same time.** Nandi's
   own condition, written into the schedule, so that no record in the basin goes unread. It is also
   what makes the school's reading public business.
6. **The translation room works from copies, not hands.** A copyist who has never held a needle
   reverses a crossing and copies a fee as though it were a knot; both errors were caught in one
   afternoon by two women who read knots.
7. **An owner can enter their own hand.** A declaration made at the owner's own word costs no fee and
   pays no reward — the keeper's smallest lawful counter to the bounty: *enter the hands first, and
   there is nothing left to claim but the person.*
8. **Nima's structure.** Office pay depends on *not* being declared, so the Office's own reader cannot
   be entered without losing her wage. She keeps the braid out of the light and reserves the right to
   say no out loud in the room.
9. **The second school is public by method:** teach in the open, tell the person what will be done
   before touching the hand, and let each one decide for themselves. The Office's reply was drafted a
   week in advance — *if the practitioner tells the subject beforehand, the practice is not
   concealment* — which is permission used as a leash.
10. **The Office does not hunt what it cannot find; it prices the door.** A woman invisible for twenty
    years cannot be caught, but the place where she knocks can be bought from somebody poor enough —
    and the arithmetic, not the cruelty, is the whole instrument.
11. **Forgery canon.** A copied strand laid two nights earlier reads crossed left-over-right, sits on a
    root that was never opened, has no gate under it, and leaves a needle wound. **Subject harmed** is
    a box on the Office's own form; room nine ticked it for the first time.
12. **The mother is teaching in the open, without permission.** She lifts knots rather than cutting
    them, returns what she took, and has enrolled a pupil this week whose knot arrived in a hand that
    never crossed two strands in the dark.
