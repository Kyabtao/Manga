## Synopsis

The Office's answer to Chapter 009 was a department: a copyist, a bigger book, and four words in
crimson on the register's reserved first line — ***PRODUCE THE HAND. REWARD RESERVED.*** Chapter 010
is the day that instrument is used.

Ira walks up the Council Stair with Jadi and with the register's line two, to produce a hand. Nobody
arrests anybody. The building does what buildings do: it seats them, numbers them, and asks for the
paperwork — and by the time they reach room nine, the bounty has already begun to work on the pot-row
below, because a reward needs no enforcement officials, only neighbours.

Room nine holds the chapter's three clocks at once: the **production** of Jadi's hand (the Office's
portable chain, read by its new reader-second — a Kshudra girl named **Nima** who can count but cannot
read a braid), the **hearing** of **Nandi** (produced as a witness and asked to identify knots from a
tray), and the register's **reserved line one**, which is not about the school at all.

The chapter's cost is Nandi's honesty. She has never lied about a knot in forty years and she does not
start in room nine: she gives kinds, years and hands — everything the school ever recorded and never
a name, because the school never wrote one. It is enough. Nandi's answers complete the translation the
Office began in Chapter 009, and they hand the Office its instrument. The Office offers the woman who
kept the shelf forty years a desk of her own — and offers, in front of her, to *hire* her rather than
charge her. Jadi's production is filed as **verified: unreadable**, which costs her the right to hold,
own, license, claim or be owed anything in the basin: legal non-existence, granted at a counter, with
a receipt she refuses to mark.

The chapter ends on the first effect of the bounty down in the pot-row: an old mender brought to the
Mendery with her palm cut open by neighbours who had heard there was money in a crimson strand — and
on Kessa's tally-thread, which is still the only record of any of it that cannot be copied.

## Synopsis — page by page (as written)

- **Ch. 010 · Pages 001–003** — the stair and the vestibule (chits, not names; the audit fee charged
  to the audited); the bounty's first claimant, declined and charged two coppers; the announcement
  that **all claims are entered at the place of keeping** — the bounty's clerk is Ira. Room nine:
  the production, the reader-second **Nima** (Kshudra, enrolled at the hatch at nine), the finding
  **unreadable**, and the grey receipt that removes Jadi's capacity to hold, own, license or be owed.
  Jadi declines to mark it. Closing hook: the slate's reserved line, in chalk — **M. SUTAR.**
- **Ch. 010 · Pages 004–006** — Nandi's hearing. The register's last line is a **hole** (the mother
  lifted the knot); the witness identifies cards in eleven seconds each, catches a reversed copy and a
  knot copied onto an Office fee; then identifies **her own enrolment — year one** — and marks the
  sheet, entering herself as unreadable. The Office offers her **rooms eleven**, a desk and a girl to
  train; she accepts on the condition that **the keeper reads every entry out loud with her.** In the
  corridor she gives **Nima** her own page (*ties two threads at once, counts to forty without a slate,
  will teach*). Ira goes home with the register and **fourteen claims** to enter at her counter.
- **Ch. 010 · Pages 007–008** — the bounty as commerce at the counter: fourteen claims, a palm with seven
  healed cuts, a sister's braided hand offered for rent money. Ira uses the keeper's discretion twice
  lawfully (fee moved to the Office; a hand entered at its owner's word so no claimant is paid). Shutters
  down: **Jadi proposes finishing the basin in the dark and declaring nobody**; Ira refuses and opens the
  **second school** — teach, tell, let each person decide. Nima bargains: *don't write me.* Hook: a
  thumbprint claim promises to produce the founder at fourth bell, and a second crimson sheet makes the
  producing party her **party of record.**
- **Ch. 010 · Pages 009–010** — the second school's first public lesson in the hatch alley (three hands
  braided with consent; one pupil leaves at the price; Jadi teaches counting). Fourth bell: a forged
  crimson strand on an unasked old woman's palm, Nandi reads *not the founder*, and the schedule's
  condition forces Ira to say the same words aloud on the record. The claimant sells **the door** —
  the hatch, the chute, the nights and half the ninth terrace's hands — and the Office accepts him as
  **party of record** and posts a grey lamp in the alley. On the step at dusk: the lifted knot returned,
  and beside it **a second knot in an untrained hand.** **END OF CHAPTER TEN.**
