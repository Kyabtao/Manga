## PANEL 1 — Wide, morning, the street fetches the paper (~14%)

**Camera:** Wide: the square, morning. At the shut hatch, the Office has posted a fresh paper — long,
narrow, one seal at the foot. Two of the **counting-lesson women** are already untying it from the
board with a basin crowd watching — and they carry it across the square toward the stall, held flat
like something live.

**Image:** The Office published its answer where the Office always publishes. The street read the
first line and decided the counter does the reading now.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> They posted it at their own hatch, in their own grammar, for a basin that reads knots. It took the
> street one morning to bring it to my counter for reading aloud. That is not rebellion. That is
> logistics.

---

## PANEL 2 — Medium-close, the reading of record (~15%)

**Camera:** Medium-close: the counter, the amendment flat under the lamp, the register open beside
it. Ira stands of record — reading aloud, level, one finger on the line. The queue is pressed in
close to hear. Kessa at the till, still.

**Image:** A public reading under one lamp: the Office rewriting a word to win an argument.

> **IRA:**
> *(of record, reading)*
> "Amendment to the count, entry clause. A person is entered who is named upon a roll kept by the
> Office. No other entry is entry."
>
> *(the pause of a keeper, not a lawyer)*
> You heard it. By their new word, whoever is not on their roll is not entered — anywhere, in any
> book, by any knot. Not because it is true. Because they have written that it is.

---

## PANEL 3 — Close, the mint clause (~14%)

**Camera:** Close: the lower clause, Ira's finger under it — the paper's second blade.

**Image:** The amendment's real target is not the school's room. It is the certificate.

> **IRA:**
> *(of record, reading)*
> "What is record: paper under stamp. What is not record: all else."
>
> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> There it is. Not one word about the school. Every word about the mint. My certificates are not
> ruled out — they are ruled *out of existence*, on taking, by definition. Their clerk finally
> learned to aim.

---

## PANEL 4 — Medium, the wobble (~14%)

**Camera:** Medium: the claimant — the worn yard hand, third time at this counter — holding his
keeper's certificate and his doubt in the same fist. The queue turns to watch how the keeper answers
doubt.

**Image:** The wobble, on-panel, unresolved: the basin's first taste of what a definition costs.

> **CLAIMANT:**
> The foreman got his own paper this morning. He says come dusk, by this, your word counts for
> nothing at my gate. He is not decided. I am not decided. Keeper — does mine still count?
>
> **IRA:**
> *(level, of record)*
> The register does not answer fear with comfort. It answers with the record. Entered, of record:
> the question. The answer is owed by dusk tomorrow — and the register will owe it *to you*, by
> name, in writing.

---

## PANEL 5 — Wide, the notice at the yard (~13%)

**Camera:** Wide, midday: the fuel-yard gate across the basin — the foreman's runner accepting a
long sealed paper from an Office errand-hand at the gate's knot-board. The yard behind: sacks,
split wood, men pausing mid-load to look at the paper. No foreman's face — the gate is the event.

**Image:** The wobble, delivered: the Office did not wait for the register's answer. It sent its
definition straight to the gates.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> My ruling will take a day and a signature they cannot erase. Their counter-move took a runner and
> a definition. That is the whole war, in logistics.

---

## PANEL 6 — Wide, the first rationed dusk (~14%)

**Camera:** Wide, dusk, the square from the stall's side: along the square, household lamps dying
one by one at the posted hour — window by window going dark. On the hatch board, a fresh knot-marked
notice: the permit-dark, the Office's lamp-hours, one seal. The stall's lamp alone stays lit — a
small warm island, the queue still at the rail in its light.

**Image:** The basin goes dark on the Office's hour. The counter does not.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> First rationed dusk. They posted the hours the basin may keep light in, sealed, at their hatch —
> as if dusk were theirs to license. Across the square, the windows obeyed. Then they looked at the
> one lamp that did not.

---

## PANEL 7 — Medium-close, the counter stays lit — FINAL HOOK (~16%)

**Camera:** Medium-close: the counter by the one small flame — trimmed low and exact. Kessa fills
the lamp from a narrow oil can, unhurried, and ties a knot into a short cord by the till: the till
now counts its own light. Ira writes. The register takes the day without complaint.

**Image:** The flame is smaller tonight, and it is not asking anything.

> **KESSA:**
> *(quiet, knotting the cord)*
> We burn by our own count now, keeper. Four hours a night, knotted. When the ration turns, we will
> know it before they post it — the till counts faster than their board.
>
> **IRA:**
> *(of record, entering)*
> Entered: the amendment, read aloud of record before the basin. Entered: the question at the yard
> gate, owed an answer by dusk tomorrow. Entered: the Office's hours — *observed, and not obeyed.*
>
> **CAPTION (smallest):**
> Enter it anyway.

## Writing & art notes

- **Three jobs, in order: the reading (the word rewritten in public); the mint clause (the wobble,
  set and not resolved); the rationed dusk (the light keeps its own hours).** The amendment must read
  as the Office's counter-grammar — procedure answering procedure. The clerk stays off-panel: the
  paper speaks, the runner runs, the gate receives. Nobody from the Office gloats on-panel.
- **The street fetching the paper is the equilibrium deepening, not a gag:** the basin cannot read
  Office grammar, and the counter reads it aloud of record — so the Office's own publication is
  answered at the counter. The counting-lesson women carry it (their third appearance: queue p001,
  counting lessons, now couriers of record).
- **The claimant's doubt is the page's heart.** He is not a coward and the foreman is not a villain:
  both men have gates to keep. Ira's answer must not comfort and must not resolve — she enters the
  question of record and owes an answer by dusk tomorrow, by name, in writing. The register's
  credibility is exactly as big as its willingness to owe things. Page 005 pays or defaults.
- **Panel 5's yard gate is the war in logistics** — one runner, one sealed paper, men pausing
  mid-load. No foreman's face. The foreman stays off-panel until his page (005) pays or defaults.
- **The rationed dusk is spectacle and threat at once:** windows going dark on the hour, one stall
  lit. Kessa's oil-count is the till's new discipline — four hours knotted, the till counts faster
  than the board. Her box stays shut; her hand is on the oil can and the cord this page.
- **The imperative ladder takes its next run:** *Enter it anyway.* (Ladder so far: knock / ask him /
  read it back / sign nothing / keep it better / ask her / tie her in / the roll is whole / rule it,
  keeper / count it, then / light it anyway / enter it anyway.)
- **Continuity:** chain-stop budget Chapter 013: ONE, unspent. Mother never on panel, never speaks —
  no thread. Loom absent. The three entered hands off-panel (the amendment targets the mint, not the
  room — their namelessness is now the Office's excuse, not their exposure). The amendment file has
  ARRIVED (sent for on Page 002). The certificate question is OPEN and owed an answer by dusk
  tomorrow. The inventory count holds: granary sealed, fuel-yards sealed, lamp-oil rationed —
  permit-dark posted. The Office's hatch stays shut pending the count; its board carries the
  amendment and the lamp-hours. Kessa keeps the till; the box stays shut.
- **§3.1:** no electric light, no modern fixtures, no printed signage. The amendment is a long
  narrow paper, knot-marks and tally groups only, one seal at the foot; the permit-dark notice,
  knot-marks and one seal; the claimant's certificate likewise. Lamp and daylight only; the rationed
  dusk is the page's signature light effect — a dark square, one lit stall.
- **Numbers:** one amendment (two clauses); one public reading; one question entered of record; one
