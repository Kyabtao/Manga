Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /

> **Page 005 note:** answers doubt with the record — in duplicate, sealed, before noon: the

## THE FOREMAN (on-panel for the first time — the gate that chose)
No full sheet; Ch. 013. **First on-panel appearance.** Keep him CAREFUL, not warm: broad,
heavy-jawed, greying stubble, leather apron over a wool coat, gate-keys at his belt, a gate-keeper's
face that gives away nothing early. Reads everything twice.

```
The Foreman / Manav / Agnikhand / ledger-brown, cautious / pays against paper /
Honour: accept the certificate or defy a record by name /
"Paid against the keeper's paper. That is this gate's rule now."
```
> **Page 005 note:** read the register's answer slow, twice — then honoured the certificate in view
> of his own yard and made the gate's rule out loud where his men could hold him to it. Then came to
> the counter in person, cap in hands, and bought the one thing the record can actually sell: his
> name, entered, not read aloud. Not a convert. A gate-keeper who chose which paper his gate
> trusts — and paid for the choice.

## THE CLAIMANT (the answer in his fist)
No full sheet; Ch. 013. The worn yard hand, fourth visit: present at the gate when the record
arrives, paid in view of the yard, and at the hatch ring at night when the trap goes up — his
certificate a promise with no road to keep it, until morning.

```
The Claimant / Manav / Agnikhand / worn brown, unpaid no longer / owed a winter /
Ask: what a paper is worth when its office stops /
"It stands till the count ends. The keeper wrote it twice."
```
> **Page 005 note:** the register's credibility, made flesh at a yard gate — a full measure-sack,
> carried home against the keeper's paper. His night face at the trap notice is the page's wobble:
> the same man the Office's new grammar is aimed at, twice in one day.

## THE RUNNER (the Office's, then the register's — one day, two roads)
No full sheet; Ch. 004, 013. Plain grey; carries what the powerful write and never says what he
thinks. Carried the amendment to the gates (Page 004); carries the register's answer to the same
gates (Page 005).

```
The Runner / Manav / Agnikhand / plain grey, uncommitted / paid by the road /
Carry: what a seal decides, to whose door it lands /
"Same gate. Different paper."
```
> **Page 005 note:** the war in logistics, wearing out shoe-leather: yesterday he delivered the
> definition; today he delivered its answer. Neither paper is his. Both doors know his knock now.
