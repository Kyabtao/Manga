**Page note:** the street chose its table. A dozen-deep queue at dawn; the widow's tally-stick ruled
in a morning (six measures, hollow-interest void — the Office's grammar, the keeper's book, the
basin's thumb-mark); the Office's own hatch shut *pending the count*; a pay-chit that needs the one
stamp in the basin; Rekhak's warning — they are counting what hands hold now; and the crack: an old
lease against the school's unnamed three, saved for exactly this morning. The register's first hard
ruling, taken of record. Hook: **"Rule it, keeper."**
