## IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the register's hand)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png)

```
Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Enforceable — and unbindable."
```
> **Page 002 note:** rules the lease of record — a finding, not a favour: the paper binds names, the
> tenancy holds hands, and the register so finds. Then answers the stamp question without becoming
> the Office: the register certifies, it does not stamp. Sets her own limit into the register — one
> certificate per disputed debt, no interest, void at the count's end, every one signed with her
> name.

## KESSA (the till; the appraisal and the bridle)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png)

```
Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"The paper is real, keeper. What it claims does not exist."
```
> **Page 002 note:** crowns the ruling with the till's verdict — genuine stock, genuine seal, would
> not lend a cup of water against it — and names the complainant as a name that never signs its own
> paper. Then, when the counter-book mints its first credit, she bridges it the same hour: a keep is
> not a mint. Certify the debt, never create it. Her box stays shut; her hand goes on the lid.

## THE CLAIMANT (the certificate's first holder)
No full sheet; Ch. 013. The worn yard hand from Page 001, back for his answer.

```
The Claimant / Manav / Agnikhand / worn brown, unpaid / owed a winter /
Ask: what a paper is worth when its office stops /
"Then his no is on file too?"
```
> **Page 002 note:** the stamp question gets its ripened answer through him — the register cannot
> stamp, so it certifies, and he carries the first keeper's certificate to a yard gate. The foreman
> honours it before last light; the basin learns the counter-book's paper is worth something by
> watching him walk home with weight in his sack.

## THE FOREMAN (off-panel — the honour)
No full sheet; Ch. 013. Never on-panel this page.

```
The Foreman / Manav / Agnikhand / ledger-brown, cautious / pays against paper /
Honour: accept the certificate or defy a record by name /
"No stamp, no pay — unless the no is on file, too."
```
> **Page 002 note:** makes the page's quiet third decision: he honours the certificate before the
> last light goes, because defying a record — in writing, with his name where the keeper's is — is
> more expensive than paying. His honour is the moment the counter-book mints credit.

## THE THREE ENTERED HANDS (unnamed — now armour)
No full sheet; Ch. 012–013. Entered by knot, never by name. Referenced this page; not shown.

```
The Three Entered Hands / Manavkin, Kshudra, Manav / Agnikhand / knot-entered, no names /
Hold: possession by knot, entry by knot /
"No names are written on it that the Office itself never entered."
```
> **Page 002 note:** Page 001 made their namelessness the lease's leverage; Page 002 converts it to
> armour. The register's finding — the tenancy holds hands, not names — means the Office's own
> grammar protects the room the school holds. Still unnamed. Still three.
