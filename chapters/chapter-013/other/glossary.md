# Chapter 013 — Glossary

New terms and usages introduced in this chapter. Chapter 012's terms are defined in
`chapter-012/other/glossary.md`.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **"Pending the count"** | The Office's notice on its shut public hatch (Ch. 013 Page 001): no stamps, no validated papers, no fees, no disputes heard — the machine cannot count, so the machine does nothing. The suspension's cost lands on its own authors and pushes the basin's paper to the keeper's counter. |
| **"No stamp, no pay"** | The paymaster's rule (Page 001): a pay-chit is worth nothing unstamped, and the only stamp in the basin is behind the shutter. The first thing the second Office cannot mend with grammar — a paper hostage to a suspension. |
| **The inventory count** | The Office's workaround (Page 001, Rekhak's warning): the count of hands is suspended, so the Office counts what hands hold — granary seals, fuel-yards, lamp-oil. Inventory does not refuse; inventory has no founder whose no stands on file. The chapter's strategic clock. |
| **The counter-book's docket** | What the street brings the register (Page 001): tally-sticks, boundary-cords, debt-braids, disputes the counting-lesson women could not settle. A queue is a verdict the street has already reached; the keeper is the hand that writes it down. |
