# THREADBORN — Chapter 05 — Page 004

**Chapter title:** *The First Pull* / *पहला खिंचाव*
**Arc:** I — The Unspooling
**Sector:** Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the Knot & Nail (afternoon)
**Page type:** The first mend — 7 panels, hook at bottom

> Beats: Page 003 — Rekhak's Sight reading, the blank strand reaches for the chain.

---

## PANEL 1 — Wide, the afternoon (~14%)
**Camera:** Afternoon, Knot & Nail: Rekhak has gone, leaving the cup empty on the counter. Ira sits with her
palm open, the braided thread contained in the mark. A dock worker approaches — a torn mark, the
routine work.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice, small):**
> He left the cup. I left the mark open. The afternoon brought work.

## PANEL 2 — Medium, the first mend (~13%)
**Camera:** Ira mends the dock worker's torn mark. The needle moves differently now — the braided thread
responds, the mark *listens*. The mend is faster, cleaner, stronger. The whorl-grain appears
in the seam — the same as always, but clearer.

> **SFX:** *tchk* (needle through skin)

## PANEL 3 — Close, the braided thread works (~14%)
**Camera:** Macro on the mend: the braided thread — ash-grey and shadowless — moves through the needle. The
blank strand does the work the sewer's thread used to do: it *holds*, it *seals*, it creates no
debt. The mend is complete. No debt-mark appears on Ira's skin.

> **CAPTION:** No debt. The thread that owes nothing mends without cost. The rule holds.

## PANEL 4 — Medium, the dock worker reacts (~13%)
**Camera:** The dock worker examines the mend: the seam is invisible, the whorl-grain is the only evidence.
He rolls his sleeve. It does not itch.

> **DOCK WORKER (dialogue):** It doesn't itch.
>
> **CAPTION:** The second itch-less mend. The basin will talk.

## PANEL 5 — Wide, the basin watches (~14%)
**Camera:** The market: the news spreads. The girl with no thread drew a thread. The girl with no debt mends
without cost. The Knot & Nail's filing queue returns — not for storage, but for mending. The
basin wants the thread that owes nothing.

> **CAPTION:** By evening, the queue was for mending. The basin's fear found a new address: hope.

## PANEL 6 — Close, Ira's palm (~13%)
**Camera:** Ira's palm: the mark is open, the thread is contained. The dormant Sutra-mark is no longer
dormant — it is *active*, held in check by the braid's balance. Ira's strand and the sewer's
strand, in equilibrium.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice, small):**
> The mark is open. The thread is held. The braid balances. I don't pull — the thread is *there*.

## PANEL 7 — Macro, the lockbox — HOOK (~19%)
**Camera:** The lockbox on the counter: Kessa's hand on the lid. Inside, seven objects. Outside, the girl
with the open mark. The box held the evidence of the thread for forty years. Now the thread
is in the girl, and the box holds the history.

> **CAPTION (none spoken):**
> The box held the thread. The girl holds the thread. The box holds the history. Both are full.
>
> **CAPTION (smaller):**
> The first pull happened without pulling. The thread was given, not borrowed. The Loom's first
> law does not apply.

---


## Writing & art notes

- **The debtless mend leaves no debt-mark.** The palm afterwards must be on panel, clean, because the absence is the event.
- **The queue changes** as the basin watches. Keep hope small, practical and unremarked — never triumphant.
