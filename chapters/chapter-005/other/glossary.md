# Chapter 005 — Glossary


| Term | Devanagari | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **The First Pull** | पहला खिंचाव | Ira's mark opening and thread emerging for the first time. |
| **Blank strand** | रिक्त-तंतु | The sewer's strand: no Sector, no Kind, no debt. |
