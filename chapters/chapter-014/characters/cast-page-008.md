## IRA SUTAR (PROTAGONIST — the keeper who bills for a name)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png)

```
Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "It tells you exactly what it will not protect."
```
> **Page 008 note:** writes the demand's copy, sends it read and sealed, hears the machine's dodge
> and turns it inside out in one entry — a disavowal names what the Office will not protect, and
> something unprotected can be protected. Then enters protection of record for whoever keeps the
> night hour, in the clerk's own form, with the space for a name left empty. She does not read that
> one aloud. That is the point of it.

## KESSA (the till — the seal, the price, and the door)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png)

```
Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"Protection of a stranger costs everything to be accused of being a fool."
```
> **Page 008 note:** seals the demand and gives the runner the one instruction that keeps it from
> being a threat; then prices the blind protection before it is entered — favouritism on one side,
> foolishness on the other — and enters both halves. Her quiet line, *a thing it will not protect is
> a thing we can*, is the page's turn.

## NANDI (the reader — the demand's grammar, and its limit)
Full sheet (Ch. 006): [`../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md`](../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png`](../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png)

```
Nandi / Manav / Agnikhand / sight-white, lens-borne / reads, and is paid /
Read: thread-years at the price of an ask, properly made / "Four knots, then the question."
```
> **Page 008 note:** ties the demand in the machine's own grammar — using only the one word that
> means *this must be answered*, and refusing the rest — and teaches the tie to the school's women
> while she does it. Old woman's craft as diplomacy: the shortest demand is the one that cannot be
> refused for its manners.

## THE RUNNER (sixth appearance — the stair, the dodge, and a pause he was not told to take)
No full sheet; Ch. 004–006, 013–014. Junior clerk in plain grey, cap in hand at the counter, then a
small figure halfway up the Council Stair.

```
The Runner / Manav / Agnikhand / plain grey, uncommitted / paid by the road /
Carry: what a seal decides — and now, what a seal refuses to decide /
"It is not an answer. I am sorry."
```
> **Page 008 note:** reads the demand's terms twice up-terrace, walks back down with a slip that is
> only a seal and one knot, and says the one thing he was not told to say. The register protects the
> fold, so it does not enter his apology — but Kessa hears it, and so does the queue.

## THE COUNTING-LESSON WOMEN (the tie practised; the demand read aloud)
No full sheet; Ch. 011–014. On the benches with cords, learning the demand's four-knot grammar —
then one of them holds the sealed paper up at the window and reads it to the square before it goes.

```
The Counting-Lesson Women / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, learning / owe each other lessons /
Read: the machine's paper aloud — and now the counter's own /
"The counter asks who is there. Answer, or say plainly that you will not."
```
> **Page 008 note:** the school's second use of the grammar it learned two pages ago: not to read a
> hold, but to write a question. The elder reads the demand publicly — the counter's law applied to
> the counter's own paper — which is the page's quiet proof that the institution now polices itself.

## THE MACHINE (off-panel — the disavowal)
No full sheet; the Office entire. Present this page as a sealed slip, a single knot-sign, and the
oldest formula in its vocabulary.

```
The Machine / — / Agnikhand / nineteen years of white spaces / counts, and does not refuse /
Disavow: the act of an individual, without authority — not on any roll /
"Not on any roll. So answered."
```
> **Page 008 note:** answers a demand for a name with the sentence it used to erase the clerk
> (Ch. 013), and does not vary a word of it. The page lets the reader remember where the formula was
> last used; nobody in-panel points at it. The dodge is real, cheap and total — and it hands the
> counter a category it can protect.
