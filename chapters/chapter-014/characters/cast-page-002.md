## NANDI (the reader — two steps of the year, and one priced refusal)
Full sheet (Ch. 006): [`../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md`](../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png`](../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png)

```
Nandi / Manav / Agnikhand / sight-white, lens-borne / reads, and is paid /
Read: thread-years at the price of an ask, properly made /
"Ask me again when your line is paid. Forward, and whole."
```
> **Page 002 note:** enters Ira's debtor line in her OWN book — small, thick, older than any Office
> roll, the old grammar, no lettering — and unrolls the oldest rod a hand's width: thirty-nine
> season-knots, unbroken. Refuses the second-chain question at her price: she reads what is ON the
> thread, and the old school counts forward, not back. The only refusal in the basin that is honest
> about being one.

> **Page 002 note:** no lines this page; their faces are the basin's reading of the shortness — ten

## THE COUNT-HOUSE HAND (off-panel — the subtraction with a knot on it)
No full sheet; Ch. 013–014. Does not appear this page; the satchel speaks: two bundles of three,
re-tagged in the machine's grammar, the permit-ledger not in it.

```
The Count-House Hand / Manav / Agnikhand / dust, wax, snow / counts for wages /
Deliver: the machine's weeks, to the keeper's grammar /
*(this week: absent, and the absence tagged)*
```
> **Page 002 note:** his missing delivery is the page's cold fact. Whoever tied the new tag knew the
> counter reads everything now — a message, not a mishap. Entered as received. The register does not
> answer messages; it holds them until they mean something on their own.
