Silhouette only: heavy coat, snow-loaded shoulders, hood and collar built so the falling snow takes
the face; the head is a shape, never a face.

```
The Snow-Heavy Figure / Manav (presumed) / Agnikhand / none given — snow takes the face / uncollected /

*(he reads the board back by touch, and re-ties what he finds)*
```

> **Page 004 note:** at dusk, at the hatch board, reading the season notice back knot by knot —
> then changing two knots on one public cord in the machine's grammar. No dialogue, no eye contact,
> no perspective that could name him. His maintenance is a message the register refuses to answer
> directly: *I will keep my hold in your sight; leave it standing.* The page's answer is entered in
> Panel 7, and he never learns it.
