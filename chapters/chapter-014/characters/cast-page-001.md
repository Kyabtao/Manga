## REKHAK VAHNI (the volunteer — the fold, entered unread)

```
Rekhak Vahni / Manav / Agnikhand / cold-white Sight thread / deep debt /
Audit: reveal target's Kind, thread colour and debt level; each use adds 2 debt to self /
"Entered unread. Under protection. My name with it — the way you held his."
```
> **Page 001 note:** his paper — carried since before the counter existed, shown as a FOLD in Ch. 013
> — comes out of his coat for the second time ever and goes into the register UNOPENED. He does not
> show it; he asks for what the clerk got, on the first day it might matter. His thumb-mark goes into
> the wax beside the keeper's knot: the basin's grammar, chosen. What the paper IS stays his — the
> register protects the fold, not the contents. That is why it can be trusted with both.

## THE COUNT-HOUSE HAND (one-off — thin, and late)
No full sheet; Ch. 013–014. A lesser clerk, snow-bound, near midnight: the first late delivery, one
bundle where there are usually three, the permit-ledger missing. The machine is under strain too.

```
The Count-House Hand / Manav / Agnikhand / dust, wax, snow / counts for wages /
Deliver: the machine's weeks, to the keeper's grammar — late, and thin /
"Sign here. Only the one this week."
```
> **Page 001 note:** arrives in P7's background trudging through snow with a visibly thin satchel.
> No explanation is offered or asked for. The miss is entered; the thinness is entered as received;
> the permit-ledger's absence stays unspoken — the winter's cold current, on the record by omission.
