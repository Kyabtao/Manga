# Chapter 002 — Locations

Everything shown or named in Chapter 2, with continuity notes against Chapter 1.

---

## THE RECKONING OFFICE — उपर-छत लेखा-कक्ष — rows of black stone desks  (up-terrace)

First seen Chapter 1 Page 005 from the visitors' rail; first seen **from inside the rows** in
Chapter 2 Page 001 Panel 2. Long parallel rows of black stone desks under tall clean windows; no ash
reaches this height. Continuity: the visitors' rail is behind and above the rows; Lekh's sorting table
sits at the rail's end.

**New canon (Ch. 002 Page 001):** every desk re-stamps the night's records twice after the silent
second — two seals, half a finger apart, one page. The Office's fear is procedural: it files. The rows
are shot wide and faceless; no individual clerk gets a face until Lekh (already named, Ch. 1 Page 005).

**Light rule:** hard clean morning light, zero ash, no warm colour. The basin gets steam and ember;
the Office gets stone and silence. Never mix the palettes in one panel.

## REKHAK'S DESK — रेखक की मेज़ — bare stone, one chair

Chapter 1 Page 005: slate open, chain running. Chapter 2 Page 001 Panel 6: slate **closed**, chain
still running, collar high — the desk is bare except for the slip, because everything else has already
been filed twice. Continuity guard: the desk never holds personal objects. Not ever. His life is the
chain in his hand.

## THE KNOT & NAIL — गाँठ और कील — Kessa's pawn stall, basin market

Chapter 1: shutters up every page, lamp lit even at noon. **Chapter 2 Page 001 Panels 3–5: shutters
down before noon — first closure in four Unspoolings.** The shutter is Kessa's alarm bell and her only
permitted interiority: she thinks with wood and rope, never with monologue. Continuity: the
counter-edge frame from Chapter 1 Page 007 Panel 2 is the stall's signature shot; reuse it in daylight
with shutters falling at frame edge to signal closure.

## BASIN MARKET STREET — बाज़ार गली — steam-pot corner

Chapter 1 Page 003: the crowd that turned its back. Chapter 2 Page 001 Panel 1: the same corner one
morning later, steam off Guthli's pot, the market working one octave too quietly. Continuity: the
steam pot is the rumour's pulpit; whoever speaks at the pot is believed by the stall-row. Ash falls
here as always.

## THE KNOT & NAIL DOORSTEP — गाँठ-और-कील की देहरी — Ira's waiting crate

New in Chapter 2 Page 002: the upturned crate in front of the shut shutter, crate-lid as work bench.
This is where the basin comes to Ira while Kessa thinks — the mend moves from the alley mouth
(Ch. 001 Page 001) to the doorstep (Ch. 002 Page 002), i.e. **the mend follows the shutters**. Continuity:
the crate, the lid, and the shutter seam behind Ira's head are the shot's fixed furniture; reuse them
every page the stall stays shut.

## THE OFFICE THRESHOLD & LEKH'S RAIL TABLE — कक्ष-देहरी और आगंतुक-रेल मेज़

New in Chapter 2 Page 002 Panel 7: a single frame holding interior and exterior — Lekh's sorting table
at the visitors'-rail end (foreground, filing drawer at his knee) and, beyond the open door, the
sunlit terrace crossing. Continuity: the threshold is the only place the Office's stone light and the
terrace's sun light meet; keep both palettes in one frame but never blend them. The filing drawer takes
minute-chits; a minute-chit is the smallest record the Office keeps (smaller than a page, bigger than
a word).
