# Chapter 002 — Glossary

New or sharpened terms in Chapter 2. The merged series glossary lives on the website; this file is the
chapter-scoped canon of record. Devanagari column is the in-world script of Agnikhand record-keeping.

| Term | Devanagari | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Re-stamp** | पुनर्मुहरण | Two seals on one page: the Council's way of making a record deniable. Up-terrace fear grammar — *"it files."* Ch. 002 Page 001 Panel 2. |
| **Call-slip** | तलब-पत्र | A summons delivered by clerk's hand, seal unbroken until the addressee. Procedure made physical. Ch. 002 Page 001 Panels 6–7. |
| **Grey wax** | धूसर मोम | The Council's seal colour — hard canon. Crimson belongs to no Sector on record; therefore any crimson seal is the principal's paperwork wearing procedure as a coat. Ch. 002 Page 001 Panel 7. |

## Usage notes
- The re-stamp is the first time fear appears in the series **as process** rather than as a face. Keep it
  bureaucratic, quiet, and doubled: two seals, half a finger apart, same page.
- Crimson wax may appear on paper again in Chapter 2, but never on a person, a door, or a gift. The
  principal acts through paperwork only (canon rule from `chapter-001/chapter-summary.md`).
- Neither term is spoken aloud on Page 001. They are seen, not named — the naming happens in Page 002
  dialogue at the earliest.
