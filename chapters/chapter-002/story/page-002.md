## PANEL 1 — Wide (~14%)

**Camera:** Basin market at noon, one day after the shutters came down. Slightly low, looking along
the stall-row so the closed Knot & Nail shutter fills the left third.
**Image:** The **Knot & Nail, shutters down** in full daylight — second day running; the market flows
around it one octave too quiet, nobody quite looking at it. On her upturned crate in front of the
shutter sits **IRA**, mender's kit open on her knee, not working: waiting. From the stair-mouth
**BHAN** approaches — dock worker, broad, salt-caked, left sleeve rolled to the shoulder: a raw
**torn edge** shows on his forearm mark. Behind, Guthli's steam pot works; its steam curls past the
shut stall like it's checking on it.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice, small):**
> Kessa shut the stall before noon and didn't say why. So I sit outside. Menders wait at the tear.

---

## PANEL 2 — Close (~13%)

**Camera:** Down onto the crate lid used as a work bench. Bhan's left forearm laid across it.
**Image:** The **tear**: same fray, same jagged mouth as the alley mend one day and one Unspooling
ago — and around it, still punched in the healed skin, **the thread-holes of Ira's old stitch**, empty.
Same tear. Same hour of work undone. Bhan's other hand rests steady; his face, at frame edge, is not
angry. Patient. That is worse. Ira's cut-finger glove hovers one inch above the fray, not touching.

> **BHAN (dialogue, flat):**
> Same tear. Same hour undid it. Night shift, full hold, nothing touched it.
>
> **IRA (dialogue, small):**
> Hold still. This'll pull.

## PANEL 3 — Medium (~14%)

**Camera:** Side-on at the doorstep, waist-up both of them, the shut shutter as backdrop.
**Image:** The mend underway: Ira's **curved needle** and its glowing filament stitching the fray's
edge, exactly her alley posture from Chapter 001 Page 001 — same tilt of head, same braced wrist.
Mid-stitch, at head height behind her, the **shutter opens a finger-width slit**. No face in it. No
eye. Only a line of interior dark and one thin bar of daylight falling across Ira's working hands.
Bhan watches the slit, not the needle.

> **SFX:** *tchk* (needle through skin — the same one as the alley)
>
> **CAPTION (none spoken):**
> Kessa thinks in wood and rope. This is her listening.

---

## PANEL 4 — Macro (~15%)

**Camera:** Extreme close on the needle-eye and the stitch-line, shallow focus; Ira's face soft at
frame top.
**Image:** The filament **doubles**. From the palm-side of Ira's glove — the stitched side, the sealed
side — a second, softer thread of light rises into the needle **unasked**, braiding with her own. Her
breath eases. Her shoulders drop a finger-width. Her eyes go soft the way hands go soft in warm water.
She does not stop it. Why would she? It feels like kindness.
**No debt-mark blooms on either of them. Nothing was pulled. Nothing is owed.**

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice, smaller):**
> And then the work got easy. I didn't ask why. That's the part I keep coming back to.

---

## PANEL 5 — Close (~13%)

**Camera:** On Bhan's forearm, mend finished; the shutter slit behind already **closed**.
**Image:** The finished seam: neat, tight, her best alley-grade stitch — and inside the stitch-line,
faint as a thumbprint in dough, a **whorl-grain**: the uncanny whorl's fingerprint pressed into the
thread's lie. No glow. No owner. Texture, not announcement. Bhan's thumb passes over it without
thinking and finds nothing to feel. Ira knots off, bites the filament, looks up at the shut shutter.

> **IRA (dialogue, to the shutter, quiet):**
> I'll be outside. Every day.

---

## PANEL 6 — Medium close (~14%) — HOOK

**Camera:** Two-shot at the crate; Bhan rolling his sleeve down, Ira's hands stopped mid-packing.
**Image:** Bhan flexes the mended arm once, twice — and stops flexing. His face is not grateful. It is
**unsettled**, the face of a man whose stair has one more step than he counted. Ira's kit-half-packed
hands go still at exactly the same moment; her mismatched eyes on his sleeve, not his face.

> **BHAN (dialogue, slow):**
> It doesn't itch anymore.
>
> **CAPTION (Ira's voice, small):**
> Fray-adjacent marks itch. Always. Ask anyone in the basin. *Always.*

## PANEL 7 — Wide (~17%) — the other room

**Camera:** Up-terrace, Office threshold: interior foreground, exterior beyond the door, one frame.
Clean light, zero ash.
**Image:** Foreground: **LEKH seated** at the visitors'-rail table (he is always seated or leaving),
sliding a **minute-chit** into the filing drawer with two fingers, eyes already on the next slate —
filing the chair-empty minute like a record. Beyond the open door, small in clean sunlight:
**REKHAK** crossing the terrace **walking** toward the re-review, crimson call-slip unbroken in one
hand, brass counting-chain **running** in the other. Procedure does not worry. It files.

> **CAPTION (none spoken):**
> Up-terrace, the hour's audit walks to meet its re-review. The Office notes the minute its Reckoner's
> chair stood empty. Two seals would be fear. One is only habit.

## Writing & art notes

- **Chain-stop budget: NOT spent.** The chain runs in Panel 7 and nowhere stops. Chapter 2's single
  stop stays unspent until late, and on something smaller than the truth.
- **Power-without-debt-mark, briefed exception:** the fold's lending creates no debt because nothing is
  pulled; the rule "no power without an on-panel mark" is satisfied in fold-form — the **whorl-grain**
  in the seam is the mark. It gets **no owner on panel**: nobody names it, nobody reacts to it as a
  sign. Bhan's thumb finds "nothing to feel".
- **The bleed feels like kindness.** Ira does not fear it in Panel 4; she relaxes into it. Her later
  horror (much later) retro-dates to this frame.
- **Kessa = shutters only.** No eye in the slit, no hand, no interior monologue, no panel inside the
  stall. The slit opens (P3) and is closed (P5). That is her whole performance this page.
- **Bhan's patience is the threat register.** A torn mark that re-tears identically with "nothing
  touched it" is sabotage grammar; do not let any character say so this page.
- **Echo rhyme:** Panel 2's "Hold still. This'll pull." and the *tchk* SFX repeat Chapter 001 Page 001
  Panel 3 verbatim — the alley mend and the doorstep mend must read as one gesture twice.
- **Rumour register untouched:** nobody on this page mentions blinking, the Spindle, or the silent
  second. The market's quiet is weather, not testimony.
- The Loom never speaks. No hum change on panel. The four notes are not hummed by anyone.
