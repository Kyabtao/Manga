## LEKH — लेख  (PAGE LEAD)
First named Ch. 002 Page 001; seated-or-leaning rule holds through Panels 1–7 until the final caption.
This page gives him his spine: the uninitialled cross-out forces the gap between *clerks never stand*
and *clerks never lie in ink*, and he moves into that gap — filing broken, keeping an off-book
minute-chit in his cuff.

```
Lekh / Manav / Agnikhand / pale cyan (unspent) / light debt /
files what he is given, remembers what he files / "Filed as found."
```
> **The stand:** the last three words of the page ("He stands up.") are his only standing in Chapter 2,
> and it is for his own record, not for a person. The Ch. 001 reservation (standing *for someone*,
> later arc) remains unspent and must stay different when it pays off.
