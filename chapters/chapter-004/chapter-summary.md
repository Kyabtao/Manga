## Canon rules added in Chapter 4 (binding forward)

1. **The cutter graduates**: from practice (Bhan's arm) to demonstration (Ira's stitch pattern)
   to lesson completion (sealing the arm permanently). The cutter's last act is payment: a seventh
   thread on Bhan's heart, with a child's first knot at one end.
2. **The stitch opens**: loop by loop, Pages 004–008. The old thread — the sewer's thread —
   releases cleanly, undamaged. The stitch was perfect work; its perfection allows clean release.
3. **The Sutra-mark is visible**: projection type, dormant but not dead. The mark was hidden behind
   the stitch for fifteen years. It carries a **braided thread**: two strands, one Ira's own, one
   the sewer's. The sewer braided their thread into Ira's mark before she could speak.
4. **The braided thread leaks**: the mark's dormant braid bleeds into Ira's mending thread. Her
   mend is now braided — two strands, stronger, still debtless.
5. **The mark flickers** at the proximity of evidence (Page 010). Not open; not pulling. Just
   flickering, the way a candle decides whether to stay lit.
6. **The cutter's child's knot**: the seventh thread carries a first-knot at one end — the kind a
   Kshudra child ties when learning to thread. The thread spans forty years, from the first knot
   to the last.
7. **The lockbox holds seven objects**: cut-end, census, posting order, gift-thread, sixth thread,
   seventh thread (child's knot), and the sewer's thread from Ira's palm.
8. **Lekh's cuff-chit grows**: he files the lockbox's existence (Page 005). The cuff now holds
   three entries.
9. **Kessa's hands tremble** for the first time (Page 005): the weight of the evidence.
10. **The Loom never speaks.** The Spindle is steady. Ash falls as always.
