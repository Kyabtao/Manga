# THREADBORN — Chapter 06 — Page 003

**Chapter title:** *The Mother's File* / *माँ की फ़ाइल*
**Arc:** II — The Mendery
**Sector:** Agnikhand — The Mendery archive
**Page type:** The archive's keeper — 7 panels, hook at bottom

> Beats: Page 002 — the charter (School of the Braided Thread, founder Sutar), the three rules,
> the binding reason (unlicensed), the principal planned for Ira.

---

## PANEL 1 — Wide, the archive at depth (~14%)

**Camera:** Deep in the archive, past the Kshudra back wall. Ira has moved beyond the charter to
the files behind it — older still, on shelves that have not been touched in decades. Dust
thick enough to write in.

**Image:** Ira's gloved hand trails through the dust. The mark's light illuminates the shelf.
Behind the charter, one more file: labelled in the same knot-script, but older.

> **CAPTION (none spoken):**
> Behind the charter, one more file. The oldest in the archive.

---

## PANEL 2 — Medium, the voice (~13%)

**Camera:** From the archive's entrance, a voice — dry, old, Kshudra-accented. Not Kessa's
voice. A different Kshudra. A figure stands in the doorway: small, broad, ancient, with hands
as large as Kessa's but older. A Kshudra who has been here longer than the files.

**Image:** The figure is silhouetted in the doorway. One eye catches the light — a Kshudra cat-eye,
but dimmer than Kessa's. This Kshudra is very old.

> **VOICE (dialogue, dry, from the door):**
> You're reading files that haven't been opened in twenty years.
>
> **VOICE (dialogue):**
> Your mother used to stand in the same spot.

---

## PANEL 3 — Close, the keeper (~14%)

**Camera:** The figure steps into the light: a very old Kshudra woman, smaller than Kessa, hands
gnarled, wearing a Mendery keeper's apron — grey, patched, older than the archive. Her name is
**Nandi** — the Mendery's keeper, the woman who has maintained the archive since before Ira's
mother was bound.

**Image:** Nandi's face is a map of the Mendery's history. Her loupe is not a jeweller's loupe
but a *thread-reader's* loupe — wider, older, the kind Kshudra used before the Council regulated
them.

> **NANDI (dialogue, dry):**
> I'm Nandi. I keep the files. Your mother kept the school. We kept each other company.

---

## PANEL 4 — Medium, the recognition (~13%)

**Camera:** Nandi's eyes on Ira's palm — the braided mark, open, glowing. Nandi's hands
tremble — not from age. From recognition.

**Image:** Nandi sees the mark and knows it. Her hands reach toward it — not to touch, to
*read*. The same gesture as Kessa, but older, slower.

> **NANDI (dialogue, slow):**
> The braid. Her braid. She said you'd come.
>
> **NANDI (dialogue):**
> She said the thread would bring you.

---

## PANEL 5 — Close, the history (~14%)

**Camera:** Nandi and Ira in the archive, the charter between them. Nandi's hand on the charter:
she can read the knot-script without a mark's help. She has read it every day for twenty years.

**Image:** Two Kshudra hands and one girl's hand on the same document. The charter is the bridge
between the keeper and the daughter.

> **NANDI (dialogue):**
> Your mother founded the school in this archive. She taught the braided thread to anyone who
> could hold a needle. The Council shut her down. The principal filed the crimson.
>
> **NANDI (dialogue):**
> I kept the files. She kept the thread. And you—

---

## PANEL 6 — Wide, the pause (~13%)

**Camera:** Nandi stops. Her eyes are on the braided mark — the blank strand, the sewer's strand,
the strand that has no Sector, no Kind, no debt. Nandi recognizes the strand.

**Image:** Nandi's face: she knows what the blank strand is. She knows who the teacher was. And
she is choosing whether to say it.

> **NANDI (dialogue, after a long pause):**
> The teacher's strand. The blank one. Your mother braided it into you herself.

---

## PANEL 7 — Macro, the revelation — HOOK (~19%)

**Camera:** Ira's face: the revelation. The sewer — the teacher — the person who sewed her palm
shut — was her *mother*. The blank strand in the braid is her mother's thread. The cut-end in
Kessa's box is her mother's thread. The supply line that ran through Bhan's arm is her mother's
thread. One woman, twenty years, the same hand-school.

**Image:** Ira's mismatched eyes, wide. Not shock. *Understanding.* The stitch, the box, the
cutter, the supply line — all her mother.

> **NANDI (dialogue, quiet):**
> Your mother sewed you shut, child. To protect you. To hide you. To keep the thread alive.
>
> **NANDI (dialogue):**
> She is the sewer. She is the cutter. She is the hand-school. And she has been running the
> supply line from inside this archive for twenty years.

---

## Writing & art notes

- **Nandi** is a new character: the Mendery's archive keeper, old Kshudra, has been here since
  before Ira's mother was bound. She knows the truth about the braid.
- **The revelation:** the sewer, the cutter, and the supply line are all Ira's mother. One woman,
  twenty years, operating from inside the Mendery. The thread-school was her creation; the stitch
  on Ira's palm was her protection.
- **The blank strand is the mother's thread** — the teacher's strand sewn into the student before
  speech. This recontextualizes every piece of evidence in the lockbox.
- **Nandi's recognition of the blank strand** is the emotional climax: she has been waiting
  twenty years for the girl to come.
- **Chain budget: unspent.** Four notes silent. Loom never speaks.
