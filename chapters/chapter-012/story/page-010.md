# THREADBORN — Chapter 12 — Page 010

**Chapter title:** *The Whole Roll*
**Arc:** III — The Second School
**Sector:** Agnikhand — the alley, the low gate, the archive, the square, the stall, the step at dusk
**Page type:** Chapter finale — 7 panels, ends with the roll whole and the sentence whole

> Beats: **the lesson finishes** — the three mid-thread hands hold steady at last, and Nima judges
> them taught; **the open walk** — the whole school to the low gate, the basin after it, the coat
> writing; **the tying-in** — Nima's hands tie the three in, then Nandi, price paid to the hour
> (*when the school ties a hand in, it ties mine*); **the founder enters** — the loose entry's long
> end met by the roll at last, and the daughter's gloved hands pull it tight; **the Office names its
> trap** — the count stops at the twenty-two, publicly, by the clerk's own mouth; **the counter-book**
> — the register open at the Mendery counter, the basin's second Office; and **the step at dusk** —
> the direction intact, the four-note sentence whole, and a page-sized silence where the fifth note
> used to hang.
>
> **This page:** a chapter ending the way a knot ends — pulled tight, no slack, nothing left to tie.
>
> **Chapter 012 budget:** ONE chain-stop, UNspent — the chapter keeps its unspent budget to the last
> page: the Office never gets its hardware moment. Mother never on panel, never speaks — her entrance
> is a knot tightening, and it is the biggest thing in the chapter. Loom never speaks, does not appear.

## PANEL 1 — Medium, the alley, morning (~14%)

**Camera:** Medium: the alley, morning, the grey lamp burning in daylight. The circle around the
crate — and in it, the **three mid-thread hands**, each holding a stranger's hand **steady**: not
badly, not bravely. Steady. **Nima** watches, arms folded, and gives the basin her judgment with a
single nod.

**Image:** Taught. The school's own word for it, given by the school's own mouth.

> **NIMA:**
> *(to the circle, level)*
> They hold. That is the whole of the lesson. The rest is years.
>
> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> The school finishes when Nima says it finishes. She says it on a morning like any other, over a
> crate, with ash in the air — and the roll that has been whole-but-for-three since the winter is
> whole at the next tying.

---

## PANEL 2 — Wide, the open walk (~13%)

**Camera:** Wide: the procession out — the school to the low gate in the open, in daylight: Nima,
the three, Kessa riding the cart with her box, Nandi in her shawl with the lens at her throat, and
the basin trailing half a street behind. On the rim road's edge, at the fixed interval, the **grey
coat** — walking with them now, writing.

**Image:** Nobody sneaks. The school was never a secret. Today the walk is the announcement.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> We go in the front, the way we went in the first time, and the basin comes to watch, and the coat
> comes to be exact. Let the Office read its own report and choke on it: *the tally-keeper entered
> the school. The school entered the school. Nobody stopped anybody.*

---

## PANEL 3 — Wide, the tying-in (~16%)

**Camera:** Wide: the archive, lamplit, racks and all — the basin packed to the walls and spilling
into the gallery. At the oldest rack, **Nima's hands** tie the three: entry-knots, tightened, one by
one — the pupils entered by their teacher. And then, to the hour, her price: Nima turns to
**Nandi**, and Nandi — chin up, lens at her throat — holds out both wrists like a woman being
measured for cuffs, and is tied in.

**Image:** The Office's most ruined reader, entered by the school that ruined her — at her own
asking, at her own price.

> **NANDI:**
> *(as the knot goes tight)*
> Forty years I served a book that lost my page.
>
> *(looking up, wet-eyed, exact)*
>
> Here is a book that keeps what it counts. Enter me whole.
>
> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> The basin does not cheer. It does something better: it goes quiet, the way a room goes quiet when
> a debt is actually paid.

---

## PANEL 4 — Close, the founder enters (~17%)

**Camera:** Close: the last knot in the room. The oldest rod, held level — the year-knot at its head,
the founder's whorled entry below it, and the **loose tying-in** at its end, long silvered thread
lying down the step toward the door. **Ira's** gloved hands come into frame: one hand takes the long
end, one hand takes the roll's own standing thread, and the pull — slow, deliberate, all the way —
**tightens the founder's entry into the school's roll.**

**Image:** She never appears. She does not have to. The knot tightening IS her entrance.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> Nobody tightens it but the book that counts her fair. The book is here. It has been here the whole
> time, in a wet room, in thread, kept by her hand — and the last hand it was waiting for is mine.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> The whorl enters beside the year. The founder of the grammar both books count in stands entered,
> whole, by her own mark, in her own school — and the note in my register is still her no, still on
> file, still older than their roll. She stands in the count that is hers and no other's.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> Do not enrol me, she wrote, forty years ago. Nobody has. The school does not enrol. It ties in.
> *There is a difference, and the difference is the whole chapter.*

---

## PANEL 5 — Medium, the Office names its trap (~15%)

**Camera:** Medium: the square, midday, the list-post. **The grey clerk**, composed, reads the
Office's progress to the basin from a slate of knot-marks — and stops. Lays the slate down on the
post's ledge. Faces the basin he posted to.

**Image:** The trap has to be named by its builder to close. He names it.

> **GREY CLERK:**
> *(public, level, terrible in its fairness)*
> The count has reached the nameless. The count cannot proceed. The founder of the roll stands
> entered upon the school's roll — and stands against entry upon ours, by her word, on file, older
> than this Office's paper. The Office will not take that roll while her word stands.
>
> **GREY CLERK:**
> The count is suspended. Not postponed. *Suspended.*
>
> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> And there it is, in front of the whole basin, by the Office's own mouth: the postponement was a
> clock. The suspension is an admission. They built a machine out of my mother's silence, and the
> same silence has stopped it cold — *and every soul in this square heard the machine get named.*

---

## PANEL 6 — Medium, the counter-book (~12%)

**Camera:** Medium: the Mendery counter, dusk, the lamp lit. The **register** lies open — the
archive pocket fat now: the no, the ask, the reading, the refusal, and the day's last thing: a slip
of the school's own knot-work recording the tying-in, witnessed. **Kessa** across the counter, box
closed, fold inside, watching Ira file the day.

**Image:** The basin's second Office, open for business, one book older than the first.

> **KESSA:**
> The box kept a pledge forty years and never asked whose. Your book keeps a roll nine days old and
> already the street brings it its paper.
>
> **IRA:**
> *(not looking up from the entry)*
> It is not a rival, Kessa. It is a keep.
>
> **KESSA:**
> *(taking up her thread)*
> So was the box, keeper. So was the box.

---

## PANEL 7 — Medium-close, the step at dusk — FINAL HOOK (~13%)

**Camera:** Medium-close: the step at dusk. The **direction knot**, silver, intact, tail down-lane.
Beside it, nothing — the space where the loose entry lay is swept clean; the entry is in the book
where it belongs. Ira sits on the step with her hands empty. And over the lane, faint and unhurried,
the **four-note sentence** comes back around — the oldest thing in her life, whole again, nothing
added, nothing hanging.

**Image:** The roll is whole. The sentence is whole. What is left is the number that does not fit —
and the door it belongs to.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> The fifth note does not come tonight. It does not need to. The question it asked has been answered
> in thread, at the archive, by everybody's hands but hers.
>
> **CAPTION (smallest):**
> The roll is whole, keeper.

## Writing & art notes

- **The page is one long exhale, and it must earn it.** Every beat lands in order: taught, walked,
- tied, entered, named, filed, whole. No new twists, no reversals — the chapter spent nine pages
  buying this page and the page must not flinch from being simple. The drama is all in knots and
  admissions; the art stays warm-lit and still.
- **Panel 3's tying-in must be procedural and tender at once.** Nima's hands are the school's hands:
  pupils entered by their teacher, one by one, no ceremony beyond the knot. Nandi's line is the
  chapter's debt paid on the page (*enter me whole*) — do not give her more than the two short
  speeches; her face does the rest. Wet-eyed, exact, chin up.
- **Panel 4 is the chapter's peak and it must be hands.** No face for the mother — the entrance is
  the pull. Two gloved hands, a long silvered end, a standing thread, and the slow draw tight. The
  captions carry the chapter's legal close: *the school does not enrol. It ties in. There is a
  difference, and the difference is the whole chapter.* That sentence is the thesis — say it once,
  and let the page rest on it.
- **Panel 5: the clerk must be fair.** The horror of the Office is procedure, and the beauty of this
  beat is that the procedure is honest: he names the founder's no, on file, older than his paper —
  and suspends, not postpones. *Suspended* is an admission dressed as a ruling; the caption says so
  once. Do not let him sneer, break, or threaten. He reads the machine its own name and stops the
  clock with it. The amendment stays armed and unsigned on Room Nine's table — the clerk's silence
  about it IS his defeat; no one mentions it on-panel.
- **Panel 6 is the register's coronation, played small.** Nine days old and the street brings it
  paper. Kessa gets the page's last warmth and its best echo: *so was the box, keeper.* The fold
  stays in the box; the note stays filed; nothing is surrendered.
- **Panel 7 closes the ladder.** The chapter's smallest captions — knock / ask him / read it back /
  sign nothing / keep it better / ask her / tie her in — land at last on the only flat sentence
  left: **"The roll is whole, keeper."** The fifth note is answered by silence; the four-note
  sentence is whole again; *year nineteen* is deliberately left UNspoken on-panel — it goes to the
  chapter-summary's last line, where the next chapter's clock starts.
- **Continuity:** chain-stop budget Chapter 012: ONE, **unspent to the end** — the Office never
  gets its hardware moment; the chest stays shut. Mother never on panel, never speaks — the
  tightening is her entrance. Loom absent. The three new hands remain UNNAMED to the last (entered
  by knot, never by name — the chapter's discipline holds). Nandi's price is paid to the hour. The
  amendment original stays armed on Room Nine's table. The ask-log's first line stands. The
  direction knot stays tied (it was retied on Page 009 and is never untied again). The step's
  swept space: the loose entry is gone into the book. The four-note sentence is whole; no fifth
  note tonight. The grey lamp burns in daylight over everything (Panels 1, 2).
- **§3.1:** no electric light, no modern furniture, no printed signage; all record-work is knots,
  rods, paper stock and seals — no lettering in art anywhere. The clerk's slate shows knot-marks
  only.
- **Numbers:** three hands taught and tied; one price paid to the hour; one founder entered; one
  count suspended (not postponed); one register, nine days old; one sentence, four notes, whole; one
  chapter, ten pages, closed. Chain-stop ONE, unspent.
