# THREADBORN — Chapter 12 — Page 002

**Chapter title:** *The Whole Roll* / *पूरी पंजी*
**Arc:** III — The Second School
**Sector:** Agnikhand — Ashfall Basin, the stall at dawn, the rim road, the low gate
**Page type:** The walk — 7 panels, ends at the first door, unopened

> Beats: **the departure** — the stall keeps, the note travels, and custody is named as weight: *the
> tether travels with it*; **the lie that is true** — Nima takes the lantern back and hands Ira the
> best cover story in the basin: *you are mending a drain*; **honest at any fee** — the Office buys
> eyes for the week and the reader takes the warrant, because his fee runs on exactness; **the seals
> go around** — behind the low gate, a gallery wall the Office reseals season after season, and the
> seals have always gone around one small door; **filed as nothing** — the log plate says *no hands*,
> the only true line the Office ever filed.
>
> **This page:** the quietest open in the series. Everything was decided on Page 001; this is
> geography.
>
> **Chapter 012 budget:** ONE chain-stop, unspent. Mother never on panel, never speaks — her question
> from Page 001 is being answered by walking, not by words. Loom never speaks, and does not appear —
> its last word rides shut in the Office's chest.

## PANEL 1 — Medium, dawn, the shuttered stall (~12%)

**Camera:** Medium: the stall at first light, shutters down. **Kessa** on the step with the empty box
in her lap. **Ira** in her walking coat — the note going into the inside pocket, where the important
papers ride.

**Image:** Custody changes hands a second time in two days, and this time both hands know the weight.

> **KESSA:**
> The stall keeps. I keep. Go.
>
> **KESSA:**
> *(not looking up)*
> A pledge in a pocket still pulls, keeper. That is what carrying is. The tether travels with it — so
> walk like the weight is yours, because it is.
>
> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> She is the only person in the basin who has never once asked me where I am going. This morning she
> is the only one who already knows.

---

## PANEL 2 — Wide, the alley, the two lamps (~14%)

**Camera:** Wide: the alley at dawn. **Nima** hangs the school's brass lantern back on its hook
inside; at the alley mouth the **grey lamp** keeps burning in daylight, the way it has since the
survey. At the circle's edge, the **three new faces**, early again.

**Image:** Two lamps, one alley. The grey one watches the door; the brass one teaches at it.

> **NIMA:**
> If anyone asks, you are mending a drain.
>
> **IRA:**
> That is not a lie.
>
> **NIMA:**
> It is the best kind. It becomes one later.
>
> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> The lantern goes back to the school, and the school goes on being watched, and inside all that,
> three hands I could not name are learning the holding. Every day I am gone, the roll gets less
> whole. Walk faster.

---

## PANEL 3 — Close, the knot on the step (~12%)

**Camera:** Close: the Mendery step. Last night's knot is still tied to nothing — old style,
shadowless — and its tail lies flat along the stone, pointing down-lane: the way that runs to the
basin's low gate. Ira crouches, reads it, and leaves it exactly as it is.

**Image:** Directions are not letters. You do not erase them. Untied, they are only thread.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> A knot tied the old way says *here, then there.* This one says: down-lane, up the ash-slope, the low
> gate. It is not a map. It is better — it is the shortest line her thread would take.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> I leave it tied. It is the only thing of hers that has ever pointed at me, and I will not be the one
> to untie it.

---

## PANEL 4 — Medium, the Office buys eyes (~14%)

**Camera:** Medium: the Office, morning. The **grey clerk** slides a day-warrant across the table to
**Rekhak Vahni** — blank paper, one knot-seal. Behind them the chest, shut, one dark cord-end showing
at its seam.

**Image:** The count is by mouth now, and mouths are slow. So the Office rents the fastest eyes it has
ever paid for.

> **GREY CLERK:**
> The count is by mouth. Mouths are fed. Eyes are cheaper. Where she walks.
>
> **REKHAK:**
> *(taking the warrant)*
> I report what I see. Exactly. You know my fee runs on exactness.
>
> **GREY CLERK:**
> I am counting on it. It is the only honest arithmetic left in this building.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> The count, it seems, is by mouth. The week is by eyes.

---

## PANEL 5 — Wide, the rim road (~14%)

**Camera:** Wide, high: the rim road climbing the ash-slope, mid-morning. The basin below — the square
a scatter of roofs, the alley a line, the grey lamp a pinprick still burning. On the road, one walker.
Far behind, at a fixed interval that never changes, a **grey coat.**

**Image:** The tail does not hurry. Paid men never hurry; wages arrive tomorrow too.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> Since the second switchback: a coat the grey of paperwork, always the same distance back. Never
> closer. Never gone.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> If it is who I think it is, he reports exactly what he sees — his fee runs on it. So let him see
> exactly this: the keeper of the register, walking to the low gate on a mend.

---

## PANEL 6 — Wide, the low gate, the seal wall (~17%)

**Camera:** Wide: the sluice gallery behind the low gate — a channel cut into living rock, black with
wet ash; the stone slab that has held the basin's water back longer than the Office has held its roll.
On the gallery's inner wall, seasons of **Office seals** — layer over layer, stamps over knot-marks
over stamps — and in the middle of all of it, a **door-shaped absence** where no seal has ever gone.

**Image:** The Office maintains this wall the way a body maintains skin. It has never once sealed the
door.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> The log plate by the frame says what the wall is: *void, no hands.* Renewed every season, in the
> Office's own hand, for as long as there have been seasons to renew it in.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> No hands. The Office's own seal-log — the only line in all its paper I have ever believed.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> Every seal on this wall goes around the door. Not one goes across it. Whatever they are keeping out
> of their books, their own paste has been holding its place for them.

---

## PANEL 7 — Medium, the door — FINAL HOOK (~17%)

**Camera:** Medium: the door itself. Older masonry, a different bond from the gallery's living rock —
set into it, small, a door with **no lock, no bar, and not one seal on it.** On the lintel, a faint
old knot-mark — the same old school of thread as the knot on my step. Ira's hand is raised, not
touching. And at the gallery mouth behind her, thin daylight breaks around a **grey coat** that has
kept its distance all morning. Arrived.

**Image:** The first door. Unopened. It was never hiding. It was filed.

> **CAPTION (Ira's voice):**
> No lock. No bar. Not one seal. They pasted paper over everything their books could see, and never
> once touched the one thing that was looking back at them.
>
> **CAPTION:**
> It was never hiding. It was filed.
>
> **CAPTION (smallest):**
> Knock, keeper.

## Writing & art notes

- **This is the quietest open the series has had, and it must stay quiet.** Everything was decided on
  Page 001; Page 002 is geography. The walk reads as work, not quest — dawn light, a road, a
  destination with a drain on it. No one explains anything on-panel louder than Nima's two lines.
- **The lie that is true is the page's engine.** Nima's cover story (*mending a drain*) is literally
  accurate and becomes false only at the very last panel — because what an honest witness sees at the
  gallery is not a drain, it is a door. Ira's Panel 5 caption turns the tail's honesty into her cover:
  *let him see exactly this.* The cover dies at the door. That is the cliffhanger.
- **Rekhak is honest at any fee, and that is the consequence engine.** He took the warrant because
  exactness is his product. He will report the door — or report *a keeper at a wall filed void*, which
  is worse. What the clerk does with an exact report is Page 003's problem. Play him completely
  unthreatening on-panel: no menace, just arithmetic.
- **The two lamps carry the theme while nobody talks.** The grey lamp (Chapter 011) watches the alley
  in daylight; the brass lantern goes back to the school's hook inside it. The school teaches on while
  the keeper walks — which is exactly what makes the roll less whole. The three new faces are early
  again; unnamed, unentered, uncounted.
- **The knot stays tied.** Mother's channel discipline: the direction is left on the step, not
  carried, not erased — *untied, it is only thread.* It is also the one thing of hers that has ever
  pointed at Ira; the refusal to untie it is the closest this page comes to sentiment. Do not add
  more.
- **Art: the seal wall is the money shot and it must be all blank.** Layered grey paper seals — stamps
  over knot-marks over stamps, no lettering anywhere — with one clean door-shaped absence. The log
  plate shows knot-marks only; the prose carries the reading (*void, no hands*). The door itself:
  older masonry, a visibly different stone bond, no lock, no bar, no seal, one faint old knot-mark on
  the lintel. **Do not put a whorl on the lintel** — the maker's-mark question stays open for a later
  page.
- **Continuity:** chain-stop budget Chapter 012: ONE, unspent. Mother never on panel, never speaks —
  her only channel this page is the knot already delivered. Loom never speaks and does not appear (the
  chest stays shut; one cord-end at the seam is allowed). The grey lamp and the lantern-hook are
  Chapter 011 fixtures. The note rides in Ira's inside pocket — the tether travels. The three new
  hands are not named, not entered, not counted.
- **§3.1:** no electric light, no modern furniture, no printed signage; the day-warrant is blank paper
  with a knot-seal. No lettering in any panel.
- **Numbers (kept small on purpose — a walk, not a ledger):** one day's walk there and back; the tail
  appears at the second switchback and its interval never changes; two lamps in one alley; one door;
  zero locks.
