## THE FIRST DOOR — YEAR NINETEEN — पहला दरवाज़ा — वर्ष उन्नीस

Where the directions lead. The knot on the keeper's step is old-style, shadowless, tied to nothing — the
kind of knot the basin stopped tying decades ago — and it says *here, then there.* The number that
closes the page is the thread-year that does not fit the basin's roll: the school's teaching is older
than any list the Office keeps. The door itself is not yet seen. It is owed a page of its own.
