**Page note:** two clocks running, one dusk. Out of Room Nine with the thesis (*I signed nothing; he
wrote everything*) converting into the page's discovery — a filed refusal is a shut door, and shut
doors open from either side, later. The race said plain at the gate (*exact at the end*); the count
audible over the yard wall, stumbling at the twenty-two; the school answering by continuing — three
mid-thread hands inside the circle now, holding other hands badly, watched like sand in a glass; the
posted paper kept alive by dusk readers. Home: *boxes first, wars after.* And the counter at
lamp-low: three papers out of one pocket, the register opened flat — a no, an ask, a reading, and a
refusal, everything the basin has that the Office wants and cannot take. Hook: **"A second Office,
keeper. Keep it better."**
