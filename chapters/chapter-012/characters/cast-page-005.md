
---

**Page note:** the basin's one newspaper, read aloud. The definition verbatim — *the count will be
taken when the roll is whole; the roll is whole when every hand taught by the school stands entered
upon it* — then the reading of record: the school's roll is whole, as of the last finished teaching.
The clerk's grammar (*the Office's roll, obviously*) fails against a basin that remembers what was
posted; his hand rises to take the paper down and drops — posted means posted, and taking it down
unmakes every paper the Office ever posted. The clock turns in public: three mid-thread faces become
the race between a mouth-count and a school. And the paper moves: he came with both papers, and the
one that comes out is a summons. Hook: **"Bring your own law, keeper."**
