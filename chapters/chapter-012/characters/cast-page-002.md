## IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार  (PROTAGONIST — the walker)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png)

```
Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "Let him see exactly this: the keeper of the register, walking to the low gate on a mend."
```
> **Page 002 note:** walks the chapter's answer instead of speaking it. Reads the step-knot literally
> and refuses to untie it; turns the tail's paid honesty into her cover — *let him see a drain-mend* —
> and is the first person in the basin's living memory to stand before the door and read the one line
> of Office paper she has ever believed: *void, no hands.* Her hand rises. The page ends before it
> lands.

## THE GREY CLERK — धूसर लिपिक  (buys eyes)
No full sheet; Ch. 009–012. The Office's voice with a pen in it.

Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/rekhak-vahni-ref.png)

> **Page 002 note:** takes the day-warrant because exactness is his product — no menace on-panel, just

> and to whom, is Page 003's problem.

## THE MOTHER — माँ  (never on panel, never speaks)
No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is the knot she left — and nothing else.

```
— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Point: the one thing of hers that has ever pointed at her daughter /
"Here, then there." — the knot stays tied. Untied, it is only thread.
```

---

**Page note:** the quietest open in the series — a walk, not a quest. The stall keeps (Kessa), the
lantern teaches (Nima), the tether travels (the note, in Ira's inside pocket), the Office buys eyes
(the clerk's day-warrant to Rekhak), and the walk ends where it was always going: behind the low gate,
at a wall of seasons of seals that have gone *around* one small door forever — no lock, no bar, not
one seal on it, the log plate reading *void, no hands*: the only true line the Office ever filed.
*It was never hiding. It was filed.* Hook: **"Knock, keeper."** — with the paid coat arriving exactly
on time to see it.
