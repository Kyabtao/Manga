## IRA SUTAR — इरा सुतार  (PROTAGONIST)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/ira-sutar-ref.png)

```
Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
Mend: cancel target debt instead of paying it / "It is not a song. It is directions."
```
> **Page 001 note:** reads her mother's only written sentence aloud in the Office's voice — and finds
> the legal spine of the chapter: the note on file is a standing no, older than the roll, and clause two
> contains nothing about the word being *yes*. Receives the fifth note on her own step — her mother's
> first request in her life — and the chapter's destination: *year nineteen.*

## KESSA — केसा  (custody, transferred)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png)

```
Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"Forty years it was hers. Nine years it was the box's. Today it is yours."
```
> **Page 001 note:** opens the box unasked and hands the note across, naming the custody transfer in
> one sentence. The empty fold stays hers; the note becomes Ira's. She never opened it in forty years —
> *a pledge is not a letter; you keep it, or you do not* — and she is the first to raise her head when
> the fifth note lands.

## THE MOTHER — माँ  (never on panel, never speaks)
No full sheet, by canon. Her presence on this page is a note on file, a fifth note at dusk, and a knot
on the step.

```
— / Manavkin / Agnikhand / shadowless, cut / unknown; never entered /
Ask: her first request in her daughter's life, in the language that leaves no record /
"Do not enrol me. — S.M." — the no on file, and the directions out of it. Year nineteen.
```

---

**Page note:** the Office posts its definition — *whole* means the school stops — and hands the count to
Rekhak, whose first finding is that the counting-cord is dead inside the Office's own chest. The blank
note leaves Kessa's box at last: *"Do not enrol me"* is the owner's standing no, the one document that
can stop clause two. At dusk the four notes return after eighteen days — and add a fifth. On the step, a
knot tied the old way: not a message, a direction. Hook: *"It is not a song. It is directions."* —
*Year nineteen.*
