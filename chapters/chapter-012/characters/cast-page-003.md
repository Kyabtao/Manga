**Page note:** the knock answers, but nobody answers it — a door filed as nothing answers by not
stopping you. Inside: the archive. The school kept its own roll all along, in thread, its own way;
the twenty-two the Office calls nameless have been on it by length for years; the oldest rod begins
with a year Ira cannot read; and the hook by the door is swept, empty, and speaks her mother's
grammar. The Office's reader enters lawfully, reads by reflex, and then makes the page's choice: the
warrant goes back unwritten — *today's report is a drain-mend* — and the price of the reading is
named. Not coin. **An ask, properly made.** Hook: *"Ask him, keeper."*
