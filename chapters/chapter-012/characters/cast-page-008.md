**Page note:** two professional readers, and a grammar older than both. Nandi's terms (*when the
school ties a hand in, it ties mine*); the open walk past the paid coat; the kept room (lamp cut,
sweep fresh, hook empty); the cradle-hold that ruin never touched. Then the double reveal, dry as a
ledger: the oldest rod's year-grammar is **the Office's own, in an older hand, from before the
Office's year one** — *this is my grammar's grandmother; the Office learned to count here* — and the
first hand the school ever taught is signed where a name would go with **a whorl**: the mark on the
note filed in Ira's register. Discovery made, not decoded. Hook: **"Ask her, keeper."**
