**Page note:** nine feet by eleven, one lamp, no audience. She attends of record and does not sit
(*seating is posture, and posture is testimony*); the cushion waits where it has waited since the
survey; the count reads aloud through the wall and stalls at the nameless. Then the third paper: the
amendment — *the roll means the Office's roll* — pre-cut, pre-sealed, needing the keeper's
counter-signature because a posting that touches the count co-signs with the register. Her counter is
a filing: *the register does not countersign corrections to papers that do not err* — which forces him
to declare his own posting wrong or leave it. He leaves it — and files her refusal in his own book:
the trap has no door because it is a trap that waits. The chain hangs dead on its stand all morning:
grammar is the only thing left in the building that pulls. Hook: **"Sign nothing, keeper."**
