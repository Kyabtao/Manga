**Page note:** taught, walked, tied, entered, named, filed, whole. The lesson finishes; the school
walks in the open; Nima's hands tie the three in and then Nandi (*enter me whole*); the founder's
entry is tightened by the last hand it was waiting for — the whorl enters beside the year. The clerk,
terrible in his fairness, names the machine to the whole basin and stops the clock: **suspended, not
postponed.** The register takes the street's paper at the counter; the box's echo lands; and on the
step at dusk the direction sits intact, the loose entry's space is swept clean, and the four-note
sentence comes back around whole. Hook: **"The roll is whole, keeper."** — *Year nineteen.*
