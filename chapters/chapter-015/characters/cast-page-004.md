## NANDI (the reader — the school declines a purchase)
Full sheet (Ch. 006): [`../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md`](../../chapter-006/characters/nandi.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png`](../../chapter-006/characters/nandi-ref.png)

```
Nandi / Manav / Agnikhand / sight-white, lens-borne / reads, and is paid /
Read: thread-years at the price of an ask, properly made /
"A permission that forbids counting is not a permission. It is a purchase."
```
> **Page 004 note:** rules from the table's head without a keeper in the chair, answers the licence in
> four knots and gives it back, writes the licensed woman into the school's own book in the old
> grammar while the Office's leaf lies beside the page — and then hands the reading to Ira so the room
> hears the entry aloud. *You are counted here; that has never depended on what you carry.*

## THE LICENSED WOMAN (one of ours, sealed and paid)
No full sheet; Ch. 011–015, of the counting-lesson women. The first of the school's own to carry an
Office leaf with her name on it, and the first to be paid for teaching.

```
The Licensed Woman / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, learning / owed a wage, and taking it /
Teach: the Office's grammar, sealed and lawful, for bread /
"If that is a betrayal, then it is one I can feed my sister with."
```
> **Page 004 note:** the page's honest knife. The licence came to her by name rather than to the
> school, so she is not deserting anything — she is accepting lawful, paid work after a winter of
> unpaid teaching, and she has a sister. Played straight: steady, undefiant, no tears; the room's only
> reaction is one woman's hand stopping halfway through a knot. She sits down again among the benches
> at the end of the page and lets her grip be corrected out of habit.

```
Ira Sutar / Manavkin / Agnikhand / ash-grey braided, blank core / zero, and climbing /
```

> **Page 004 note:** enters the answer as it is tied, reads the school's entry aloud at Nandi's
> insistence so nobody can say it was done quietly, and closes the day with the register's coldest
> line: the school now contains its own licensed teacher, and the count proves it — which is precisely
> what the licence was written to prevent.

## KESSA (the till — the doorway arithmetic)
Full sheet (Ch. 001): [`../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md`](../../chapter-001/characters/kessa.md) · Art: [`../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png`](../../chapter-001/characters/kessa-ref.png)

```
Kessa / Kshudra / Agnikhand / knotted grey / light /
Appraise: reveal and cancel target debt; her knots cannot be cut /
"The Office pays her. The till does not. Then the till has nothing to forgive her for."
```
> **Page 004 note:** arrives in the doorway with the till's slate and the exact accounting nobody else

> coming from the Office and not from the counter. She is the page's instrument for making the split
> a matter of arithmetic rather than loyalty, and she is told to sit down.

## THE READER (the counting-lesson women — the answer tied and published)
No full sheet; Ch. 011–015. Ties the school's four-knot answer through the licence's corner and nails
it up beside the term, then stands back while the street reads it unassisted.

```
The Reader / Manav / Agnikhand / undyed, learning / owes lessons, and is owed them /
Read: everything delivered, aloud, at the counter of record /
"Grammar, yes. Count, no. That is the shortest way to say a thing we have already said once."
```
> **Page 004 note:** the closing of the school's public correspondence with the Office: answered in
> the same four knots used on the charter, returned to the same board, and left for the quarter to
> read alone — the first Office-grammar joke the street gets without help.

## THE MACHINE (off-panel — the licence addressed by name)
No full sheet; the Office entire. Present this page as nine leaves with nine names on them, one of
them accepted.

```
The Machine / — / Agnikhand / nineteen years of white spaces / counts, and does not refuse /
License: not the school — the women, one leaf at a time, so that each may choose /
"An unfiled roll is an uncounted school."
```
> **Page 004 note:** never appears, never speaks, and wins its point anyway: the school refuses the
> purchase, and the Office gets a licensed teacher inside the school's benches by addressing her
> rather than the institution. The counter's answer is not exclusion but a ledger line, and that is
> what the page is for.
